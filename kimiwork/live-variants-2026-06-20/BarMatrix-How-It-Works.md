---

### Body Copy

> After the forensic tag identifies your trap, Flagship assigns you a **repair task**. Not a 50-question marathon. Not a 2-hour lecture. One targeted task that hits the same tension point until the pattern stops misfiring.
>
> Flagship uses three repair tools:

---

### Repair Tool 1: Red-Zone Drills

> **Red-Zone Drills**
>
> Targeted micro-sets of 8–12 questions that all hit the same tension point as your trap pattern. If your top trap is "Overbroad Hearsay Myth," the drill gives you 8–12 questions where the purpose of the offer is the key to the correct answer. You practice catching the trigger until the pattern rewires.
>
> Each drill takes 15–20 minutes. One drill per day. That's it.

---

### Repair Tool 2: Boot Camps

> **Boot Camps**
>
> Intensive repair sessions for your highest-priority traps. A deep dive into the pattern, the trigger facts, the structural moves the MBE uses, and the common variations. Boot Camps are 30–45 minutes and are assigned when a trap is particularly stubborn or cross-subject.

---

### Repair Tool 3: Timed Mixed Sets

> **Timed Mixed Sets**
>
> Exam-simulation sets that test whether the repair stuck. If you still fall for the trap, Flagship assigns a deeper drill. If you catch it consistently, the Pattern Mastery Board advances and Flagship moves to your next trap.
>
> This is the verification step. The repair is not complete until you prove it under pressure.

---

### The Pattern Mastery Board

> **Pattern Mastery Board**
>
> A real-time visualization of your repair progress. Watch your red zones turn green as you stop falling for each trap. See your top 3 traps shrink, your mid-tier traps stabilize, and your mastery percentage climb.
>
> The Pattern Mastery Board is not a score. It is a **pattern tracker.** It tells you which traps are repaired, which are in progress, and which are still active.

### Section Headline

> ## How it works, answered.

---

### Q1: Is the diagnostic really a "diagnostic" or is it just a practice test?

> It is a diagnostic, not a practice test. A practice test measures your score. The diagnostic measures your **patterns**. It is weighted toward the highest-attractiveness wrong answers — the ones the MBE engineers to be most tempting. The goal is to surface your trap patterns, not to give you a score.

---

### Q2: How is the Red-Zone Map different from a subject-level performance report?

> A subject-level report (e.g., "You are weak in Evidence") tells you where you miss. The Red-Zone Map tells you **why** you miss. It maps your patterns across subjects, not within them. Your top trap may appear in Evidence, Criminal Procedure, and Contracts — but the subject is irrelevant. The pattern is the problem.

---

### Q3: What if I don't agree with my forensic tag?

> The forensic tag is based on your answer choice and the structural analysis of the question. If you believe the tag is wrong, you can flag it for review. But in most cases, students say the tag is "weirdly accurate" — it describes exactly what they were thinking when they picked the wrong answer.

---

### Q4: How long does each repair drill take?

> 15–20 minutes. Each drill is a targeted micro-set of 8–12 questions. One drill per day. If you have time for your course's daily assignment, you have time for one BarMatrix repair task.

---

### Q5: What happens when I finish repairing all my traps?

> The Red-Zone Map updates as you repair. When your top traps are fixed, Flagship moves to the next tier. Most students find that their "red zones" shrink significantly within 3–4 weeks. At that point, the focus shifts to maintenance: timed mixed sets, boot camps for stubborn patterns, and pre-exam simulation.

---

### Q6: Does this work for repeat takers?

> Yes. Repeat takers are often the best fit for BarMatrix because they already know the rules. Their problem is not knowledge — it is pattern repetition. The diagnostic identifies the traps they've been falling for across multiple attempts, and the repair path is designed to break those patterns.

---

### Q7: Can I use this without a bar course?

> BarMatrix is designed to sit alongside a full bar course. It does not teach the rules from scratch. If you are not enrolled in a course and need to learn the law, BarMatrix is not the right tool. Start with a full course, then add BarMatrix as the repair layer.
