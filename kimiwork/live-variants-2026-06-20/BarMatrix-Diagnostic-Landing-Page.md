Production-ready copy and layout notes for the free MBE Trap Diagnostic landing page. This is the primary conversion page for organic traffic, paid ads, Reddit, and email referrals. The goal is simple: get the visitor to start the 15-minute diagnostic with zero friction.

>

But neither of them diagnoses *why* your wrong answers are attractive. Neither of them maps the specific trap patterns that the MBE engineers to catch you — and reuses, cycle after cycle, across subjects and questions.

---

### The 5 Trap Patterns (Visual List)

> **The five traps that cost students the most points:**
>
> 1. **Overbroad Hearsay Myth** — You see "out of court" and assume "hearsay." You never check the purpose of the offer.
> 2. **Stale Rule** — You recognize the doctrine and apply the old rule. But the trigger fact changed: new party, different timeline, modified jurisdiction.
> 3. **Wrong Timing** — You know the rule but miss the timing trigger in the facts. The statute of limitations has run. The July 2026 cohort window passed.
> 4. **Wrong Party** — You apply the rule to the wrong person. The defendant lacks standing. The plaintiff has no capacity.
> 5. **Narrowing to Two and Picking Wrong** — You eliminate two answers, pick the one that feels right, and it's wrong. The MBE engineered both to look plausible. Your instinct is the trap.

## A/B Testing Ideas for Diagnostic Landing Page

| Test | Variant A | Variant B | Hypothesis |
|------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Hero headline** | "Your MBE score is stuck. We know why." | "15 minutes. No credit card. Your personal MBE trap profile." | Specificity (time + no card) outperforms problem statement |
| **Hero visual** | Red-Zone Map mockup | Diagnostic screenshot | Product-in-action outperforms abstract visualization |
| **CTA button text** | "Start Free Diagnostic" | "See My Red Zones" | Product-specific CTA outperforms generic action |
| **Trust signals** | 5 bullet points (as written) | 3 bullet points (most important only) | Fewer signals = less clutter, higher CTA visibility |
| **FAQ format** | Accordion (collapsed) | All expanded | Expanded FAQ improves SEO and reduces anxiety |
| **Social proof placement** | Below "The Method" | Above "The Problem" | Early social proof increases trust before pain amplification |
| **Final CTA** | "Your traps are already mapped" | "Don't bring the same traps into the exam" | Fear-driven close outperforms curiosity-driven close |
| **Email capture** | Optional (no email required) | Required (email to see results) | Optional email increases diagnostic starts; required email increases nurture list but reduces starts |
