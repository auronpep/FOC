# BarMatrix "Who It's For" Page Copy

## Document Purpose

Production-ready copy and layout notes for the BarMatrix "Who It's For" page. This page qualifies the audience — clearly defining who should use BarMatrix and who should not. It reduces mismatched expectations, decreases refunds, and builds trust through honest disqualification.

**Target audience:** Students who are unsure whether BarMatrix is the right tool for their situation. They may be first-time takers, repeat takers, working students, or students already enrolled in a full course.  
**Primary goal:** Diagnostic starts (for qualified visitors).  
**Secondary goal:** Honest disqualification (for unqualified visitors — they leave without wasting time).  
**Tone:** Honest, direct, slightly contrarian. "We are not for everyone. Here's who we are for."

---

### Section Headline

> ## Not built for you if...

---

### Card 1: The Full Course Seeker

> **🚫 The Full Course Seeker**
>
> You need a comprehensive bar review course that covers all components: MBE, essays, performance tests, and state-specific subjects. You need lectures, outlines, daily assignments, and essay grading.
>
> **This is you if:**
> - You are looking for a one-stop shop for all bar prep
> - You need essay grading or PT preparation
> - You want a structured daily schedule covering all subjects
> - You are not enrolled in any bar course and need to start from scratch
>
> **BarMatrix is not for you.** We are MBE-only. We do not offer lectures, outlines, essay grading, or PT prep. Try Barbri, Themis, Kaplan, or BarMax for a full course.

---

### Card 2: The Lecture Learner

> **🚫 The Lecture Learner**
>
> You learn best by listening to lectures or watching video explanations. You need someone to walk you through the rules, the exceptions, and the edge cases. You want a professor explaining the law to you.
>
> **This is you if:**
> - You need audio or video lectures to understand the law
> - You want a teacher explaining the rules, not a tool diagnosing your mistakes
> - You are still learning the foundational rules and need instruction
> - You prefer passive learning (listening/watching) to active practice
>
> **BarMatrix is not for you.** We do not offer lectures. We assume you already know the rules (from your course or prior study). We focus on the application layer: catching the traps. Try BarMax (audio lectures) or your full course for rule instruction.

---

### Card 3: The Guarantor

> **🚫 The Guarantor**
>
> You want a guaranteed pass. You want a refund if you fail. You want a specific score promise: "Do this and you will get 70%." You need certainty.
>
> **This is you if:**
> - You will only buy a product with a pass guarantee
> - You want a specific score promise (e.g., "You will improve by 10 points")
> - You need a refund policy as a safety net
> - You are uncomfortable with risk or uncertainty
>
> **BarMatrix is not for you.** We do not guarantee outcomes. We guarantee a diagnosis: you will know your top trap patterns after the free diagnostic. Whether you repair them is up to you. We will not make promises we cannot control. Try AdaptiBar (105% guarantee) or BarMax (pass guarantee) if you need a safety net.

---

### Card 4: The Early Starter

> **🚫 The Early Starter**
>
> You are a 1L or 2L who wants to get a head start on bar prep. You have not yet taken bar-specific courses. You need to learn the foundational rules before you can apply them.
>
> **This is you if:**
> - You are more than 6 months away from the bar exam
> - You have not yet taken Evidence, Criminal Procedure, or other MBE subjects
> - You need to learn the black-letter law from scratch
> - You are looking for a "preview" of bar prep, not a repair system
>
> **BarMatrix is not for you.** Our diagnostic assumes you have a baseline understanding of MBE rules. If you don't know the hearsay rule yet, we cannot diagnose why you fall for the hearsay trap. Start with your bar course or a general MBE supplement when you are closer to the exam.

---

### Card 5: The Already Passing

> **🚫 The Already Passing**
>
> Your MBE score is already 70%+. You are consistently getting questions right. You don't feel stuck. You are confident in your MBE performance and just need to maintain.
>
> **This is you if:**
> - Your MBE percentage is 70% or higher and stable
> - You rarely narrow to two and pick wrong
> - You can identify your own mistakes without help
> - You just need occasional practice, not pattern repair
>
> **BarMatrix is not for you.** You don't need a repair system. You need maintenance. Use AdaptiBar or UWorld for occasional practice questions. Save your money. BarMatrix is for students who are stuck, not students who are soaring.

### Q1: I'm a first-time taker. Is BarMatrix for me?

> If your MBE score is stuck despite doing practice questions, yes. If you are scoring consistently above 70% and feel confident, probably not. The diagnostic is free — take it and see if your patterns are the problem.

---

### Q2: I'm a repeat taker. Is BarMatrix worth it?

> Repeat takers are often the best fit. If your MBE score was the reason you failed, and you have already used a full course or supplement, the diagnostic will show you the traps you've been repeating. The repair path is designed to break them.

---

### Q3: I work full-time. Do I have time for this?

> Each repair task is 15–20 minutes. One task per day. The diagnostic is 15 minutes. If you have time for your course's daily assignment, you have time for BarMatrix.

---

### Q4: I already have AdaptiBar. Do I need BarMatrix too?

> Many students use both. AdaptiBar for volume and practice. BarMatrix for pattern diagnosis and repair. They are complementary. If your score is stuck despite AdaptiBar, the diagnostic will show you why.

---

### Q5: What if I take the diagnostic and my traps don't seem like a big deal?

> Then BarMatrix is probably not the right tool for you. The diagnostic is designed to be honest. If your patterns are minor or random, we will tell you. We would rather you not buy than buy and be disappointed.

---

### Q6: Can I use BarMatrix without a bar course?

> BarMatrix is designed as a companion, not a standalone. If you are not enrolled in a course and need to learn the rules from scratch, start with a full course first. Add BarMatrix as the repair layer once you have the foundation.

---

### Q7: What if I'm not sure after the diagnostic?

> Don't buy. The diagnostic is free. The decision is yours. If you are unsure, use the self-diagnosis method I outlined in the "How It Works" page and see if your score improves on its own. You can always come back.
