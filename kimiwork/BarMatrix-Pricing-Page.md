# BarMatrix Pricing Page Copy

## Document Purpose

Production-ready copy and layout notes for the BarMatrix Flagship pricing page. This page converts diagnostic completers and direct visitors into paid Flagship enrollments. It must handle the $999/$499 price point, the "why is this worth it?" objection, and the "will this conflict with my course?" hesitation.

**Target audience:** Students who have completed the free diagnostic (primary) or who have heard about BarMatrix and are comparing options (secondary).  
**Primary goal:** Flagship purchases (Stripe checkout).  
**Secondary goal:** Diagnostic starts (for visitors who skipped the diagnostic).  
**Exit condition:** Purchase or bounce.

> Yes. BarMatrix is designed as a companion to Barbri, Themis, Kaplan, or any full course. Most students do their course's daily assignment first, then spend 15–30 minutes on one BarMatrix repair task. It does not conflict with your course's schedule or content.

> Many students use both. AdaptiBar and BarMax are excellent for question volume and rule learning. BarMatrix is for pattern diagnosis and repair. They are complementary, not competitive. Use your question bank for practice. Use BarMatrix to fix the traps that keep your score stuck.

## A/B Testing Ideas for Pricing Page

| Test | Variant A | Variant B | Hypothesis |
|------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| **Hero headline** | "BarMatrix Flagship — July 2026 Founding Cohort" | "Fix your top MBE trap patterns. One repair task at a time." | Benefit-driven headline outperforms product-name headline |
| **Price presentation** | "$499 (was $999)" | "$250 today + $249 in 30 days" | Payment plan reduces sticker shock and increases conversion |
| **"Most Popular" badge** | On $499 card | On payment plan card | Badge placement influences choice |
| **Value proposition section** | ROI calculation (11 points) | Testimonial-focused (Sarah's story) | Social proof outperforms math for emotional buyers |
| **Companion positioning** | Included on page | Removed from page | Companion framing reduces anxiety for course users |
| **FAQ** | Accordion (collapsed) | All expanded | Expanded FAQ increases trust and reduces bounce |
| **Final CTA** | "Enroll Now — $499" | "Fix My Traps Before the Exam" | Action-oriented CTA outperforms price-oriented CTA |
| **Countdown timer** | Real countdown | Static text "Enrollment closes [DATE]" | Real countdown creates urgency; static text feels more honest |
| **Enrollment cap** | "500 spots remaining" | No cap mentioned | Scarcity increases urgency; but false scarcity destroys trust |
| **Secondary CTA** | "Start with the free diagnostic" | "See the full feature list" | Free diagnostic reduces friction for hesitant buyers |
