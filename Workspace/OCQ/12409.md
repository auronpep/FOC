Here is Question Number 12409. A farmer owned 500 acres of land on which she grew wheat. By a valid written contract, she agreed to deliver all her wheat to a company to be used by that company in the production of bread for sale to the general public. Because the bread contained slivers of the blade from the farmer's harvesting machine, the customer who bought the sandwich lacerated the lining of his throat when he tried to eat it.
