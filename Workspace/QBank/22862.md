


BARMATRIX Q#: 22862
Outline_code: 94030100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Removal to Federal Court
Question: A State A doctor was injured by a faulty surgical saw while visiting a hospital in State B. He sued the State B hospital and the State B nurse who handed him the saw in State A state court for $250,000. The lawyer for the hospital sought to have the case removed to federal court, but the nurse refused. Sixty days later, the doctor joined the saw's manufacturer as a defendant. The manufacturer convinced both the hospital and the nurse that removal to federal court was appropriate and filed a notice of removal 28 days after the manufacturer was served. The hospital and nurse both joined in the manufacturer's removal notice. May the claim be removed to federal court?
Answer A: A: Yes, because removal can occur at any time before trial with the approval of all defendants.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the manufacturer's removal was made 28 days after the manufacturer's service.
Answer C: C: No, because the manufacturer's removal was made more than 25 days after the manufacturer's service.
Answer D: D: No, because over 60 days have passed since the doctor initiated the lawsuit.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Defendants may join in another defendant's timely removal. When the manufacturer was served, he had 30 days for removal. He removed in 28 days, and at that point, the other defendants could join in the removal even if they didn't do so earlier. A is incorrect because defendants cannot remove at any time before trial. C and D are incorrect because the manufacturer had 30 days for removal and the other defendants could join that removal.
