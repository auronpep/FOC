Question: Mary agreed to buy 100 woven baskets from a seller for $5,000. The seller delivered 100 baskets, but 15 had loose handles. Mary inspected the baskets, kept all of them, resold most of them, and promptly notified the seller of the defect. Mary then refused to pay anything. Which result is most likely under Article 2?
Answer A: Mary owes nothing because the goods were imperfect.
Answer B: Mary may reject the goods after keeping and reselling them because she gave notice.
Answer C: Mary must pay the contract price, but may recover or offset damages for the nonconforming baskets.
Answer D: Mary owes only the reasonable resale value of the baskets, not the contract price.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: After accepting goods, a buyer generally may not reject them simply because they were nonconforming. The buyer must pay the contract price for accepted goods, but may seek damages or an offset for the breach if proper notice is given.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because acceptance does not allow the buyer to keep goods without paying. B: B is wrong because keeping and reselling the goods is inconsistent with rejection. D: D is wrong because the contract price remains the baseline for accepted goods, subject to damages.
