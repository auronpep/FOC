Outline: Power of termination
Question: Moses conveyed Gardenacre “to Aaron and his heirs, but if the land is ever used as a theater, Moses may enter and terminate the estate.” Before any theater use occurred, Moses executed a valid written release to Aaron of “all rights of entry and powers of termination retained in Gardenacre.” No one else has any future interest. What estate does Aaron hold after the release?
Answer A: A fee simple subject to condition subsequent, because the original deed still mentions theater use.
Answer B: A fee simple absolute, because the retained power of termination has been released to the present owner.
Answer C: A fee simple determinable, because the release changes the right of entry into a possibility of reverter.
Answer D: A life estate, because the release removed only Moses's enforcement remedy.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Aaron holds a fee simple absolute. The only future interest limiting Aaron's estate was Moses's right of entry. When Moses validly released that power to Aaron, the condition was extinguished and Aaron's ownership became absolute.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the controlling question is whether any enforceable future interest remains after the release. C: C is wrong because releasing a power of termination does not create a possibility of reverter. D: D is wrong because neither deed created a life estate.
lb_rule_reference: Release of power of termination / merger into full fee
