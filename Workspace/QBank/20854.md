Question: Daniel conveyed Stoneacre “to Jonah for as long as the land is used for a school, and when the land is no longer used for a school, it shall revert to Daniel.” Jonah later stopped operating the school and used the building only for storage. Who owns Stoneacre after the school use ends?
Answer A: Jonah, because storage is related to school operations.
Answer B: Jonah, unless Daniel files a lawsuit to terminate the estate.
Answer C: Daniel and Jonah as tenants in common.
Answer D: Daniel, because the deed created an automatically terminating estate.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: “For as long as” is durational language, and the deed expressly says the land shall revert when school use ends. Jonah held a fee simple determinable. When the school use ended, the estate automatically terminated and title reverted to Daniel.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the deed required use as a school, not merely storage. B: B is wrong because automatic termination does not require reentry. C: C is wrong because the deed creates successive interests, not co-ownership.
lb_rule_reference: Fee simple determinable / express reverter
