Question: A state requires voters to present one of several forms of free government-issued identification at the polls. Voters who lack identification may cast a provisional ballot and verify identity within a short period. The rule is facially neutral and does not bar a class of voters from voting. What is the best scrutiny framework?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny automatically applies because every election rule affects voting
Answer B: No review applies because election administration is a political question
Answer C: A balancing framework applies because the rule is a reasonable, nondiscriminatory election regulation rather than a severe burden
Answer D: Rational basis always applies to any election regulation
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The rule regulates election mechanics and includes free ID and provisional-ballot options, so ordinary election balancing is the likely framework.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Ordinary, nondiscriminatory election regulations are balanced against state interests rather than automatically receiving strict scrutiny. B: Courts regularly review election-administration rules under constitutional standards. D: Severe burdens on voting can trigger strict scrutiny; ordinary rules use balancing, not a flat rational-basis rule.
lb_rule_reference: Severe burdens on voting receive strict scrutiny, but reasonable, nondiscriminatory election-administration rules are evaluated through a balancing framework rather than automatic strict scrutiny.
