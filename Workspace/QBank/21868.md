Question: A buyer, Aaron, orders 200 blue cups from a merchant seller, Deborah. Deborah promptly ships 200 red cups with a note saying, “We are out of blue cups; these red cups are sent only as an accommodation if you can use them.” Aaron rejects the red cups and sues Deborah for breach, arguing that any shipment accepts a goods order. What is the best analysis under Article 2?
Answer A: Deborah accepted and breached because every nonconforming shipment is acceptance and breach.
Answer B: Deborah's shipment was a counteroffer because she seasonably notified Aaron that the nonconforming goods were offered only as an accommodation.
Answer C: No contract can ever be formed by shipment of goods.
Answer D: A contract formed only if Aaron signed a new mirror-image acceptance of the red cups.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A nonconforming shipment usually operates as acceptance and breach unless the seller seasonably notifies the buyer that the shipment is offered only as an accommodation. Deborah's note made the red-cup shipment a counteroffer rather than an acceptance.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because it ignores the accommodation notice. C: C is wrong because conforming shipment can accept, and nonconforming shipment can accept absent accommodation notice. D: D is wrong because Article 2 does not require common-law mirror-image acceptance language for the accommodation analysis.
lb_rule_reference: UCC_goods
lb_student_diagnostic: Student treated all shipments as acceptance or ignored the accommodation notice.
