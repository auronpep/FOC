Question: Vera sends a text to a shop owner: "Leave $2,000 in the mailbox tomorrow or I will break your windows next week." The owner leaves the money in the mailbox the next day while Vera is nowhere nearby. Vera later picks it up. What is the best theft-related offense?
Answer A: Robbery, because the owner acted out of fear
Answer B: Extortion, because Vera obtained property through a wrongful future threat
Answer C: Larceny only, because Vera physically picked up the money from the mailbox
Answer D: No offense, because the owner voluntarily left the money
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Vera coerced a later transfer by threatening future property damage. That is the extortion route, not a robbery taking.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The threat was for future harm and the money was not taken from the person or immediate presence by immediate force or intimidation. C: The defining feature is the wrongful threat that induced the owner to place the money for Vera. D: A transfer made under wrongful threat is coerced, not clean consent.
lb_rule_reference: Robbery requires force or intimidation used in taking property from the person or immediate presence; extortion can involve threats of future harm causing a later transfer.
