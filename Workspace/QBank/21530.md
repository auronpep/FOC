Outline: Larceny by trick
Question: Rita tells Sam that a cashier's check is genuine and immediately payable. Rita knows the check is counterfeit. Relying on the statement, Sam signs over title to his used car and hands Rita the keys. What is Rita's best theft offense?
Answer A: False pretenses, because Rita obtained title by a false statement of present fact
Answer B: Larceny by trick, because every fraud-induced handover is larceny by trick
Answer C: Embezzlement, because Rita later converted the car
Answer D: No offense, because Sam voluntarily signed the title
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Sam intended to transfer title to the car because of Rita's lie about the check's validity.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Larceny by trick involves possession only; title passed here. C: Rita did not first receive lawful possession as a bailee or agent. D: Consent induced by a material false present fact supports false pretenses.
lb_rule_reference: When a victim intends to transfer title because of the defendant's false statement of present or past fact, the offense is false pretenses rather than larceny by trick.
