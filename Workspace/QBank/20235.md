Outline: Burden of production and burden of persuasion
Question: Mary sues Reuben in federal court for breach of contract. Reuben asserts the affirmative defense that Mary released the claim. Reuben produces a signed release, and Mary testifies that her signature was obtained by fraud. Reuben argues that once he produced the release, Mary had to disprove the release. Which statement is correct?
Answer A: Producing the release satisfies Reuben's burden of persuasion.
Answer B: Mary must disprove the release because she is the plaintiff.
Answer C: Reuben may satisfy a burden of production by producing the release, but he retains the burden of persuasion on his affirmative defense.
Answer D: The court must disregard Mary's fraud testimony because it contradicts a writing.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A party asserting an affirmative defense generally bears the burden of proving it. Producing evidence of a release may satisfy a production burden, but it does not automatically shift the burden of persuasion to Mary.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because producing evidence is not the same as persuading the factfinder. B: B is wrong because the plaintiff does not automatically bear the burden to disprove an affirmative defense. D: D is wrong because testimony challenging the validity of a release is not automatically disregarded.
