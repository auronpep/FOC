Question: In a diversity medical-malpractice action, the plaintiff seeks discovery of communications between the defendant doctor and a peer-review committee. The governing state law recognizes a peer-review privilege for malpractice claims. No federal claim is pleaded. Which privilege law should the federal court apply?
Answer A: Federal common-law privilege, because the courthouse is federal
Answer B: State privilege law, because state law supplies the rule of decision
Answer C: The privilege law of the state where the doctor went to medical school
Answer D: No privilege law, because discovery relevance defeats all privileges
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The malpractice claim is state-law only, so state privilege law controls the privilege question.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: When state law supplies the rule of decision in a civil case, state privilege law governs. C: Training location does not choose privilege law for this federal diversity action. D: Privileged matter is protected even when relevant.
lb_rule_reference: Under the federal evidence privilege source rule, state privilege law applies to claims or defenses for which state law supplies the rule of decision.
