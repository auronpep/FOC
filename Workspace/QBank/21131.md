Question: Pavel sends Rosa an encrypted message asking her to set fire to a rival's delivery van that night. Rosa reads the message and immediately refuses. Pavel is arrested before he contacts anyone else. He is charged with solicitation to commit arson. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Pavel is not guilty because Rosa refused and no agreement formed
Answer B: Pavel is guilty because the request was communicated with intent that Rosa commit the crime
Answer C: Pavel is not guilty because he did not personally attempt to burn the van
Answer D: Pavel is guilty only if Rosa was paid in advance
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Solicitation requires intentional communication seeking another person's criminal conduct. Rosa's refusal does not undo the completed solicitation.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Solicitation is not conspiracy. The request was communicated with intent that Rosa commit arson, so solicitation was complete when Rosa received it. C: The charge is solicitation. Solicitation punishes the intentional request itself. D: Compensation is evidence of intent, not an element of solicitation.
lb_rule_reference: Solicitation is complete when the defendant intentionally communicates a request, command, or encouragement that another person commit a crime, regardless of whether the other person agrees.
