Question: A buyer orders 1,000 specially packaged bottles from a merchant seller and requests prompt action. The seller immediately begins packaging the bottles, a reasonable way to accept under the circumstances, but does not notify the buyer for several weeks. The buyer, believing no one accepted the order, obtains the bottles elsewhere and cancels. Under Article 2, what is the buyer's best argument?
Answer A: The seller could accept only by completed shipment, so beginning performance could never matter.
Answer B: The buyer may treat the offer as having lapsed before acceptance if the seller began performance but failed to notify the buyer of acceptance within a reasonable time.
Answer C: The seller accepted only if it privately intended to be bound.
Answer D: The buyer was bound forever because beginning performance always creates an irrevocable option without any notice concern.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Under Article 2, when beginning requested performance is a reasonable mode of acceptance, the offeror who is not notified of acceptance within a reasonable time may treat the offer as having lapsed before acceptance. The point is notice of acceptance when performance has begun but is not apparent to the offeror.
