Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Domicile
Question: Maya grew up in Nevada and keeps her voter registration, driver's license, and permanent home there. She rents an apartment in Oregon for a nine-month medical fellowship and plans to return to Nevada afterward. She sues an Oregon citizen in federal court for $90,000 under state law. For diversity purposes, what is Maya's citizenship?
Answer A: Oregon, because she currently resides there
Answer B: Both Oregon and Nevada, because individuals have citizenship wherever they live during a year
Answer C: Nevada, because she remains domiciled there despite temporary residence in Oregon
Answer D: No state, because she is away from her permanent home
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Maya is physically in Oregon temporarily and lacks intent to remain there indefinitely.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Residence alone does not equal domicile. B: Individuals generally have one domicile at a time for diversity citizenship. D: Temporary absence does not erase domicile.
lb_rule_reference: A natural person's state citizenship is domicile: physical presence plus intent to remain indefinitely.
