Outline: Motion for judgment on the pleadings
Question: A diversity plaintiff seeks to amend after limitations expires. Federal Rule 15(c)(1)(B) may not independently allow relation back, but the state law supplying the statute of limitations has a more generous relation-back rule that would allow the amendment. May the federal court consider the state relation-back rule?
Answer A: Yes, Rule 15(c)(1)(A) permits relation back when the limitations law allows it
Answer B: No, federal court can never use state relation-back law
Answer C: No, because amendments after limitations are always barred
Answer D: Yes, but only if the defendant consents
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 15(c) includes a route using the law that supplies the limitations period.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Rule 15(c)(1)(A) expressly points to it. C: Relation back is the exception being tested. D: Consent is not required if the rule permits relation back.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 15(c)(1)(A) can incorporate a more generous relation-back rule from the limitations law.
