Question: In a federal employment trial, Hannah testifies that she saw Boaz post a notice on the break-room door. Boaz objects that Hannah cannot testify because the notice itself was taken down and not offered. Hannah is not testifying to the exact wording of the notice, only that Boaz posted a notice. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because the notice itself is required for any testimony about it.
Answer B: Sustain the objection because testimony about a posted notice is always hearsay.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because Hannah may testify from personal knowledge that she saw Boaz post a notice.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if Hannah can recite the exact words.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Hannah's testimony proves an observed event: that Boaz posted a notice. Because she is not proving the notice's contents, the missing document does not bar her personal-knowledge testimony.
