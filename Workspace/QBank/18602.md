Outline: Expectation damages
Question: Eli contracted to sell 300 bags of seed to Hannah for $40 per bag. Hannah repudiated before delivery. At the time and place for tender, the market price was $35 per bag. Eli did not resell the seed but incurred $250 in reasonable incidental damages and saved no expenses. What is Eli's damages measure under the ordinary seller market-damages rule?
Answer A: $250, the incidental damages only.
Answer B: $1,500, the contract-market differential only.
Answer C: $12,000, the full contract price.
Answer D: $1,750, the contract-market differential plus incidental damages.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The seller's ordinary market measure is the unpaid contract price minus the market price at the time and place for tender, plus incidental damages, less expenses saved. The differential is $1,500 (($40 - $35) × 300), plus $250 incidental damages, for $1,750.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because it omits the contract-market differential. B: B is wrong because it omits recoverable incidental damages. C: C is wrong because a seller cannot recover the full price merely because the buyer repudiated when the goods are resellable and the price-action requirements are not met.
lb_rule_reference: UCC §2-708(1)
