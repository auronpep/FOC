Question: Isaac conveyed Whiteacre “to Rebekah and her heirs, but if Rebekah stops living on Whiteacre before she turns 30, then to Jacob and his heirs.” Rebekah is 25, lives on Whiteacre, and has not breached the condition. Which statement best describes the current interests?
Answer A: Rebekah has a fee simple subject to executory limitation, and Jacob has a shifting executory interest.
Answer B: Rebekah has a fee simple determinable, and Isaac has a possibility of reverter.
Answer C: Jacob has a contingent remainder because his possession is postponed.
Answer D: Rebekah has only a life estate because the deed refers to her age.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Rebekah has a present fee simple subject to executory limitation. She owns and possesses Whiteacre now, but her estate may be divested if she stops living there before age 30. Jacob's future interest is shifting because it would cut short Rebekah's prior estate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because the future interest is in Jacob, not Isaac, and the deed does not create a possibility of reverter. C: C is wrong because Jacob's interest follows a fee simple and would divest it; it is not a remainder. D: D is wrong because the age reference is a condition, not a measuring life for a life estate.
lb_rule_reference: Present possessory fee / shifting executory interest before breach
