Question: A state requires independent candidates to file nominating petitions with a modest number of signatures by a deadline several months before the general election. The rule applies to all independent candidates and is designed to prevent voter confusion. Which standard is most appropriate?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny automatically applies because every ballot-access rule burdens voting
Answer B: A balancing framework applies unless the burden is severe or discriminatory
Answer C: No review applies because election administration is entirely political
Answer D: Intermediate scrutiny applies because candidates are quasi-suspect
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Ordinary election-administration rules are assessed by weighing burden against state interests.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Reasonable, nondiscriminatory election rules are not automatically subject to strict scrutiny. C: Courts review many ballot-access and voting-burden claims. D: Candidate status is not a quasi-suspect classification.
lb_rule_reference: Severe burdens on voting or ballot access receive strict scrutiny, but reasonable, nondiscriminatory election regulations are evaluated through a balancing framework.
