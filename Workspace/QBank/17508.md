


BARMATRIX Q#: 17508
Outline_code: 44040300
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Constitutional Law
Subtopic: Individual Rights
Outline: Equal Protection Clause
Question: In which one of the following fact situations is the defendant most likely to be properly convicted of violating a state law that prohibits depriving any person of a right conferred by the Equal Protection Clause of the Fourteenth Amendment to the United States Constitution?
Answer A: A: The defendant, the president of a university operated by a religious organization, refused to admit certain students solely because of their religion.
Answer B: B: The defendant, a federal court official, prevented certain persons from serving on federal juries solely because of their race.
Answer C: C: The defendant, the principal of a public high school, expelled certain students solely because they were pregnant.
Answer D: D: The defendant, the vice president of a bank, refused to grant loans to certain applicants solely because of their place of national origin.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The defendant in A is employed by a religious organization. The defendant in B is a federal official. The defendant in D works in private enterprise. Since none of them is an agency of the state, A, B, and D are incorrect.
