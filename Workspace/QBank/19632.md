Outline: Uniform Commercial Code (UCC)
Question: Daniel, a manufacturer, agreed to build a custom grain-sorting machine for Miriam for $60,000. The machine would be movable when finished. Daniel designed the machine to Miriam's specifications, but the price was for the completed machine rather than consulting services. Before delivery, the parties disputed whether Article 2 applied. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Article 2 applies because specially manufactured movable goods are still goods.
Answer B: Common law applies because custom design work always turns the transaction into a services contract.
Answer C: No contract can form until the machine is completed and identified by serial number.
Answer D: Article 2 cannot apply unless the machine is a standard off-the-shelf product.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Article 2 defines goods to include specially manufactured goods if they are movable at the time of identification to the contract. The fact that Daniel designed and manufactured the machine to Miriam's specifications does not by itself remove the goods transaction from Article 2.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because customization does not automatically make the transaction service-dominant. C: C is wrong because a contract to sell future or specially manufactured goods can form before completion. D: D is wrong because Article 2 is not limited to off-the-shelf goods.
