Outline: Issue and Claim Preclusion
Question: In a small first suit worth $500, a manufacturer litigates a defect issue and loses. The manufacturer had little incentive to appeal. A new plaintiff who deliberately waited to see the first result then sues for $5 million and seeks offensive nonmutual issue preclusion on the same defect issue. What is the best answer?
Answer A: Offensive nonmutual issue preclusion is automatic whenever the issue is the same
Answer B: The court should consider fairness, including incentive to litigate and the new plaintiff's wait-and-see behavior
Answer C: Nonmutual issue preclusion is never allowed
Answer D: Claim preclusion automatically applies because the same issue appears
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Offensive nonmutual use is discretionary and fairness-sensitive.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Fairness limits matter, especially for offensive use. C: Mutuality has been relaxed in many settings. D: Claim preclusion requires same parties or privies and same claim, not just same issue.
lb_rule_reference: Courts consider whether the party to be bound had a full and fair opportunity and adequate incentive to litigate, and whether use would be unfair.
