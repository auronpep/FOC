Question: Daniel conveyed Blueacre “to Esther and her heirs, but if Esther has no child before Esther turns 35, then to Moses and his heirs.” Esther is 30 and has no child. What estate does Esther have now?
Answer A: A contingent remainder, because her ownership depends on having a child.
Answer B: A fee simple subject to executory limitation.
Answer C: A fee simple absolute until she actually turns 35 without a child.
Answer D: A life estate measured by the period before she turns 35.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Esther has a fee simple subject to executory limitation. She received present possession and ownership immediately, but her estate may be cut short if she reaches age 35 without a child. Moses holds a shifting executory interest.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Esther has a present possessory estate, not a future interest. C: C is wrong because the estate is defeasible now even before the condition occurs. D: D is wrong because a time limit or age condition is not the same as a life estate.
lb_rule_reference: Fee simple subject to executory limitation / condition tied to age
