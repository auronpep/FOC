Question: During a man's trial for embezzlement, the prosecution sought to enter into evidence a newspaper article that tended to show the man was aware of problems at his company several months before his alleged embezzlement began. Is the newspaper article admissible?
Answer A: A: Yes, because a newspaper is self- authenticating.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the article is an admission by a party opponent.
Answer C: C: No, because the article has not been authenticated by its author.
Answer D: D: No, because the article has not been authenticated by an official designee from the newspaper.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: FRE 902 provides that periodicals are self- authenticating. B is incorrect because there is nothing indicating that the man made any kind of admission in the article. C and D are incorrect because periodicals are self- authenticating.
