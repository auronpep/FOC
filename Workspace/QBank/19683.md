Question: Daniel conveyed Blueacre “to Esther for life.” Several years later, while Esther was still alive, Daniel conveyed “all of my remaining interest in Blueacre” to Moses by valid deed. What interest does Moses have?
Answer A: A present fee simple absolute, because Daniel used the phrase “all of my remaining interest.”
Answer B: A possibility of reverter, because Esther's estate will end automatically at death.
Answer C: A right of entry, because Moses must retake the property after Esther dies.
Answer D: Daniel's reversion, which becomes possessory when Esther's life estate ends.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Moses receives Daniel's reversion. A reversion is a retained future interest and may be transferred. Moses does not receive present possession because Esther's life estate remains possessory until it ends.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Daniel's remaining interest was future, not present possessory ownership. B: B is wrong because a possibility of reverter follows a fee simple determinable. C: C is wrong because no condition subsequent or power of termination is involved.
lb_rule_reference: Alienability of reversion / transferee takes retained estate
