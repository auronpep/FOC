Question: In a federal contract trial, Ruth offers a photocopy of a handwritten addendum to prove the exact payment term. Boaz objects and produces testimony that the original addendum has different handwriting in the disputed paragraph and may have been altered before copying. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Admit the photocopy because duplicates are always admissible.
Answer B: Admit the photocopy only if Ruth agrees not to rely on the disputed paragraph.
Answer C: Exclude the photocopy, or require the original or other satisfactory proof, because a genuine question has been raised about the original's authenticity.
Answer D: Exclude the photocopy because copies are never allowed to prove contract terms.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Duplicates are generally admissible, but not when a genuine question is raised about the original's authenticity or when admission would be unfair.
