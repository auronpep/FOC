Question: Abraham executed and delivered a valid deed conveying Blackacre “to Sarah and her heirs, but if Sarah uses the land as an inn during Sarah's life, then to Isaac and his heirs.” Sarah later opened an inn on Blackacre during her life. Who owns Blackacre after Sarah opens the inn?
Answer A: Sarah, because Abraham used the words “and her heirs.”
Answer B: Abraham, because the condition caused title to revert to the grantor.
Answer C: Isaac, because Sarah's estate automatically shifted to the named third party.
Answer D: Sarah, subject only to Abraham's right to sue for damages.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Isaac owns Blackacre. The deed gave Sarah a fee simple subject to executory limitation and gave Isaac a shifting executory interest. When Sarah used the land as an inn during her life, the stated event occurred and Sarah's estate automatically shifted to Isaac.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because words of inheritance do not erase an express executory limitation. B: B is wrong because the future interest was given to Isaac, not retained by Abraham. D: D is wrong because the deed creates an ownership shift, not merely a covenant for damages.
lb_rule_reference: Fee simple subject to executory limitation / third-party gift-over
