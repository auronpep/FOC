Question: Isaac sells Leah a parcel of farmland. The contract states, “Buyer has had full opportunity to inspect irrigation access and assumes all risk as to water availability.” Both parties believe the parcel has a usable irrigation hookup. In fact, the hookup had been disconnected before signing. Leah seeks rescission for mutual mistake. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Leah may rescind because both parties were mistaken about the hookup.
Answer B: Leah may rescind because irrigation is always a basic assumption in farmland sales.
Answer C: Leah may rescind unless Isaac personally inspected the hookup.
Answer D: Leah may not rescind because the contract assigned the water-availability risk to her.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Risk allocation defeats a mutual mistake defense. The contract expressly gave Leah inspection opportunity and assigned water-availability risk to her. Even though the mistake concerned an important existing fact, Leah bore the risk.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A and B are wrong because basic assumption and mutual error do not overcome express risk allocation.
