Question: In a federal products-liability trial, Sarah offers testimony about a machine part before calling the technician who will authenticate the part. Sarah explains that the technician is delayed but will testify later that morning. The defendant objects that foundation must always be completed before any related testimony. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because foundation must always precede any related testimony.
Answer B: Overrule the objection because the court has discretion to control the order of proof and may allow testimony subject to later foundation.
Answer C: Sustain the objection unless the jury first finds that the part is authentic.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if the defendant waives all foundation objections.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The court has reasonable control over the mode and order of presenting evidence. It may allow testimony subject to later foundation when doing so is a reasonable order-of-proof ruling.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A treats sequencing as rigid. C misassigns the timing and decisionmaker.
