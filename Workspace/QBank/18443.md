Subtopic: Motions
Outline: Motion for judgment on the pleadings
Question: After the amendment-as-of-course period expires, Abigail seeks leave to amend. Her proposed amendment repeats the same legally impossible claim the court has already dismissed twice and adds no new facts. The defendant argues amendment would be futile. May the court deny leave?
Answer A: No, leave to amend must always be granted
Answer B: No, futility is never relevant
Answer C: Yes, the court may deny leave when the proposed amendment would be futile
Answer D: Yes, but only if the defendant proves bad faith
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A proposed amendment that still fails as a matter of law need not be allowed.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Leave is freely given, not automatic forever. B: Futility is a recognized reason to deny leave. D: Futility can independently support denial.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 15(a)(2) is generous but not automatic.
