Question: Joshua offers to pay Hannah $1,200 to translate a set of letters. The offer says, “You may accept by promising to do the translation or by beginning the work by Monday.” On Sunday, Hannah replies, “I promise to translate the letters by the end of the month.” Joshua attempts to revoke on Monday morning before Hannah begins work. What is the best analysis?
Answer A: No contract formed because Hannah had not begun translating when Joshua revoked.
Answer B: No contract formed because the offer was a gift promise until Hannah finished the translation.
Answer C: A bilateral contract formed when Hannah made the invited promise on Sunday.
Answer D: No contract formed because Joshua received no benefit before revocation.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The offer expressly allowed acceptance by a promise or by beginning work. Hannah chose the promise route on Sunday, forming a bilateral contract before Joshua attempted to revoke.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because beginning work was only one permitted acceptance route. B: B is wrong because Joshua sought translation in exchange for payment. D: D is wrong because formation by promise does not require that the offeror already receive performance.
lb_rule_reference: common_law
lb_student_diagnostic: Student used the performance route even though the offer allowed acceptance by promise.
