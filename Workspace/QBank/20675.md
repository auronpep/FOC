Question: A state requires every voter to pay a $20 "election administration fee" before receiving a ballot in state elections. The state says the fee helps fund polling places and applies equally to all voters. What is the best Equal Protection analysis?
Answer A: Rational basis applies because wealth is not a suspect classification
Answer B: Strict scrutiny applies because the fee conditions voting on payment
Answer C: The fee is valid automatically because it applies to every voter equally
Answer D: No constitutional issue exists because elections must be funded somehow
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Conditioning the franchise on payment severely burdens voting and cannot be justified as ordinary administration.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The law burdens the fundamental right to vote, so the problem is not only wealth classification. C: Equal application of a payment condition still burdens the right to vote. D: Funding needs do not allow a state to make voting depend on paying a fee.
lb_rule_reference: A state may not condition voting in public elections on payment of a tax or fee; severe burdens on the fundamental right to vote receive strict scrutiny.
