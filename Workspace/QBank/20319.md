Question: In a federal contract trial, a corporation named Bethel Foods is a party. Its lawyer designates Deborah, the company's operations manager, as the company representative at counsel table. Deborah is also expected to testify about shipping records. Opposing counsel asks the court to exclude Deborah under the witness-exclusion rule. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Exclude Deborah because a witness may never also serve as a party representative.
Answer B: Exclude Deborah unless she testifies before all other witnesses.
Answer C: Permit Deborah to remain as the company's designated representative, even though she is expected to testify.
Answer D: Permit Deborah to remain only if the opposing party stipulates that her testimony will be truthful.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: When a party is not a natural person, one officer or employee designated as the party's representative by its attorney is exempt from exclusion. That representative may remain even if she will be a witness.
