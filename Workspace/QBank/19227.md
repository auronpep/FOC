Question: A state makes it a crime to burn, tear, or deface a state flag "in a manner that communicates contempt for the state." A protester burns the flag during a rally while criticizing state policy. What is the best First Amendment analysis?
Answer A: The law is content based because it punishes conduct only when the conduct communicates contempt
Answer B: The law is content neutral because it regulates burning and tearing, not words
Answer C: The protester has no claim because flag burning is never expressive
Answer D: The law receives rational basis review because flags are government property
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The law turns on the message expressed by the flag treatment.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: The statute does not ban all burning or tearing; it bans contemptuous expression. C: Flag burning during protest can be expressive conduct. D: The fact pattern concerns a criminal law targeting expressive message, not a government-property management rule.
lb_rule_reference: A law banning flag treatment because it is disrespectful targets expression and is subject to strict scrutiny.
