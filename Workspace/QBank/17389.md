Question: In a federal products case, the parties dispute several matters. Which matter, if not subject to reasonable dispute, is the kind of adjudicative fact governed by Rule 201?
Answer A: A general policy proposition that product warnings make consumers more careful.
Answer B: A broad economic theory about how manufacturers respond to tort liability.
Answer C: A judge's general view about whether punitive damages deter corporations.
Answer D: The fact that the accident involving Hannah and Samuel occurred on a particular highway within the court's territorial jurisdiction.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 201 governs judicial notice of adjudicative facts, meaning case-specific facts about the parties and events. The location of Hannah and Samuel's accident is an adjudicative fact. The other choices are legislative or policy-type propositions used in legal reasoning, not adjudicative facts governed by Rule 201.
