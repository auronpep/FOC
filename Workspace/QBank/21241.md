Subtopic: Verdicts and Judgments
Outline: Issue and Claim Preclusion
Question: In Suit 1, a court enters judgment for Nathan because Miriam's claim is time-barred. The judge also comments that Nathan probably did not breach the contract. In Suit 2 on a different contract, Nathan argues that the comment precludes Miriam from disputing breach. Does issue preclusion apply to the breach comment?
Answer A: Yes, because any judicial statement is preclusive
Answer B: Yes, because Miriam lost Suit 1
Answer C: No, because the breach comment was not essential to the limitations judgment
Answer D: No, because issue preclusion applies only after jury verdicts
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The judgment rested on limitations; the breach observation was unnecessary.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Issue preclusion does not attach to unnecessary comments. B: Losing a case does not make every issue preclusive. D: Court determinations can be preclusive when the elements are met.
lb_rule_reference: A finding that was unnecessary to the judgment usually does not bind a later court.
