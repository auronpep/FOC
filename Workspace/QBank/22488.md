


BARMATRIX Q#: 22488
Outline_code: 55020100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Contracts
Subtopic: Formation of Contracts
Outline: Offer and acceptance
Question: A woman had a large tree in her backyard that was in danger of falling down on her house. The woman became worried when she read a weather report stating that a big storm was going to hit the town on Monday. The woman texted a friend of hers who worked as a landscaper and texted, “If you take that dangerous tree down by Sunday, I will pay you $500.” On Saturday, the woman looked out her window and saw the friend trimming the lower branches off the tree to take it down. The woman went to her neighborhood pool and returned that evening. When she went into the backyard, she saw that about half of the tree had been taken down. Stuck under a rock was a note that said, “I need to get a big ladder. I'll be back first thing Monday morning.” Neither the storm nor the friend arrived on Monday. A week later, the friend still hadn't returned. The woman sued the friend for breach of contract. Should the court rule in the woman's favor?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the friend's part performance implied acceptance of the contract.
Answer B: B: Yes, under the doctrine of equitable estoppel.
Answer C: C: No, because the friend never accepted the woman's offer.
Answer D: D: No, because the friend still had time to cure.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The part performance of a contract implies an enforceable promise to complete it if the offer was for one complete contract, the offeree did part of the actual performance of the contract and not mere preparation, and the offeror knew of the offeree's beginning of performance. Here, the friend is the offeree. The woman asked the friend to take down the entire tree, the friend actually took down some of the tree, and the woman saw him working on it. Consequently, the friend breached the contract when he failed to finish taking down the tree. B is incorrect because equitable estoppel is not needed to make the contract enforceable. C is incorrect because the friend accepted the offer by beginning performance. D is incorrect because the agreed time for performance (Sunday) has already passed.
