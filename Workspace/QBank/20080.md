Question: Jacob signed a written power authorizing Nathan “to sell and convey my field to Hannah for eighty thousand dollars.” Nathan instead signed and delivered a deed conveying the field to Deborah for the same price. Deborah accepted and paid Nathan. Jacob had not authorized a conveyance to Deborah. Who owns the field?
Answer A: Jacob, because Nathan's written authority was limited to a conveyance to Hannah.
Answer B: Deborah, because Nathan had written authority to sell the field.
Answer C: Deborah, because she paid the same price stated in the written authority.
Answer D: Hannah, because she was the person named in the power of attorney.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source claimed is an agent-executed deed, and the decisive validity gate is the scope of written authority. Jacob authorized Nathan to convey the field to Hannah, not to Deborah. Payment of the same price does not expand the authority lock, so Nathan's deed did not pass title to Deborah.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because written authority to sell is limited by its terms when it names the permitted grantee. C: C is wrong because matching the price does not cure the unauthorized recipient. D: D is wrong because Hannah did not receive a delivered deed; the failed deed to Deborah does not transfer title to Hannah.
lb_rule_reference: Scope of written agency authority / named grantee
