Question: A state requires drivers over age seventy-five to renew their licenses in person every two years. Younger drivers may renew online every six years. Older drivers challenge the rule under Equal Protection. What level of scrutiny applies?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny, because older adults are a suspect class
Answer B: Intermediate scrutiny, because age is like sex
Answer C: Rational basis, because age classifications are generally reviewed deferentially
Answer D: No review, because driver's licenses are privileges
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The state needs only a rational relationship to a legitimate safety interest.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Age is not a suspect classification. B: Equal Protection doctrine does not treat age as quasi-suspect. D: Government benefit rules are still subject to Equal Protection review.
lb_rule_reference: Age is not a suspect or quasi-suspect classification; age lines generally receive rational basis review.
