Outline: Unilateral contract
Question: Aaron posts a notice: “I will pay $2,000 to anyone who completely paints my barn by Friday.” Ruth sees the notice and begins painting the barn on Monday morning. After Ruth has painted one wall, Aaron tells her that he revokes the offer. Ruth continues and finishes the barn by Friday. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: No contract can arise because Aaron's promise was a gratuitous reward offer.
Answer B: Ruth accepted the offer merely by promising herself that she would finish the barn.
Answer C: Aaron's revocation was effective because Ruth had not yet completed the requested performance.
Answer D: Ruth's beginning the requested performance made the offer temporarily irrevocable, and her completion accepted it.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Under the common-law unilateral-contract rule, beginning the requested performance creates option-like protection against revocation, but the offeree accepts the unilateral offer only by completing the requested performance. Ruth began the requested painting before Aaron tried to revoke and then completed the work by the deadline.
