Question: David conveyed a field “to Samuel while the land is used for farming.” Samuel later conveyed the field to Nathan. Nathan stopped farming and built a warehouse on the field. Before Nathan built the warehouse, David died and left all his property to Esther. Who owns the field after Nathan builds the warehouse?
Answer A: Nathan, because Samuel conveyed the field by deed.
Answer B: Samuel, because Nathan violated the original limitation.
Answer C: Esther, because the defeasible estate automatically ended when farming stopped.
Answer D: Nathan, unless Esther first exercises a right of entry.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Samuel held a fee simple determinable and could transfer that estate to Nathan. Nathan took the same defeasible estate. When farming stopped, the estate automatically ended and ownership shifted to the holder of the possibility of reverter, David's successor Esther.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Samuel could not convey greater title than he owned. B: B is wrong because Samuel transferred his defeasible estate and did not regain ownership upon breach. D: D is wrong because a fee simple determinable terminates automatically; no right of entry is required.
lb_rule_reference: Fee simple determinable / transferee takes same estate
