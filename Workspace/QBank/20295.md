Outline: Form and scope of examination
Question: In a federal breach-of-contract trial, Peter testifies on direct about delivery dates. On cross-examination, the defendant asks only whether Peter's calendar was accurate. On redirect, Peter's lawyer asks about the defendant's unrelated insurance coverage. The defendant objects that the question exceeds the scope of cross-examination and is improper new matter. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Overrule the objection because redirect examination may cover any relevant issue as of right.
Answer B: Sustain the objection or require the matter to be taken up only with the court's permission because the question introduces new matter not raised on cross.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because insurance questions are always proper on redirect.
Answer D: Sustain the objection only if the question is also hearsay.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The court controls the mode and order of examination. Redirect ordinarily responds to matters raised on cross. A new, unrelated insurance topic is not automatically proper on redirect, though the court may allow new matter in its discretion.
