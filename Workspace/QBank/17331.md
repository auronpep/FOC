Question: Elijah conveyed Clinicacre “to Elisha and his heirs, but if Elisha stops using the land as a clinic during Elisha's life, then to Hannah.” Elisha stopped using the land as a clinic during his life. Elijah has taken no action. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Elisha owns Clinicacre until Elijah exercises a right of entry.
Answer B: Elijah owns Clinicacre because the clinic condition was breached.
Answer C: Hannah owns Clinicacre because the estate automatically shifted to the named third party.
Answer D: Elisha owns Clinicacre in fee simple absolute because Elijah did not reenter.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Hannah owns Clinicacre. The deed gave the future interest to a third party, so Elisha held a fee simple subject to executory limitation. When Elisha stopped using the land as a clinic during his life, the estate shifted automatically to Hannah. Elijah did not need to reenter because Elijah had no right of entry.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the deed does not reserve a right of entry in Elijah. B: B is wrong because the gift-over is to Hannah, not Elijah. D: D is wrong because Elisha's estate was defeasible and was cut short by the stated event.
lb_rule_reference: Third-party executory interest / no grantor power of termination
