Outline: Mistake / Lack of Mutual Assent
Question: Benjamin agrees to sell Deborah “all 100 sealed crates of temple lamps in Warehouse A” for a lump sum. Both parties believe there are 100 sealed crates in Warehouse A. Unknown to both, only 40 crates remained there before the contract was signed because a warehouse worker had moved the rest days earlier. The number of crates was central to the agreed price. Deborah seeks to avoid the contract. Which statement is best?
Answer A: Deborah cannot avoid because she agreed to buy all crates in the warehouse, whatever the number.
Answer B: Deborah cannot avoid because the contract used a lump sum price.
Answer C: Deborah may avoid because both parties were mistaken about an existing quantity fact that was a basic assumption and materially affected the exchange.
Answer D: Deborah may avoid only if Benjamin knew the worker had moved the crates.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The parties' shared belief that 100 crates existed in the warehouse was a basic assumption of the bargain and materially affected the agreed exchange. Because neither party allocated the risk to Deborah, the contract is voidable for mutual mistake.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the facts show the parties bargained on the assumption of 100 crates, not an unknown quantity. B: B is wrong because a lump-sum price does not automatically allocate the risk of a material quantity mistake. D: D is wrong because mutual mistake does not require Benjamin's knowledge.
lb_rule_reference: Mutual mistake / quantity of identified subject matter
