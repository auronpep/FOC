Outline: Receipt of stolen property
Question: Mina buys a used camera at a normal price from a neighbor she trusts. Nothing suggests the camera is stolen. The next day, Mina learns the neighbor stole it. Mina immediately gives the camera to police. Is Mina guilty of receiving stolen property?
Answer A: Yes, because the camera was stolen before Mina bought it
Answer B: Yes, because later knowledge always relates back to the purchase
Answer C: No, because she lacked knowledge when she received control and promptly returned the camera after learning
Answer D: No, because receiving stolen property applies only to cash
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The required knowledge did not exist when Mina received the camera.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The offense also requires knowledge or belief at receipt. B: Later knowledge does not retroactively supply the required receipt-time mens rea. D: Cameras and other personal property can be the subject of the offense.
lb_rule_reference: Receiving stolen property requires knowing or believing the property is stolen at the time the defendant receives control.
