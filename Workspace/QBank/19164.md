Question: Miriam conveyed Lakeacre “to Aaron when Aaron turns 25.” Aaron was 24 when the deed was delivered. Miriam retained possession. Aaron later turned 25. Who owns Lakeacre when Aaron turns 25?
Answer A: Miriam, because she retained possession after delivery.
Answer B: Aaron, because his springing executory interest became possessory when he turned 25.
Answer C: Miriam and Aaron as joint tenants.
Answer D: Miriam, unless Aaron obtains a second deed after turning 25.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Aaron owns Lakeacre when he turns 25. Before that event, Miriam retained the present estate. Aaron held a springing executory interest, which became possessory and divested Miriam when the stated age condition occurred.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Miriam's retained possession was subject to Aaron's future interest. C: C is wrong because the deed does not create a joint tenancy. D: D is wrong because Aaron does not need a second deed once the condition in the original deed is satisfied.
lb_rule_reference: Springing executory interest / condition satisfied
