Question: A driver entered a nolo contendere plea to reckless driving arising from a collision. In a later civil action for the same collision, the injured plaintiff offers the nolo plea against the driver to prove fault. The driver objects under Rule 410. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Admit the plea because Rule 410 applies only in later criminal cases.
Answer B: Admit the plea because a nolo contendere plea is equivalent to a party admission in any later case.
Answer C: Exclude the plea because Rule 410 bars use of a nolo contendere plea against the defendant who made it in a civil or criminal case.
Answer D: Exclude the plea only if it was later withdrawn.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 410 prohibits using a nolo contendere plea against the defendant who made the plea in a civil or criminal case. The plaintiff offers it against the driver to prove fault, so it is excluded.
