Question: After negotiations, Mary and Paul agree on price, duties, and start date for Paul to manage Mary's farm. Before leaving, Mary says, “No one is bound until both of us sign the formal management agreement my lawyer drafts.” Paul nods. The next day, before any writing is signed, Mary hires someone else. Did Mary and Paul form a contract before the formal writing?
Answer A: Yes, because agreement on price, duties, and start date always forms a contract.
Answer B: No, because the parties objectively made a signed formal writing a condition to being bound.
Answer C: Yes, because Paul's nod was a signature substitute.
Answer D: No, but only because the price might not be adequate.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Parties can agree that no contract will exist until a formal writing is signed. Here, Mary expressly stated that no one was bound until both signed the formal agreement, and Paul agreed. The writing was a condition to formation, not a mere later memorial.
