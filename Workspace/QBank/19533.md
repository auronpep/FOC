Question: Gabriel, a citizen of Arizona, sues Desert Data LLC in federal court for $200,000. The LLC was organized in New Mexico and has its main office in Colorado. One of its members is a limited partnership with a partner who is a citizen of Arizona. Is complete diversity present?
Answer A: Yes, because the LLC was organized in New Mexico and has its main office in Colorado
Answer B: No, because the LLC takes the citizenship of all members, including the Arizona partner traced through the member partnership
Answer C: Yes, because only managing members count
Answer D: No, because LLCs may never appear in federal court
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The Arizona partner gives the LLC Arizona citizenship, matching Gabriel.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: That is the wrong test for an LLC. C: Citizenship of all members or partners is considered. D: LLCs can litigate in federal court if jurisdiction exists.
lb_rule_reference: An LLC or partnership is a citizen of every state of which its members or partners are citizens, traced through entity layers.
