Outline: Unilateral contract
Question: Rachel promises Samuel, “I will pay you $600 if you deliver these sealed records to the courthouse by noon.” Samuel delivers the records at 11:30 a.m. Rachel dies later that afternoon. Rachel's estate refuses to pay, arguing that Rachel died before she received Samuel's invoice. Is Samuel entitled to payment from the estate?
Answer A: No, because the offer terminated at Rachel's death before Samuel invoiced the estate.
Answer B: No, because Samuel did not give notice before completing the performance.
Answer C: No, because Rachel's promise was a gratuitous promise to make a payment.
Answer D: Yes, because Samuel completed the requested performance before Rachel died, forming the unilateral contract.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Samuel accepted Rachel's unilateral offer by completing the requested delivery before Rachel's death. The later invoice was not the act requested for acceptance.
