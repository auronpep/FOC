


BARMATRIX Q#: 22237
Outline_code: 33040101
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Evidence
Subtopic: Hearsay and Circumstances of Its Admissibility
Outline: Hearsay rule
Question: A woman sued a hospital claiming that she was fired because of racial discrimination. At trial, the woman sought to introduce any hospital records regarding complaints by other employees based on instances of racial discrimination at work. She wanted to show that the hospital had many instances of racial discrimination before hers. If the defense objects to the admission of the records, should the objection be sustained?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the records were produced in anticipation of litigation.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the records are hearsay not within any recognized exception.
Answer C: C: No, because they are records kept in the ordinary course of business.
Answer D: D: No, because the records show the hospital had notice of the hostile environment.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Hearsay is an out- of- court statement used to prove the truth of the matter asserted. Here, the woman is trying to use the records with the employees' out- of- court statements to prove the fact that the hospital had engaged in racial discrimination with many other employees. Consequently, it was hearsay not within any recognized exception. A is incorrect because there is no indication that the records were prepared in anticipation of litigation. C and D are incorrect because the records are inadmissible hearsay.
