Question: Rebecca bought 100 air filters from Simeon. After inspecting and using them for a week, Rebecca accepted the filters but timely notified Simeon that they were nonconforming because they did not meet the promised filtration level. Simeon argues that Rebecca's acceptance means she must pay the full price and has no remedy. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Simeon is correct because acceptance eliminates all buyer remedies.
Answer B: Rebecca must pay for accepted goods but may recover damages for the nonconformity if she gave the required notice.
Answer C: Rebecca may keep the goods without paying anything because they were nonconforming.
Answer D: Rebecca's only remedy is cover, even though she accepted and kept the filters.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Acceptance changes the buyer's remedy posture, but it does not eliminate damages for nonconformity after timely notice. The buyer remains obligated to pay for accepted goods, subject to damages for the breach.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because accepted-goods damages remain available with timely notice. C: C is wrong because acceptance does not let the buyer keep goods for free. D: D is wrong because cover is a rejected/non-delivery substitute-goods remedy, not the only remedy after acceptance.
lb_rule_reference: UCC §2-714; UCC §2-607
