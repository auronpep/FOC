Question: Priya, a citizen of Delaware, sues HarborTech Corp. in federal court for $100,000 under state law. HarborTech is incorporated in Nevada, but its headquarters and nerve center are in Delaware. Is complete diversity present?
Answer A: Yes, because HarborTech is incorporated in Nevada
Answer B: Yes, because a corporation can have only one citizenship
Answer C: No, because Priya and HarborTech are both Delaware citizens through HarborTech's principal place of business
Answer D: No, but only because the claim is based on state law
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The Delaware nerve center makes HarborTech a Delaware citizen, destroying complete diversity.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Incorporation is only one citizenship. Principal place of business also counts. B: Corporations have dual citizenship for diversity. D: State-law claims are proper in diversity when Section 1332 is satisfied.
lb_rule_reference: For Section 1332, corporate citizenship includes state of incorporation and principal place of business.
