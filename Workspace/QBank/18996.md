Question: A California citizen sued Harbor LLC in federal court for $500,000. Harbor LLC was organized in Delaware and has its headquarters in Nevada. One of Harbor LLC's members is a California citizen. The plaintiff argues diversity exists because the LLC was not formed or headquartered in California. Is complete diversity present?
Answer A: Yes, because an LLC is a citizen only of its formation state and headquarters state
Answer B: Yes, because the amount in controversy exceeds $75,000
Answer C: No, because Harbor LLC shares California citizenship through its California member
Answer D: No, because LLCs can never be parties in federal court
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The LLC is a citizen of every state of its members. A California member destroys complete diversity against a California plaintiff.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: That rule is for corporations, not LLCs. B: Amount does not overcome a citizenship collision. D: LLCs can be parties; their citizenship is determined through members.
lb_rule_reference: For diversity, an unincorporated entity takes the citizenship of all of its members, not its formation state or headquarters alone.
