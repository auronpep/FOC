


BARMATRIX Q#: 22232
Outline_code: 31010502
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Evidence
Subtopic: Presentation of Evidence
Outline: Prior inconsistent statement
Question: During a trial for negligence, a witness takes his oath and testifies that the defendant was the sole cause of a car accident. During cross- examination, the defendant asked the witness if the witness's position as High Poobah in the Church of the Potato Chip Sausage King meant that he did not take his oath to tell the truth seriously since a known belief of the Church of the Potato Chip Sausage King was that there was no God. Upon proper objection, will the court require the witness to answer the defendant's question?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the witness is a high official in the religious organization.
Answer B: B: Yes, because it goes straight to the effectiveness of the witness's oath.
Answer C: C: No, because religious beliefs cannot be used to attack or enhance witness credibility.
Answer D: D: No, because the witness's religious beliefs are not being used to support his credibility.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Under FRE 610, a witness's religious beliefs cannot be used to enhance or attack the witness's credibility. A is incorrect because the witness's position in the church would not change the analysis. B is incorrect because religious beliefs cannot be used to attack the credibility of a witness. D is incorrect because the court would not allow the statements even if those statements supported the witness's apparent truthfulness.
