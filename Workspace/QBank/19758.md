Subtopic: Presentation of Evidence
Outline: Roles of judge and jury
Question: In a federal civil trial, Isaac shows a contract to the witness stand and immediately asks that the jury be allowed to read it. The opposing party objects that no witness has yet identified the contract. The judge says Isaac may question a witness about the document first and may ask to publish it to the jury after a sufficient foundation is laid. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The judge's ruling is proper because the court may control the order in which a document is identified and shown to the jury.
Answer B: The judge's ruling is improper because a party may publish any exhibit to the jury before foundation.
Answer C: The judge's ruling is improper because authentication is always a jury question only.
Answer D: The judge must exclude the contract permanently because Isaac tried to show it too early.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The judge may control the mode and order of presenting exhibits. Requiring a foundation before publishing a document to the jury is a reasonable presentation-control ruling.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because exhibits are not automatically published before foundation. C: C is wrong because the court controls the admissibility and presentation sequence even when the jury later weighs the evidence. D: D is wrong because a premature request to publish does not require permanent exclusion if foundation can be laid.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 611(a) / publishing exhibit after authentication
