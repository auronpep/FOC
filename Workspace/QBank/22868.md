MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Pretrial Procedures
Outline: Discovery
Question: A woman sued a nursing home in federal district court for damages. The woman claimed that her mother was injured when the nursing home failed to adequately supervise one of its overnight nurses. The woman served a request for production of documents related to the nursing home, its hiring and staffing procedures, and any complaints brought against staff in the past. The nursing home produced several documents, but withheld some documents because it believed those documents were protected as attorney work product. The woman thought the nursing home was wrong in withholding the documents, and believed she was entitled to the documents, sanctions, and attorney's fees involved in getting the documents from the nursing home. What is the woman's best course of action if she wants the documents, sanctions, and attorney's fees?
Answer A: A: Make a good faith effort to talk with the nursing home and obtain the discovery without court intervention.
Answer B: B: File a motion to compel discovery and include a specific request for sanctions and attorney's fees.
Answer C: C: File a motion to compel discovery, which automatically carries the possibility of sanctions and attorney's fees.
Answer D: D: File a motion to dismiss and include a specific request for sanctions and attorney's fees.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Before a party files a motion to compel discovery, Fed. R. Civ. P. 37 requires the party to meet in good faith or attempt to meet in good faith with the party failing to make disclosure to try to obtain the requested discovery without court intervention. Consequently, before the woman does anything, she needs to try to meet with the nursing home and see if she can get the withheld documents without the court's help. Consequently, B, C, and D are incorrect.
