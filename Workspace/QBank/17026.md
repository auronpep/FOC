Outline: Gap-Fillers
Question: A delivery company bought cargo drones from a manufacturer under a contract that included software configuration and a short training session. Most of the price was allocated to the drones. The signed writing required each drone to include a “standard battery set,” but did not define the phrase. In three prior drone contracts between the same parties, the manufacturer included four batteries per drone under the same phrase. The manufacturer now offers testimony that the broader drone industry usually treats two batteries as a standard set. The delivery company offers the prior-contract evidence. Should the court admit the prior-contract evidence to interpret “standard battery set”?
Answer A: Yes, because the transaction is predominantly for goods and the parties' course of dealing may explain or supplement the term if reasonably consistent.
Answer B: No, because the configuration and training services make the entire transaction a services contract.
Answer C: No, because general trade usage automatically controls over the parties' prior course of dealing.
Answer D: No, because the court must use a default gap-filler whenever the writing fails to define a phrase.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The narrow rule is that a goods-dominant mixed transaction can use UCC meaning sources, including course of dealing, to explain or supplement a term. The trigger facts are the price allocation to drones, the undefined technical phrase, and three prior contracts between the same parties. The decisive locks are source law and hierarchy. The prior-contract evidence is admissible as course of dealing if established and reasonably consistent with the express terms.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because incidental software configuration and training do not necessarily defeat Article 2 when goods predominate. C: C is wrong because it reverses the hierarchy by preferring general trade usage over private course of dealing. D: D is wrong because the court checks agreement sources before default gap-fillers.
lb_rule_reference: UCC 2-202 / course of dealing in mixed transaction
