Question: A bakery owned by Leah sends a supplier this message: “I offer to buy all the flour my bakery requires for the next year at $10 per bag, delivery monthly.” The supplier accepts. Leah later argues no contract formed because the message did not specify a fixed number of bags. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: No contract formed because a requirements quantity is always too indefinite and illusory.
Answer B: No contract formed unless the court finds $10 per bag was an adequate price.
Answer C: No contract formed because the message was only a price quote.
Answer D: A contract can form because the quantity is measured by Leah's good-faith requirements.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: A requirements contract can satisfy the quantity requirement because the buyer's obligation is measured by actual good-faith requirements. Leah's message was not indefinite merely because it lacked a fixed number.
