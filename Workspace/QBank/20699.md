Question: Mary conveyed Memorialacre “to Joseph for as long as the land remains a family cemetery.” The land still remains a family cemetery. Which statement best describes the current interests?
Answer A: Joseph has a fee simple absolute because the limitation has not been violated.
Answer B: Mary has a right of entry because cemetery use is a condition subsequent.
Answer C: Mary has a present possessory estate because the land will eventually stop being used as a cemetery.
Answer D: Joseph has a fee simple determinable, and Mary has a possibility of reverter.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Joseph has a fee simple determinable because “for as long as” is durational language. Mary has a possibility of reverter. The limitation has not yet been violated, so Joseph remains in possession, but Mary's retained future interest exists now.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because compliance does not convert a defeasible fee into a fee simple absolute. B: B is wrong because the language is durational, not conditional with a power of termination. C: C is wrong because Mary's interest is future, not present possessory, while the condition is satisfied.
lb_rule_reference: Current compliance / future interest remains
