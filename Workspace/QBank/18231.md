Outline: Sources and Scope of Privileges and Policy Exclusions
Question: Sarah sues Boaz in federal court, asserting a federal discrimination claim and a supplemental State A negligent-infliction-of-emotional-distress claim. State A recognizes a privilege for confidential communications with a licensed grief counselor. Boaz seeks Sarah's grief-counseling records and expressly offers them only to contest the emotional-distress element of the State A claim, not to prove or disprove the federal discrimination claim. Which privilege-law statement is most accurate for this offered use?
Answer A: Federal privilege law automatically controls because the complaint includes a federal claim and the case is pending in federal court.
Answer B: State privilege law should be consulted for this claim-specific use because the records are offered solely on a claim for which state law supplies the rule of decision.
Answer C: No privilege can apply because supplemental jurisdiction converts the State A claim into a federal claim for evidence purposes.
Answer D: Both federal and state privileges automatically exclude the records without regard to the claim, the offered purpose, or any exception.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 501 requires source-law routing by the claim or defense for which the privileged evidence is offered. Although the case includes a federal claim, Boaz offers the records only on the state-law emotional-distress claim. For that limited use, state privilege law should be consulted because state law supplies the rule of decision for that claim. The decisive axis is claim-specific privilege source, not the case caption as a whole.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because it treats the presence of a federal claim as resolving every privilege question in the case. The offered use matters. C: C is wrong because supplemental jurisdiction does not erase the state-law character of the state claim. D: D is wrong because privileges do not automatically exclude without source-law, holder, waiver, and exception analysis.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 501 mixed-claim source law
lb_student_diagnostic: Student treats mixed federal/state cases as all-or-nothing instead of asking which claim or defense the evidence is being used to prove.
