Question: A statute bans "dangerous tools" in courthouses. A state court has authoritatively construed the term to mean weapons capable of causing serious bodily injury and not ordinary pens, keys, or phones. A challenger argues the phrase is still vague because edge cases are possible. What is the best answer?
Answer A: The narrowing construction matters because it can provide fair notice and limit discretion
Answer B: The statute is void whenever any hard edge case can be imagined
Answer C: Narrowing constructions are irrelevant under due process
Answer D: Strict scrutiny applies because courthouses are public forums
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Authoritative narrowing can cure or reduce vagueness by clarifying the statute's reach.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Ordinary ambiguity at the margins does not automatically make a law void. C: Courts consider authoritative constructions when evaluating notice and discretion. D: The issue is due-process vagueness of a weapons rule, not speech forum doctrine.
lb_rule_reference: A narrowing construction can reduce vagueness when it gives fair notice and limits enforcement discretion.
