Outline: Privileges
Question: Thief grabbed Owner's messenger bag from a cafe chair and ran. Owner immediately chased Thief down the sidewalk, caught up within one block, and pulled the bag from Thief's hand. Owner used no more force than necessary to recover the bag. Thief stumbled but was not seriously injured. Thief sues Owner for battery. Can Owner rely on a recapture privilege?
Answer A: No, because a property owner may never use force to recover chattels
Answer B: No, because Owner should have filed a lawsuit instead of chasing
Answer C: Yes, because Owner acted immediately in fresh pursuit and used reasonable nondeadly force to retake the bag
Answer D: Yes, but only because Thief was not seriously injured
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The bag was wrongfully taken, Owner pursued at once, and the force used was limited to recovering the chattel.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Prompt fresh pursuit after wrongful taking can privilege reasonable nondeadly force. B: The fresh-pursuit privilege allows immediate reasonable recapture in narrow circumstances. D: The privilege depends on wrongful taking, fresh pursuit, and reasonable force. Injury level matters but is not the only requirement.
lb_rule_reference: Reasonable nondeadly force may be privileged to recapture a chattel when the owner acts promptly in fresh pursuit after wrongful taking.
