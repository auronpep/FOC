Question: Esther paid Aaron for a recorded right to enter Aaron's quarry road twice each month and remove gravel from a marked pit for three years. Esther owns no neighboring land, and the right is not tied to any parcel of Esther's. What is the best classification of Esther's interest?
Answer A: A license because Esther owns no neighboring dominant parcel.
Answer B: An easement appurtenant because the right uses a road on Aaron's land.
Answer C: A profit in gross because Esther may enter and remove gravel without any dominant parcel.
Answer D: Title by adverse possession because Esther will remove part of the land.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A profit allows entry onto another's land to remove resources such as gravel or minerals. Because the right benefits Esther personally and is not attached to a dominant parcel, it is a profit in gross.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the absence of a dominant parcel points to an in-gross right, not automatically a license. B: B is wrong because road use is incidental to the resource-removal right and no dominant parcel is benefited. D: D is wrong because a limited recorded removal right is not title by adverse possession.
