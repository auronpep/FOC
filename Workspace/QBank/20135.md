Outline: Motion for new trial
Question: In federal court, a jury awards plaintiff $1,000 on a claim where the judge believes the minimum supportable award is $20,000. Plaintiff asks the judge to deny a new trial by simply increasing the award to $20,000 over defendant's objection. May the federal judge use additur in this way?
Answer A: Yes, because federal judges may freely increase jury awards
Answer B: Yes, because remittitur and additur are identical
Answer C: No, federal court may not use additur over defendant's objection
Answer D: No, because federal courts can never order a new trial
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Federal courts may use remittitur but not additur.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Additur is not available in federal court. B: Federal practice treats them differently. D: Rule 59 authorizes new trials in proper circumstances.
lb_rule_reference: In federal court, a judge may condition denial of a new trial on plaintiff accepting a reduced award, but may not increase an inadequate jury award by additur.
