Subtopic: Rights in Land
Outline: Creation
Question: Aaron granted Miriam, as owner of Lake Lot, “a right to cross the path on Aaron's land to launch and retrieve small boats at the shoreline.” Miriam began leaving two boats, fuel cans, and fishing gear on Aaron's shoreline throughout the summer so that she would not have to carry them from Lake Lot. Aaron sues to stop the storage. Which is the best result?
Answer A: Aaron should prevail because a right to cross, launch, and retrieve boats does not include seasonal storage of boats and gear on the servient land.
Answer B: Miriam should prevail because any boat-related activity is within a boat-launch easement.
Answer C: Miriam should prevail because the easement makes her a co-owner of the shoreline.
Answer D: Aaron should prevail only if the stored items make Lake Lot completely inaccessible.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The grant permits crossing Aaron's land to launch and retrieve small boats. It does not grant storage rights on Aaron's shoreline. Seasonal storage of boats, fuel, and gear imposes a different and greater burden than temporary launch access.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because a subject-matter connection to boats is not enough. C: C is wrong because the easement does not convey shoreline ownership. D: D is wrong because Aaron need not show complete inaccessibility to stop an unauthorized use.
lb_rule_reference: Launch access / storage outside scope
