Question: A commercial retailer sold a portable heater with a defective shutoff switch. The heater ignited a couch in a small office. A coworker entered the room to pull an employee away from the flames and suffered burns. The coworker had not bought or used the heater. Which plaintiff-class analysis is strongest?
Answer A: The coworker is barred because only buyers and users can recover in strict products liability
Answer B: The coworker can be a foreseeable rescuer plaintiff if the defective heater created the emergency
Answer C: The coworker is barred unless the retailer was negligent
Answer D: The coworker wins automatically because all rescuers recover in every products case
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The defect created a fire emergency, and a reasonable rescue response is foreseeable. The coworker's lack of purchase does not bar the claim.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Products liability can protect foreseeable rescuers and bystanders physically injured by the product danger. C: Strict products liability does not require negligent retail conduct when the products elements are met. D: The rescuer still must connect injury to a defective product and satisfy the products-liability gates.
lb_rule_reference: A rescuer injured while reasonably responding to a danger created by a defective product can be within the foreseeable plaintiff class.
