Outline: Requirements for valid deed
Question: Naomi signed and delivered to Ruth a deed conveying Naomi's field. Ruth was alive when Naomi delivered the deed and Ruth accepted it. Before Ruth recorded the deed, Ruth died. Ruth's will left all of her property to Boaz. Naomi argues that the field returned to her because Ruth died before recording. Who owns the field?
Answer A: Naomi, because Ruth died before the deed was recorded.
Answer B: Naomi, because a grantee must remain alive until recording.
Answer C: Boaz, because title vested in Ruth at delivery and then passed under Ruth's will.
Answer D: The county recorder, until Boaz records the deed.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source is a deed, and the decisive validity gate is whether the grantee was alive and able to take when the deed was delivered. Ruth was alive at delivery and accepted the deed, so title vested in her then. Recording was not required for validity between Naomi and Ruth; after Ruth died, the field passed under Ruth's will to Boaz.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because later death before recording does not undo title that already vested. B: B is wrong because recording is not the point at which the grantee must be alive to take title. D: D is wrong because the recorder has no ownership interest; recording is a notice and priority device.
