Question: A landowner announces, “I am accepting bids to repair my roof. Submit written bids by Friday.” Joshua submits a bid to do the work for $12,000. Before the landowner accepts any bid, Joshua tries to withdraw. The landowner argues that the announcement was an offer and Joshua accepted by bidding. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The landowner's bid solicitation was an offer to every contractor who submitted a bid.
Answer B: Joshua accepted the landowner's offer because his bid was written and signed.
Answer C: The solicitation invited offers; Joshua's bid was the offer, which had not yet been accepted.
Answer D: A contract formed because Joshua's bid stated a definite price.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A request for bids normally solicits offers from contractors. Joshua's bid was the offer. Unless another rule makes the bid irrevocable, no contract formed until the landowner accepted the bid.
