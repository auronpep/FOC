Question: City inspectors find a restaurant's refrigeration system has failed and food is being held at unsafe temperatures. To prevent immediate public health risks, the city temporarily closes the restaurant on the spot and provides a prompt hearing two days later to challenge the closure. What is the best procedural due process analysis?
Answer A: The closure is invalid because a pre-deprivation hearing is always required before the government acts
Answer B: The closure is valid only if the restaurant waives every future hearing
Answer C: Immediate closure may be allowed because of the health emergency if prompt post-deprivation process is provided
Answer D: Due process is irrelevant because restaurants have no property interests in licenses or operation
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Due process timing is flexible when pre-deprivation process would undermine urgent public safety action.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Emergencies can justify immediate action with prompt post-deprivation process. B: Emergency action does not require a total waiver; prompt later process is important. D: A protected interest may exist; the stronger point is timing and adequacy of process.
lb_rule_reference: Due process does not always require a pre-deprivation hearing; when quick action is necessary or pre-deprivation process is impracticable, prompt post-deprivation process may satisfy due process.
