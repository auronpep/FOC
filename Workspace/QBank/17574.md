Question: A court order bars a blogger from publishing any future posts about a local official unless the official first reviews and approves the post. The order was entered before trial in a defamation case. What First Amendment doctrine is most directly implicated?
Answer A: Prior restraint, because the order requires advance approval before publication
Answer B: No First Amendment doctrine, because blogs are not newspapers
Answer C: Rational basis, because defamation is a tort
Answer D: Commercial speech, because the blogger earns ad revenue
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The order suppresses speech before it occurs and is presumptively invalid.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Prior-restraint doctrine protects speakers beyond traditional newspapers. C: A prepublication injunction is not ordinary post-publication tort liability. D: Earning ad revenue does not convert commentary about officials into commercial advertising.
lb_rule_reference: Prior restraints on speech are heavily disfavored and carry a strong presumption against constitutional validity.
