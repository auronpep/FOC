Question: Hannah finds Samuel's missing horse wandering near a road and returns it to Samuel. Only after returning the horse does Hannah learn that Samuel had posted a $1,000 reward for anyone who found and returned it. Hannah asks Samuel for the reward. Is Samuel contractually bound to pay Hannah the reward?
Answer A: Yes, because Samuel received the benefit he requested in the notice.
Answer B: Yes, because returning lost property is always consideration for any existing reward.
Answer C: Yes, because Hannah would have relied on the reward if she had known of it.
Answer D: No, because Hannah did not know of the offer when she performed and therefore could not accept it.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Acceptance requires knowledge of the offer. Hannah completed the act before learning of the reward, so her performance did not manifest acceptance of Samuel's offer.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because benefit to Samuel is not enough without acceptance. B: B is wrong because the requested performance must be done with knowledge of the offer. C: C is wrong because hypothetical reliance is not acceptance.
lb_rule_reference: common_law
lb_student_diagnostic: Student treated benefit to the offeror as a substitute for knowledge and acceptance.
