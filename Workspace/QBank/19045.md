Outline: Independent contractor and nondelegable duties
Question: A festival hired a fireworks contractor after ignoring public records showing repeated safety violations and suspended licenses. The contractor negligently launched fireworks too close to spectators. The contractor controlled the launch details. What is the best theory against the festival?
Answer A: Direct negligence for hiring an obviously unsafe contractor
Answer B: Automatic respondeat superior because all contractors are employees
Answer C: No possible liability because independent contractors always insulate principals
Answer D: Strict liability for every public event injury
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The festival's own selection decision is the alleged breach.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: The facts say the contractor controlled the work details. C: Direct negligence remains available. D: The claim is based on negligent selection, not strict liability for all events.
lb_rule_reference: A principal may be directly liable for negligently selecting, supervising, or instructing an unsafe independent contractor even if vicarious liability is unavailable.
