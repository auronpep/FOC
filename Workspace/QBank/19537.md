Question: A New York citizen sues a Georgia citizen in federal court in Illinois under diversity jurisdiction. The accident occurred in Indiana. The parties dispute whether Illinois or Indiana substantive tort law applies. Which choice-of-law rules should the federal court use?
Answer A: Federal common-law choice-of-law rules
Answer B: Illinois choice-of-law rules, because the federal court sits in Illinois
Answer C: Indiana choice-of-law rules automatically because the accident happened there
Answer D: Georgia choice-of-law rules because the defendant lives there
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A federal diversity court uses the choice rules of the forum state.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Klaxon rejects an independent federal diversity choice-of-law system. C: The accident location may matter under the forum state's choice rules, but it does not choose the choice rules. D: Defendant citizenship is not the Klaxon anchor.
lb_rule_reference: Under Klaxon, federal courts sitting in diversity use forum-state choice-of-law rules to select substantive law.
