Question: Hannah posts signs saying, “I will pay $1,000 to anyone who returns my lost ox before Sunday night.” Caleb sees the sign, searches for the ox, and returns it on Sunday afternoon. Hannah refuses to pay, saying public notices are never offers. Is Hannah liable on a contract theory?
Answer A: No, because public notices can never be offers.
Answer B: No, because Caleb did not send Hannah a written promise before searching.
Answer C: No, because a reward is always a gift promise.
Answer D: Yes, because the reward notice invited acceptance by performance and Caleb completed the requested performance with knowledge of the offer.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: A reward notice can be a unilateral offer when it invites specified performance. Caleb knew of the offer and completed the requested act before the deadline, forming a unilateral contract.
