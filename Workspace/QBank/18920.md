Question: At trial, both parties introduce evidence and argue without objection about an unpleaded contract modification issue. After trial, one party moves to amend the pleadings to conform to that issue. The opponent objects that unpleaded issues can never be considered. What is the best answer?
Answer A: The issue may be treated as raised if it was tried by express or implied consent
Answer B: The issue must be ignored because pleadings freeze forever at filing
Answer C: The issue is automatically added even if the opponent objected and was prejudiced
Answer D: The issue can be added only before any answer is served
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 15(b) covers issues tried by consent.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Rule 15 permits later amendments in defined circumstances. C: Consent and prejudice matter. D: Rule 15(b) specifically addresses trial-stage issues.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 15(b) allows amendment to conform to evidence for issues tried by consent.
