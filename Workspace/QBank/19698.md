Question: Daniel signed a recorded instrument granting Hannah, the owner of the adjoining lot, “an easement prohibiting Daniel and Daniel's successors from excavating below the marked support line along the boundary.” The instrument gives Hannah no general right to enter Daniel's land. What is the best classification of Hannah's right?
Answer A: An express negative easement because Hannah may prevent a specified use of the burdened land.
Answer B: A revocable license because Hannah has no right to enter Daniel's land.
Answer C: An affirmative easement because all easements require physical entry onto the servient parcel.
Answer D: A profit because the restriction concerns excavation below the surface.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A negative easement restrains the servient owner from using land in a specified way. The instrument prohibits excavation below a support line. Hannah need not have a general entry right for the restraint to operate as a negative easement.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because lack of general entry does not make a recorded negative easement a license. C: C is wrong because not all easements authorize the holder to physically enter the servient land. D: D is wrong because a profit allows removal of resources; Hannah is preventing excavation.
lb_rule_reference: Negative easement / support restraint
