Outline: Motion for judgment on the pleadings
Question: A plaintiff mails a waiver-of-service request to a defendant in Oregon on March 1. The defendant signs and returns the waiver on March 20. The plaintiff files the waiver with the federal court. The defendant serves an answer on April 23. The plaintiff moves for default, arguing that the answer was due 21 days after the waiver was returned. Is the plaintiff correct about the answer deadline?
Answer A: Yes, because every answer is due 21 days after service or waiver
Answer B: Yes, because the answer deadline runs from the date the waiver is returned
Answer C: No, because the answer was due 60 days after the waiver request was sent
Answer D: No, because waiver of service eliminates the need to answer
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The defendant returned the waiver, so the answer deadline was 60 days after March 1. An April 23 answer is within that period.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Waiver of service has a special answer deadline. A U.S. defendant gets 60 days from the date the waiver request was sent. B: The rule counts from the date the waiver request was sent, not the return date. March 1 is the anchor. D: The defendant waived formal service, not the duty to respond to the complaint. Waiver extends the response deadline.
lb_rule_reference: For a defendant in the United States who timely returns a waiver of service, the answer is due 60 days after the request for waiver was sent.
