Question: A state health plan covers one medical procedure for therapeutic use but excludes the same procedure for cosmetic use. The exclusion applies to all patients regardless of sex. A challenger argues intermediate scrutiny applies because some women are more likely to seek the cosmetic use. What is the best tier-selection answer?
Answer A: Intermediate scrutiny automatically applies because the rule may affect more women in practice
Answer B: Rational basis generally applies because the plan distinguishes medical uses rather than sex classifications
Answer C: Strict scrutiny applies because all healthcare rules involve fundamental rights
Answer D: No Equal Protection review applies because benefit design is immune
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The law classifies by use of a procedure and applies to all patients.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The classification drawn is medical use, not sex as such, absent proof of sex classification or purpose. C: Importance does not automatically create a fundamental-right classification. D: Benefit classifications still receive Equal Protection review, even if deferential.
lb_rule_reference: Identify the classification actually drawn; a medical-use classification generally receives rational-basis review unless it classifies by suspect status, sex as such, or burdens a fundamental right.
