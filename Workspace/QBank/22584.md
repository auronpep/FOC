


BARMATRIX Q#: 22584
Outline_code: 55020100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Contracts
Subtopic: Formation of Contracts
Outline: Offer and acceptance
Question: A man texted a woman and offered to sell her his lawnmower for $50 if she agreed to buy the lawnmower by next week. The woman immediately sent the man a reply that said, “I'll give you $40.” The man texted back, “Nope—$50.” The woman texted the next day and said, “OK—I'll pay $50.” The man texted back, “You are very rude. I'm not selling you my lawnmower.” The woman sued the man to enforce the agreement. Should the court rule in the woman's favor?
Answer A: A: No, because the woman made a counteroffer.
Answer B: B: No, because the man rejected the woman's offer to pay $50.
Answer C: C: Yes, because the woman agreed to pay $50 for the lawnmower.
Answer D: D: Yes, because the woman's offer to pay $40 was a mere inquiry.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A counteroffer simultaneously rejects the initial offer, terminates the offeree's power of acceptance, and makes a new offer. While offerees do not make a counteroffer if they make a mere inquiry, they do make a counteroffer if they are clear that they will not accept the current offer. Here, the woman clearly stated that she would only pay $40. This was a counteroffer that terminated her power of acceptance. B is incorrect because the potential deal was already terminated when the woman made the counteroffer. C and D are incorrect because the agreement was terminated when the woman made a counteroffer.
