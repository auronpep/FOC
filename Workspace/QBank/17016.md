Question: A city places a winter display in front of city hall with a creche, a menorah, a decorated tree, and signs describing several winter holidays. No ceremony or prayer accompanies the display. Which answer best states the analysis?
Answer A: The display is automatically invalid because it includes a creche
Answer B: The display is automatically valid because it also includes secular items
Answer C: The display should be evaluated in context, including whether it endorses or coerces religious observance
Answer D: The display is valid only if the city removes every reference to religion
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The full setting and message matter in Establishment analysis.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Context matters; religious symbols are not automatically unconstitutional. B: Context helps but does not create an automatic safe harbor in every case. D: The Establishment Clause does not require total exclusion of religious references.
lb_rule_reference: Government holiday displays involving religious symbols require context-sensitive Establishment analysis rather than automatic invalidation or automatic approval.
