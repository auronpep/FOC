Question: Rachel conveyed Meadowacre “to Esther and her heirs, but if Esther fails to live on the land for at least one year before Esther turns 30, then to Elizabeth and her heirs.” Esther was 25 when the deed was delivered and immediately took possession. What estate does Esther have immediately after the conveyance?
Answer A: A fee simple subject to executory limitation.
Answer B: A contingent remainder.
Answer C: No estate until she lives on the land for one year.
Answer D: A life estate measured by her reaching age 30.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Esther receives a present possessory fee simple estate, but it is subject to divestment if the stated condition occurs. Because the divesting future interest is given to Elizabeth, a third party, Esther has a fee simple subject to executory limitation.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because Esther has a present possessory estate, not a future interest. C: C is wrong because living on the land for one year is not a condition precedent to possession; Esther takes immediately. D: D is wrong because the deed is not measured by Esther's life.
lb_rule_reference: Fee simple subject to executory limitation / present possessory estate
