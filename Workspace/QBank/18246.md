Outline: Diversity Jurisdiction
Question: A patient brings a state-law medical negligence claim in federal court under diversity jurisdiction. The forum state's statute caps noneconomic damages for that claim at $400,000. The jury awards $900,000 in noneconomic damages. No federal statute or Federal Rule addresses the cap. Should the federal court apply the state damages cap?
Answer A: Yes, because the cap defines the scope of the state-created remedy
Answer B: No, because federal courts always enter the jury's full damages award
Answer C: No, because damages are always procedural
Answer D: Yes, but only if the parties are citizens of the same state
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Damages limits attached to state substantive claims generally apply in diversity.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Federal forum does not free a state-law claim from substantive state limits. C: Remedy limits can be substantive for Erie purposes. D: Same-state citizenship would defeat diversity; it is not the Erie trigger.
lb_rule_reference: State substantive rules defining or limiting recovery apply under Erie absent a controlling valid federal directive.
