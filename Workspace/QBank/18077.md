Question: Jonah conveyed Springacre “to Ezra and his heirs, but if Paul graduates from college before Paul turns 25, then to Paul and his heirs.” Paul is 22 and has not graduated. Ezra is in possession. What interest does Paul have?
Answer A: A possibility of reverter.
Answer B: A vested remainder subject to open.
Answer C: A shifting executory interest.
Answer D: No interest unless Ezra later signs another deed.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Paul has a shifting executory interest. If Paul graduates before age 25, his interest will divest Ezra's present fee simple estate. The future interest is created in a third party and cuts short a prior grantee's estate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because a possibility of reverter is retained by a grantor. B: B is wrong because no life estate supports a remainder, and the interest is not vested. D: D is wrong because Paul's interest was created in Jonah's deed and does not require Ezra to sign a later deed.
lb_rule_reference: Shifting executory interest / third party condition
