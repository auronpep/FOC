Question: Isaac conveyed Whiteacre “to Rebekah for life, then to Jacob for life.” The deed did not state who would take Whiteacre after Jacob's death. Rebekah and Jacob are both alive. Which interest does Isaac retain?
Answer A: A possibility of reverter, because Whiteacre will return to Isaac automatically if Jacob dies.
Answer B: A shifting executory interest, because Isaac's interest will cut short Jacob's estate.
Answer C: A reversion in fee simple after the successive life estates end.
Answer D: A contingent remainder, because Isaac might never outlive Rebekah and Jacob.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Isaac retains a reversion in fee simple. He conveyed two successive life estates but did not convey the remaining fee simple after those life estates. The retained future interest is a reversion because it remains in the grantor.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because a possibility of reverter follows a fee simple determinable, not successive life estates. B: B is wrong because Isaac's interest does not cut short Jacob's life estate; it waits for the natural expiration of the granted estates. D: D is wrong because remainders are created in transferees, not retained by the grantor.
lb_rule_reference: Successive life estates / reversion in fee
