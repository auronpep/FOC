Question: Caleb posts a notice offering $500 to anyone who finds and returns his missing tablet computer. Before seeing the notice, Miriam finds the computer on a bus and returns it to Caleb because she knows it belongs to him. After returning it, Miriam learns of the reward and demands payment. Is Caleb contractually bound to pay the reward?
Answer A: Yes, because Caleb received the benefit he requested in the notice.
Answer B: No, because Miriam did not know of the reward offer when she performed.
Answer C: Yes, because Miriam suffered the detriment of returning the computer.
Answer D: No, but only because Miriam did not promise in writing to search for the computer.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A unilateral reward offer is accepted by performance in response to the offer. A person who performs without knowing of the offer has not accepted it. Miriam returned the computer before learning of the reward.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A: a benefit to Caleb is not acceptance if Miriam did not know of and act in response to the offer. C: the issue is not whether Miriam did something useful; it is whether her performance accepted the offer. D: a written promise was unnecessary, but knowledge of the reward was necessary.
