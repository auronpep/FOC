Outline_code: 74020102
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Homicide
Outline: Manslaughter
Question: A man and his son were playing baseball in their backyard. The man hit the ball and the wooden bat they were using split. The broken bat piece flew over the fence and smashed the neighbor's back window. The neighbor had just had the window replaced with very expensive glass. The neighbor came outside and picked up a big broken shard of glass. The man said that he would pay to replace the window and that it was no big deal. The neighbor, annoyed, threw the broken shard at the man's head and speared him in the chest. The man was stabbed in the heart and died instantly. Of which of the following crimes may the neighbor be properly convicted?
Answer A: A: Murder.
Answer B: B: Voluntary manslaughter, because the neighbor was sufficiently provoked by the expensive broken window.
Answer C: C: Voluntary manslaughter, because the neighbor was in the heat of passion when he threw the glass.
Answer D: D: Involuntary manslaughter, because there was no indication that the neighbor intended to kill the man.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Murder requires malice. While there is no indication that the neighbor intended to kill the man, he did throw a big glass shard at him in anger. As such, the neighbor's actions could be construed as an attempt to kill or inflict serious bodily injury. Because of this, the neighbor could be convicted for murder.B and C are incorrect because voluntary manslaughter occurs when an intentional killing is done in the heat of passion or anger. Here, the killing occurred by accident and the neighbor was only annoyed. This would take the killing out of the definition of voluntary manslaughter. D is incorrect because the neighbor acted with malice by throwing a big shard of glass at the man. As such, murder is the appropriate charge.
