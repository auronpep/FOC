Question: In a federal shoplifting trial, the store offers an automatically generated checkout video showing the register area. The store's technician, Abigail, explains how the system records, stores, and time-stamps videos and says this file came from that system. The defendant objects that no person saw the video being made. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Exclude the video because only a human eyewitness can authenticate video evidence.
Answer B: Exclude the video because automated systems are hearsay declarants.
Answer C: Admit the video if the process/system evidence is sufficient to support a finding that it is what the store claims.
Answer D: Admit the video only if the defendant appears in court wearing the same clothes.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Authentication can be supplied by evidence describing a process or system and showing it produces an accurate result. The proponent need not call a person who watched the recording occur.
