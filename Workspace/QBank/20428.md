Question: Aaron paid Naomi for an annual pass allowing him to use Naomi's private boat ramp during posted hours. The pass stated that Naomi retained control of the ramp and could suspend access for safety or maintenance. Aaron owns no land benefited by the pass. What interest did Aaron most likely receive?
Answer A: A license to enter and use the ramp under the pass terms.
Answer B: An easement in gross because Aaron paid money for access.
Answer C: A profit because Aaron used the ramp to reach water.
Answer D: Fee title to the ramp during the pass year.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A paid recreational pass generally creates a license: permission to enter and use land for a limited purpose under the owner's control. Payment alone does not create an easement or estate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because paid access alone does not show intent to create a nonpossessory property interest. C: C is wrong because Aaron is not removing resources from Naomi's land. D: D is wrong because a pass does not transfer title.
lb_rule_reference: License / paid access does not create estate
