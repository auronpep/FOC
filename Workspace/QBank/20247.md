Outline: Finality of Judgment
Question: A complaint has three unrelated claims against two defendants. The district court dismisses all claims against one defendant and writes, "The dismissed defendant is out of the case." The order does not direct entry of judgment and does not state that there is no just reason for delay. Claims against the other defendant remain. Does the order become immediately appealable under Rule 54(b)?
Answer A: Yes, because one defendant is fully dismissed
Answer B: Yes, because Rule 54(b) applies automatically in every multi-party case
Answer C: No, because the court did not expressly direct entry of judgment or determine that there is no just reason for delay
Answer D: No, because Rule 54(b) applies only to criminal cases
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The required certification language and judgment direction are missing.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Dismissing one party is not enough without the Rule 54(b) certification requirements. B: Rule 54(b) requires express action by the district court. D: Rule 54(b) is a federal civil procedure rule for multi-claim or multi-party civil cases.
lb_rule_reference: In a multi-claim or multi-party action, Rule 54(b) requires an express direction for entry of judgment and an express determination that there is no just reason for delay.
