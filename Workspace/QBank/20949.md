Outline: Bias
Question: Naomi sues Caleb and Joshua after a three-car crash. Before trial, Naomi settles with Joshua. Under the agreement, Joshua pays a small amount now and owes nothing more if Naomi recovers at least $100,000 from Caleb; otherwise Joshua must pay substantially more. Naomi calls Joshua to testify that Caleb caused the crash. Caleb seeks to cross-examine Joshua about the settlement agreement only to show Joshua's financial motive to favor Naomi. How should the court rule under Rule 408?
Answer A: Exclude the evidence because Rule 408 bars all evidence of settlement agreements.
Answer B: Permit the evidence for the limited purpose of showing Joshua's possible bias or prejudice, subject to ordinary balancing and limiting-instruction controls.
Answer C: Permit the evidence as substantive proof that Caleb caused the crash because Joshua settled.
Answer D: Exclude the evidence because settlement evidence may never be used for impeachment.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 408 bars compromise evidence when offered to prove or disprove the validity or amount of a disputed claim or to impeach by prior inconsistent statement or contradiction. But the rule permits other purposes, including showing a witness's bias or prejudice. Caleb offers the settlement only to show Joshua's financial stake in Naomi's recovery, so limited-purpose use is available.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Rule 408 is not an absolute settlement-evidence bar. Purpose controls. C: C is wrong because Rule 408 does not permit using settlement evidence as substantive proof of liability or fault. D: D is wrong because Rule 408 bars certain impeachment uses, such as prior inconsistent statement or contradiction, but expressly permits bias or prejudice uses.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 408 permits settlement evidence for witness bias/prejudice purpose
lb_student_diagnostic: Student cannot separate prohibited liability or contradiction use from permitted bias use.
