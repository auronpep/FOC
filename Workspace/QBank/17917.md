Question: Abraham owned a quarry. He agreed to sell Sarah 500 tons of stone, with Abraham responsible for cutting the stone from the quarry and loading it onto Sarah's trucks. Before any stone was cut, Abraham tried to treat the contract as a land transaction rather than a goods contract. Which source-law classification is most accurate?
Answer A: Common law governs because stone in the quarry is always land until physically removed.
Answer B: Common law governs because Sarah will eventually take the stone away in trucks.
Answer C: Article 2 may govern because this is a contract for the sale of minerals or similar materials to be removed from realty by the seller.
Answer D: Neither Article 2 nor common law governs until the stone has actually been cut.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A contract for the sale of minerals or similar materials to be removed from realty is a contract for the sale of goods under Article 2 if the seller is to sever them. Abraham agreed to cut and load the stone. That points to Article 2.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Article 2 specifically covers certain goods to be severed from realty. B: B is wrong because the buyer's later transportation does not defeat Article 2 when the seller severs the materials. D: D is wrong because a contract to sell goods to be severed can be formed before severance.
lb_rule_reference: UCC_goods
lb_student_diagnostic: Student sees realty and skips the goods-to-be-severed source-law rule.
