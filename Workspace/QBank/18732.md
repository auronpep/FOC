Question: A New York citizen sued Atlas Corp. for $120,000 in federal court. Atlas Corp. is incorporated in New York and has its principal place of business in Ohio. Atlas argues diversity exists because its headquarters is in Ohio. Is there complete diversity?
Answer A: Yes, because only the principal place of business matters for corporations
Answer B: Yes, because the plaintiff seeks more than $75,000
Answer C: No, because Atlas is a New York citizen through incorporation
Answer D: No, because corporations have no citizenship
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The plaintiff and Atlas both have New York citizenship, so complete diversity is absent.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A corporation is also a citizen of its state of incorporation. B: Amount cannot cure shared citizenship. D: Corporations have statutory citizenship for diversity.
lb_rule_reference: Complete diversity fails if any plaintiff shares citizenship with any defendant through either corporate citizenship prong.
