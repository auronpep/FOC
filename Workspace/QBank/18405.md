Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Personal Jurisdiction
Question: An Alabama resident traveled voluntarily to Oregon for a weekend hiking trip. While eating lunch in Oregon, she was personally served with process in an Oregon lawsuit arising from a contract dispute in Alabama. She had no other Oregon contacts. Oregon's rules allow personal service on individuals physically present in the state. Is personal jurisdiction likely proper?
Answer A: No, because the lawsuit is unrelated to Oregon
Answer B: No, because the defendant has no continuous and systematic Oregon contacts
Answer C: Yes, because she was personally served while voluntarily present in Oregon
Answer D: Yes, but only because the contract was made in Oregon
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Physical presence plus personal service on an individual remains a traditional PJ basis when presence is voluntary and service is valid.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Tag jurisdiction is a traditional basis for individuals voluntarily present in the forum; relatedness is not the controlling inquiry. B: The question is not corporate general jurisdiction; it is personal service on an individual in the forum. D: The facts say the contract dispute arose in Alabama; jurisdiction rests on tag service, not contract contacts.
lb_rule_reference: An individual personally served while voluntarily and physically present in the forum can be subject to personal jurisdiction there.
