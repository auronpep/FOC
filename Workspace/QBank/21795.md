Subtopic: Rights in Land

Question: Moses owned a field next to Ruth's vineyard. Moses orally told Ruth, “You may use the dirt path across my field to reach the stream whenever you want.” Ruth used the path for one season. She made no improvements, spent no money in reliance on the statement, and received no written grant. Moses then told Ruth to stop using the path. What interest does Ruth most likely have?
Answer A: An express easement, because Moses orally used words of permission.
Answer B: A revocable license, because the permission was oral and no reliance or written property grant appears.
Answer C: A prescriptive easement, because Ruth used the path openly for one season.
Answer D: An easement by necessity, because the path was more convenient than other routes to the stream.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A license is permission to enter or use land without creating a property interest. An easement is an interest in land and generally requires a writing unless another doctrine, such as estoppel or prescription, applies. Ruth had only oral permission and no reliance facts.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because oral permission ordinarily does not create an express easement. C: C is wrong because the use was permissive and lasted only one season. D: D is wrong because convenience does not establish strict necessity, and the facts involve permission rather than landlocked access at severance.
lb_rule_reference: Oral permission / revocable license
