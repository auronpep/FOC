Question: Jonathan leased a portable printing press from Abigail for six months. The written lease was silent about who must provide toner. Jonathan argues that Article 2's sale-of-goods rules automatically provide the meaning rule because the printing press is movable. What is the best response?
Answer A: Article 2 automatically governs because the press is movable.
Answer B: Article 2's sale-of-goods rules do not automatically govern because the transaction is a lease, not a sale.
Answer C: Real-property law governs because the press will sit on the floor.
Answer D: The parol evidence rule cannot apply to leases under any doctrine.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Article 2 governs transactions in goods involving sales, not every transaction involving a movable item. A lease of equipment requires a separate source-law analysis and should not automatically import Article 2 sale rules.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because movability alone is not enough; transaction type matters. C: C is wrong because the press does not become real property merely by sitting on a floor. D: D is wrong because leases can have their own writing and interpretation rules even if Article 2 sales rules do not control.
lb_student_diagnostic: Movable-object equals Article 2 overread.
