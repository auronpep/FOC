Outline: Subsequent remedial measures
Question: A consumer is injured when a ladder collapses. Two weeks later, the manufacturer adds a locking brace to the same model. In a products-liability action, the consumer offers the post-accident design change to prove that the original ladder was defectively designed and needed an additional warning. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Admit the evidence because later design changes always prove the product was defective.
Answer B: Exclude the evidence because subsequent remedial measures are not admissible to prove negligence, culpable conduct, product defect, design defect, or need for a warning.
Answer C: Admit the evidence because Rule 407 applies only to negligence, not product cases.
Answer D: Exclude the evidence only if the manufacturer admits liability.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 407 bars subsequent remedial measures when offered to prove negligence, culpable conduct, product defect, design defect, or need for a warning. The consumer offers the later brace for those prohibited purposes. The decisive axis is remedial measure plus barred purpose.

B is wrong because Correct answer C is wrong because Rule 407 expressly covers product defect, design defect, and warning-need purposes. D is wrong because The exclusion does not depend on an admission of liability. D: D is wrong because The exclusion does not depend on an admission of liability.
