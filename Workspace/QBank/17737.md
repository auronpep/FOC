Outline: Equal Protection Clause
Question: A water-storage district performs only narrow water-management functions and allocates votes based on land ownership affected by the district's projects. A resident with no affected land challenges the voting scheme under one-person-one-vote. What is the district's best response?
Answer A: One-person-one-vote applies identically to every body that holds any election
Answer B: Because the district is limited and special-purpose, ordinary one-person-one-vote rules may not apply in the same way
Answer C: No constitutional rule applies because water is property
Answer D: Strict scrutiny applies because every resident has a fundamental right to vote in every district election
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The district's narrow function and land-based impact support special-purpose treatment.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Limited special-purpose districts may be treated differently from general governmental bodies. C: Elections for public bodies can still implicate Equal Protection. D: Special-purpose districts can be exceptions to the standard one-person-one-vote model.
lb_rule_reference: One-person-one-vote rules apply strongly to general governmental bodies, but limited special-purpose districts may receive different treatment.
