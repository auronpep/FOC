Question: A century-old war memorial shaped like a cross stands in a public park among plaques and monuments honoring local soldiers. The city maintains the park but does not require anyone to participate in religious activity. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The memorial is automatically unconstitutional because it includes a religious symbol
Answer B: The memorial is automatically valid because it is old
Answer C: The court should consider the monument's history, context, and coercive effect rather than applying an automatic rule
Answer D: The memorial is valid only if the city adds symbols from every religion
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The facts call for context-sensitive Establishment analysis.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Religious symbolism is evaluated in historical and contextual setting. B: History is relevant but not a complete answer in every case. D: The Constitution does not impose a universal all-symbols requirement.
lb_rule_reference: A religious symbol on public property is not automatically unconstitutional; courts consider history, context, coercion, and whether the government is sponsoring religion.
