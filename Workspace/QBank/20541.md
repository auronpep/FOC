Question: Lydia hired Paul to train her workers in inventory practices for one month. Paul also agreed to provide ten inexpensive handheld scanners for workers to use during the training. Ninety percent of the contract price was for Paul's training time. The writing is silent on whether Lydia may keep the scanners after the training. Which source-law route best fits the dispute?
Answer A: UCC Article 2 controls because scanners are movable goods.
Answer B: Common law controls because the predominant purpose is training services and the scanners are incidental.
Answer C: UCC Article 2 controls because any contract that mentions equipment is a sale of goods.
Answer D: No source-law choice is needed because silence about the scanners means Lydia automatically owns them.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: This is a mixed transaction, but the predominant purpose is training services. The scanners are incidental to the training. The dispute about whether Lydia may keep the scanners should begin with common-law interpretation and any agreement-specific facts.
