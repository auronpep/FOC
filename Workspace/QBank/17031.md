Question: Hannah executed and delivered a valid deed conveying Riveracre “to Samuel when Samuel turns 25.” Samuel is 20 years old. The deed does not give present possession to anyone else. What interest does Hannah have immediately after delivery of the deed?
Answer A: A fee simple subject to Samuel's springing executory interest.
Answer B: A possibility of reverter, because Samuel might never turn 25.
Answer C: A right of entry, because Hannah must reenter if Samuel turns 25.
Answer D: No interest, because delivery of the deed immediately transferred Riveracre to Samuel.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Hannah retains the present fee simple, subject to Samuel's springing executory interest. Samuel does not receive possession until he turns 25. Until then, Hannah retains the estate; the law does not leave a gap in possession.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because no fee simple determinable was created. C: C is wrong because no condition subsequent or power of termination is involved. D: D is wrong because deed delivery does not eliminate the stated condition precedent to Samuel's possession.
lb_rule_reference: Springing executory interest / grantor retains present estate
