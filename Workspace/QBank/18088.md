Question: In a federal civil case, Daniel and Esther stipulate in writing that the shipment arrived on May 10. At trial, Daniel objects when Esther asks the court to read the stipulation to the jury, arguing that the written stipulation is hearsay. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because every written statement offered in court is hearsay.
Answer B: Sustain the objection unless the delivery driver testifies.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because a party stipulation is a proof vehicle that establishes the stipulated fact within its scope.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if the stipulation is notarized.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A stipulation between the parties can establish the stipulated fact. The issue is the effect of the stipulation, not hearsay from an absent declarant.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A mislabels the stipulation. B adds unnecessary testimony. D invents a notarization requirement. B: A mislabels the stipulation. B adds unnecessary testimony.
