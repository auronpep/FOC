Stem / Fact Pattern: The defendant and his friend decided to rob a woman at an ATM. When the woman withdrew her money, the defendant hit her with a brick and his friend grabbed her purse. As the two men were fleeing the scene, a police officer yelled for them to stop. When they did not do so, he shot them both. The defendant’s friend later died from his injuries. If the defendant is put on trial for felony murder, how should the court rule?
Answer A: A: Guilty, because his friend died during the commission of a felony.
Answer B: B: Guilty, because his friend died during the commission of a dangerous felony.
Answer C: C: Not guilty, because his friend was killed by a police officer.
Answer D: D: Not guilty, because the felony was completed when his friend was shot.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Felony murder is a foreseeable killing that occurs during the course of a felony. In the majority of states, a person cannot be found guilty of felony murder for the death of a cofelon if that co- felon is killed by the police. Here, since the defendant’s friend was shot by a police officer, it is unlikely he would be found guilty of felony murder. Therefore, A and B are incorrect. D is incorrect because a death that occurs while fleeing the scene still meets the requirements of felony murder, whether or not the felony itself is technically completed.
