Question: A city adopts an ordinance reserving 25% of all public construction contracts for firms owned by members of specified racial groups. The city says the ordinance is meant to remedy "historic societal discrimination," but it has not identified discrimination in its own contracting program or considered race-neutral alternatives. Which Equal Protection analysis applies?
Answer A: Rational basis applies because the ordinance has a remedial purpose
Answer B: Strict scrutiny applies, and the city must prove a compelling remedial interest and narrow tailoring
Answer C: Intermediate scrutiny applies because public contracts are economic benefits
Answer D: The ordinance is valid automatically because diversity is always a compelling interest
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The ordinance expressly classifies by race. A generalized reference to societal discrimination and no race-neutral-alternative analysis are serious narrow-tailoring problems.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Express racial classifications receive strict scrutiny regardless of benign motive. C: The classification is racial, so the economic context does not lower the tier. D: Strict scrutiny also requires narrow tailoring, and not every asserted diversity or remedial interest is sufficient.
lb_rule_reference: An express racial classification receives strict scrutiny even when the government describes the program as remedial; the government must prove a compelling interest and narrow tailoring.
