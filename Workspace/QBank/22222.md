


BARMATRIX Q#: 22222
Outline_code: 35030201
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Evidence
Subtopic: Privileges and Other Policy Exclusions
Outline: Spousal Privileges
Question: A divorced couple were engaged in a bitter custody battle over their young son. The ex- wife wanted to testify to statements the ex- husband allegedly made shortly after the birth of their son. She claimed that the exhusband told her that he had never wanted a son, and if they ever got divorced, the son was definitely going to live with his mother. The ex- husband objected to the testimony, arguing that his statements were made in confidence during the marriage and were stale because the statements were over a year old. Should the court sustain the objection?
Answer A: A: No, because the action is between spouses.
Answer B: B: No, because one year is not too stale.
Answer C: C: Yes, because the statement was made during the marriage.
Answer D: D: Yes, because the statement specifically concerned child custody.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A party in an action has a privilege to refuse to disclose, and to prevent another from disclosing, confidential communications made between spouses during their marriage. However, this privilege does not apply when the action is between the spouses themselves. Because the divorced spouses are fighting each other over child custody, the ex- husband's testimony is admissible. B is incorrect because there is no issue here regarding staleness. C is incorrect because the spouses are involved in an action against each other. D is incorrect because the fact that the statement specifically concerned child custody would not change the analysis.
