Outline: Unilateral contract
Question: David tells Mary, “I will pay you $300 if you deliver these ten sacks of grain to my barn before sunset.” Mary delivers the sacks to David's barn while David watches. Mary says nothing after unloading the sacks. David refuses to pay, arguing that Mary did not separately notify him that she accepted. Is David correct?
Answer A: No, because David saw Mary complete the requested performance, so no separate notice of acceptance was needed.
Answer B: Yes, because every unilateral contract requires formal written notice after performance.
Answer C: Yes, because unloading the sacks was merely a vague promise to perform later.
Answer D: Yes, because David's offer terminated when Mary finished performance without speaking.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Mary accepted the unilateral offer by completing the requested performance, and David knew of the performance because he watched it occur. A separate notice requirement serves no purpose when the offeror already knows acceptance has occurred.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B: notice is not always required after performance, especially when the offeror knows the performance occurred.
