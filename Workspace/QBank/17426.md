Question: Gina emails a business owner: "Pay me $5,000 by Friday or I will falsely tell your customers that you sell contaminated food." The owner wires the money the next day because he fears reputational damage. What is the best theft-related offense?
Answer A: Extortion, because Gina obtained property by a wrongful threat of future reputational harm
Answer B: Robbery, because all threats used to get property are robbery
Answer C: No offense, because the owner chose to wire the money
Answer D: Larceny by trick, because Gina used words rather than a weapon
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The threat coerced payment over time and did not require immediate force from the person or presence.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Robbery involves force or intimidation in taking from person or immediate presence; future threat points to extortion. C: Coerced consent induced by wrongful threat can support extortion. D: The key is coercive threat, not deception obtaining possession.
lb_rule_reference: Extortion involves obtaining property by wrongful threat, including threats of future reputational or economic harm, rather than immediate force at the taking.
