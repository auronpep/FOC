Question: A plaintiff sues two defendants in federal court. The district court dismisses all claims against Defendant A but leaves claims against Defendant B pending. The court does not mention Rule 54(b) or direct entry of final judgment. May the plaintiff immediately appeal the dismissal of Defendant A as a final judgment?
Answer A: Yes, because one defendant is completely out of the case
Answer B: Yes, because any dismissal is final
Answer C: No, not absent Rule 54(b) certification or another appeal route
Answer D: No, because dismissals are never appealable
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The case continues against Defendant B, and the court gave no Rule 54(b) direction.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Claims against another defendant remain unresolved. B: Partial dismissals require Rule 54(b) certification or another exception. D: Dismissals can be appealed when finality or a proper exception exists.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 54(b) requires express direction for entry of judgment and an express finding of no just reason for delay.
