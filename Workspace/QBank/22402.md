Outline_code: 74020102
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Homicide
Outline: Manslaughter
Question: A woman was walking from the parking lot into a mall when she saw a man walking inside with several handguns. The woman turned around and walked back to her car because she suspected the man might be planning to shoot people in the mall. On her way back to her car, she passed a police patrol car with two officers sitting inside but didn't say anything. When the woman got back home, she turned on the news and saw that the man she had seen had shot several people at the mall before being apprehended. Out of guilt, the woman went to the police station and confessed to what she had seen. The jurisdiction defined all crimes as they were defined at common law. What crime is the woman most likely guilty of committing?
Answer A: A: Murder.
Answer B: B: Voluntary manslaughter.
Answer C: C: Involuntary manslaughter.
Answer D: D: No crime.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The woman did not commit any crime because she did not have a legal duty to warn anyone about the man walking into the mall, despite the fact she suspected he was about to commit a crime he did in fact commit. Consequently, A, B, and C are incorrect.
