


BARMATRIX Q#: 22861
Outline_code: 94050100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Venue
Question: A construction company entered into a contract to build a childcare center. The company then entered into a subcontract with an electrical contractor for the center's wiring. The subcontract included a valid forum selection clause designating the federal district court in State A as the forum for any disputes under the contract. After failing to receive certain payments, the electrical contractor sued the construction company in federal district court in State B. The construction company moved to have the case transferred to the federal district court in State A as dictated by the forum selection clause. If the electrical contractor opposes the motion, what does the electrical contractor have to show?
Answer A: A: That the forum selection clause should not be enforced in the interest of justice.
Answer B: B: That the forum selection clause should not be enforced because of exceptional circumstances unrelated to the convenience of the parties.
Answer C: C: That the forum selection clause should not be enforced because the construction company had unfair bargaining power.
Answer D: D: That the forum selection clause should not be enforced because the original venue was proper.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Unless exceptional circumstances unrelated to the convenience of the parties exist, a court will enforce a valid forum selection clause because it represents the parties' agreement as to the most proper forum for any claims. Atlantic Marine Construction Co. v. United States District Court for the Western District of Texas, 571 U.S. 49 (2013). A is incorrect because it states an incorrect standard. C is incorrect because the question states that the forum selection clause was valid. D is incorrect because the forum selection clause will be enforced even if the original venue was proper.
