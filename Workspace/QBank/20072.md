Question: In a federal civil trial, both parties stipulate that the contract was signed on June 1. At the close of evidence, Eve asks the court to instruct the jury that the signing date is established by the stipulation. Adam objects that the jury must be free to reject every fact. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because juries may reject any stipulation.
Answer B: Sustain the objection because a stipulation is merely an attorney argument.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because the court may treat the stipulated fact as established and instruct the jury accordingly.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if a witness also testifies to the date.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A stipulation can remove a fact from dispute. The court may instruct the jury that the stipulated fact is established, depending on the stipulation's scope.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A and B understate stipulations. D adds an unnecessary witness requirement. B: A and B understate stipulations. D adds an unnecessary witness requirement. D: A and B understate stipulations. D adds an unnecessary witness requirement.
