Question: Samuel's Lot 1 is uphill from Joseph's Lot 2. A recorded easement provides that stormwater from Lot 1 may drain through an existing ditch on Lot 2. Joseph wants to fill the ditch and argues that Samuel's land is burdened because the water starts on Samuel's land. Which statement best describes the burdened and benefited parcels?
Answer A: Lot 1 is servient because the water begins there, and Lot 2 is dominant because it receives the water.
Answer B: Both lots are servient because water crosses both parcels.
Answer C: Neither lot is dominant because drainage easements cannot be appurtenant.
Answer D: Lot 1 is dominant because it benefits from drainage, and Lot 2 is servient because the ditch burden is on Lot 2.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The dominant parcel is the land benefited by the easement, and the servient parcel is the land burdened by the easement. Lot 1 benefits from being able to drain water, while Lot 2 bears the ditch burden.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the starting point of the water does not determine servient status. B: B is wrong because crossing both parcels does not make both servient. C: C is wrong because drainage rights may operate as easements appurtenant when they benefit land.
