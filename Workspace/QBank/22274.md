


BARMATRIX Q#: 22274
Outline_code: 75070000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Constitutional Protection of Accused Persons (Criminal Procedure)
Outline: Fourth Amendment
Question: A local jewelry store was robbed by two women wearing devil costumes. Police officers believed a local woman and her friend were responsible for the robbery. The officers obtained valid arrest warrants for the woman and her friend and went to the woman's apartment to arrest her. When they knocked on the door, they heard noises inside that led them to believe that the woman was attempting to flee. The officers announced themselves and entered the apartment. Just as the officers entered the apartment, the woman came out of the bedroom and closed the door behind her. The officers immediately arrested her. During the arrest, one of the officers went into the bedroom to see if the friend was inside. In the bedroom was a closed closet door. When the officer opened the closet, he saw two devil costumes that matched the descriptions given by witnesses. At trial, the woman moved to suppress the devil costumes. Should the costumes be suppressed?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the officers did not have a valid search warrant for the apartment.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the closet door was closed.
Answer C: C: No, because the bedroom search was incident to the arrest.
Answer D: D: No, because the costumes were discovered during a protective sweep.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: To protect the safety of police officers or others, arresting officers may conduct a quick protective sweep of the premises for potential attackers. Maryland v. Buie, 494 U.S. 325 (1990). If officers discover evidence in plain view during this sweep, the evidence can be admitted at trial. Here, the officers knew the woman had an accomplice. Consequently, it was reasonable for the officer to enter the bedroom and open the closet to look for any potential attackers. A is incorrect because the officers did not need a search warrant if they were conducting a valid protective sweep. B is incorrect because a closed closet was a valid place to quickly search for attackers. C is incorrect because the costumes were discovered during a protective sweep.
