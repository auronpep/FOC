


BARMATRIX Q#: 22141
Outline_code: 82010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Nature and Ownership of Land
Question: A law student rented an apartment from her landlord according to a signed lease agreement. The agreement was for three years and contained a prohibition on assignments. The student paid rent on time for two years. Then the student got an opportunity to study abroad, so she assigned the apartment to a visiting professor for six months of the eight months left on the lease. The professor agreed to pay rent and moved in. The student planned to move back for the last two months of the lease to study for the bar and attend graduation. The professor moved in but never paid any rent before he moved back out after six months. When the student returned home, the landlord demanded that she pay the six months of back rent left unpaid by the professor. Who is liable for the six months of back rent?
Answer A: A: The professor, because he agreed to pay six months of rent.
Answer B: B: The professor, because he accepted the assignment.
Answer C: C: The student, because the lease prohibited assignments.
Answer D: D: The student, because the professor was a subtenant.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: If a tenant retains any part of a leasehold interest when he or she transfers some of that interest to another, a sublease is created. A sublessor is not liable for any rent because there is no relationship between the landlord and the subtenant. Here, the student only transferred six months of the eight months left on the lease. Thus, this was a sublease, and the landlord cannot recover lost rent from the professor. A is incorrect because the professor's agreement to pay does no change the analysis. B and C are incorrect because this situation did not create an assignment.
