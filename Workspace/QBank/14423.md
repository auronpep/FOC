Question: A woman bought on credit and took delivery of a telescope from a seller for an agreed price of $100. The woman promised to pay the seller, "as soon as I am able." The woman subsequently repudiated this promise and refused to pay the seller anything. What effect does this quoted language have on enforceability of the promise?
Answer A: A: It makes the promise voidable.
Answer B: B: It makes the promise illusory.
Answer C: C: It requires the woman to prove her inability to pay.
Answer D: D: It requires the seller to prove the woman's ability to pay.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: D is correct. Because the woman made a promise to pay as soon as she was able, she will have to pay it once it is proven that she has $100. A
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect. Nothing in this promise would give one of the parties grounds to void the contract, such as duress or undue influence from the seller. B is incorrect. The woman's promise to pay $100 as soon as she could is enough to serve as consideration to make this contract enforceable, not illusory. C is incorrect. As the party who benefits from this eventual performance, the seller bears the burden of proving the condition required for enforcement has been satisfied.
