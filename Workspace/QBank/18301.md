Question: Boaz agreed to sell Ruth 2,000 bushels of wheat that were growing in his field and would be harvested the next month. The written agreement was silent about delivery sacks. Ruth offers evidence of trade usage requiring sealed sacks for wheat of that type. Which law supplies the content-and-meaning route?
Answer A: UCC Article 2, because identified growing crops to be severed and sold can be goods.
Answer B: Real-property law, because the wheat was growing in the soil when the contract was made.
Answer C: Common law services doctrine, because harvesting requires labor.
Answer D: No law of sales applies until the wheat is actually harvested.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Growing crops identified to the contract and to be severed and sold are treated as goods for Article 2 purposes. Article 2 supplies the meaning route, including possible use of trade usage when consistent.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because the crop sale is not the sale of the land. C: C is wrong because harvesting labor does not make the predominant transaction services. D: D is wrong because Article 2 can apply to future goods and growing crops identified to the contract.
lb_student_diagnostic: Crop-as-realty source-law trap.
