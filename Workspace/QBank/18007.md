Outline: Renewed Motion for Judgment as a Matter of Law
Question: Defendant made a proper Rule 50(a) motion before the case went to the jury. Judgment on the jury verdict was entered on May 1. Defendant filed a renewed Rule 50(b) motion on June 5. Is the renewed JMOL motion timely?
Answer A: Yes, because a preserved Rule 50(a) motion allows renewal at any time
Answer B: Yes, because 60 days is the normal deadline for every post-judgment motion
Answer C: No, because June 5 is more than 28 days after May 1
Answer D: No, because renewed JMOL must be filed before the verdict
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The motion is untimely under Rule 50(b).
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Preservation is necessary but not enough; the Rule 50(b) deadline still applies. B: Rule 50(b) uses a 28-day deadline after entry of judgment. D: Rule 50(a) is pre-verdict; Rule 50(b) is post-verdict.
lb_rule_reference: A Rule 50(b) renewed JMOL motion must be filed no later than 28 days after entry of judgment.
