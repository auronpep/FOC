


BARMATRIX Q#: 22338
Outline_code: 74020102
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Homicide
Outline: Manslaughter
Question: A man tried to rob a convenience store. The man entered, pointed a gun at the clerk, and told the clerk to hand over the money. The clerk screamed and jumped into a walk- in safe, pulling the door closed behind him and locking it. The man panicked, fled to his car, and drove off without taking anything. As he sped down the street at 10 miles per hour over the posted speed, the man cut off a school bus that then crashed into a car that was illegally parked on the road. The bus driver died in the crash. Driving 10 miles per hour over the posted speed limit was a misdemeanor. Which is the most serious crime of which the man can be convicted?
Answer A: A: Felony murder, because the bus driver's death occurred during the commission of a felony.
Answer B: B: Involuntary manslaughter, because the bus driver's death was accidental.
Answer C: C: Misdemeanor manslaughter, because the man's misdemeanor violation caused the bus driver's death.
Answer D: D: No crime, because the man did not complete the robbery and the bus driver's death was due to the illegally parked car.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: If a death occurs during a felony or attempted felony, the defendant is guilty of felony murder. Importantly, the felony does not end until the defendant reaches a place of safety. Here, the man committed attempted robbery at the store, a felony. The death occurred while the man was still fleeing, so the felony was not over yet. Thus, the most serious crime the man can be convicted of is felony murder. B is incorrect because the simple fact the murder was accidental would not take it out of the felony- murder rule. C is incorrect because the overriding felony was still ongoing. D is incorrect, because the man committed attempted robbery and the illegally parked car would not change the fact that the bus driver was killed during the man's commission of an attempted felony.
