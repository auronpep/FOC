Question: Naomi orally agreed to buy 500 sacks of grain from Joseph for $3 per sack. Joseph delivered 140 sacks. Naomi inspected the sacks, stored them in her warehouse, and resold them. Naomi refused to accept or pay for the remaining 360 sacks and raised the Statute of Frauds. How many sacks are enforceable under the UCC Statute of Frauds exception?
Answer A: None, because the total contract price exceeded $500 and no writing was signed.
Answer B: The contract is enforceable for the 140 sacks Naomi received and accepted.
Answer C: The contract is enforceable for all 500 sacks because Naomi accepted part of the shipment.
Answer D: The contract is enforceable only if Joseph proves a merchant confirmation was sent.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Under UCC §2-201, an oral goods contract can be enforced with respect to goods that have been received and accepted. Naomi's storage and resale show acceptance of the 140 sacks, but that exception does not enforce the remaining 360 sacks.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because received-and-accepted goods are a UCC exception. C: C is wrong because the exception applies only to the accepted quantity. D: D is wrong because merchant confirmation is a separate route and is not necessary for the accepted goods.
lb_rule_reference: UCC §2-201 / goods received and accepted
