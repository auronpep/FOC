Question: In a federal civil trial, Ruth sues Naomi for breach of a shipping contract. At Ruth's request, and based on an official government publication, the court properly takes judicial notice that a federal holiday fell on the delivery date. The case goes to the jury. What should the court tell the jury about the noticed fact?
Answer A: The jury may, but is not required to, accept the noticed fact as conclusive.
Answer B: The jury must accept the noticed fact as conclusive.
Answer C: The jury must disregard the noticed fact because judicial notice is for the judge only.
Answer D: The jury must decide the noticed fact only if Naomi introduces contrary evidence.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: In a civil case, once the court properly takes judicial notice of an adjudicative fact, the court must instruct the jury to accept the noticed fact as conclusive.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because that permissive instruction applies in criminal cases, not civil cases. C: C is wrong because the jury may be instructed on a properly noticed fact. D: D is wrong because the civil instruction is conclusive, not dependent on rebuttal evidence.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 201(f) / civil jury instruction
