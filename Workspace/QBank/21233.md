Question: Rachel conveyed Vineyardacre “to Joseph when Joseph turns 30.” The jurisdiction follows the modern rule that executory interests are transferable. When Joseph was 25, he validly conveyed “all of my interest in Vineyardacre” to Benjamin. Joseph later turned 30. Who owns Vineyardacre when Joseph turns 30?
Answer A: Benjamin, because he acquired Joseph's transferable springing executory interest.
Answer B: Rachel, because a springing executory interest is always personal and nontransferable.
Answer C: Joseph, because only Joseph personally could satisfy the age condition.
Answer D: Rachel and Benjamin as tenants in common.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Benjamin owns Vineyardacre. The stem states that the jurisdiction follows the modern rule allowing transfer of executory interests. Joseph validly transferred his springing executory interest to Benjamin. When Joseph turned 30, the condition tied to Joseph occurred and the transferred interest became possessory in Benjamin.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because the stem supplies the modern transferability rule. C: C is wrong because Joseph could be the measuring person for the condition even after transferring the interest. D: D is wrong because the deed and transfer do not create co-ownership; Benjamin's interest becomes possessory when the condition occurs.
lb_rule_reference: Modern transferability of executory interests
