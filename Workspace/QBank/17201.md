Question: Sarah conveyed Houseacre “to Mary for life, then to Joseph if Joseph graduates from medical school before Mary dies.” Joseph died without graduating from medical school. Mary later died. The deed contains no other disposition. Who owns Houseacre after Mary dies?
Answer A: Joseph's estate, because Joseph was the named remainderman.
Answer B: Mary's estate, because Mary was the last person in possession.
Answer C: No one, because the contingent remainder failed.
Answer D: Sarah or Sarah's successor, because Sarah retained a reversion that became possessory when the contingent remainder failed.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Sarah or Sarah's successor owns Houseacre. Joseph's remainder was contingent on graduating before Mary died. Joseph died without satisfying that condition, so the contingent remainder failed. Because the deed made no other disposition, Sarah's retained reversion became possessory when Mary's life estate ended.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Joseph never satisfied the condition precedent. B: B is wrong because Mary held only a life estate, which ended at her death. C: C is wrong because the grantor's reversion fills the gap when the contingent remainder fails.
lb_rule_reference: Failed contingent remainder / grantor reversion becomes possessory
