Subtopic: Privileges and Other Policy Exclusions
Outline: Attorney-client
Question: Peter, a federal prosecutor, subpoenas Abigail, Daniel's defense lawyer, for records showing only the dates and amounts of legal fees Daniel paid during the year before his arrest. The subpoena excludes narrative invoice descriptions, legal research entries, strategy notes, and communications seeking or giving legal advice. Daniel objects that all records in Abigail's file are attorney-client privileged. How should the court rule on the attorney-client privilege objection to the fee-date and fee-amount records?
Answer A: Overrule it, because the subpoena seeks fee-payment facts rather than confidential legal-advice communications or litigation-preparation materials.
Answer B: Sustain it, because any record held by a lawyer in a client file is attorney-client privileged.
Answer C: Sustain it, because the government seeks the records for an incriminating purpose.
Answer D: Sustain it as work product because all billing records are prepared in anticipation of litigation.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Attorney-client privilege protects confidential communications made for legal advice. The subpoena is limited to fee dates and amounts and excludes advice, strategy, and narrative descriptions. Those payment facts are generally not privileged merely because a lawyer possesses them. The decisive axis is protected object, not the incriminating effect of the evidence.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because lawyer possession does not transform every record into a privileged communication. C: C is wrong because incriminating effect does not itself create attorney-client privilege. The question is whether the object sought is a confidential legal-advice communication. D: D is wrong because ordinary billing dates and fee amounts are not automatically work product. Work product protects qualifying litigation-preparation materials, not all financial records in counsel's file.
lb_rule_reference: Fee amounts usually are not confidential legal communications
lb_student_diagnostic: Student overprotects lawyer-file material and fails to name the protected object.
