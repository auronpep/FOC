Question: Isaac agreed in a signed writing to sell Rachel “the small bronze loom in the west room” for $3,000. The court finds the writing final but not complete and exclusive. Before signing, Isaac orally promised two things: first, that he would include the loom's spare shuttle; second, that Rachel could instead take the large silver loom in the east room for the same price. Rachel offers evidence of both oral promises. Which ruling is best? Which ruling is best?
Answer A: Admit the spare-shuttle promise but exclude the large-silver-loom promise.
Answer B: Admit both promises because the writing was not complete and exclusive.
Answer C: Exclude both promises because both were made before signing.
Answer D: Admit the large-silver-loom promise but exclude the spare-shuttle promise.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The spare-shuttle promise is a consistent additional term that may supplement a partial integration. The large-silver-loom promise contradicts the written identification of the small bronze loom in the west room and is barred.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because partial integration does not admit contradictory terms. C: C is wrong because prior timing alone does not exclude consistent additional terms in a partial integration. D reverses the correct split. D: B is wrong because partial integration does not admit contradictory terms. C is wrong because prior timing alone does not exclude consistent additional terms in a partial integration. D reverses the correct split.
