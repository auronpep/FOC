Question: In a diversity action for state-law fraud, the forum state's substantive law requires clear and convincing evidence of reliance. The plaintiff argues that the federal court should use a preponderance standard because federal procedure controls trial conduct. Which burden should the federal court apply?
Answer A: The state clear-and-convincing burden, because it is tied to the state-law fraud claim
Answer B: The preponderance standard, because all burdens of proof are federal procedure
Answer C: No burden of proof, because juries decide facts freely
Answer D: The defendant's preferred burden, because Erie avoids forum shopping
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A burden attached to a state substantive claim normally follows the state right into diversity court.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Some burdens are part of the substantive state-law right or defense. C: A jury still applies the governing burden. D: Erie is not a party-preference rule.
lb_rule_reference: Erie applies state rules bound up with substantive rights, including burdens of proof on state-law elements or defenses.
