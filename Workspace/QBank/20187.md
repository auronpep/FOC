Question: Peter is sued for willful violation of a regulatory order. Peter testifies, “I acted in good faith because my lawyer told me the order did not apply.” Peter then refuses to produce communications with that lawyer about whether the order applied. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the privilege because Peter did not quote the lawyer's exact words.
Answer B: Find waiver as to communications about the advice Peter placed at issue by relying on it as a defense.
Answer C: Find waiver as to every confidential communication Peter has ever had with any lawyer.
Answer D: Sustain the privilege because advice of counsel can be asserted without allowing discovery.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A party who affirmatively relies on legal advice as a claim or defense may waive privilege for communications concerning that advice, because fairness permits the opponent to test the asserted reliance. The decisive axis is at-issue use of privileged advice.
