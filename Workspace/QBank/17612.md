Question: Isaac signed a written grant giving Rebecca “the right to enter Isaac's orchard each September for six years and pick and remove up to 200 baskets of apples.” The grant did not give Rebecca any other access or development right. How should Rebecca's right be classified?
Answer A: An affirmative easement only, because Rebecca must enter the orchard.
Answer B: A revocable license because the right is seasonal.
Answer C: An easement appurtenant because apples grow on land.
Answer D: A profit because Rebecca may enter the land and remove produce from it.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: A profit is the right to enter another's land and remove resources or produce from it. Rebecca's right to pick and remove apples is a profit. The entry is incidental to the resource or produce removal.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because entry alone is not the controlling feature; removal of produce is. B: B is wrong because seasonal duration does not make a written grant revocable. C: C is wrong because no dominant parcel is identified, and the right is to remove produce rather than to benefit land through use.
lb_rule_reference: Profit / harvest and removal
