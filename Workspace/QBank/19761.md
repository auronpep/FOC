Outline_code: 82010200
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Future Interests
Question: The landowner was the owner of a parcel of realty on which there were several buildings and a gold mine. The landowner lived in one of the buildings and personally worked the gold mine, earning a comfortable living by selling the gold that he removed. The other buildings were vacant. When the landowner died, his will devised the realty “to my friend, but if my friend should die without issue from his wife, to the mailman.” The landowner's friend and his wife did not have any children. Which of the following statements is correct about the mailman's interest in the realty?
Answer A: A: The landowner's will gave the mailman a vested remainder in the property.
Answer B: B: The landowner's will gave the mailman a contingent remainder in the property.
Answer C: C: The landowner's will gave the mailman a shifting executory interest in the realty.
Answer D: D: The mailman has no interest in the realty.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Consequently, A, B, and D are incorrect.
