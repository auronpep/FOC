Question: Deborah owns uphill Lot 1. Sarah owns downhill Lot 2. Sarah's deed states that “Lot 2 is burdened by a restriction prohibiting structures over 18 feet, for the benefit of Lot 1.” Deborah seeks to enforce the restriction after Sarah begins building a 25-foot addition. Which statement best describes the benefit and burden?
Answer A: Lot 2 is dominant because Sarah's construction occurs there.
Answer B: Lot 1 is servient because Deborah is the person trying to enforce the restriction.
Answer C: There is no benefited land because negative restrictions cannot benefit parcels.
Answer D: Lot 1 is benefited, and Lot 2 is burdened by the negative land-use restriction.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: For a negative land-use restriction, the benefited parcel is the land protected by the restriction, and the burdened parcel is the land whose owner must refrain from the restricted use. The deed expressly states that Lot 2 is restricted for the benefit of Lot 1.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the location of construction identifies the burdened land, not the dominant land. B: B is wrong because the enforcing owner's parcel is benefited, not servient. C: C is wrong because negative restrictions can protect benefited land when properly created.
