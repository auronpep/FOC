Question: Naomi executed and delivered a valid deed conveying Forestacre “to Peter when Peter returns to the state.” Peter has not returned. Naomi remains in possession. How should Peter's future interest be classified?
Answer A: A shifting executory interest, because Peter's interest is conditional.
Answer B: A springing executory interest, because it will divest Naomi, the grantor, if the condition occurs.
Answer C: A vested remainder, because Peter is ascertainable.
Answer D: A right of entry, because Peter must take action to claim possession.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Peter has a springing executory interest. His interest does not divest another grantee; it will divest Naomi, the grantor, if Peter returns to the state. Naomi retains the present estate until that condition occurs.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the interest springs from the grantor rather than shifting from another grantee. C: C is wrong because there is no prior life estate to support a remainder. D: D is wrong because a right of entry is a grantor's retained power after a condition subsequent, not Peter's future interest.
lb_rule_reference: Springing executory interest / divests grantor
