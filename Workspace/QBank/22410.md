


BARMATRIX Q#: 22410
Outline_code: 73030801
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Other Crimes
Outline: Receipt of stolen property
Question: A man went to stay with his brother for a few weeks. While staying at his brother's house, the man started worrying that his brother was dealing drugs. A few days later, the man met a police officer at a party. The man asked the police officer about what kinds of signs he should look for to determine whether his brother was dealing drugs. The police officer told him the best thing to do would be to ask his brother flat out if he was dealing drugs. If not that, maybe the man should look around and see if he finds any hidden drugs or paraphernalia somewhere in his brother's house. The next day, the brother left to be with friends and the man searched the house. In a closed wooden box, he found a significant amount of methamphetamine. The man called the police officer from the party, and the officer came and confiscated the drugs. The brother was charged with methamphetamine possession. The brother filed a motion to exclude the methamphetamine from evidence. Should the court grant the motion?
Answer A: A: No, because the brother left the man alone in the house.
Answer B: B: No, because the man found the methamphetamine.
Answer C: C: Yes, because the police officer directed the man's search.
Answer D: D: Yes, because the search violated the man's right to privacy by opening the closed wooden box.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The Fourth Amendment right against unreasonable searches does not count for searches by private individuals who are not acting as agents of government officials. Here, while the police officer might have given the man the idea to search, he certainly did not direct him to do so. Thus, the methamphetamine should not be excluded from evidence. A is not as good an answer as B because there is no issue of consent to search if the man is a private individual. C is incorrect because the officer may have given the man the idea for the search, but he did not direct the search. D is incorrect because the man was not a state actor or agent.
