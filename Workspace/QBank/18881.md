Question: Isaac conveyed Whiteacre “to Rebekah for life, then to Jacob and his heirs.” Rebekah is alive and living on Whiteacre. Which statement best describes the parties' interests?
Answer A: Rebekah has a life estate, and Jacob has a vested remainder in fee simple.
Answer B: Rebekah has a fee simple absolute because she is currently entitled to possession.
Answer C: Jacob has no legal interest until Rebekah dies.
Answer D: Isaac has a possibility of reverter because the deed limits Rebekah's possession.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Rebekah has a life estate because the deed gives her possession “for life.” Jacob has a vested remainder in fee simple because he is an ascertainable person and his right to possession follows naturally at the end of Rebekah's life estate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because present possession does not equal fee simple ownership. C: C is wrong because a remainder is a present future interest; Jacob's possession is postponed, but his legal interest exists now. D: D is wrong because Isaac did not retain a possibility of reverter. A possibility of reverter follows a fee simple determinable, not an ordinary life estate followed by a remainder.
