Outline: Nature and Type
Question: Abraham orally told Sarah, “You may cross the path through my pasture to reach the river, but I am not signing a deed and I can stop this whenever I choose.” Sarah crossed the path on weekends for two summers. She paid nothing for the permission, made no improvements, and spent no money in reliance on the path. Abraham then told Sarah that she could no longer use it. Sarah claims she has an easement. What is the best classification of Sarah's right?
Answer A: An express easement because Abraham identified a specific path.
Answer B: A revocable license because Sarah had permission to use the land but no property interest was created.
Answer C: A prescriptive easement because Sarah's use was open for two summers.
Answer D: An easement appurtenant because Sarah repeatedly used the path to reach the river.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A license is permission to enter or use land without creating a property interest. Abraham gave oral permission, expressly refused to sign a deed, reserved revocation power, and Sarah made no reliance expenditure. That points to a revocable license, not an easement.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because identifying a path does not create an express easement without intent and the required writing. C: C is wrong because permissive use is not adverse and two summers do not establish prescription. D: D is wrong because repeated use does not create an appurtenant easement, and no dominant parcel is identified.
