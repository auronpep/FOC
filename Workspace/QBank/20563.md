


BARMATRIX Q#: 20563
Outline_code: 62050402
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Torts
Subtopic: Other Torts
Outline: Invasion of privacy
Question: The following day, the manufacturer of the cigars placed several advertisements in newspapers. All the advertisements said, “Jimmy Stoker the Famous Comedian says our cigars are the best cigars made. He always smokes them, and you should too.” If the comedian asserts a claim against the cigar company for invasion of privacy by misappropriation of identity, the court should find for
Answer A: A: the cigar company, because the comedian had in fact made the statement that appeared in the advertisement.
Answer B: B: the cigar company, because the advertisement constituted a constitutionally protected form of commercial expression.
Answer C: C: the comedian, because when he made the statement on the television show, it was unforeseeable that the cigar company would use it in its advertising.
Answer D: D: the comedian, because the cigar company used his name to sell its product without his permission.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Misappropriation of identity is an invasion of privacy that consists of the unauthorized use of the plaintiff's identity for a commercial purpose. Since the cigar company used the comedian's name without his permission for the purpose of selling its product, it has committed this tort against the comedian.
Wrong Answer Explanations: Although the truth of the statement made in the advertisements might justify its publication for some purposes, its use for a commercial purpose is not privileged. A is therefore incorrect. Although the protection of the First Amendment to the United States Constitution has been extended to commercial expression, it has not been extended to the unauthorized use of another's identity for a commercial purpose. B is therefore incorrect. C is incorrect because liability for misappropriation of identity does not depend on the foreseeability of the defendant's conduct.
