Outline: Prior inconsistent statement
Question: Hannah sues David after slipping in David's store. The parties dispute whether the floor was wet. During settlement negotiations, David says, “The floor was wet, but I will pay only a nuisance amount.” At trial, David testifies that the floor was dry. Hannah offers David's settlement statement solely to impeach him by contradiction and prior inconsistent statement, not to show bias. Should the court admit David's settlement statement for that impeachment purpose?
Answer A: Yes. A statement used only for impeachment is not offered for its truth, so Rule 408 does not apply.
Answer B: Yes. David's statement is a party-opponent statement, and that status overrides the settlement rule.
Answer C: No. Rule 408 bars use of compromise-negotiation statements to impeach by prior inconsistent statement or contradiction.
Answer D: Yes. Rule 408 excludes only the dollar amount of a settlement offer, not factual assertions made during negotiations.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 408 excludes compromise offers and statements made during compromise negotiations when used to prove or disprove the validity or amount of a disputed claim, and it also bars using such evidence to impeach by prior inconsistent statement or contradiction. David's statement was made during settlement negotiations about the disputed floor condition, and Hannah offers it solely for contradiction/PIS impeachment. That specific impeachment route is barred.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Rule 408 expressly reaches impeachment by prior inconsistent statement or contradiction. The not-for-truth label does not solve this policy exclusion. B: B is wrong because party-opponent status is a hearsay route, not a trump over an independent policy exclusion. D: D is wrong because Rule 408 covers statements made during compromise negotiations, not merely dollar amounts or formal offers.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 408(a) bars compromise evidence used to impeach by prior inconsistent statement or contradiction
lb_student_diagnostic: Student uses the word impeachment as a universal admission route and misses Rule 408's specific contradiction/PIS bar.
