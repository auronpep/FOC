Question: In a federal civil trial, Hannah calls Paul. Her first substantive question is, “Tell the jury everything that happened during the six months you worked for Jonah.” Jonah objects that the question calls for a narrative answer. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Overrule the objection because a witness on direct may always testify in free narrative form.
Answer B: Sustain the objection or require more specific questions in the court's discretion to make the testimony effective and avoid wasting time.
Answer C: Sustain the objection only if Paul is hostile.
Answer D: Overrule the objection because narrative questions are allowed on direct but forbidden on cross.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The court has reasonable control over the mode and order of examining witnesses. It may require focused questions rather than an unlimited narrative to avoid confusion and waste of time.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A makes narrative testimony a right. C wrongly ties narrative control to witness hostility. D invents a categorical direct/cross rule.
