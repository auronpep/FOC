Question: A borrower asked a lender to lend her $1,000. The lender replied that he would do so only if the borrower's father would guarantee the loan. At the borrower's request, the father mailed a signed letter to the lender: "If you lend $1,000 to my daughter, I will repay it if she doesn't." On September 15, the lender, having read the father's letter, lent $1,000 to the borrower, which the borrower agreed to repay in installments of $100 plus accrued interest on the last day of each month beginning October 31. The father died on September 16. Later that same day, unaware of the father's death, the lender mailed a letter to the father advising that he had made the $1,000 loan to the borrower on September 15. The borrower did not pay the installments due on October 31, November 30, or December 31, and has informed the lender that she will be unable to make repayments in the foreseeable future. On January 15, the lender is entitled to a judgment against the borrower for which of the following amounts?
Answer A: A: Nothing, because if he sues before the entire amount is due, he will be splitting his cause of action.
Answer B: B: $300 plus the accrued interest, because the borrower's breach is only a partial breach.
Answer C: C: $1,000 plus the accrued interest, because the borrower's unexcused failure to pay three installments is a material breach.
Answer D: D: $1,000 plus the accrued interest, because the failure to pay her debts as they come due indicates that the borrower is insolvent and the lender is thereby entitled to accelerate payment of the debt.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: B is correct. Although the borrower failed to make three repayments of the debt and expressed her inability to repay the debt in the future, the lender cannot accelerate the debt unless the contract specified otherwise and can only recover the three months of unpaid debt due and outstanding as of January 15. A
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect. The lender can recover the amount that is due as of January 15. C is incorrect. The breach will be treated as a minor breach. The damages to the lender are less than a third of the total loan as of January 15. Therefore, the lender may sue immediately for the unpaid debt from September through December. D is incorrect. The debt may not be accelerated unless the contract expressly so provides.
