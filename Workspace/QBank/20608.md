Question: Deborah conveyed Orchardacre “to Sarah and her heirs, but if Sarah fails to build a home on the land within two years, then to Samuel and his heirs.” Sarah immediately took possession. One year has passed, and Sarah has not yet built a home. Which statement best describes the current interests?
Answer A: Sarah has a fee simple subject to executory limitation, and Samuel has a shifting executory interest.
Answer B: Sarah has no estate until the home is built.
Answer C: Samuel has a possibility of reverter because the land may return automatically.
Answer D: Deborah has a right of entry because Sarah has not yet built the home.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Sarah has a fee simple subject to executory limitation, and Samuel has a shifting executory interest. Sarah took possession immediately. Her estate will be divested only if she fails to build a home within the two-year period.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because building the home is not a condition precedent to Sarah's initial possession. C: C is wrong because Samuel is a third party, not the grantor, and his interest is executory. D: D is wrong because the deed gives no right of entry to Deborah and the two-year period has not yet expired.
lb_rule_reference: Executory limitation / condition based on failure to perform
