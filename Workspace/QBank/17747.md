Outline: Motion to strike
Question: In an answer to a contract complaint, the defendant includes pages of inflammatory allegations about the plaintiff's unrelated family disputes. The allegations have no connection to any claim or defense. The plaintiff moves to strike that matter. Which rule best fits the motion?
Answer A: Rule 12(f), because the matter may be immaterial or scandalous
Answer B: Rule 12(b)(1), because scandalous matter defeats subject-matter jurisdiction
Answer C: Rule 12(b)(6), because the plaintiff's claim must fail
Answer D: Rule 56, because pleadings are always summary judgment evidence
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 12(f) permits striking improper matter from pleadings.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Jurisdiction is not the issue. C: The motion targets content in the answer, not whether the complaint states a claim. D: The issue is pleadings, not record proof.
lb_rule_reference: A motion to strike is not the same as a Rule 12(b)(6) merits dismissal.
