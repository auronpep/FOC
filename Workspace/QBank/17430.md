Outline: Unilateral contract
Question: Naomi posts a notice: “I will pay $1,000 to anyone who repairs my irrigation pump.” Peter sees the notice, buys tools and replacement parts, and drives to Naomi's farm. Before Peter touches the pump or begins the repair, Naomi tells Peter that she revokes the offer. Is Naomi's revocation effective?
Answer A: No, because Peter spent money in preparation to perform.
Answer B: Yes, because Peter had not begun the requested performance before Naomi revoked.
Answer C: No, because Peter's drive to the farm was a promise to complete the repair.
Answer D: No, because any reward notice creates an option contract once read.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Preparations to perform, even costly preparations, do not necessarily begin the requested performance for a unilateral offer. Because Peter had not begun repairing the pump before revocation, Naomi's revocation was effective absent another irrevocability route.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A: mere preparatory reliance is not the same as beginning the requested performance under this unilateral offer.
