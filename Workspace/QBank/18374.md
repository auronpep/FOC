Question: Isaac posts a notice offering $800 to anyone who finds and returns his missing horse. Joseph sees the notice and plans to search the next morning. That night, before Joseph begins searching or finds the horse, Isaac dies. Joseph later finds and returns the horse to Isaac's estate and demands the reward. Is the estate contractually bound to pay Joseph?
Answer A: No, because Isaac died before Joseph accepted by performance or began performance that would make the offer irrevocable.
Answer B: No, because reward offers are never enforceable.
Answer C: Yes, because Joseph saw the reward notice before Isaac died.
Answer D: Yes, because Joseph relied by planning to search the next morning.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: An ordinary offer terminates upon the offeror's death before acceptance unless an option or other irrevocability route exists. Joseph had not begun or completed the requested performance before Isaac died.
