Question: A school took elementary students on a museum field trip. Teachers knew one student had wandered away twice that morning. During lunch, the teachers left the children unsupervised near an exit. The same student left the museum and was injured crossing a street. Which duty statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The school may owe a duty because it had a special relationship with and custody over the student
Answer B: The school owed no duty because no one must rescue a stranger
Answer C: The school owed no duty unless it physically caused the street injury
Answer D: The school is automatically liable for any injury during a field trip
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Schools supervising young students owe reasonable protective care, especially when they know of a wandering risk.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: The student was not a stranger; the school had custody and a special relationship. C: It is not the only exception. Special relationships can also create duties. D: The duty is reasonable care, not automatic liability for every field-trip injury.
lb_rule_reference: Special relationships, such as school-student custody, can create duties to take reasonable protective measures.
