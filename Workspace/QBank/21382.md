Outline: Nature and Type
Question: Abraham owned Fieldacre in fee simple, including both the surface estate and the mineral rights. Abraham signed and delivered to Isaac a quitclaim deed of “all my right, title, and interest in the mineral rights under Fieldacre.” Isaac accepted. Isaac later claimed that because Abraham owned the whole parcel, the quitclaim deed gave Isaac both the surface estate and the mineral rights. What did Isaac receive?
Answer A: Only Abraham's mineral-rights interest under Fieldacre, because the quitclaim deed conveyed the described interest and gave no warranty or enlargement beyond that description.
Answer B: All of Fieldacre, because a quitclaim deed from a full owner always transfers every interest the owner has in the parcel.
Answer C: Nothing, because a quitclaim deed cannot transfer mineral rights.
Answer D: All of Fieldacre only if Isaac paid fair market value for the surface estate as well.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source is a quitclaim deed, and the decisive deed-type/description lock is the interest described in the instrument. Abraham owned more than the mineral rights, but the deed conveyed only his right, title, and interest in the mineral rights. The quitclaim form gives no warranty and does not expand the described estate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because the parcel owner's total ownership does not override the deed's limited description. C: C is wrong because mineral rights can be conveyed by deed. D: D is wrong because value does not enlarge the interest described in the deed.
lb_rule_reference: Quitclaim deed of a limited described interest conveys only the described interest and gives no title covenants
