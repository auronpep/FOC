Question: A manufacturer agreed to build custom shelves for a store owned by Lydia. The contract stated, “Manufacturer's duty to begin work is subject to Buyer providing final shelf measurements by March 1.” Lydia refused to provide measurements because she wanted to hire a cheaper manufacturer, then claimed the manufacturer breached by not beginning work. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The manufacturer breached because beginning work was an express duty.
Answer B: Lydia may enforce the contract because the manufacturer did not substantially perform.
Answer C: The manufacturer's duty to begin work did not mature because Lydia failed to provide the measurements required by the condition.
Answer D: Lydia may recover in restitution because conditions never affect the existence of contract duties.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The manufacturer's duty to begin work was expressly conditioned on Lydia providing measurements. Lydia's failure to provide the measurements meant the duty did not mature; she cannot turn the unfulfilled condition into the manufacturer's breach.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the duty was conditional, not absolute. B: B is wrong because substantial performance is not the issue before the duty to begin arose. D: D is wrong because a condition can determine whether a duty is presently enforceable.
lb_rule_reference: Good-faith cooperation / condition excused
