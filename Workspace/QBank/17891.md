Question: Elkanah executed and delivered a valid deed conveying Riveracre “to Deborah when Deborah opens a medical office on the land.” Deborah has not opened a medical office. Who owns the present possessory estate before the office is opened?
Answer A: Deborah, because the deed was delivered.
Answer B: Deborah and Elkanah as tenants in common.
Answer C: Elkanah, because the condition precedent to Deborah's possession has not occurred.
Answer D: No one, because title is suspended until Deborah opens the office.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Elkanah owns the present possessory estate. Deborah has a springing executory interest that becomes possessory only if she opens the medical office. Until then, the grantor retains the estate; title is not suspended.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because delivery does not satisfy the express condition. B: B is wrong because the deed does not create shared possession. D: D is wrong because there is no gap in seisin; Elkanah retains ownership during the waiting period.
