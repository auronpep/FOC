Outline_code: 82010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Nature and Ownership of Land
Question: A landlord and his friend entered into a lease agreement for a small house on the landlord's property. The landlord and the friend had known each other for a long time, and the landlord had often helped his friend out of trouble. The friend's doctor asked the landlord to keep an eye on the friend. The landlord loved his friend, but he didn't know how long the rental arrangement would really last because the friend had a tendency to wreck rental properties. The friend promised to be on his best behavior this time. The landlord said he had to protect himself, so he had the friend sign a lease agreement that stated the lease term as “this term will continue until the landlord, in his sole discretion, decides to terminate the lease. At that point, the friend has six hours to vacate the premises.” The property was in a jurisdiction that followed the majority rule. What type of tenancy does the friend have?
Answer A: A: A term of years tenancy with an automatic end.
Answer B: B: A periodic tenancy based on the friend's rental payments.
Answer C: C: An at- will tenancy that may only be terminated by the landlord.
Answer D: D: An at- will tenancy that may be terminated by either the landlord or the friend.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: An at- will tenancy is a tenancy with no fixed duration that may be terminated by the landlord or the tenant at any time. Although this particular lease states that only the landlord can terminate the lease, most courts would interpret such a lease as giving the right of termination to either party. Otherwise, the landlord could hold the friend hostage. A is incorrect because a term of years tenancy has a set termination date. This lease did not. B is incorrect because the lease did not state any recurring period for the duration of the lease. C is incorrect because most courts would also give the friend the power to terminate the lease.
