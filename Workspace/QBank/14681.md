Question: A defendant, in desperate need of money, decided to hold up a local convenience store. Determined not to harm anyone, he carried a toy gun that resembled a real gun. In the store, he pointed the toy gun at the clerk and demanded money. A customer who entered the store and saw the robbery in progress pulled his own gun and fired at the defendant. The bullet missed the defendant but struck and killed the clerk. The defendant was charged with felony murder. His best argument for being found NOT guilty is that he
Answer A: A: did not intend to kill.
Answer B: B: did not commit the robbery because he never acquired any money from the clerk.
Answer C: C: did not intend to create any risk of harm.
Answer D: D: is not responsible for the acts of the customer.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: D is correct. The defendant's best argument for being found not guilty of felony murder is that he is not responsible for the customer's actions in shooting the clerk. The clerk was killed by the customer, a third party who is not the defendant or an accomplice. The defendant's best defense is that he is not responsible for the death of an innocent party by a third party.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect. Felony murder does not require that the defendant have the intent to kill. It is sufficient that the defendant has the intent to commit the underlying felony. In this case, the defendant had the intent to commit a robbery, which is all that is needed. B is incorrect. To convict a defendant for felony murder, the prosecution must prove the defendant committed or attempted to commit a proper underlying felony. In this case, the defendant did not need to complete the robbery in order to be liable for felony murder. It is enough that the death occurred during the commission of the felony. C is incorrect. Felony murder requires the intent to commit the underlying felony. It does not matter that the defendant intended the harm or not, as long as the harm was a foreseeable result.
