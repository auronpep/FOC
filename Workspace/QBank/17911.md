Question: A city requires large commercial dance halls with hundreds of patrons to obtain safety permits. A dance hall owner claims strict scrutiny because patrons associate socially on the premises. What is the best response?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny applies because all social interaction is intimate association
Answer B: Rational regulation is more likely because the business hosts broad commercial gatherings, not intimate relationships
Answer C: The law is void for vagueness because permits are involved
Answer D: The owner wins because business operation is a fundamental right
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The permit rule regulates a commercial venue and does not target close family or intimate relationships.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Intimate association protects close personal relationships, not every large commercial gathering. C: No vague standard is described; the claim is overbroad substantive association. D: Ordinary commercial operation is not a fundamental substantive due process right.
lb_rule_reference: Constitutional protection for intimate association is strongest for close personal relationships, not every large or commercial association.
