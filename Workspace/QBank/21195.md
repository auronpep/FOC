Question: Isaac signed a deed conveying Groveacre to Jacob, handed it to Jacob, and said, “This is your land now, but keep peace in the family and do not record the deed until after my funeral.” Jacob accepted and kept the deed. Isaac died six months later. Who owned Groveacre during the six months before Isaac died?
Answer A: Isaac, because a reference to Isaac's funeral made the deed testamentary.
Answer B: Jacob, because Isaac's present-transfer words and delivery passed title immediately despite the delayed-recording instruction.
Answer C: Isaac, because Jacob had not recorded the deed before Isaac's death.
Answer D: Jacob only if Isaac's heirs consented after the funeral.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source is a deed, and the decisive delivery lock is present intent. Isaac said the land was Jacob's now and handed Jacob the deed. The reference to a later funeral concerned recording, not the time title would pass. Title passed immediately between the parties.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because not every death-related instruction is testamentary; the question is whether title was intended to pass now. C: C is wrong because recording is not required for delivery between grantor and grantee. D: D is wrong because heirs cannot undo a completed lifetime delivery merely by withholding consent.
lb_rule_reference: Present delivery is not defeated by an instruction not to record until later
