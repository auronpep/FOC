Outline_code: 61020603
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Torts
Subtopic: Negligence
Outline: Comparative negligence
Question: The jurisdiction had a statute that provided that “In any negligence action, a plaintiff's recovery shall not be barred by that plaintiff's fault, but the recovery of said plaintiff shall be diminished in proportion to that plaintiff's fault.” Which of the following correctly states the sum to which the car driver is entitled?
Answer A: A: $6,000 ($10,000 less 40 percent).
Answer B: B: $4,000 ($10,000 less 60 percent).
Answer C: C: $10,000.
Answer D: D: 0.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The statute given is typical of the “pure comparative negligence” approach. Since the car driver's loss amounted to $10,000, and since it was 40 percent his own fault, 40 percent of $10,000 (or $4,000) must be deducted from his recovery. The balance that he is entitled to collect is $6,000. Therefore, B, C, and D are incorrect.
