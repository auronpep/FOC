Outline_code: 73020000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Other Crimes
Outline: Crimes against the Person
Question: A man paid for a cup of coffee with a credit card and sat down at a table. As he was sitting, he reached into his pocket and realized his wallet was missing. He walked back to the counter and asked the barista if she had his wallet. She said she didn't, but the man didn't believe her. He raised his fist and said, “If you don't give me my wallet back, I'm breaking your nose!” The barista, who had severe heart and anxiety conditions, had a heart attack and died from fright. What is the most serious crime the man should be found guilty of committing?
Answer A: A: Murder.
Answer B: B: Voluntary manslaughter.
Answer C: C: Involuntary manslaughter.
Answer D: D: Assault.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Generally, when an intentional battery or assault results in an unintended death, the defendant is guilty of involuntary manslaughter. Importantly, the man was only attempting to regain his wallet. Since this would not count as a robbery, the man could not be convicted of felony murder. A is incorrect because the barista's death was not caused intentionally or as the result of a felony. B is incorrect because voluntary manslaughter is a provoked intentional killing. Here, the man did not intend to kill the barista. D is incorrect because involuntary manslaughter is a more serious crime than assault.
