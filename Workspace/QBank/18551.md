Outline_code: 73030400
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Other Crimes
Outline: Robbery
Question: The victim, who lived alone, was a collector of antiques. One day, the defendant followed the victim to work. Knowing that the victim's valuable antiques collection was stored in her home, the defendant phoned the victim at work and told her that he had placed a bomb in her home. He said that if she immediately paid him $1,000 in cash, he would give the police information necessary for them to defuse the bomb. If she did not pay him, he said would detonate the bomb, destroying her home and her collection of antiques. The victim paid the defendant as instructed. In reality, the defendant had not placed a bomb in the victim's home. What is the most serious offense of which the defendant is likely to be convicted?
Answer A: A: Robbery.
Answer B: B: Extortion.
Answer C: C: Larceny by trick.
Answer D: D: Embezzlement.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Robbery consists of obtaining property from another by using or threatening force against a person. Since the defendant made no threat of harm to a person, A is incorrect. Larceny by trick is a larceny committed by making a misrepresentation that induces the victim to part with temporary possession of personal property. Since the victim did not expect her cash to be returned, she was not parting with temporary possession of it, and C is incorrect. Embezzlement is a criminal conversion of personal property by one in lawful custody of it. Since the defendant was not in lawful custody of the victim's money, D is incorrect.
