Question: Detectives gave Omar Miranda warnings. After eight hours of questioning, they told him, "If you confess to the robbery, you will go home tonight and the prosecutor will drop the case." The detectives had no authority to make that promise and knew it was false. Exhausted, Omar confessed. What is the best voluntariness analysis?
Answer A: The confession is admissible because Omar received Miranda warnings
Answer B: The confession is admissible because all promises of leniency are permitted interrogation tactics
Answer C: The confession is inadmissible because the false promise and prolonged questioning made it involuntary
Answer D: The confession is inadmissible only if Omar was physically harmed
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The detectives knowingly promised a benefit they could not deliver after a lengthy interrogation. Under the totality, the confession was coerced.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Warnings do not cure a confession produced by coercive false promises and prolonged pressure. B: A false promise of immediate release and dismissal, after prolonged questioning, can overbear the will. D: Coercion can arise from threats, promises, and psychological pressure, not just physical violence.
lb_rule_reference: Promises of leniency or benefit are evaluated under the totality of circumstances and can render a confession involuntary when coercive.
