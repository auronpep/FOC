Question: A diversity plaintiff files in federal court in State A despite a valid mandatory forum-selection clause requiring suit in federal court in State B. The case is transferred to State B to enforce the clause. The plaintiff argues State A choice-of-law rules must travel under the ordinary Section 1404 transfer rule. What is the best answer?
Answer A: The plaintiff is correct because transfer never affects choice-of-law rules
Answer B: The transferee court generally applies State B choice-of-law rules after transfer enforcing the valid clause
Answer C: Federal common law chooses the most plaintiff-friendly law
Answer D: The case must be dismissed because transfer cannot enforce a forum clause
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A plaintiff who violated the forum-selection clause does not receive the usual transferor-law benefit.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Forum-clause enforcement is a recognized exception to ordinary transferor-law carryover. C: The analysis is about the forum-selection transfer effect, not preference. D: Transfer can enforce a federal-forum selection clause in an appropriate case.
lb_rule_reference: When a plaintiff files outside the contracted forum and the case is transferred to enforce a valid forum-selection clause, the transferee forum ordinarily applies its own choice-of-law rules.
