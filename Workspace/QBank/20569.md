Question: Hannah licensed a hall to Joshua for a concert. The contract stated, “The hall license remains effective unless the fire marshal limits occupancy below 100 people before the concert date.” Two days before the concert, the fire marshal issued an order limiting occupancy to 60 people. Hannah refused to provide the hall. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Hannah breached because the hall license was already effective when signed.
Answer B: Joshua may compel use of the hall because substantial performance of the concert plan is enough.
Answer C: Hannah breached unless the fire marshal's order made performance impossible.
Answer D: Hannah's duty to provide the hall was discharged by the condition subsequent stated in the contract.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The contract created an existing license that would end if the fire marshal limited occupancy below 100 before the concert. That terminating event occurred, so Hannah's duty to provide the hall was discharged by the condition subsequent.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because an existing duty may be cut off by a condition subsequent. B: B is wrong because substantial performance does not override the terminating condition. C: C is wrong because the contract's condition subsequent controls without needing a separate impossibility analysis.
lb_rule_reference: Condition subsequent / duty cut off
