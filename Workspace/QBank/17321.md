Question: In a federal civil trial, Ruth testifies for the plaintiff. The defendant calls Naomi, who has lived in Ruth's town for many years, to testify that Ruth's reputation in the community is that she is not truthful. The plaintiff objects that no one has first attacked Ruth's credibility. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because a witness's character for untruthfulness may be proved only after the witness first claims to be truthful.
Answer B: Sustain the objection because reputation evidence may never be used to attack a witness's credibility.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because a witness's character for untruthfulness may be attacked by reputation testimony.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if Naomi also describes specific lies Ruth told.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: FRE 608(a) permits a witness's credibility to be attacked by testimony about the witness's reputation for having a character for untruthfulness. A prior attack is required before supporting truthful character, not before attacking untruthfulness.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the sequence limit applies to supporting truthful character, not attacking untruthfulness. B: B is wrong because reputation is one of the permitted forms under Rule 608(a). D: D is wrong because specific lies are not required and are not the direct proof form for a Rule 608(a) reputation attack.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 608(a) / reputation attack for untruthfulness
lb_student_diagnostic: Tests the attack/support sequence and the permitted reputation form for untruthfulness.
