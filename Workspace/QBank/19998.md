Outline: Duty of reasonable prudent person in prevailing circumstances
Question: An adult who had just received a driver's license panicked while merging and braked in a freeway lane without checking traffic. A following car struck her. She argues she should be judged by the care of a reasonable beginner driver. Which standard applies?
Answer A: The care of a reasonable beginner driver
Answer B: The care of a reasonably prudent driver under the circumstances
Answer C: No standard applies because freeway driving is inherently risky
Answer D: The driver's subjective fear controls
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Adult driving is judged by the ordinary objective standard, not a beginner-specific standard.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Inexperience generally does not lower the adult driving standard. C: Risky activities are exactly where reasonable care matters. D: The negligence standard is objective and is not reduced to personal fear.
lb_rule_reference: Inexperience generally does not lower the reasonable-person standard for adult activities such as driving.
