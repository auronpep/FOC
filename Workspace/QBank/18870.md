Question: Benjamin posts a reward: “$500 to anyone who returns my lost scroll.” Esther, who has not seen or heard of the reward, finds the scroll and returns it to Benjamin. After returning it, Esther learns of the reward and demands $500. Is Benjamin contractually obligated to pay the reward?
Answer A: No, because Esther did not know of the offer when she performed the requested act.
Answer B: Yes, because Benjamin received the benefit he requested.
Answer C: Yes, because every reward notice is enforceable regardless of the performer's knowledge.
Answer D: Yes, because Esther accepted by demanding payment after learning of the reward.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A unilateral contract requires performance in response to the offer. Esther completed the act before knowing of the reward, so her performance was not acceptance of Benjamin's offer.
