Outline: Contingent and vested remainders
Question: Naomi conveyed Greenacre “to Ruth for life, then to Boaz if Boaz survives Ruth.” Boaz is alive. The deed contains no further disposition. What interest, if any, does Naomi retain immediately after the conveyance?
Answer A: No interest, because Boaz is alive and named in the deed.
Answer B: A reversion, because Boaz's contingent remainder may fail if he does not survive Ruth.
Answer C: A possibility of reverter, because Greenacre might return to Naomi automatically.
Answer D: A right of entry, because Boaz must satisfy a condition.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Naomi retains a reversion. Boaz's remainder is contingent because he must survive Ruth before he can take possession. If Boaz does not survive Ruth, the deed gives Greenacre to no one else, so Naomi's retained reversion would become possessory.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because naming Boaz does not eliminate Naomi's retained interest when Boaz's remainder is contingent. C: C is wrong because a possibility of reverter follows a fee simple determinable, not a life estate followed by a contingent remainder. D: D is wrong because a right of entry follows a condition subsequent in a defeasible fee, not a condition precedent to a remainder.
