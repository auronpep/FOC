


BARMATRIX Q#: 22425
Outline_code: 55020100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Contracts
Subtopic: Formation of Contracts
Outline: Offer and acceptance
Question: A cowbell manufacturer sent a letter to a retail music store. The letter stated, “I will sell you 50 cowbells at $5 per cowbell. This offer is good for 30 days from today's date.” The manufacturer signed and dated the letter. The retail store got the letter but didn't respond because it wasn't sure if cowbells would be a big seller that holiday season. Two weeks later, the cowbell manufacturer got a letter from a university marching band supplier saying that it would buy as many cowbells as the manufacturer could make at $7 per cowbell. The manufacturer accepted the supplier's offer and called the retail store to tell it that it revoked its offer to sell the store cowbells. Based on the manufacturer's earlier offer, the store had planned to make a big push to sell cowbells, although it hadn't informed the manufacturer by the time the manufacturer called to revoke. The store sued, arguing that the manufacturer could not revoke its offer. Did the manufacturer effectively revoke its offer?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the store had not communicated any acceptance to the manufacturer.
Answer B: B: Yes, because there was no consideration to support keeping the offer open.
Answer C: C: No, because the store had 30 days to accept the offer from the date of the letter.
Answer D: D: No, because the manufacturer did not communicate its revocation in writing.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Under UCC §2- 205, a merchant's promise to hold an offer open is an irrevocable firm offer if the offer is in writing, signed by the merchant, and expressly states that it will be held open. A firm offer can be irrevocable for up to 90 days. Here, the cowbell manufacturer was a merchant, the offer was signed and in writing, and the merchant stated that it would hold the offer open for 30 days. Consequently, the manufacturer's offer met all the requirements of a firm offer and the manufacturer could not revoke that offer for the promised 30 days. A is incorrect because the store did not need to communicate any acceptance to the manufacturer for 30 days by the terms of the manufacturer's firm offer. B is incorrect because a merchant's firm offer does not need consideration to be irrevocable. D is incorrect because the firm offer gave the store 30 days to accept.
