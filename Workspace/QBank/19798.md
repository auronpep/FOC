Question: Hannah owns uphill Lot 1. Samuel owns downhill Lot 2. A recorded easement provides that stormwater from Lot 1 may drain through an existing ditch on Lot 2. Samuel argues that Lot 1 must be servient because the water starts there. Which statement is correct?
Answer A: Lot 1 is servient because the water begins on Lot 1, and Lot 2 is dominant because it receives the water.
Answer B: Both lots are servient because water crosses both parcels.
Answer C: Neither lot can be dominant because drainage rights cannot be easements.
Answer D: Lot 1 is dominant because it benefits from drainage, and Lot 2 is servient because the ditch burden is on Lot 2.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Dominant and servient status depends on legal benefit and burden, not on where water begins. Lot 1 benefits from drainage; Lot 2 bears the ditch burden. Lot 1 is dominant and Lot 2 is servient.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the physical starting point of the water does not decide the legal burden. B: B is wrong because crossing facts do not make both parcels servient. C: C is wrong because drainage rights may operate as easements.
lb_rule_reference: Drainage easement / benefited and burdened land
