Question: A state prosecutes a felony by information after a preliminary hearing rather than by grand jury indictment. The defendant argues that every federal criminal procedure guarantee in the Bill of Rights automatically applies to the states. What is the best response?
Answer A: The defendant is correct because every Bill of Rights provision is incorporated against the states without exception
Answer B: The grand jury indictment requirement has not been incorporated against the states
Answer C: Incorporation happens through Article III rather than the Fourteenth Amendment
Answer D: The state must use a grand jury only if the defendant is indigent
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: States need not use federal grand jury indictment procedures.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The grand jury indictment requirement is a classic nonincorporated exception. C: Incorporation of rights against states is a Fourteenth Amendment due process doctrine. D: Indigency does not create a state grand jury indictment requirement.
lb_rule_reference: Most Bill of Rights protections apply to the states through the Fourteenth Amendment Due Process Clause, but the Fifth Amendment grand jury indictment requirement has not been incorporated.
