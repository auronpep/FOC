Question: A defendant believes the plaintiff's complaint contains a frivolous legal contention. The defendant serves a Rule 11 sanctions motion on the plaintiff. Ten days later, the plaintiff amends the complaint to remove the challenged contention. The defendant then files the sanctions motion anyway. How should the court rule on the filed Rule 11 motion?
Answer A: Grant it because the defendant served the motion before the plaintiff corrected the complaint
Answer B: Deny it because the challenged contention was withdrawn within the safe-harbor period
Answer C: Grant it automatically because any frivolous contention requires monetary sanctions
Answer D: Deny it only if the plaintiff proves the original complaint was correct
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The motion should not be filed when the challenged paper is timely withdrawn or corrected.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Service starts the safe harbor; correction within 21 days prevents filing the motion. C: Rule 11 includes procedure and discretion; it does not impose automatic monetary sanctions in every case. D: The safe-harbor procedure can bar filing even without adjudicating the original contention's correctness.
lb_rule_reference: A Rule 11 motion for sanctions must be served but not filed if the challenged paper is withdrawn or corrected within the 21-day safe-harbor period.
