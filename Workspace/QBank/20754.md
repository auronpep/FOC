Outline: Judicial notice
Question: During a federal civil jury trial, the judge announces that she has taken judicial notice from an official map that the distance between two towns is 12 miles. The judge had not warned the parties before making the announcement. Rachel immediately asks to be heard, arguing that the map measures a different route than the one disputed in the case. What should the judge do?
Answer A: Give Rachel an opportunity to be heard on the propriety of taking notice and the nature of the noticed fact.
Answer B: Deny the request because a party may be heard only before the court takes notice.
Answer C: Automatically declare a mistrial because the court took notice before hearing from Rachel.
Answer D: Tell the jury to decide whether the court was allowed to take judicial notice.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A party is entitled to be heard on timely request regarding judicial notice, even if the court took notice before notifying the party. The proper response is to allow Rachel to address the propriety and nature of the noticed fact.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because Rule 201 preserves a hearing right even when the court acted first. C: C is wrong because the remedy is not automatically mistrial. D: D is wrong because the judge decides the propriety of judicial notice.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 201(e) / hearing after notice
