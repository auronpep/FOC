Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Fee simple absolute
Question: Elijah conveyed Cedaracre “to John and his heirs, but if the land is ever used as a tavern, Elijah may enter and terminate the estate.” John later opened a tavern on the land. Elijah has not entered, sued, or otherwise acted to terminate John's estate. Who owns Cedaracre now?
Answer A: Elijah, because title automatically reverted when the tavern opened.
Answer B: John, subject to Elijah's right of entry.
Answer C: John, in fee simple absolute because the deed used the words “and his heirs.”
Answer D: The tavern operator, because the land is now being used commercially.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The deed created a fee simple subject to condition subsequent. The phrase “but if” plus the grantor's power to “enter and terminate” gives Elijah a right of entry. John's estate does not end automatically; Elijah must act to terminate it.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because automatic reverter applies to a fee simple determinable. C: C is wrong because “and his heirs” does not erase the condition. D: D is wrong because business operation does not confer fee ownership.
lb_rule_reference: Fee simple subject to condition subsequent / right of entry
