Subtopic: Privileges and Other Policy Exclusions
Outline: Subsequent remedial measures
Question: John is injured while using a press made by a manufacturer. At trial, the manufacturer's engineer, Solomon, testifies, “Before this accident, no safer guard design was possible for this press.” John then offers evidence that, after the accident, the manufacturer installed a different guard that made the same operation safer. John offers the evidence only to impeach Solomon's feasibility claim and asks for a limiting instruction. How should the court rule under Rule 407?
Answer A: Exclude the evidence because Rule 407 bars all post-accident safety measures for every purpose.
Answer B: Admit the evidence for the limited purpose of genuine impeachment or disputed feasibility, subject to ordinary balancing and a limiting instruction.
Answer C: Admit the evidence to prove that the original guard was defective because the later guard was safer.
Answer D: Admit the evidence automatically for any impeachment purpose, without asking whether the impeachment use is genuine or whether limiting controls are needed.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 407 bars subsequent remedial measures when offered to prove negligence, culpable conduct, product defect, design defect, or need for warning. The rule allows other purposes, including impeachment or disputed feasibility, when the use is genuine and not merely a backdoor to prove defect or fault. Solomon made a broad feasibility claim that no safer design was possible; the later guard directly impeaches that claim. The proper route is limited admission subject to ordinary balancing and limiting controls.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Rule 407 is not an absolute bar. It permits other purposes such as impeachment and disputed feasibility. C: C is wrong because defect or negligence is the prohibited purpose Rule 407 is designed to block. D: D is wrong because impeachment is not a magic label. The court must police whether the impeachment use is genuine and may use balancing and limiting instructions.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 407 permits genuine impeachment or disputed feasibility use, but not a defect-purpose disguise
lb_student_diagnostic: Student either excludes all repairs or uses impeachment as a backdoor for defect proof.
