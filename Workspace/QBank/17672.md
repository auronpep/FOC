Question: In a federal defamation trial, Eve offers a cropped copy of a one-page flyer to prove the flyer called her dishonest. Adam objects that the cropped copy omits the bottom paragraph, which appears to qualify the accusation. Eve has the full original but refuses to produce it. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Admit the cropped copy because every duplicate is admissible to the same extent as an original.
Answer B: Admit the cropped copy but instruct the jury to ignore the missing paragraph.
Answer C: Exclude the cropped copy because the circumstances make it unfair to admit the incomplete duplicate while the full original is available.
Answer D: Exclude the cropped copy only because defamation cases require live testimony instead of documents.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Duplicates are generally admissible, but not when a genuine problem or unfairness exists. A cropped copy omitting qualifying text while the original is available is unfair.
