Outline: Expectation damages
Question: Lydia contracted with Abraham to maintain the refrigeration system in Lydia's fish market. Abraham breached by missing one inspection. During the same month, a citywide seafood recall unrelated to the refrigeration system caused Lydia's sales to collapse. Lydia seeks the month's entire lost profits, but she cannot connect the loss to the missed inspection. Can Lydia recover those lost profits from Abraham?
Answer A: Yes, because Abraham breached the maintenance contract.
Answer B: Yes, because the amount of lost profits can be calculated from Lydia's past sales records.
Answer C: No, because Lydia has not shown that Abraham's breach caused the claimed lost profits.
Answer D: No, because lost profits in service contracts are never recoverable.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Reasonable certainty about amount is not enough. The lost profits also must be caused by the breach. If the sales collapse resulted from an unrelated recall, Lydia cannot recover those profits from Abraham.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because breach alone does not establish every claimed loss. B: B is wrong because proof of amount does not prove causation. D: D is wrong because lost profits can be recoverable in service contracts if the required gates are met.
