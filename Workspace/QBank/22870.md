


BARMATRIX Q#: 22870
Outline_code: 95070303
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Pretrial Procedures
Outline: Class actions
Question: A man bought $50,000 worth of Quesocoins, a digital currency. A week later, a newspaper published a story showing that Quesocoin's management had been artificially inflating the value of Quesocoins. A week after that, Quesocoin lost all of its value and Quesocoin became worthless. Everyone who owned Quesocoins suffered a significant financial loss. The man filed a class action in federal district court. There were thousands of owners of Quesocoins, and discovering their identities and giving them notice would likely be expensive and time consuming. Does the man have to give the other owners of Quesocoin notice?
Answer A: A: Yes, because absent class members are always entitled to notification from the class representative.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the class action involves a common question.
Answer C: C: No, because the size and cost of notice makes notice to the other class members unreasonable.
Answer D: D: No, because securities class actions require special notification from the court.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Under Fed. R. Civ. P. 23, a class representative in a class action has to give notice to all absent class members if the class action involves a common question. Here, all of the Quesocoin owners were damaged by the management's artificial inflation of Quesocoins, so there is a common question and all class members need to receive notice. A is incorrect because the class representative does not always have to give notice to the absent class members. For example, if it is cheaper and easier for the defendant to give notice, the court may order it to do so. Oppenheimer Fund v. Sanders, 437 U.S. 340 (1978). C is incorrect because the expense and time involved in giving notice to thousands of Quesocoin owners is unlikely to be unreasonable in this instance. D is incorrect because securities class actions do not require special notification from the court.
