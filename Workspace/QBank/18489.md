Question: Abraham orally told Nathan to sign a deed conveying Abraham's warehouse to Caleb. Nathan signed and delivered the deed to Caleb. After Caleb accepted, Abraham orally told several neighbors, “I approve what Nathan did.” Abraham never signed any deed, power of attorney, or written ratification. Caleb recorded the deed. Who owns the warehouse?
Answer A: Caleb, because Abraham orally ratified Nathan's act after the deed was delivered.
Answer B: Caleb, because recording the deed supplied the missing authority.
Answer C: Caleb, because an oral ratification is enough whenever the grantee relied on the deed.
Answer D: Abraham, because Nathan lacked written authority and Abraham did not sign a written adoption or ratification of the deed.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source claimed is an agent-executed deed, and the decisive validity gate is written authority or written adoption by the owner. Nathan had only oral authority, and Abraham later gave only oral approval. Recording and reliance do not cure the missing written authority lock, so title remains with Abraham.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because oral ratification does not satisfy the writing requirement for an agent's deed authority. B: B is wrong because recording is a priority and notice mechanism, not a cure for invalid execution. C: C is wrong because reliance does not replace the deed-authority writing requirement.
lb_rule_reference: Unauthorized agent deed / oral ratification insufficient
