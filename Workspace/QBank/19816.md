Question: The complaint and answer have been filed. No counterclaim remains unanswered. Trial is six months away. The defendant moves for judgment on the pleadings, arguing the complaint and answer show the plaintiff has no legal claim. Is Rule 12(c) procedurally available?
Answer A: Yes, because the pleadings are closed and the motion is early enough not to delay trial
Answer B: No, because Rule 12(c) must be filed before any answer
Answer C: No, because Rule 12(c) is available only after trial
Answer D: Yes, because Rule 12(c) lets the court weigh deposition testimony
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: That is the basic timing requirement for Rule 12(c).
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Rule 12(c) comes after pleadings close. C: It is a pretrial pleadings motion. D: Rule 12(c) ordinarily tests the pleadings, not outside evidence.
lb_rule_reference: Judgment on the pleadings tests legal sufficiency on the pleadings after pleadings are closed.
