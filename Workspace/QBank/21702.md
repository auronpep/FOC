Question: Hannah's will devised Silveracre “to Mary, provided that the land is used only as a residence. If the land is not so used, my executor may enter and terminate Mary's interest.” Mary later converted the residence into a restaurant. The executor has not acted. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Mary's estate automatically ended when she opened the restaurant.
Answer B: Mary owns the land in fee simple absolute because the devise did not use the phrase “so long as.”
Answer C: Mary has only a tenancy at sufferance because she violated the condition.
Answer D: Mary owns a fee simple subject to condition subsequent until the executor exercises the right of entry.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The devise uses conditional language and gives the executor power to enter and terminate Mary's interest. That creates a fee simple subject to condition subsequent. Mary's estate continues unless and until the right of entry is exercised.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because automatic termination is not the rule for a condition subsequent. B: B is wrong because the devise does contain conditional language and an express termination power. C: C is wrong because violation of a deed or will condition does not convert fee ownership into a landlord-tenant tenancy.
lb_rule_reference: Devise / condition subsequent
