Question: Samuel conveyed Marketacre “to Paul if Paul passes the bar examination before age 28.” Paul is 25 and has not passed the bar examination. Samuel remains in possession. What interest does Paul have now?
Answer A: A present fee simple subject to condition subsequent.
Answer B: A vested remainder because Paul is named in the deed.
Answer C: A life estate until age 28.
Answer D: A springing executory interest.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Paul has a springing executory interest. Paul does not receive present possession unless he satisfies the condition before age 28. Until then, Samuel retains the present estate, and Paul's interest will divest Samuel if the condition occurs.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Paul did not receive present possession. B: B is wrong because no supporting life estate creates a remainder. C: C is wrong because the deed does not grant Paul possession for life or for a term; age 28 is part of the condition precedent.
lb_rule_reference: Springing executory interest / no present estate in conditional grantee
