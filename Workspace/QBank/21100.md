Question: Hannah conveyed Valleyacre “to Samuel when Samuel graduates from college.” Samuel has not graduated. Hannah remains alive and in possession. How should the interests be classified immediately after the conveyance?
Answer A: Hannah retains a present fee simple subject to Samuel's springing executory interest.
Answer B: Hannah has a reversion after Samuel's life estate.
Answer C: Samuel has a vested remainder because he is named in the deed.
Answer D: Samuel has a present fee simple absolute because the deed was delivered.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Hannah retains the present fee simple subject to Samuel's springing executory interest. Samuel's right to possession is conditioned on graduating from college. If the condition occurs, his interest will divest Hannah, the grantor.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because Hannah did not convey a life estate or lesser present estate to Samuel. C: C is wrong because there is no prior life estate to support a remainder, and Samuel's interest will divest the grantor if it becomes possessory. D: D is wrong because Samuel's possession is subject to a condition precedent.
lb_rule_reference: Grantor retained estate subject to springing executory interest
