Question: Naomi sues Eli in federal court to enforce a contract clause requiring written notice before cancellation. Naomi offers testimony from her office clerk that cancellation letters were always logged and mailed the same day, and a log entry shows Eli's letter was mailed. Eli denies receiving it. Naomi asks for a jury instruction that receipt is established. What instruction should the court give?
Answer A: The judge should decide receipt conclusively because the log is a business record.
Answer B: The judge should require Naomi to prove receipt with the original letter before the jury may consider the log.
Answer C: The jury may consider the routine mailing proof and Eli's denial in deciding receipt; it need not treat receipt as established.
Answer D: The jury may consider the log only to impeach Eli, not as evidence relevant to receipt.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Routine mailing proof and a log entry can support an inference or presumption of receipt, but Eli's denial leaves the issue for the jury. The jury may consider the proof; receipt is not conclusively established.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because admissibility of the log does not make the judge the factfinder on receipt. B: B is wrong because the issue is receipt, not necessarily the content of the original letter. D: D is wrong because the proof is substantive evidence on receipt, not merely impeachment.
