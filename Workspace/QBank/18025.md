Outline: Nonresponsive answers
Question: Sarah sues Abraham for breach of contract. On direct examination, Sarah asks her own witness Rachel, “Did you inspect the shipment when it arrived?” Rachel answers, “Yes, and Sarah is a greedy person who sues everyone.” Sarah immediately moves to strike the statement that she is greedy. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Deny the motion because a party may never move to strike an answer by the party's own witness.
Answer B: Grant the motion because the statement was a volunteered, nonresponsive opinion that did not answer the inspection question.
Answer C: Deny the motion because the statement concerns Sarah's character and character evidence is always admissible in civil cases.
Answer D: Grant the motion only if Abraham first agrees that Rachel is biased.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The lawyer who asked the question may move to strike a nonresponsive answer by the witness. Rachel's statement that Sarah is greedy did not answer whether Rachel inspected the shipment and is not a proper response to the question.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the examining party may seek to strike a nonresponsive answer by that party's own witness. C: C is wrong because the statement is not automatically admissible character evidence. D: D is wrong because the motion does not depend on the opposing party's agreement about bias.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 103(a)(1); FRE 611(a) / examining party motion
