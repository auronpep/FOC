Outline: Liquidated damages
Question: Esther contracted with David to rent a banquet hall for a professional conference for $20,000. The contract provided that if Esther canceled within seven days of the event, she would pay $4,000 as liquidated damages. When the contract was made, the parties reasonably expected that a late cancellation could cause hard-to-measure staffing, scheduling, and lost-opportunity losses, and $4,000 was a reasonable forecast of those losses. Esther canceled three days before the event. David rebooked the hall and proved only $1,500 in net actual loss. What amount may David recover for Esther's cancellation?
Answer A: $4,000, because the liquidated-damages amount was a reasonable forecast of difficult-to-prove loss when the contract was made.
Answer B: $1,500, because liquidated damages cannot exceed the actual loss ultimately proved.
Answer C: Nothing, because David rebooked the hall and therefore mitigated the loss.
Answer D: $5,500, because David may add the proved actual loss to the liquidated-damages amount.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The remedy interest is expectation under common law, measured by a valid stipulated damages term. The breach posture is Esther's cancellation, and the loss category is a liquidated amount rather than a fresh actual-damages formula. The penalty gate is satisfied because the late-cancellation losses were difficult to prove at contracting and $4,000 was a reasonable forecast. The formula is the enforceable liquidated amount: $4,000. The result scope is limited to the clause; David does not also recover the same actual loss separately.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because it uses an actual-loss-only shortcut and treats a lower later loss as automatically making the clause a penalty, triggering WAL-REM-015. C: C is wrong because mitigation does not erase an otherwise valid liquidated-damages clause. D: D is wrong because it stacks actual damages on top of the stipulated amount for the same cancellation loss, an overcompensation error under WAL-REM-008.
