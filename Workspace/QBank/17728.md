Question: Congress prohibits knowingly shipping stolen electronics across state lines. A defendant argues Congress cannot punish him because the theft occurred entirely within one state and only the shipment crossed state lines afterward. What is the best response?
Answer A: Congress may regulate use of interstate channels to move stolen goods
Answer B: Congress lacks power unless the theft itself happened in multiple states
Answer C: Congress lacks power because stolen goods are outside commerce
Answer D: Congress may regulate only if the defendant personally owns a shipping company
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The statute targets shipment across state lines, a channel of interstate commerce.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: The regulated act includes interstate shipment. C: Congress can keep harmful or unlawful items out of interstate channels. D: The statute can reach a person who uses interstate channels.
lb_rule_reference: Congress may regulate the channels of interstate commerce, including keeping harmful or stolen goods from moving through those channels.
