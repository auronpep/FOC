Question: In a federal lease dispute, Martha offers a scanned copy of a signed lease to prove the rent amount. Lazarus objects that only the original lease may be admitted, but he raises no genuine question about authenticity and identifies no unfairness from using the scan. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Exclude the scan because the original is always required to prove a lease term.
Answer B: Admit the scan to the same extent as the original absent a genuine authenticity question or unfairness.
Answer C: Admit the scan only if the jury is instructed that it is weaker evidence than the original.
Answer D: Exclude the scan unless Martha proves the original was destroyed without bad faith.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A duplicate is generally admissible to the same extent as the original unless a genuine question is raised about the original's authenticity or the circumstances make it unfair to admit the duplicate.
