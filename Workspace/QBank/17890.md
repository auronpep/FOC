Outline: Duty of reasonable prudent person in prevailing circumstances
Question: After drinking voluntarily, a restaurant patron stumbled into a server carrying hot soup. The patron argues that the jury should compare him only to a reasonably careful intoxicated person. What is the usual standard?
Answer A: A reasonably careful intoxicated person standard
Answer B: The ordinary reasonably prudent person standard
Answer C: No standard, because intoxicated people cannot be negligent
Answer D: Strict liability for all intoxicated conduct
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The patron is judged by ordinary reasonable care despite voluntary intoxication.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Voluntary intoxication generally does not lower the objective standard. C: Voluntary intoxication is not immunity from negligence. D: The claim remains negligence under the objective standard.
lb_rule_reference: Voluntary intoxication generally does not lower the reasonable-person standard.
