Outline: Running with the land
Question: A deed from Isaac to Rebecca stated, “The owner of Lot 4, and all successors, shall pay one-half of the reasonable cost of maintaining the shared private road.” A separate clause granted Lot 4 a right to use the road. The dispute concerns only the payment clause. How should the payment clause be classified for Rights in Land routing?
Answer A: A profit because the payment helps preserve the road surface.
Answer B: A license because Rebecca may use the road only if she pays.
Answer C: An affirmative real covenant because it is a promise to pay land-related maintenance costs.
Answer D: A negative easement because the payment discourages road damage.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A promise by owners and successors to pay reasonable road-maintenance costs is an affirmative covenant. The separate use right may be an easement, but the payment obligation is routed through covenant doctrine.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because no resources are being removed from the land. B: B is wrong because the payment clause is a promise, not mere permission. D: D is wrong because a payment duty is not a restraint on the servient owner's use of land.
lb_rule_reference: Real covenant / affirmative maintenance promise
