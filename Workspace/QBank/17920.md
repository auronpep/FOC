Question: David tells Abigail, “If you refrain from fishing in the north pond for 30 days, I will pay you $300.” Abigail knows of the offer and does not fish in the north pond for 30 days. David refuses to pay, arguing that Abigail never promised him anything. Is David liable?
Answer A: No, because Abigail never gave David a return promise.
Answer B: No, because forbearance from fishing cannot be consideration unless David receives property.
Answer C: Yes, because Abigail accepted the unilateral offer by completing the requested forbearance.
Answer D: Yes, but only because Abigail's silence counted as a promise on the first day.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A unilateral offer may be accepted by completing the requested act or forbearance. Abigail knew of the offer and completed the requested 30-day forbearance, forming the contract.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the offer invited performance, not a promise. B: B is wrong because forbearance can be a bargained-for legal detriment. D: D is wrong because acceptance occurred by completed forbearance, not by implied promise on the first day.
lb_rule_reference: common_law
lb_student_diagnostic: Student demanded a return promise even though the offer requested forbearance performance.
