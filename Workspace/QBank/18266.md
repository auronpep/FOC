Question: Moses signed a deed conveying Desertacre to Aaron and handed it to Moses's lawyer. Moses told the lawyer, “Keep this in my file. Do not give it to Aaron unless I call you later and confirm. Return it to me whenever I ask.” Aaron knew nothing about the deed. Moses died before giving further instructions. What is the best result?
Answer A: Aaron owns Desertacre because a deed handed to any third party is delivered.
Answer B: Aaron owns Desertacre because Moses signed the deed before death.
Answer C: Aaron owns Desertacre only if the lawyer records the deed after Moses dies.
Answer D: Moses's estate owns Desertacre because Moses retained control and gave no present, irrevocable delivery instruction.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source is a deed, and the decisive delivery lock is retained control. Moses gave the deed to his own lawyer for safekeeping, required later confirmation, and kept the power to demand return. That is not present, irrevocable delivery to Aaron or to Aaron's agent.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because third-party possession is not enough when the third party remains under the grantor's control. B: B is wrong because signature alone is not delivery. C: C is wrong because post-death recording cannot cure lack of lifetime delivery.
lb_rule_reference: Delivery to a third party fails when the grantor's agent must wait for later confirmation and return the deed on request
