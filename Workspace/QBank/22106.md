


BARMATRIX Q#: 22106
Outline_code: 82010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Nature and Ownership of Land
Question: A tenant leased an apartment under a oneyear lease, payable month- to- month. The lease stated that it would automatically renew for successive one- year periods if either the tenant or the landlord failed to give notice of termination at least one month before the end of the current lease year. The lease also stated that the parties would keep each other informed regarding the use of the property. Over a year passed, and neither party talked with the other. At this point, what is the most likely state of the lease?
Answer A: A: The tenant has an at- will tenancy that can be terminated at any time.
Answer B: B: The tenant has a periodic month- tomonth tenancy that can be canceled with one month's notice.
Answer C: C: The tenant has a periodic year- to- year tenancy that can only be canceled according to the lease terms.
Answer D: D: The tenant has a term of years tenancy of one year.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: For a periodic tenancy, the tenancy continues from period to period without a set termination date until one of the parties gives proper notice. The lease agreement stated that it would renew each year unless there was proper notice of termination, so it is a periodic tenancy. However, because neither party acted in any way, a court will likely find that the tenant now has a month- to- month periodic tenancy because this is how he pays rent. This gives each side a chance to clarify what they want, doesn't bind the landlord to a one- year lease in what might be a mistake, and does not put the tenant on the street without at least a month to get settled. In the interests of equity, this is the most likely outcome. A is incorrect because an at- will lease can be terminated at any time, and this lease may only be terminated at the end of a period. C is incorrect because an entire year is a long time to hold either the tenant or the landlord on the property without any agreement. Consequently, a court would likely require some type of action on the part of the parties to continue the lease. D is incorrect because a term of years tenancy ends on a set date without notice. Here, the lease requires notice of termination.
