Question: A town ordinance allows political signs up to 30 days before an election, event signs up to 10 days before an event, and ideological signs at any time but only in smaller sizes. The town says the ordinance is meant to reduce clutter and applies evenly to all speakers within each category. What is the best First Amendment classification?
Answer A: Content neutral, because the town's purpose is traffic safety and aesthetics
Answer B: Content based, because officials must classify the sign by its communicative topic to apply the rule
Answer C: Content neutral, because every political sign is treated the same as every other political sign
Answer D: Unregulated conduct, because signs are physical objects
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Political, event, and ideological signs are treated differently based on what they say.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A benign purpose does not make a facial subject-matter distinction content neutral. C: Equal treatment within a content category does not erase the content line between categories. D: Signs communicate messages and are protected speech.
lb_rule_reference: A law that treats speech differently by subject matter, topic, or message is content based on its face and triggers strict scrutiny.
