Outline: Joinder of parties
Question: Three passengers injured in the same bus crash sue the bus company in one federal action. Each passenger alleges negligence arising from the same driver conduct and the same collision, though each suffered different injuries. Is permissive joinder of the plaintiffs proper under Rule 20?
Answer A: No, because each plaintiff suffered a different injury and must file separately
Answer B: Yes, because the claims arise from the same occurrence and share common factual or legal questions
Answer C: Yes, because Rule 20 automatically supplies subject matter jurisdiction over every joined plaintiff
Answer D: No, because permissive joinder is available only to defendants, not plaintiffs
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 20 allows plaintiffs to join when the claims arise from the same transaction or occurrence and share a common question. Same accident liability satisfies both requirements.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The claims arise from the same crash and share common liability questions. C: Rule 20 permits party joinder; it does not itself create jurisdiction. D: Rule 20 expressly covers permissive joinder of plaintiffs and defendants.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 20 plaintiff joinder requires the same transaction, occurrence, or series of transactions or occurrences, plus a common question of law or fact.
