Question: Rachel sends Noah a message: “I will pay you $700 if you either promise by Wednesday to repair my fence or simply begin the repair by Wednesday.” On Tuesday, without sending a promise, Noah arrives and begins repairing the fence. Rachel then tries to revoke. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: No contract was formed because Noah did not use words of promise.
Answer B: A contract was formed when Noah began the repair in a manner invited by the offer.
Answer C: No contract was formed because Rachel's promise to pay for work was a gift promise.
Answer D: A contract could be formed only if Noah signed a written acceptance.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The offer expressly allowed acceptance by beginning performance. Noah accepted by beginning the repair before Rachel revoked, forming a contract on the invited method of acceptance.
