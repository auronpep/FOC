Question: After a consumer class is certified, the named plaintiff and defendant negotiate a settlement that would release the class claims. They ask the clerk to enter a voluntary dismissal immediately, arguing that the named plaintiff is the party who filed the case. What is the proper response?
Answer A: The dismissal may be entered automatically because the named plaintiff controls all class claims
Answer B: The settlement or dismissal requires court approval because absent class members' interests are at stake
Answer C: Court approval is required only if the defendant denies liability in the settlement agreement
Answer D: The class must be decertified automatically before any settlement can be considered
Correct Answer: B
lb_rule_reference: Once class claims are certified, settlement, voluntary dismissal, or compromise requires court approval under Rule 23(e), with procedures protecting absent class members.
