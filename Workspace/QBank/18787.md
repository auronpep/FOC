Outline: Freedom of speech and expression
Question: A city ordinance bars all persons from knowingly standing within eight feet of another person entering a courthouse after that person says they do not want to receive leaflets or counseling. The rule applies to all messages and speakers. Protesters can still speak, hold signs, and leaflet outside the eight-foot distance. What is the best classification?
Answer A: Content based, because many affected speakers protest court policies
Answer B: Content neutral TPM, subject to intermediate scrutiny
Answer C: No First Amendment issue, because leafleting is conduct only
Answer D: Viewpoint discrimination, because it affects protesters more than supporters
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The ordinance regulates approach distance and applies to all messages while preserving alternative channels.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The rule applies regardless of message and turns on location and consent, not subject matter. C: Leafleting and counseling are expressive activity. D: Disparate practical effect is not automatically viewpoint discrimination without a message-based line.
lb_rule_reference: A content-neutral buffer rule in a traditional public forum must be narrowly tailored to a significant interest and leave open ample alternative channels.
