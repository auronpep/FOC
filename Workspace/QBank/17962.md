Question: A retailer posts a notice in its window: “First 5 customers who enter today and present this coupon may buy one tablet for $50. No rain checks.” Rachel is the third customer to enter with the coupon and asks to buy a tablet. The retailer refuses, saying advertisements are never offers. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The notice could not be an offer because advertisements are never offers.
Answer B: Rachel made only a preliminary inquiry because she did not use the word “accept.”
Answer C: The retailer could refuse because a goods offer may be accepted only by shipment.
Answer D: The notice was sufficiently specific and limited to operate as an offer, and Rachel accepted by performing the stated act.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: A public advertisement can be an offer when it is sufficiently definite, limited, and invites a specific act. The notice promised tablets to the first five customers who presented the coupon. Rachel performed the invited act while still within the limited group.
