


BARMATRIX Q#: 22797
Outline_code: 95070400
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Pretrial Procedures
Outline: Discovery
Question: A woman sued a psychiatrist for damages she claimed resulted from the psychiatrist hypnotizing her during a counseling session. The woman's attorney wanted to know whether the psychiatrist had any malpractice insurance that might cover the woman's claim, and whether that insurance might have an exclusion for alternative methods of treatment. What does the woman's attorney need to do to find out whether the psychiatrist has an insurance policy?
Answer A: A: Serve the psychiatrist with an interrogatory asking the relevant question.
Answer B: B: Depose the psychiatrist regarding whether she has an insurance policy.
Answer C: C: File a request for production with the court.
Answer D: D: Nothing.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: “A party must, without awaiting a discovery request, provide to the other parties . . . any insurance agreement under which an insurance business may be liable to satisfy all or part of a possible judgment.” Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(a)(1)(A). Consequently, the attorney does not have to do anything to get the information she wants. Therefore, A, B, and C are incorrect.
