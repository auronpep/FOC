Question: A buyer contracted to receive grain in sealed sacks. After a shortage of sealed sacks, the buyer told the seller, “Use open sacks this month; I will accept them.” The seller bought open sacks and packed the entire month's grain in them. The buyer then sent a notice the night before delivery stating that it would require sealed sacks after all. Which statement is most accurate under Article 2?
Answer A: The buyer may always retract a waiver before delivery occurs.
Answer B: The seller breached because open sacks do not conform to the original writing.
Answer C: The buyer's retraction is ineffective if it would be unjust because the seller materially changed position in reliance on the waiver.
Answer D: The buyer's waiver is ineffective unless supported by new consideration.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Article 2 allows waiver retraction by reasonable notice unless retraction would be unjust because of material reliance. The seller materially relied by buying and using open sacks for the month's grain.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because retraction is limited by reliance. B: B is wrong because the buyer's waiver can alter the consequence for the executory performance. D: D is wrong because waiver is not dependent on new consideration under this route.
lb_rule_reference: UCC §2-209(5) / reliance blocks retraction
