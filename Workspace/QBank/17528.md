Question: A state requires all women, but no men, applying for firefighter jobs to take an additional endurance test. The state argues that average physical differences justify the rule, but it does not explain why the ordinary test is inadequate for each applicant. What is the best Equal Protection analysis?
Answer A: Rational basis applies because physical differences between sexes are real
Answer B: Intermediate scrutiny applies, and the state must show an important objective and substantial relation without relying on overbroad stereotypes
Answer C: Strict scrutiny applies because sex is a suspect classification like race
Answer D: The law is valid automatically because firefighting is dangerous
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The law classifies by sex and must satisfy intermediate scrutiny.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Sex classifications receive intermediate scrutiny. C: Sex classifications generally receive intermediate scrutiny, not strict scrutiny. D: Important objective alone is not enough; fit must be shown.
lb_rule_reference: Sex classifications receive intermediate scrutiny; even when the government invokes real differences, the classification must be substantially related to an important objective and not rest on overbroad stereotypes.
