Question: A buyer sends a purchase order for 500 sacks of seed. A merchant seller sends back an acknowledgment with a different delivery term. Neither party signs the other's form. The seller ships the seed, the buyer accepts it, and the buyer pays part of the price. A dispute later arises over whether any contract was formed. What is the best answer?
Answer A: No contract was formed because the writings were not mirror images.
Answer B: No contract was formed because neither party signed the other party's form.
Answer C: A contract was formed by the parties' conduct recognizing the existence of a contract, even if the exact moment of formation is uncertain.
Answer D: No contract was formed because any different term in an acknowledgment prevents Article 2 formation.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Under Article 2, a contract for sale of goods may be made in any manner sufficient to show agreement, including conduct by both parties recognizing the existence of a contract. Shipment, acceptance, and partial payment can establish formation even if the writings do not match and the exact formation moment is uncertain.
