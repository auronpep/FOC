Outline: Reliance damages
Question: Daniel contracted with Abigail to rent Abigail's hall for a public lecture. Abigail breached. Daniel's expected ticket profits are too speculative, but he proves that before the breach he reasonably spent $7,000 printing programs and advertising the lecture. Daniel also claims $50,000 for embarrassment because the lecture was cancelled. What is Daniel's best reliance recovery?
Answer A: $57,000, because all losses caused by breach are reliance damages.
Answer B: $7,000, for the reasonable preparation expenses.
Answer C: $50,000, because embarrassment is the largest loss.
Answer D: Nothing, because lost profits are speculative.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Reliance damages cover reasonable expenditures made in reliance on the contract. Ordinary contract remedies generally do not include embarrassment or emotional-distress-style loss for a cancelled commercial event. Daniel's proven reliance expenses are $7,000.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because it adds noncompensable embarrassment damages. C: C is wrong because embarrassment is not the reliance measure. D: D is wrong because uncertain profits do not bar proven reliance expenses.
lb_rule_reference: Reliance damages / ordinary contract loss limits
