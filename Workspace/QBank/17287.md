Question: A state denies free public elementary education to children who lack lawful immigration status. The state argues undocumented persons are not a suspect class and education is not a fundamental right. What is the best analysis?
Answer A: Ordinary rational basis automatically applies because undocumented status is never relevant to Equal Protection
Answer B: The law receives special, more searching review because it denies basic public education to undocumented children
Answer C: Strict scrutiny applies because education is always a fundamental right
Answer D: The law is automatically valid because states control education
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The context combines children, immigration status not their fault, and basic education.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The denial of basic education to children receives special scrutiny beyond ordinary rational basis. C: Education is not generally treated as a fundamental right for Equal Protection purposes. D: State control remains subject to Equal Protection.
lb_rule_reference: Undocumented persons are not a suspect class, but laws denying basic public education to undocumented children receive special, more searching review.
