Question: In a federal criminal trial, the prosecutor says that Mary, the alleged victim, has a statutory right to attend the trial even though she may testify later. The defendant, Jacob, invokes the witness-exclusion rule and asks that Mary be removed from the courtroom with the other witnesses. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Exclude Mary because any victim who might testify must be sequestered.
Answer B: Permit Mary to remain if a statute authorizes her presence despite the witness-exclusion request.
Answer C: Exclude Mary unless she agrees to testify first.
Answer D: Permit Mary to remain only if Jacob consents.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 615 does not authorize excluding a person who is authorized by statute to be present. If Mary has such a statutory right, the court should not remove her merely because she may testify.
