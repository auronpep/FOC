Subtopic: Inchoate Crimes
Outline: Attempt
Question: Nora decides to rob a jewelry courier as he leaves a hotel. She buys a mask, loads a replica pistol, waits in the hotel garage at the time the courier is scheduled to leave, and steps behind a pillar where the courier must pass. Police arrest Nora before the courier enters the garage. Nora is charged with attempted robbery. Which statement best describes the attempt charge?
Answer A: Nora is not guilty because she had not yet confronted the courier or demanded property
Answer B: Nora is guilty because she intended robbery and took a substantial step toward it
Answer C: Nora is not guilty because buying a mask is always mere preparation
Answer D: Nora is guilty only if the courier saw her in the garage
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Nora moved beyond planning by appearing at the crime scene at the planned time, masked and armed with the tool she intended to use. Those acts strongly corroborate her intent to rob the courier.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Attempt does not require completion of the last act. Lying in wait at the planned scene with the tools for the crime strongly corroborates criminal purpose. C: The facts are not limited to buying a mask. The total conduct includes waiting at the planned scene at the planned time. D: Attempt liability focuses on Nora's intent and substantial step. Victim awareness is not required for attempted robbery.
lb_rule_reference: A defendant who intends the target crime and takes conduct strongly corroborating that intent, such as lying in wait at the planned crime scene with necessary tools, has taken a substantial step beyond mere preparation.
