Outline_code: 44040100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Constitutional Law
Subtopic: Individual Rights
Outline: State Action Requirement
Question: A student attended a privately owned medical college. One weekend during the school semester, the student was arrested by city police for participating in a demonstration against the government's position on nuclear disarmament. When the student was brought before a judge for arraignment, however, the court dismissed the charge with the consent of the public prosecutor. The following day, the dean of college called the student into her office. The dean referred to the arrest and said that because his conduct had embarrassed the school, the student was expelled. The student subsequently sued the college for damages resulting from his dismissal. The most effective argument in support of the student's claim is that the dismissal violated the student's right
Answer A: A: to an administrative hearing.
Answer B: B: to due process.
Answer C: C: to freedom of expression.
Answer D: D: under an implied contract with the college.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Constitutional guarantees of due process and freedom of expression protect only against government action. Since the college is privately owned, it is not bound to give the student due process or to avoid interfering with his freedom of expression. Common law and contract rights are thus the only ones that he may hope to enforce against the university.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A, B, and C are therefore incorrect.
