Question: A city ordinance makes it a crime to "stand around in a way that annoys police officers." The ordinance gives officers no objective criteria and has been enforced against people based on personal dislike. What is the strongest due process challenge?
Answer A: The ordinance is void for vagueness because it lacks fair notice and invites arbitrary enforcement
Answer B: The ordinance is valid because police need discretion
Answer C: The ordinance is vague only if it regulates speech
Answer D: The ordinance fails only if every application is unclear
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The standard turns on officer annoyance and supplies no objective guide.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Due process forbids standardless discretion that invites arbitrary enforcement. C: Vagueness doctrine applies beyond speech, though speech concerns can heighten review. D: The central defect is lack of fair notice and standardless enforcement discretion.
lb_rule_reference: A law is void for vagueness if it fails to give ordinary people fair notice or invites arbitrary enforcement through standardless discretion.
