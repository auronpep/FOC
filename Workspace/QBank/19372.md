Question: Isaac contracted with Rachel to buy an antique lamp. Both Isaac and Rachel mistakenly believed the lamp still existed in Rachel's storage room. As part of the price, Isaac promised to pay Rachel's friend Naomi $4,000. Before performance, they discovered that the lamp had been destroyed months earlier without either party's knowledge. Isaac rescinded the contract based on the mistake. Naomi sues Isaac for $4,000. What is the likely result?
Answer A: Naomi cannot enforce because Isaac's defense to the underlying contract is available against her beneficiary claim.
Answer B: Naomi can enforce because she was not mistaken about the lamp.
Answer C: Naomi can enforce because beneficiaries are protected from contract-avoidance defenses.
Answer D: Naomi cannot enforce only if Rachel also sues Isaac.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Naomi's beneficiary right depends on the contract that made Isaac's payment promise. If Isaac can avoid or rescind that contract because of a valid mistake defense, Naomi takes subject to that defense and cannot enforce the payment promise.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because Naomi's own knowledge is not the issue; she enforces a contract subject to Isaac's defenses. C: C is wrong because beneficiaries are not insulated from contract invalidity or avoidance defenses. D: D is wrong because Rachel's separate lawsuit is irrelevant to Isaac's defense against Naomi.
