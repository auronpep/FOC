Question: A city requires all food vendors to operate on Saturdays but lets officials grant exemptions for "good cause." Officials routinely grant exemptions for staffing problems, sporting events, and family vacations, but deny a vendor's exemption request for Sabbath observance. What is the best Free Exercise analysis?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny may apply because the city grants individualized secular exemptions while denying a religious exemption
Answer B: Rational basis always applies because the Saturday rule does not mention religion
Answer C: The vendor automatically wins without any scrutiny analysis
Answer D: The city may deny all religious exemptions if it grants at least one secular exemption
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Discretionary exemptions can defeat general applicability when religious reasons are treated less favorably.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: General applicability can fail through exemptions and administration, not only text. C: The next step is strict scrutiny, not automatic victory. D: Selective secular exemptions are what create the Free Exercise problem.
lb_rule_reference: A law with individualized exemptions may trigger strict scrutiny when religious reasons are denied while comparable secular reasons are granted.
