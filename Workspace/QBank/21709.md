Question: A state permits home schooling but requires parents to teach basic math, reading, and civics and to submit annual proof of instruction. Parents object that any home-school regulation violates substantive due process. What is the best due process answer?
Answer A: The parents automatically win because parental liberty bars all education regulation
Answer B: The state automatically wins because parents have no education liberty
Answer C: The regulation is likely permissible because it sets baseline educational requirements without eliminating parental choice
Answer D: Strict scrutiny applies because civics is political speech in every setting
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The state regulates minimum education while still allowing home schooling.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Parental liberty does not block reasonable baseline education requirements. B: Parents do have a protected role; the issue is whether the regulation reasonably preserves that role. D: The fact pattern is framed as parental education liberty and baseline schooling regulation, not viewpoint-based speech compulsion.
lb_rule_reference: Parents have a protected role in directing education, but the state may impose reasonable educational requirements and safety rules that do not eliminate parental choice.
