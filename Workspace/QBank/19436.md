Question: Abraham executed and delivered a valid deed conveying Blackacre “to Sarah.” The deed did not say “and her heirs,” did not limit Sarah's use of the land, and did not give any future interest to Abraham or to anyone else. What estate does Sarah receive?
Answer A: A life estate, because the deed does not say “and her heirs.”
Answer B: A fee simple absolute, because modern law generally does not require words of inheritance.
Answer C: A fee simple subject to condition subsequent, because Abraham did not expressly give up the right to retake the land.
Answer D: A tenancy at will, because the deed does not specify a duration.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Sarah receives a fee simple absolute. Under the modern rule, a deed that conveys land without limiting language generally passes the grantor's full estate, even if the deed does not use the phrase “and her heirs.”
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because it applies the old common-law words-of-inheritance requirement. C: C is wrong because no conditional language or right of entry appears in the deed. D: D is wrong because a deed conveying ownership is not a lease or revocable occupancy arrangement.
lb_rule_reference: Fee simple absolute / modern conveyance default
