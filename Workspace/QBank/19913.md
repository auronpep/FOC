Question: Deborah posts a notice: “$1,000 to the first person who returns my missing painting.” Hannah sees the notice and returns the painting on Monday morning. Elijah, who also saw the notice, arrives Monday afternoon with the same painting's empty frame and demands the reward because he spent all night searching. Who, if anyone, accepted Deborah's offer?
Answer A: Hannah accepted because she was the first person to complete the requested performance.
Answer B: Both Hannah and Elijah accepted because both relied on the reward notice.
Answer C: Elijah accepted because he exerted more effort searching.
Answer D: Neither accepted because public reward offers are not offers.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The reward offer was limited to the first person who returned the painting. Hannah completed the requested performance first. Elijah's later effort did not accept the limited offer.
