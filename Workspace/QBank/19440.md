Question: A contractor sends Miriam a signed document titled “Proposal.” It states, “I will build the storage room described in your plans for $18,000, beginning June 1. Sign below if you accept.” Miriam signs and returns it the next day. The contractor refuses to perform, arguing that a document called a proposal cannot be an offer. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The document was an offer because it manifested present commitment and invited Miriam's signature as acceptance.
Answer B: No contract formed because a proposal label always means preliminary negotiation.
Answer C: No contract formed because every construction contract requires a separate later formal agreement.
Answer D: No contract formed because the price was stated as a quote.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Labels are not dispositive. The document said “I will build,” included the work, price, start date, and invited Miriam's signature. Objectively, it manifested present commitment, and Miriam accepted as invited.
