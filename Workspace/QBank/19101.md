Question: Martha agreed in writing to buy 1,000 custom chairs from Nathan for use in a theater. Nathan would manufacture the chairs after the order. The writing was silent about whether the chairs had to include armrests. Martha offers evidence of trade usage in theater seating. Which source-law route should the court use?
Answer A: Common law because the chairs were not existing when the contract was signed.
Answer B: UCC Article 2 because specially manufactured movable goods are goods.
Answer C: Real-property law because the chairs will be placed in a theater.
Answer D: No contract law applies until Nathan finishes manufacturing all chairs.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Specially manufactured movable items can be goods under Article 2. The chairs are movable goods even though they will be made after the contract. Article 2 supplies the source-law route for trade usage and content/meaning.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because goods can be specially manufactured or future goods. C: C is wrong because intended use in a building does not make the chairs real property. D: D is wrong because a contract to sell future or specially manufactured goods can be enforceable before completion.
lb_student_diagnostic: Specially manufactured goods/source-law error.
