Question: Joshua orally agreed to buy 600 tools from Caleb for $5 each. Joshua paid Caleb for 200 tools, and Caleb accepted the payment. No writing was signed. Before delivery, Joshua refused to buy the remaining 400 tools. For what quantity is the oral contract enforceable under UCC §2-201?
Answer A: For none of the tools, because there is no signed writing.
Answer B: For all 600 tools, because part payment validates the entire oral contract.
Answer C: For a reasonable quantity chosen by the court.
Answer D: For the 200 tools for which payment was made and accepted.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Under UCC §2-201, an oral goods contract that lacks a sufficient writing is enforceable with respect to goods for which payment has been made and accepted. The exception is limited to that quantity, here 200 tools.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because payment made and accepted is a UCC exception. B: B is wrong because part payment does not validate the entire contract. C: C is wrong because the court does not supply a reasonable quantity for the SOF exception.
lb_rule_reference: UCC §2-201 / payment made and accepted
