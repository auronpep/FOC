Question: In a federal civil trial, the judge uses a special verdict form. The jury answers written questions about what happened during contract negotiations but does not state which party wins. After receiving the answers, the judge applies the contract law instructions to the findings. What is the proper division of labor?
Answer A: The jury must be sent back because a special verdict is invalid unless it names the winner
Answer B: The judge must disregard the written answers and conduct a new bench trial
Answer C: The jury finds the facts in writing, and the court applies the law to enter judgment
Answer D: The parties, not the court, apply the law after the jury answers the questions
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: That is the ordinary function of a special verdict.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A special verdict can ask for factual findings without a general winner. B: The jury's factual findings are the point of the special verdict form. D: The court applies the governing law and enters the judgment.
lb_rule_reference: With a special verdict, the jury makes specific written findings on factual issues, and the court applies the law to those findings and enters judgment.
