Question: In a federal civil case, the judge instructs the jury, “You may consider both direct and circumstantial evidence. Neither type is automatically entitled to greater weight.” Caleb objects, arguing that direct evidence must be given greater weight. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because direct evidence always receives greater legal weight.
Answer B: Sustain the objection because circumstantial evidence may be used only in criminal cases.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because the jury may weigh both types of evidence without an automatic hierarchy.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if there is no direct evidence in the case.
Correct Answer: C
