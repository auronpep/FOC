


BARMATRIX Q#: 22765
subject_display: Civil Procedure
topic: Jurisdiction and Venue
subtopic: Federal Subject-Matter Jurisdiction
difficulty: 94010000
Stem / Fact Pattern: A man from the Northern District of State A got in an auto accident in the Eastern District of State B with a truck driver from the Western District of State C, a security guard from the Western District of State C, and a doctor from the Southern District of State D. The man sued the truck driver, the security guard, and the doctor in federal court based on diversity jurisdiction. In which judicial districts would venue be proper for the man’s action?
Answer A: A: The Eastern District of State B.
Answer B: B: The Northern District of State A, the Western District of State C, and the Southern District of State D.
Answer C: C: The Western District of State C and the Southern District of State D.
Answer D: D: The Western District of State C.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Under 28 U.S.C. §1391, venue is proper in (a) the judicial district where any defendant resides, so long as all defendants are residents of the state in which the district is located; (b) a judicial district where a substantial part of the events giving rise to the claim occurred; or, (c) if no district in the United States satisfies either (a) or (b), a judicial district where any defendant is subject to the court’s personal jurisdiction in regard to such action. Here, since not all the defendants are citizens of the same state, the only appropriate venue would be the Eastern District of State B, where the accident occurred. Therefore, B, C, and D are incorrect.
