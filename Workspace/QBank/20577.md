Outline: Motion for judgment on the pleadings
Question: John files a complaint. The defendant serves an answer on June 1. On June 15, John serves his first amended complaint as a matter of course. The defendant objects that once an answer is served, amendment always requires leave. Is the objection correct?
Answer A: Yes, answers cut off all amendment-as-of-course rights immediately
Answer B: No, the first amendment within the Rule 15(a)(1) window may be made as of course
Answer C: Yes, because amendments are never allowed in federal court
Answer D: No, because a party may amend as of course an unlimited number of times
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The June 15 amendment is within 21 days after service of the answer.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Rule 15 provides a limited post-answer window for one amendment as of course. C: Rule 15 governs amendments. D: The as-of-course right is limited.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 15(a)(1) creates limited amendment-as-of-course rights before leave is required.
