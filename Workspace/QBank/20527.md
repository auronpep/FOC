Question: Noah signed a deed giving Ruth “the right to enter the wooded tract each fall for five years and cut and remove up to 40 cedar trees per year.” The deed does not give Ruth any other use of the tract. How is Ruth's right best classified?
Answer A: A profit because Ruth may enter the land and remove timber from it.
Answer B: An affirmative easement only, because any right to enter land is an easement rather than a profit.
Answer C: A revocable license because Ruth's use is limited to five years.
Answer D: A real covenant because the deed limits the number of trees Ruth may cut.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A profit is the right to enter another's land and remove natural resources or part of the land's produce, such as timber, minerals, or gravel. Ruth has a right to enter and remove cedar trees, so the best classification is a profit.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because the resource-removal feature makes the right a profit rather than merely an easement. C: C is wrong because a limited duration does not make a deeded resource-removal right revocable permission. D: D is wrong because the limit on quantity defines the profit's scope; it does not convert the right into a covenant.
lb_rule_reference: Profit / right to remove natural resources
