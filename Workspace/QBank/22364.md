Outline_code: 75070000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Constitutional Protection of Accused Persons (Criminal Procedure)
Outline: Fourth Amendment
Question: The police received an anonymous tip that a young male wearing a plaid shirt and standing at a bus stop was carrying a gun. Two officers responded to the scene and found three males, one of whom was 15 years old and wearing a plaid shirt. After frisking him, the officers found a gun. At his trial, the boy asked the court to suppress the gun as evidence. Should his motion be granted?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the tip was anonymous.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the tip only stated that the boy was wearing a plaid shirt and standing at the bus stop.
Answer C: C: No, because the frisk was valid under the officer safety exception.
Answer D: D: No, because the boy was carrying a gun at the bus stop as stated by the anonymous tip.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Reasonable suspicion for a search can be based on an anonymous tip, but that tip must have indicia of reliability that shows that the tipster has inside information, such as predictive information. Florida v. J.L., 529 U.S. 266 (2000). Here, the tip only stated that a boy in a plaid shirt was standing at a bus stop. Consequently, there was no way to test the tip's validity, and the stop and search was unlawful. A is incorrect because a search can be based on an anonymous tip if the tip has sufficient indicia of reliability. C is incorrect because the officer safety exception would not make the search lawful. D is incorrect because the fact that the boy actually had a gun would not make the search lawful.
