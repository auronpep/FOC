Outline: Judicial notice
Question: In a federal civil trial, Abigail objects that a shipment could not have crossed the state line in one hour. Before notifying the parties, the judge consults an official state highway map and takes judicial notice of the distance between the two towns. When court reconvenes, Jacob promptly asks to be heard on whether the map supports the noticed fact. What should the court do?
Answer A: Allow Jacob to be heard on the propriety of taking judicial notice and the nature of the fact noticed.
Answer B: Refuse to hear Jacob because judicial notice, once taken, cannot be challenged.
Answer C: Automatically withdraw the noticed fact because the judge acted before notifying the parties.
Answer D: Submit the question of judicial notice to the jury for a binding decision.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A party is entitled, on timely request, to be heard on the propriety of taking judicial notice and the nature of the noticed fact. That right exists even when the court takes notice before notifying the parties.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because Rule 201 gives a timely opportunity to be heard. C: C is wrong because taking notice before notice to the parties does not automatically invalidate it; the party must be heard on request. D: D is wrong because the judge decides whether judicial notice is proper.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 201(e) / opportunity to be heard
