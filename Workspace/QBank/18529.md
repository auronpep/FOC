Outline: Freedom of speech and expression
Question: A city allows marches in downtown streets but requires all weekday marches to use a route that avoids the hospital emergency entrance between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m. The route rule applies to all marches regardless of message. Protesters may use the main avenue on weekends or after 7 p.m. What standard most likely applies?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny, because streets are traditional public forums
Answer B: Intermediate time, place, and manner review, because the rule is content neutral and leaves alternative routes and times
Answer C: Rational basis, because traffic control is a police-power issue
Answer D: No First Amendment review, because marches include conduct
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The rule targets traffic and emergency access, not message, and preserves alternative channels.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Traditional public forum status does not make every regulation content based. C: Speech in a traditional public forum receives First Amendment scrutiny. D: Marches are expressive activity even though they include movement.
lb_rule_reference: In a traditional public forum, a content-neutral time, place, and manner restriction must be narrowly tailored to serve a significant government interest and leave open ample alternative channels.
