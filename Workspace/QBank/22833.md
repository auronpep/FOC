


BARMATRIX Q#: 22833
Outline_code: 94010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Federal Subject-Matter Jurisdiction
Question: A builder, a citizen of State A, filed a lawsuit in State A federal court against an independent contractor, a citizen of State B. The builder claimed breach of a contract worth over $500,000 in damages. The contract had a forum selection clause stating that State C was the sole venue for litigating contract disputes. Under State B law, such clauses were void. However, under State A, State C, and federal law, such clauses were enforceable. The majority of likely witnesses lived and worked in State B. The contractor would like to have the claim transferred from the State A federal court. Which of the following is the most appropriate venue for transfer?
Answer A: A: State B, because forum selection clauses are void under State B law.
Answer B: B: State B, for the convenience of the witnesses and parties.
Answer C: C: State C, because forum selection clauses are valid under State C law.
Answer D: D: State C, because forum selection clauses were valid under State A law.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: 28 U.S.C. §1404 governs a court's ability to transfer an action for the convenience of the parties and witnesses. Because the majority of witnesses lived and worked in State B, and at least one of the parties is from State B, it seems like it is the most convenient forum for the claim. A, C, and D are incorrect because federal law governs transfers of venue.
