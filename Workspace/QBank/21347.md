Question: After a nonjury trial, the district judge enters judgment for defendant with a one-line order: "Judgment for defendant." The order contains no findings of fact and no conclusions of law. Plaintiff objects under Rule 52. What is plaintiff's best argument?
Answer A: Rule 52 required findings of fact and conclusions of law after the bench trial
Answer B: Rule 52 applies only to jury trials
Answer C: The judge never needs to explain a civil judgment
Answer D: Findings are required only if both parties request them before trial
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: In a nonjury trial, the judge must state factual findings and legal conclusions sufficient for review.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Rule 52 is specifically important in bench trials. C: Rule 52 requires explanation in actions tried without a jury. D: The rule does not depend on both parties requesting findings.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 52 requires findings of fact and conclusions of law in actions tried without a jury.
