Question: In a federal products-liability trial, Caleb offers the ladder involved in his fall. Before trial, Caleb replaced two broken rungs with new ones. Caleb wants the ladder admitted as the ladder's exact condition at the time of the accident. The manufacturer objects. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Admit the ladder as real evidence of its accident condition because it is the same physical ladder.
Answer B: Exclude or limit the ladder as proof of its accident condition unless the alteration is explained; it may be usable for a limited illustrative purpose if not misleading.
Answer C: Admit the ladder as conclusive proof that the rungs were safe.
Answer D: Exclude the ladder because real evidence is never admissible in products cases.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: An object may be relevant real evidence, but if its condition has materially changed, the court should not allow it to mislead the jury as the object's accident condition. It may be limited or used illustratively if properly explained.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A ignores the alteration. C gives the exhibit conclusive effect. D is categorically false. C: A ignores the alteration. C gives the exhibit conclusive effect.
