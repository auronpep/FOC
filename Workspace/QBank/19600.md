Question: Isaac owned an old barn. He agreed to sell the barn's lumber to Caleb for $4,000. The agreement required Caleb, not Isaac, to dismantle the barn and remove the lumber. Before Caleb began removing anything, a dispute arose over whether Article 2 controlled the agreement. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Common law likely controls because materials from a structure are treated as goods under Article 2 only when the seller is to sever them.
Answer B: Article 2 controls because all materials attached to land become goods immediately when a price is stated.
Answer C: Article 2 controls because any buyer can convert realty into goods by promising to remove it.
Answer D: No contract can form until the lumber is physically separated from the barn.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: For a structure or its materials to be removed from realty, Article 2 treats the contract as a goods contract if the seller is to sever the materials. Here, Caleb as buyer must dismantle and remove the lumber, so the Article 2 seller-severance route does not apply.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because a stated price does not automatically make attached structures Article 2 goods. C: C is wrong because buyer severance is not enough for this structure-materials rule. D: D is wrong because a common-law contract may form before removal.
lb_rule_reference: common_law
lb_student_diagnostic: Student overextends Article 2's goods-to-be-severed rule to buyer-severed structure materials.
