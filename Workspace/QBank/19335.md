Question: In a federal theft trial, before any witness has testified that Moses took any money, the prosecutor asks Aaron, “After Moses hid the stolen money in the shed, what did he say to you?” Moses objects to the question. What is the best ground for the objection?
Answer A: The question is leading because it suggests the answer.
Answer B: The question assumes a fact not yet in evidence.
Answer C: The question is improper because no party may ask about a shed.
Answer D: The question is improper because all questions about money must be supported by a document.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The question assumes as true that Moses hid stolen money in the shed, even though that fact has not been established. That is the precise objection.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is less precise; the central defect is the unsupported factual premise. C invents a nonexistent rule.
