Question: Complete diversity exists. Plaintiff sues in federal court and demands $100,000 under a state consumer statute. The statute expressly caps total recovery, including fees and penalties, at $50,000 for this claim. Defendant moves to dismiss for lack of amount in controversy. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Deny dismissal because the complaint demands $100,000
Answer B: Deny dismissal because complete diversity is enough by itself
Answer C: Grant dismissal because the statutory cap makes it legally certain that recovery cannot exceed $75,000
Answer D: Grant dismissal only if both parties are citizens of the same state
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The cap fixes maximum recovery below the jurisdictional threshold.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A demand cannot overcome a legal cap that makes higher recovery impossible. B: Diversity jurisdiction requires complete diversity and amount in controversy. D: The problem states complete diversity; the defect is amount.
lb_rule_reference: If governing law caps recovery below $75,000, the amount-in-controversy requirement is not satisfied even if the complaint demands more.
