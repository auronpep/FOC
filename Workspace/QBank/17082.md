Subtopic: Negligence
Outline: Duty of reasonable prudent person in prevailing circumstances
Question: A driver was traveling at the speed limit when a large metal object suddenly fell from a truck ahead. With no time for perfect judgment, the driver swerved and clipped a parked car. The driver had not contributed to the emergency. How should the emergency affect the negligence standard?
Answer A: It creates automatic immunity from negligence
Answer B: It is ignored because negligence never considers context
Answer C: It is considered in deciding what a reasonably prudent person would do in that emergency
Answer D: It lowers the standard only if the driver is a beginner
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The jury measures the driver's conduct against reasonable care under emergency circumstances she did not create.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Emergency is considered in applying reasonable care, but it is not automatic immunity. B: Objective care is applied under the circumstances, including a sudden emergency not of the defendant's making. D: Emergency is a circumstance; beginner status generally does not lower the adult standard.
lb_rule_reference: A sudden emergency not caused by the defendant is considered in deciding what a reasonably prudent person would do.
