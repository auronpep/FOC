Question: In a federal civil trial, Esther calls her own friendly witness, Mark, on direct examination. Esther's lawyer asks, “You saw Solomon run the red light before the collision, didn't you?” Solomon objects. What is the best objection?
Answer A: Leading.
Answer B: Hearsay.
Answer C: Lack of personal knowledge.
Answer D: Nonresponsive.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The question suggests the desired answer and is asked on direct examination of a friendly witness. The best objection is leading.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because the question asks what Mark saw, not an out-of-court statement. C is not the best objection because the question itself asserts Mark saw the event; lack of foundation might arise if no basis is shown, but the immediate defect is leading.
