Question: In a federal securities trial, Isaac offers a spreadsheet summarizing 200 anonymous online comments to prove that the comments were true. Isaac has not shown that the comments are admissible. The opposing party objects to the spreadsheet summary. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Admit the spreadsheet because summaries are always admissible if they save time.
Answer B: Admit the spreadsheet only as a substitute for testimony by the anonymous commenters.
Answer C: Exclude the spreadsheet because a Rule 1006 summary cannot make inadmissible underlying material admissible for truth.
Answer D: Exclude the spreadsheet only if the spreadsheet was prepared after litigation began.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A summary is a proof vehicle for admissible voluminous materials; it does not cure inadmissible underlying hearsay or other defects.
