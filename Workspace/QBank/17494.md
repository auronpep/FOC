Question: Lydia asks a painter, “What is your lowest price to paint my store?” The painter replies, “I estimate $6,000, depending on paint costs and wall condition.” Lydia says, “I accept your offer.” The painter refuses to do the work. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The painter made an offer because Lydia asked for a lowest price.
Answer B: The painter did not make an offer because the reply was an estimate subject to further variables, not a present commitment.
Answer C: Lydia's statement created a contract because acceptance always turns an estimate into an offer.
Answer D: The UCC makes the estimate enforceable because the price term can be open.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The painter's response was a preliminary estimate, not a present commitment. It was expressly dependent on future cost and condition variables. Lydia could not create a contract by accepting a non-offer.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A: WM-FM-010 price quote categorical rule; C: WM-FM-011 vague acceptance inflation; D: WM-FM-021 UCC/common-law modification swap C: A: WM-FM-010 price quote categorical rule; C: WM-FM-011 vague acceptance inflation; D: WM-FM-021 UCC/common-law modification swap D: A: WM-FM-010 price quote categorical rule; C: WM-FM-011 vague acceptance inflation; D: WM-FM-021 UCC/common-law modification swap
