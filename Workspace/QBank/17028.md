Outline: Right to Counsel
Question: After indictment, the prosecutor sends defense counsel a formal plea offer that would cap Nina's sentence at two years. Counsel forgets to tell Nina about it. The offer expires, Nina is convicted at trial, and she receives eight years. Nina proves she would have accepted the offer. What is Nina's strongest constitutional claim?
Answer A: She may have an ineffective-assistance claim because counsel failed to communicate a formal plea offer during plea bargaining
Answer B: She has no claim because effective assistance applies only during trial testimony
Answer C: She has no claim because she received a trial after the offer expired
Answer D: She wins automatically without proving she would have accepted the offer
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Plea bargaining is a critical part of the prosecution. Failure to communicate a favorable formal offer can be deficient and prejudicial if the defendant would have accepted it.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Effective assistance also applies during plea negotiations. C: A fair trial does not automatically cure lost plea-bargaining prejudice. D: Ineffective assistance still requires prejudice, including proof about the lost plea outcome.
lb_rule_reference: The Sixth Amendment right to effective assistance applies during plea bargaining, including counsel's duty to communicate formal plea offers and provide competent advice.
