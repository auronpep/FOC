Question: Abigail agreed to buy candle jars from Esther under a signed goods contract requiring a plain white label. The contract required signed written modifications. After signing, Abigail orally asked Esther to use a red holiday label instead. Esther produced jars with the red label, and Abigail later tried to reject them for failing to match the original label specification. Which additional fact would most strengthen Esther's argument that Abigail cannot retract the holiday-label change?
Answer A: Before signing, Abigail's advertising mockups had once shown red labels.
Answer B: A few other candle sellers in the market used red labels during the winter.
Answer C: After signing, Abigail approved final red-label proofs, Esther printed nonreturnable custom labels, and Abigail accepted and resold the first red-label shipment.
Answer D: The red labels made the jars easier for Abigail to resell at a higher price.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The narrow rule is that a failed oral modification can operate as a waiver, and waiver of an executory term may be unretractable when retraction would be unjust because of material reliance. The trigger facts in C are post-signing approval, nonreturnable custom labels, and acceptance/resale of the first changed shipment. The decisive lock is material reliance plus course performance.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because pre-signing mockups risk a parol-evidence route and do not prove reliance on a later change. B: B is wrong because general market practice is weaker than the parties' own approval and performance. D is tempting because it shows benefit, but benefit alone does not prove that Esther materially changed position in reliance on Abigail's waiver. D: A is wrong because pre-signing mockups risk a parol-evidence route and do not prove reliance on a later change. B is wrong because general market practice is weaker than the parties' own approval and performance. D is tempting because it shows benefit, but benefit alone does not prove that Esther materially changed position in reliance on Abigail's waiver.
