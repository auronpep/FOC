Outline: Strict scrutiny (suspect) classifications
Question: A city requires firefighter applicants to pass a physical agility test. The test is facially neutral, and the record shows the city adopted it after safety studies. Statistics later show that applicants from one racial group pass at a lower rate. No evidence shows racial hostility, procedural irregularity, or discriminatory purpose. What is the Equal Protection consequence of the disparate pass rate?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny applies because disparate impact alone proves racial classification
Answer B: The burden automatically shifts to the city to prove a compelling interest
Answer C: Disparate impact alone does not trigger heightened Equal Protection scrutiny
Answer D: The test is immune from all constitutional review because employment is not a right
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A neutral test with disparate impact remains outside strict scrutiny unless the challenger proves discriminatory purpose.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Equal Protection requires discriminatory purpose for heightened scrutiny of a facially neutral rule. B: Without discriminatory purpose, strict scrutiny and its burden framework do not automatically apply. D: Government employment rules can still be challenged; the issue is the applicable scrutiny.
lb_rule_reference: A facially neutral law with racially disparate effects does not receive strict scrutiny unless discriminatory purpose is shown.
