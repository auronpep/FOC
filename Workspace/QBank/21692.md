Question: Moses says to Abigail, “I am thinking about selling my wagon. I might take $4,000 if I decide to sell.” Abigail immediately replies, “I accept. I will buy it for $4,000.” Moses refuses to sell. Was Moses's statement an offer?
Answer A: Yes, because any statement containing a price is an offer.
Answer B: Yes, because $4,000 is adequate consideration for a wagon.
Answer C: No, because Moses expressed possible future interest rather than present commitment to sell.
Answer D: No, but only because Abigail did not mail her acceptance.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: An offer requires objective manifestation of present willingness to enter a bargain so that assent will conclude it. Moses's words—“thinking about” and “might take”—show tentative negotiation, not present commitment.
