Question: In a federal harassment trial, Deborah offers a screenshot of a message she received from Elijah. Deborah testifies that she took the screenshot from her phone, that it accurately shows the message, and that the phone was later lost. Elijah objects that Deborah must produce the original phone. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because the physical phone is always the original writing.
Answer B: Sustain the objection because screenshots are never admissible.
Answer C: Overrule the objection if Deborah's testimony sufficiently authenticates the screenshot and no genuine dispute makes its use unfair.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if Elijah admits sending the message.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A screenshot can be authenticated by testimony and circumstances. The physical phone is not automatically required if the screenshot or duplicate is adequately authenticated and no unfairness/genuine authenticity problem controls.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is a best-evidence overstatement. B is false. D overdemands opponent admission. B: A is a best-evidence overstatement. B is false. D overdemands opponent admission. D: A is a best-evidence overstatement. B is false. D overdemands opponent admission.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 901(a); FRE 1002 / screenshot and original phone
lb_student_diagnostic: Tests electronic documentary proof and original-phone false positive.
