Outline_code: 75070000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Constitutional Protection of Accused Persons (Criminal Procedure)
Outline: Fourth Amendment
Question: The police believed that a local bicycle shop had a stolen police bicycle in its possession. A magistrate issued a search warrant for a search of the shop's premises to find the police bicycle. When the police conducted the search, they immediately found the police bicycle in the shop's showroom. The police arrested the shop owner. One of the officers went out to a storage shed in the back of the property and opened the door. Inside, the officer found a stack of bicycles marked as belonging to a city bicycle rental program. The officer called in his discovery and found that the bicycles had been reported stolen a month before, At his trial for receiving stolen property, the shop owner claimed he had no idea his shop contained stolen property. He moved to prevent introduction of the city bicycles into evidence. Should the court grant the owner's motion?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the city bicycles were discovered after the police had already found the police bicycle.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the city bicycles were in a separate storage shed.
Answer C: C: No, because the city bicycles were in a shed on the shop's property.
Answer D: D: No, because the city bicycles were relevant to show that the owner knew his shop contained stolen property.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Once a warrant's purpose has been completed, the police no longer have the authority to continue the search. Here, the officers had already found the subject of the warrant, the police bicycle, so they no longer had the authority to continue the search. B is incorrect because the police had a search warrant for the shop's premises. A search of a geographical location's “premises” also allows a search of buildings on those premises. Here, the shed was on the back of the property, so it would likely be included within the search warrant. This makes A a better answer. C and D are incorrect because the authority of the search was over once the officers discovered the police bicycle.
