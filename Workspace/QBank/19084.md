Question: A state criminal defendant is convicted of a serious offense by a nonunanimous jury verdict. He argues that the unanimity requirement applies to the state through the Fourteenth Amendment. What is the best incorporation answer?
Answer A: The right can apply to the state through Fourteenth Amendment Due Process
Answer B: The Bill of Rights directly binds states without any Fourteenth Amendment route
Answer C: No criminal procedure rights are incorporated against states
Answer D: The claim belongs only under Equal Protection because juries vote
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The unanimity requirement for serious criminal jury verdicts is incorporated against the states.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: State application generally runs through Fourteenth Amendment incorporation. C: Most core criminal procedure protections are incorporated, including jury unanimity for serious offenses. D: Jury unanimity is a criminal procedure incorporation issue.
lb_rule_reference: Most fundamental criminal procedure protections apply to the states through Fourteenth Amendment Due Process, including the Sixth Amendment unanimous jury requirement in serious criminal cases.
