Outline: Freedom of religion
Question: Two factions of a church dispute who is the rightful senior pastor. Resolving the dispute would require a civil court to decide which faction correctly interpreted church doctrine and ordination rules. What should the civil court do?
Answer A: Decide which faction has the better theological interpretation because courts decide all leadership disputes
Answer B: Avoid resolving the dispute if doing so requires deciding religious doctrine or internal church governance
Answer C: Always decide for the faction with more members
Answer D: Decide the doctrine only if both factions request a ruling
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The religion clauses protect church autonomy in doctrinal and ecclesiastical matters.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Civil courts may not decide religious doctrine or internal ecclesiastical questions. C: Church governance may be hierarchical, congregational, or doctrinal; civil courts cannot choose by religious merits. D: Consent does not authorize a civil court to resolve religious truth.
lb_rule_reference: Civil courts generally may not resolve disputes that require deciding questions of religious doctrine, discipline, or internal church governance.
