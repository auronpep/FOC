Question: In a federal fraud trial, Eve stipulates that a letter offered by Noah is authentic, meaning that she wrote it. Noah then argues that Eve also stipulated that every statement in the letter is true. Eve objects. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Overrule the objection because an authenticity stipulation admits truth of all contents.
Answer B: Sustain the objection because the stipulation established authorship, not the truth or legal effect of every statement in the letter.
Answer C: Sustain the objection because stipulations are never binding.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if the letter is a duplicate.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A stipulation must be read according to its scope. Stipulating that a document is authentic does not necessarily stipulate that every assertion in it is true.
