Question: Priscilla paid Apollos for a one-year license to use Apollos's patented irrigation method. Apollos also gave Priscilla a printed manual explaining the method. The written license is silent about whether Priscilla may sublicense the method to others. Priscilla argues that Article 2 governs because the manual is movable. What source-law answer is best?
Answer A: Article 2 governs because a printed manual changed hands.
Answer B: Common law or intellectual-property contract principles govern because the transaction is primarily a license of rights, not a sale of goods.
Answer C: Article 2 governs because all written contracts involving movable paper are goods contracts.
Answer D: No contract law applies because patent licenses cannot have content terms.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The transaction's primary object is a license to use intellectual-property rights. The printed manual is incidental. Article 2's sale-of-goods rules should not be imported merely because a movable manual was delivered.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A and C are wrong because the manual is not the transaction's true object.
