Question: David contracted with Solomon to build a small storage building on David's land. Solomon would supply lumber, nails, and doors as part of the work. The final writing was silent about interior shelving. David offers evidence that the parties orally agreed before signing that shelving was included. Which source-law classification should come first?
Answer A: UCC Article 2 because lumber, nails, and doors are goods.
Answer B: Common law construction/services principles because the predominant purpose is construction of a building on land.
Answer C: Article 2 because every building contract contains movable materials.
Answer D: Real-property title law alone because no contract principles apply to construction.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The predominant purpose is construction services and improvement of land, not a sale of the component materials. The oral shelving term should be analyzed under common-law content and meaning rules, not Article 2 by default.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A and C are wrong because materials incorporated into a construction project do not automatically make the contract a sale of goods. D is wrong because contract principles still govern construction agreements.
