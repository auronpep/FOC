Question: In a federal civil trial, Daniel calls Amos to testify about a contract signing. Daniel's lawyer asks another witness, “Because Amos is a respected elder in his church, is he more likely to be telling the truth?” The opposing party objects. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Overrule the objection because religious status always proves truthfulness.
Answer B: Overrule the objection because any community status may be used to support credibility.
Answer C: Sustain the objection because religious belief or status may not be used to support a witness's credibility.
Answer D: Sustain the objection only if Amos has already been impeached.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Evidence of a witness's religious beliefs or opinions is not admissible to attack or support the witness's credibility. The question uses Amos's religious status to support truthfulness.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because religious status cannot be used to prove credibility. B is too broad; community status tied to religious belief is barred for this credibility purpose. B: A is wrong because religious status cannot be used to prove credibility. B is too broad; community status tied to religious belief is barred for this credibility purpose. D is wrong because impeachment status does not make religious belief admissible to support credibility. D: D is wrong because impeachment status does not make religious belief admissible to support credibility.
