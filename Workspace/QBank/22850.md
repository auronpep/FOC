


BARMATRIX Q#: 22850
Outline_code: 94020000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Personal Jurisdiction
Question: A man, a resident of State A, saw an online advertisement for a rifle in State B. The man went to State B, signed a sales contract with the seller, and bought the rifle. When hunting season arrived, the man took the rifle to go hunting in a forest in State A. The man saw what he thought was a bear and accidentally shot a woman from State B who had decided to spend the day hiking in State A. The woman sued the man for negligence in State B. Does the State B court have personal jurisdiction over the man in this case?
Answer A: A: No, because the woman was a citizen of State B.
Answer B: B: No, because the woman's claim did not arise from the man's contacts with State B.
Answer C: C: Yes, because the man purchased the rifle in State B.
Answer D: D: Yes, because the rifle seller was a citizen of State B.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: There are two types of personal jurisdiction. “Specific” jurisdiction arises when there is a close connection between the plaintiff's cause of action and the defendant's contacts with the jurisdiction. “General” jurisdiction arises when the defendant is “essentially at home” in the jurisdiction through repeated contacts with that jurisdiction. International Shoe Co. v. Washington, 326 U.S. 310 (1945). Here, the State B court would not have general personal jurisdiction over the man because his only contact with State B was purchasing the rifle, which would not be enough contacts to make him “essentially at home” in State B. Additionally, there would be no specific personal jurisdiction because the woman's negligence claim is not closely connected to the rifle purchase. A is incorrect because personal jurisdiction over the man would not be dependent upon the citizenship of the woman. C is incorrect because the purchase of the rifle was not a sufficient contact nor was it closely connected to the woman's negligence claim. D is incorrect because personal jurisdiction over the man would not be dependent on the citizenship of the rifle seller.
