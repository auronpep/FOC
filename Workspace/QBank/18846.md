Question: In a federal property trial, Esther asks to call her surveyor, Samuel, before her first fact witness because Samuel must leave that afternoon for surgery. The defendant objects that experts must always testify after all fact witnesses. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because expert witnesses must always testify last.
Answer B: Overrule the objection if the court reasonably permits the witness to be called out of the usual order to make the presentation effective and avoid wasting time.
Answer C: Sustain the objection unless the jury first decides that Samuel's testimony is necessary.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if the defendant waives cross-examination.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The court may control the mode and order of presenting witnesses and evidence. Allowing a witness to testify out of the usual sequence for scheduling and efficiency reasons can be within that discretion.
