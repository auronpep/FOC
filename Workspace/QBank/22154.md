


BARMATRIX Q#: 22154
Outline_code: 31010103
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Evidence
Subtopic: Presentation of Evidence
Outline: Presumptions and inferences
Question: A farmer had a life insurance policy that would not pay benefits if the farmer died by suicide. One day, the farmer's wife went out to the fields and found the farmer dead from a gunshot wound with a shotgun lying near his body. The farm was miles away from the nearest neighbor, and no one else was anywhere near the farmer when his wife found him. The county sheriff determined that it was unlikely the gun discharged from the farmer dropping it, although there was evidence that it had accidentally discharged in the past. The county coroner listed the cause of death as “suicide.” The farmer's wife made a claim for benefits, and the life insurance company filed an action in federal court, claiming that she was not entitled to benefits because the farmer had committed suicide. In determining whether the farmer committed suicide, which of the following statements is correct?
Answer A: A: There is a conclusive presumption that the gun discharged by accident based on the county sheriff's statement.
Answer B: B: There is a conclusive presumption the farmer did commit suicide because the coroner listed his cause of death as “suicide.”
Answer C: C: There is a rebuttable presumption the farmer did commit suicide because the coroner listed his cause of death as “suicide.”
Answer D: D: There is a rebuttable presumption that the farmer did not commit suicide because the cause of death was in dispute.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: A presumption allows a party to show the existence of a particular fact without specific proof. If there is a presumption, the party challenging the presumption has the burden of providing evidence to rebut the presumption. FRE 301. A common rebuttable presumption is the presumption against suicide. In civil cases, if the cause of death is in dispute, there is a presumption that the dead person did not commit suicide. Here, the cause of death is in dispute and this is a civil case, so the insurer must provide evidence that the farmer did in fact commit suicide. A and B are incorrect because a conclusive presumption is one that cannot be rebutted by opposing evidence. For example, a conclusive presumption exists if a state law makes it impossible for a child under a certain age to be guilty of a crime. In that case, no matter how much evidence exists that the child committed the action, there is still no way for the child to be convicted of the crime. C is incorrect because the coroner report is irrelevant to the issue of presumption.
