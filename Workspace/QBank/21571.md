Question: An adult driver with a chronic cognitive condition became confused by ordinary traffic signs and drove into a marked one-way street the wrong way. She argues the jury must judge her only by what was reasonable for her personal cognitive capacity. How is the standard of care generally framed?
Answer A: By the defendant's personal cognitive capacity only
Answer B: By the ordinary reasonably prudent person standard
Answer C: By strict liability because the driver had a condition
Answer D: By a child standard because mental disability is always treated like childhood
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The general rule keeps the standard objective despite mental disability.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Mental disability generally does not reduce the objective negligence standard. C: The issue is negligence standard, not strict liability. D: Child status and adult mental disability are treated differently for negligence standards.
lb_rule_reference: Mental disability generally does not reduce the objective reasonable-person standard in negligence.
