Question: A city adopts a race-conscious remedy after a court finds the city intentionally excluded minority contractors from a specific public-works program for many years. The remedy is limited to that program, expires after two years, and requires individualized review. Which statement best describes the Equal Protection analysis?
Answer A: The racial classification triggers strict scrutiny, and the city must prove a compelling remedial interest and narrow tailoring
Answer B: The remedy receives rational basis because it is designed to help a disadvantaged group
Answer C: The remedy is automatically invalid the moment strict scrutiny applies
Answer D: The remedy receives intermediate scrutiny because contracting is economic activity
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Race triggers strict scrutiny, but the analysis still asks whether the remedial interest and tailoring satisfy that standard.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Benign racial classifications still trigger strict scrutiny. C: Strict scrutiny is not an automatic no; the government may satisfy it in narrow circumstances. D: The classification is racial, so strict scrutiny applies.
lb_rule_reference: Express racial classifications trigger strict scrutiny; the government must prove a compelling interest and narrow tailoring, but strict scrutiny is not a label-only automatic outcome.
