Question: Leah owned a life estate in Greenacre under a deed that conveyed the land “to Leah for life, then to Joseph.” Leah later executed a deed conveying “all of my interest in Greenacre” to Moses. Joseph did not consent to the transfer. What did Moses receive?
Answer A: Fee simple absolute, because Leah conveyed all of her interest.
Answer B: No interest, because a life estate cannot be transferred.
Answer C: Leah's life estate, measured by Leah's life.
Answer D: A tenancy in common with Joseph.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Moses receives Leah's life estate, measured by Leah's life. A life tenant may transfer the life estate, but the transferee receives no greater estate than the life tenant owned. Joseph's remainder is not destroyed.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Leah did not own a fee simple. B: B is wrong because life estates are generally transferable. D: D is wrong because Joseph is a remainderman, not a current co-tenant with Leah or Moses.
lb_rule_reference: Life tenant transfer / transferor cannot convey more than owned
