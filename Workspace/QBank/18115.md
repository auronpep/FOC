Question: In a federal civil trial, Hannah gives confusing testimony about the date of a delivery. The judge asks several neutral questions to clarify whether she means Monday or Tuesday. Counsel objects in front of the jury that the judge may not question any witness. Which ruling is correct?
Answer A: The objection should be sustained because only parties may ask questions.
Answer B: The judge may examine a witness, and a party may object then or at the next opportunity when the jury is not present.
Answer C: The judge may question Hannah only if both parties first agree to the questions in writing.
Answer D: The judge may question Hannah only after declaring her hostile.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The court may examine a witness regardless of who called the witness. A party may object to the court's examining a witness either at that time or at the next opportunity when the jury is not present.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Rule 614 permits court examination. C invents a written-consent requirement. D confuses court examination with leading/hostility concepts.
