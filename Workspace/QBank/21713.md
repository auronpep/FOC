Question: Isaac tells Mary, “I will pay you $2,000 if you tile my porch by Friday.” The offer does not ask Mary to promise. Mary begins tiling on Wednesday. On Thursday, after the porch is half tiled, Isaac tells Mary that he revokes. Mary finishes the porch by Friday. What is the best analysis?
Answer A: No contract can arise because Isaac's promise was a gift promise until Mary finished.
Answer B: Isaac could revoke at any time before Mary completed the porch because no bilateral promise had been made.
Answer C: Isaac's revocation was effective because Mary had not yet fully accepted.
Answer D: Mary's beginning of the requested performance made the unilateral offer temporarily irrevocable, and completion accepted the offer.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: When an offer invites acceptance by performance, beginning the invited performance commonly creates an option-like protection against revocation. Acceptance occurs on completion, and Mary completed by Friday.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Isaac sought performance in exchange for payment. B: B is wrong because part performance of the invited act can protect the offeree from revocation. C: C is wrong because although acceptance was not complete until performance was complete, Isaac could not freely revoke after Mary began the requested performance.
lb_rule_reference: common_law
lb_student_diagnostic: Student confused acceptance by completion with the separate irrevocability created by beginning performance.
