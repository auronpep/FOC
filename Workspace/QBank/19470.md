Question: Sarah contracted with Jacob to buy Jacob's shop. Jacob promised Sarah that he would pay Hannah $10,000. Hannah did not know of the promise. Before Hannah learned of it, Sarah and Jacob amended the contract to reduce Hannah's payment to $6,000. Sarah then told Hannah about the amended promise and asked whether Hannah accepted. Hannah replied, “Yes, I accept.” If Hannah sues Jacob, what is the most she can enforce?
Answer A: $10,000, because the original promise was made for her benefit.
Answer B: $10,000, because later assent relates back to the original contract date.
Answer C: Nothing, because an intended beneficiary must be aware at formation.
Answer D: $6,000, because Hannah's rights vested only after the amendment and therefore only as amended.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Sarah and Jacob modified the promise before Hannah's rights vested. Hannah's later assent vested her rights only in the amended promise. She may enforce the $6,000 promise, not the original $10,000 promise.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because intended status did not freeze the original amount before vesting. B: B is wrong because later assent does not relate back to bar an earlier valid modification. C: C is wrong because awareness at formation is not required for intended status.
