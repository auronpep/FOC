Question: A merchant manufacturer and a merchant wholesaler signed a contract for 10,000 bolts at $2 per bolt. The signed contract stated that no modification would be effective unless made in a signed writing. After signing, the manufacturer orally said it would reduce the price for future shipments to $1.80 per bolt. Before the next shipment, the manufacturer withdrew the offer. The wholesaler had not ordered replacement goods, changed its resale prices, or accepted any shipment invoiced at $1.80. When the next invoice arrived at $2 per bolt, the wholesaler deducted twenty cents per bolt. Can the wholesaler enforce the oral price reduction?
Answer A: Yes, because Article 2 never allows parties to require signed written modifications.
Answer B: Yes, because the parol evidence rule does not bar later oral changes.
Answer C: Yes, because a buyer's expectation of a lower price is enough to waive a signed no-oral-modification clause.
Answer D: No, because the signed no-oral-modification clause is effective between the merchants and there are no performance, waiver, or reliance facts supporting the oral reduction.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The narrow rule is that Article 2 allows parties to require signed written modifications, although an ineffective oral modification can sometimes operate as a waiver. The trigger facts are the signed merchant-to-merchant no-oral-modification clause, the unperformed oral price statement, and the absence of acceptance, payment practice, or material reliance. The decisive lock is waiver absence: no consideration is required under Article 2, but the signed-writing clause still matters when no waiver facts exist.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Article 2 permits signed no-oral-modification clauses. B: B is wrong because avoiding PER does not itself make the oral change enforceable; the signed-writing clause still must be addressed. C: C is wrong because expectation alone is not waiver or material reliance.
lb_rule_reference: UCC 2-209(2), (4) / NOM clause without waiver
