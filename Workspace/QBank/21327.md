Question: A state requires all trucks using mountain roads to carry tire chains during winter storms. The rule applies equally to in-state and out-of-state trucks. Out-of-state carriers incur some extra costs, but the state shows the rule reduces crashes on icy roads. What standard applies?
Answer A: Pike balancing applies because the law is evenhanded and imposes only incidental commerce burdens
Answer B: Strict scrutiny applies because any cost to interstate carriers is discrimination
Answer C: No review applies because facially neutral laws are always valid
Answer D: Article IV privileges and immunities supplies the only test
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The rule is nondiscriminatory and tied to local safety benefits.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Incidental burdens from evenhanded laws use Pike, not automatic discrimination analysis. C: Evenhanded laws can still be invalid if burdens are clearly excessive. D: The rule burdens goods and carriers in interstate commerce, not citizen privileges as such.
lb_rule_reference: A nondiscriminatory law with incidental effects on interstate commerce is upheld unless the burden on interstate commerce is clearly excessive in relation to the putative local benefits.
