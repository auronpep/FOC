Question: A plaintiff brings a diversity action seeking legal damages on a state-law fraud claim. The forum state's practice would allow the judge to decide the factual issues without a jury. The plaintiff timely demands a jury in federal court. What source controls the federal jury-trial question?
Answer A: Federal law, because jury-trial allocation in federal court is federally governed
Answer B: State law, because Erie makes every state courtroom practice binding
Answer C: The defendant's choice, because the defendant removed or answered in federal court
Answer D: No law controls because juries are discretionary in diversity cases
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Federal law governs the right to jury trial and judge-jury allocation in federal court, while state law supplies the substantive fraud elements.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Erie does not override the federal interest in federal judge-jury allocation. C: Jury-trial rights are not chosen unilaterally by the opposing party. D: Federal jury-trial law applies; it is not a matter of unguided discretion.
lb_rule_reference: In federal court, the right to a jury trial and the judge-jury allocation are governed by federal law, including the Seventh Amendment, even in diversity cases.
