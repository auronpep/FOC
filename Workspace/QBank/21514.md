MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Specific and General Jurisdiction
Question: Wren Analytics LLC is a data firm incorporated and operating entirely in the State of Briston. Wren was contacted remotely by Harlow Systems Inc., a company headquartered in the State of Moredin, which asked Wren to perform a one-time data-analytics study of Harlow's customer base. Wren and Harlow negotiated by phone and email, with all communications initiated from Briston. Wren performed all of its analytical work from its Briston office and delivered results electronically. The written engagement letter, prepared in Briston, contains no choice-of-law clause. Wren submitted one invoice, which Harlow paid from Moredin. After Harlow published the results of the study in a way Wren disputes, Wren filed suit against Harlow in a Moredin federal court for breach of contract. Harlow moves to dismiss for lack of personal jurisdiction, arguing it has no contacts with Moredin arising from a project it conducted entirely in Briston. Wait — the plaintiff is Wren, which is in Briston, and it is suing in Moredin, where Harlow (the defendant) is based. Does the Moredin court have personal jurisdiction over Harlow?
Answer A: Yes, because Harlow entered into a contract with Wren, a Briston company — contracting with a forum-state company is sufficient to establish minimum contacts with Briston, so Harlow is subject to Briston jurisdiction
Answer B: Yes, because Harlow's payment from Moredin to Wren in Briston constitutes an economic act directed at Briston sufficient for Briston to exercise jurisdiction
Answer C: Yes, because Harlow is incorporated and headquartered in Moredin — Moredin is Harlow's home state and Moredin therefore has general jurisdiction over Harlow for any claim, including this contract dispute
Answer D: No, because the contract was negotiated remotely and performed entirely in Briston, so Harlow has no meaningful contacts with Moredin arising from this particular transaction
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Wren filed suit in Moredin federal court — Harlow's own home state. Moredin has general personal jurisdiction over Harlow because Harlow is incorporated and headquartered in Moredin. A corporation's state of incorporation and principal-place-of-business state are its home states for general-jurisdiction purposes under *Daimler* and *Goodyear*. The Moredin court may hear any claim against Harlow, including this breach-of-contract claim arising from a Briston-performed project. The motion to dismiss should be denied.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: This answer confuses which forum is at issue and why. The plaintiff filed in Moredin — Harlow's state. The question is whether Moredin has jurisdiction over Harlow. Since Harlow is incorporated in and headquartered in Moredin, Moredin has general jurisdiction over Harlow as its home state. The analysis for Moredin PJ over Harlow is straightforward on general-jurisdiction grounds — this distractor tests whether students correctly apply PJ analysis to the right party and forum. B: Again, the forum is Moredin, not Briston. Payment from Harlow's Moredin office to Wren in Briston is Harlow acting in its own state — it is not a Harlow contact with any other forum. This distractor further tests whether students correctly identify the forum and the nature of the contact being analyzed. D: This answer runs the specific-jurisdiction analysis when general jurisdiction over Harlow in Harlow's home state (Moredin) is the obvious and dispositive basis for PJ. Because Harlow is incorporated and headquartered in Moredin, Moredin has general jurisdiction over Harlow for any claim. There is no need to analyze whether the specific contract gave rise to Moredin contacts when Harlow is at home in Moredin.
lb_rule_reference: A contract alone does not establish personal jurisdiction. Courts applying *Burger King* examine where the contract was negotiated, where performance occurred, choice-of-law, and the parties' course of dealing. A one-time, remotely negotiated contract with performance entirely outside the forum, combined with no other forum contacts, usually fails purposeful availment.
