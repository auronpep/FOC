Question: Nathan signed and recorded a document granting Sarah “an easement to maintain one billboard on the west edge of Nathan's land for 10 years.” Sarah uses the billboard for her advertising business and owns no neighboring parcel that benefits from it. What is the best classification of Sarah's right?
Answer A: A revocable license because Sarah owns no dominant estate.
Answer B: A commercial easement in gross because the written use right benefits Sarah's business rather than a parcel.
Answer C: An easement appurtenant to Nathan's land because the billboard is physically located there.
Answer D: A profit because Sarah earns income from advertisements.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A commercial easement in gross benefits a person or business rather than a dominant parcel. Sarah has a recorded easement to maintain a billboard for her business, and no benefited parcel is identified.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because lack of a dominant estate does not make a written easement revocable. C: C is wrong because the servient land is burdened, not benefited, by the billboard. D: D is wrong because earning advertising income is not removing resources from Nathan's land.
