


BARMATRIX Q#: 22622
Outline_code: 44040203
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Constitutional Law
Subtopic: Individual Rights
Outline: Procedural due process
Question: A group of investors bought an abandoned building in an up- and- coming part of the city with the plan to turn it into a bar. The investors applied for the necessary permits, but the city denied the permits without stating a reason. The investors challenged the denial, but the city said any permit denials were final decisions that could not be appealed. The investors sued, arguing that it had a right to appeal the permit denials. If the court rules in favor of the investors, which of the following is the most likely reason?
Answer A: A: The denial of the permits was an unconstitutional taking.
Answer B: B: The denial of the permits violated procedural due process.
Answer C: C: The denial of the permits violated substantive due process.
Answer D: D: The denial of the permits violated the Equal Protection Clause.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The Constitution protects citizens against deprivations of life, liberty, and property without the due process of law. Due process is split into procedural and substantive due process. Procedural due process gives citizens a right to be heard before a deprivation occurs. Here, the city denied the permits without any hearing or reasoning. Thus, if the court rules in the investors' favor, it is likely because the city violated the investors' procedural due process rights. A is incorrect because a taking involves the taking of private property for public use. Here, the permits were not issued and there is no indication that the failure to issue the permits took away all use of the property. As the question reads, it seems the investors were only prohibited from opening a bar. C is incorrect because substantive due process involves fundamental rights. Here, the investors do not have a fundamental right to open a bar. D is incorrect because the equal protection clause is implicated when the state treats citizens differently. Here, there are no facts regarding the reason for the permit denials, which makes B a better answer.
