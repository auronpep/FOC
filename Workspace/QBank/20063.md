Question: During a nationwide strike by workers at private data centers that process federal benefits, the President orders the Secretary of Commerce to take temporary possession of the data centers and operate them until the strike ends. Congress recently rejected a bill that would have authorized such seizures and instead enacted a statute saying that private data centers may not be taken over during labor disputes unless Congress later authorizes the takeover by statute. The President argues that uninterrupted benefits processing is essential to national stability and that the emergency gives the executive branch inherent authority to act. Which statement best describes the President's constitutional authority?
Answer A: The order is likely valid because an emergency allows the President to exercise inherent domestic lawmaking authority
Answer B: The order is likely invalid because the President is acting against Congress's express limitation and is at the lowest ebb of authority
Answer C: The order is valid because the President supervises executive agencies that administer the benefits program
Answer D: The order is valid only if the data centers serve customers in more than one state
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Congress addressed the precise problem and barred seizure without later statutory authorization. The President's power is at its lowest point, and generalized emergency or executive-management concerns do not override Congress's domestic rule.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Youngstown rejects emergency as an automatic source of domestic seizure power when Congress has withheld or forbidden the authority. C: Supervisory power over executive agencies does not create authority to take possession of private property contrary to a statute. D: The issue is presidential authority, not whether Congress could regulate interstate commerce. Congress has already spoken against this executive takeover.
lb_rule_reference: Presidential power is weakest when the President acts contrary to Congress's express or implied will; emergency and Commander in Chief labels do not create domestic lawmaking power against a statute.
