Question: Joshua carefully excavated on his own lot to build a basement. Despite his reasonable care, Joseph's adjoining land, which was vacant and in its natural condition, collapsed into the excavation. Joseph sues Joshua for damage to the land itself. Which is the best result?
Answer A: Joshua is liable because an adjoining owner has a strict right of lateral support for land in its natural condition.
Answer B: Joshua is not liable because he used reasonable care.
Answer C: Joshua is liable only if Joseph had an express easement of support.
Answer D: Joshua is not liable unless Joseph proves the excavation was a nuisance.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A landowner has a right to lateral support for land in its natural condition. If excavation removes that support and causes the land itself to collapse, liability is generally strict. Joseph's land was vacant and in its natural condition.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because reasonable care is not a defense to strict liability for loss of lateral support of natural land. C: C is wrong because lateral support is a property right, not merely an express easement. D: D is wrong because nuisance is not the required route.
lb_rule_reference: Lateral support / land in natural condition / strict liability
