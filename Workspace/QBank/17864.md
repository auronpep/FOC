Question: Isaac emails Jacob: “I offer to sell you my field for $200,000. If you accept by Friday, we will close next month.” Jacob emails back on Thursday, “I accept.” Isaac says no contract formed because they have not yet signed closing papers. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Isaac's email was only a preliminary negotiation because it concerned land.
Answer B: Isaac's email was an offer, and Jacob's timely acceptance formed a contract, even though later closing papers remained.
Answer C: No contract formed because parties never have a contract until every later document is signed.
Answer D: No contract formed unless Jacob's acceptance stated that the price was fair.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Isaac's email manifested present commitment and invited acceptance. Later closing papers may memorialize or implement the deal, but they do not necessarily prevent formation when the parties already assented to essential terms.
