Question: Congress requires every adult to purchase a minimum amount of home solar equipment or make a payment to the federal government. Congress argues that everyone will eventually use electricity, and the national energy market is important. Which analysis is most accurate?
Answer A: The purchase mandate is valid under the Commerce Clause because everyone is inevitably part of the energy market
Answer B: The Commerce Clause alone does not support compelling market entry, but the payment should be separately analyzed under the taxing power
Answer C: The mandate is invalid under every enumerated power once commerce authority fails
Answer D: The mandate is valid because Congress has a general power to solve national markets
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The analysis separates commerce authority from possible taxing authority.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Commerce power regulates existing activity; inevitability does not convert inactivity into commerce. C: A payment can sometimes be sustained as a tax even when commerce authority fails. D: Congress still needs an enumerated source of power.
lb_rule_reference: Congress may regulate existing commerce activity, but the Commerce Clause does not permit Congress to compel individuals to enter a market; taxing power must be analyzed separately.
