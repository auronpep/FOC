Stem / Fact Pattern: Three brothers held Blackacre as joint tenants. Faced with serious credit card debt, the eldest brother conveyed his interest to a friend of his who agreed to pay off the debt. What is the effect of the conveyance to the friend?
Answer A: A: The two younger brothers and the friend hold the property as joint tenants.
Answer B: B: The two younger brothers and the friend hold the property as tenants in common.
Answer C: C: The two younger brothers hold a twothirds interest in the property as joint tenants.
Answer D: D: The friend holds a one- third interest as a joint tenant with the brothers
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A joint tenancy means that two or more people own a single, unified interest in the property. A joint tenancy also means the parties to the joint tenancy share a right of survivorship (meaning that if one dies, the other joint tenants become owners of the deceased tenant’s interest). There are several ways in which a joint tenancy can be destroyed, and severance normally results in a tenancy in common. A tenancy in common has no right of survivorship. Importantly, if there are three or more original joint tenants, a conveyance by one of them to a stranger will create a tenancy in common between the stranger and the original joint tenants. However, the joint tenancy will continue between the original joint tenants. Here, the conveyance creates a tenancy in common between the friend and the two younger brothers for one- third of the property, but the younger brothers’ joint interest in the remaining two- thirds of the property survives. Consequently, if the friend dies, his interest will go to his heirs or devisees. If one of the brothers dies, his interest will go to his brother. A, B, and D are therefore incorrect.
