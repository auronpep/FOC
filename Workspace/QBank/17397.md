Outline: Issue and Claim Preclusion
Question: Ruth sues Boaz for breach of contract. The court dismisses the action without prejudice because Ruth failed to pay the filing fee. Ruth promptly pays the fee and refiles the same contract claim against Boaz. Does claim preclusion bar the second action?
Answer A: Yes, because any dismissal creates claim preclusion
Answer B: Yes, because the same contract is involved
Answer C: No, because the first dismissal was without prejudice and not a merits judgment
Answer D: No, because claim preclusion never applies to contract cases
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A dismissal without prejudice generally does not bar refiling the same claim.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Claim preclusion requires a qualifying final judgment on the merits. B: Same transaction alone is not enough without a qualifying prior judgment. D: Contract claims can be precluded when the elements are met.
lb_rule_reference: A non-merits dismissal without prejudice generally leaves the plaintiff free to refile.
