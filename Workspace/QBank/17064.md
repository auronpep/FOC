Outline: Commerce power
Question: Congress prohibits transporting counterfeit medical devices on interstate highways. A defendant arrested while driving the devices from one state to another argues that Congress may not regulate because the devices were sold locally after the trip ended. Which commerce-power category best supports the statute?
Answer A: Regulation of the channels of interstate commerce
Answer B: Regulation of noneconomic local activity substantially affecting commerce
Answer C: Regulation under the spending power
Answer D: Regulation under a general federal police power to protect health
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Congress may keep interstate highways and other commerce channels free from harmful or illicit uses.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: The direct hook is transportation through interstate channels, not aggregation of local noneconomic conduct. C: No federal funds or spending condition appears. D: Congress needs an enumerated power; the valid hook here is commerce channels.
lb_rule_reference: Congress may regulate the channels of interstate commerce, such as highways, waterways, air routes, and internet channels used for interstate trade.
