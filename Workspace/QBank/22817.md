Subtopic: Pretrial Procedures
Outline: Discovery
Question: A plaintiff filed an action in federal district court and served the defendant with the summons, complaint, and 25 interrogatories. The interrogatories stated that they were to be answered within 30 days. In response, the defendant wanted to obtain a protective order. What is the defendant's best argument?
Answer A: A: The parties have not yet conferred to arrange for initial disclosures and prepare a discovery plan.
Answer B: B: The number of interrogatories exceed the number permitted without permission from the court.
Answer C: C: The number of interrogatories exceed the number permitted without an agreement between the parties.
Answer D: D: The defendant has not yet filed an answer to the complaint.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Under Fed. R. Civ. P. 26(d)(1), a party may not seek discovery from any source before the parties have held a planning and discovery conference. B and C are incorrect because Fed. R. Civ. P. 33 allows parties to serve 25 interrogatories without a court order or agreement. D is incorrect because Fed. R. Civ. P. 26 does not prevent interrogatories from being served before a party's answer.
