Outline: Roles of judge and jury
Question: In a federal criminal trial, the prosecutor offers a statement made by Naomi, a witness who is unavailable. David objects that the statement is inadmissible hearsay. David asks to testify at the preliminary hearing about facts showing Naomi was not unavailable, and asks that the hearing be held outside the jury's presence. What should the court do?
Answer A: Deny the request because hearsay foundation is always decided by the jury.
Answer B: Hold the preliminary hearing outside the jury's presence because David is a criminal defendant and requests to testify on the preliminary matter.
Answer C: Allow David to testify only if he agrees to testify before the jury on the merits.
Answer D: Let the jury hear David's preliminary testimony and decide both admissibility and guilt.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: When a criminal defendant is a witness on a preliminary question and requests it, the court must conduct the hearing outside the jury's hearing.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the court decides preliminary admissibility issues. C: C is wrong because preliminary testimony does not require merits testimony. D: D is wrong because the jury does not decide the admissibility gate.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 104(c)(2), 104(d)
