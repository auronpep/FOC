Outline_code: 74020102
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Homicide
Outline: Manslaughter
Question: A woman kept several live tigers in cages in the back of her property in a suburban area. At night, she'd let the tigers out to roam free around her backyard. One of the tigers hopped the backyard's short fence and attacked a man walking by on the sidewalk. The man eventually died as a result of the attack. What is the most serious crime of which the woman can be convicted?
Answer A: A: Murder.
Answer B: B: Voluntary manslaughter.
Answer C: C: Involuntary manslaughter.
Answer D: D: No crime.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A conviction for murder can be supported by extremely negligent conduct that a reasonable person would realize created an unjustifiable and high risk of death or serious bodily injury. Here, the woman let tigers run around her suburban backyard. This is probably enough to meet the standard. B is incorrect because there needs to be some adequate provocation. Here, there wasn't any provocation at all. C is incorrect because the woman's conduct rose to the level of extremely negligent conduct required for depraved heart murder. As such, C is not the strongest answer even if the woman did not intend to kill anyone. D is incorrect because the woman could be convicted of murder.
