Question: A state employee disability plan excludes pregnancy-related leave from one category of optional supplemental benefits. The plan does not classify employees as male or female, and the challenge is brought only under the Equal Protection Clause, not under any employment statute. Which scrutiny point is most accurate?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny applies because pregnancy involves bodily autonomy
Answer B: Intermediate scrutiny automatically applies because every pregnancy classification is a sex classification
Answer C: Rational basis is the default Equal Protection tier absent a sex classification or proof of sex-based purpose
Answer D: No scrutiny applies because benefit plans are never state action
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: On these facts, the plan draws a pregnancy-benefit line rather than a facial sex line, and the problem excludes statutory claims.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The question asks Equal Protection classification tier, not substantive due process. B: Equal Protection doctrine does not automatically equate pregnancy classification with sex classification. D: A state employee benefit plan is government action.
lb_rule_reference: A pregnancy classification is not automatically treated as a sex classification for Equal Protection purposes, though other statutes or proof of sex stereotyping may change the analysis.
