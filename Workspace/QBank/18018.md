Question: A bank holds a fund claimed by three people. Two claimants are from different states, but the stakeholder bank shares citizenship with one claimant. The bank invokes statutory interpleader and deposits the fund with the court. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Statutory interpleader fails because the stakeholder must be diverse from every claimant
Answer B: Statutory interpleader may be proper because minimal diversity among adverse claimants can suffice
Answer C: Statutory interpleader fails because deposit of the fund is never allowed
Answer D: Statutory interpleader is available only when all claimants are citizens of the same state
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The bank's shared citizenship with one claimant does not defeat statutory interpleader if the statutory requirements are met.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Statutory interpleader focuses on minimal diversity among adverse claimants. C: Deposit or bond is part of statutory interpleader practice. D: Minimal diversity requires at least two adverse claimants from different states.
lb_rule_reference: Statutory interpleader has special jurisdictional rules, including minimal diversity among adverse claimants and a lower amount threshold, plus deposit or bond requirements.
