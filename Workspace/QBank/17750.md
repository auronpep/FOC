Question: In a federal contract trial, Rebekah is asked on cross-examination, “You did not read the invoice before signing it, correct?” Rebekah answers, “That is a dishonest question, and Isaac's lawyer has been trying to trick everyone all morning.” Isaac moves to strike the answer. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Deny the motion because witnesses may freely comment on lawyers during cross-examination.
Answer B: Deny the motion because the answer concerns the credibility of Isaac's lawyer.
Answer C: Grant the motion only if Isaac proves the statement is false.
Answer D: Grant the motion because the answer is nonresponsive and argumentative rather than testimony answering the question.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Rebekah did not answer whether she read the invoice. She attacked counsel and argued about the question. The court may strike the nonresponsive, argumentative answer and direct the witness to answer the question.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the court controls abusive or argumentative responses. B: B is wrong because counsel's credibility is not the witness's testimony issue. C: C is wrong because the remedy does not depend on proving the insult false.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 611(a) / argumentative nonresponsive answer
