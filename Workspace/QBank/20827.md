Question: In a federal robbery trial, the judge sustains an objection to a question and tells the witness, “Do not answer that question.” The witness nevertheless says, “Peter confessed to me.” Peter immediately moves to strike and asks for an instruction to disregard. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Grant the motion and instruct the jury to disregard the answer.
Answer B: Deny the motion because the witness's answer was short.
Answer C: Deny the motion because a judge cannot strike testimony after the jury hears it.
Answer D: Grant the motion only if the prosecutor also objects.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The witness answered after the court had directed him not to answer. The court should strike the volunteered matter and instruct the jury to disregard it to prevent use of inadmissible evidence.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because shortness does not make an improper answer admissible. C: C is wrong because evidence heard by the jury can be stricken and limited by instruction. D: D is wrong because Peter, the affected party, may seek the remedy.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 103(d); FRE 611(a) / answer despite instruction not to answer
