Subtopic: Privileges and Other Policy Exclusions
Outline: Subsequent remedial measures
Question: Eve is injured when a home exercise machine's guard fails to cover a pinch point. In a federal products-liability action governed by the Federal Rules of Evidence, Eve offers evidence that, after her injury, the manufacturer redesigned the guard and added a stronger warning label. Eve offers the evidence to prove that the original design was defective and that the original warning was inadequate. How should the court rule under Rule 407?
Answer A: Admit the evidence because Rule 407 applies only to negligence claims, not products-liability claims.
Answer B: Admit the evidence automatically because a later redesign always proves feasibility.
Answer C: Admit the evidence because the design change is relevant and relevant evidence is generally admissible.
Answer D: Exclude the evidence for this purpose because Rule 407 bars subsequent remedial measures offered to prove a product defect, design defect, or need for a warning or instruction.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Federal Rule 407 bars evidence of later measures that would have made an earlier injury less likely when offered to prove negligence, culpable conduct, product defect, design defect, or need for a warning or instruction. Eve offers the post-injury redesign and warning change for exactly those barred product-liability purposes. Feasibility, ownership, control, or genuine impeachment might be different if disputed, but those are not the stated purposes.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the federal rule expressly reaches product defect, design defect, and warning-need uses; it is not negligence-only. B: B is wrong because feasibility is a permitted alternative purpose only if genuinely disputed and offered for that purpose. Eve offers the changes to prove defect and inadequate warning. C: C is wrong because relevance does not override an independent public-policy exclusion.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 407 includes product defect, design defect, and need for warning or instruction
lb_student_diagnostic: Student uses a negligence-only version of Rule 407 or lets relevance/feasibility language override the actual offered purpose.
