MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Torts
Subtopic: Strict Liability and Products Liability
Outline: Abnormally Dangerous Activities
Question: In a rural farming county, nearly every orchard uses ordinary low-toxicity crop spray during spring. A grower used the standard spray at the recommended concentration on a calm morning. A neighboring jogger smelled the spray from the public road, became nauseated, and sued the grower for strict liability, alleging that crop spraying is dangerous. No evidence shows that the spray was unusually toxic, that the method was uncommon in the area, or that careful use could not control the risk. Is the jogger likely to prevail on a strict liability claim for an abnormally dangerous activity?
Answer A: Yes, because any activity involving chemicals is abnormally dangerous
Answer B: No, because ordinary crop spraying common to the farming community is not abnormally dangerous on these facts
Answer C: No, because the jogger was on a public road rather than the grower's land
Answer D: Yes, because the jogger suffered physical symptoms after smelling the spray
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The activity was common in that rural county, used ordinary spray, and lacked facts showing unavoidable high risk. The claim may sound in negligence if the grower breached care, but strict liability is not established.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Danger alone is not enough. The facts describe a common agricultural practice using ordinary low-toxicity spray, with no showing that reasonable care cannot control the risk. C: Strict liability for an abnormally dangerous activity is not defeated merely because the plaintiff is off the defendant's land. The better reason is that this activity is not abnormally dangerous in context. D: Injury does not by itself make an activity abnormally dangerous. The classification depends on the nature and context of the activity and whether the abnormal risk caused the harm.
lb_rule_reference: An activity is not abnormally dangerous merely because it involves some danger; the risk, inability to eliminate it by care, and uncommonness in context matter.
