Question: On March 1, an apartment complex received a letter from a retailer offering to sell the apartment complex 1,200 window air conditioners suitable for the apartments in the complex's buildings. The retailer's offer stated that it would remain open until March 20, but that the apartment complex's acceptance must be received on or before that date. On March 16, the apartment complex posted a letter of acceptance. On March 17, the retailer telegraphed the apartment complex to advise that it was revoking the offer. The telegram reached the apartment complex on March 17, but the apartment complex's letter did not arrive at the retailer's address until March 21. As of March 22, which of the following is a correct statement?
Answer A: A: The telegram revoking the offer was effective upon receipt.
Answer B: B: The offer was revocable at any time for lack of consideration.
Answer C: C: The mail was the only authorized means of revocation.
Answer D: D: Under the terms of the retailer's offer, the apartment complex's attempted acceptance was ineffective.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: D is correct. Because the retailer's firm offer stated that the apartment complex's acceptance must be received on or before March 20, and the apartment complex's acceptance did not arrive until March 21, it was ineffective in establishing a contract. A
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect. This was a UCC firm offer, which means it was irrevocable until March 20, despite the usual rule that revocation of an offer is valid upon receipt. B is incorrect. A firm offer is considered irrevocable even without consideration. C is incorrect. As a firm offer, it was not revocable by any means.
