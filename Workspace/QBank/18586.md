Outline: Privileges and Immunities Clause of Article IV, Section 2
Question: A corporation incorporated in State A challenges State B's rule charging out-of-state corporations a higher filing fee to do business. The corporation brings only an Article IV Privileges and Immunities claim. What is State B's strongest response to that claim?
Answer A: Article IV protects corporations whenever they do business across state lines
Answer B: The corporation is not a citizen for Article IV Privileges and Immunities purposes
Answer C: Article IV applies only to criminal defendants
Answer D: The corporation wins automatically because filing fees are always fundamental privileges
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A corporation must look to other doctrines; Article IV P/I is unavailable to it.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Article IV protects citizens, not corporations. C: Article IV often protects civil economic rights of natural citizens. D: The claim fails at the citizen threshold before reaching the privilege question.
lb_rule_reference: Article IV Privileges and Immunities protects citizens of other states, not corporations.
