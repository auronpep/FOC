Question: Leah sends Daniel a written offer: “I will pay you $900 if you promise by Friday to repair my fence next week.” On Thursday, Daniel replies, “I promise to repair the fence next week.” Before Daniel begins work, Leah tells Daniel that she has changed her mind and will not pay him. If Daniel later performs the repair, what is the best analysis?
Answer A: No contract was formed because Daniel had not begun performance when Leah tried to cancel.
Answer B: A bilateral contract was formed when Daniel promised to perform before the deadline.
Answer C: No contract was formed because Daniel's promise benefited only Leah.
Answer D: A contract could be formed only if Leah also signed a second writing after Daniel's promise.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The offer invited acceptance by a return promise. Daniel accepted by promising before the stated deadline, creating a bilateral contract. Leah's later attempted cancellation came after acceptance.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because performance was not required for acceptance; the offer asked for Daniel's promise. C: C is wrong because consideration is a bargained-for exchange, not a requirement that both sides receive the same kind of benefit. D: D is wrong because the fact pattern does not make a second signed writing a condition of formation.
lb_rule_reference: common_law
lb_student_diagnostic: Student treated a bilateral offer as if it could be accepted only by performance.
