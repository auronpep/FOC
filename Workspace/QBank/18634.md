Question: Joshua made two valid deeds. First, he conveyed Northacre “to Caleb so long as Northacre is used as a garden.” Second, he conveyed Southacre “to Ruth on condition that Southacre is used as a market; if the condition is breached, Joshua may reenter and terminate the estate.” Caleb stopped using Northacre as a garden. Ruth stopped using Southacre as a market. Joshua has taken no action as to either parcel. Which statement is correct?
Answer A: Joshua owns both parcels automatically.
Answer B: Caleb and Ruth both keep ownership because use restrictions in deeds are always covenants.
Answer C: Caleb keeps Northacre until Joshua reenters, but Joshua owns Southacre automatically.
Answer D: Joshua owns Northacre automatically, but Ruth owns Southacre until Joshua exercises the right of entry.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Northacre was conveyed by durational language, creating a fee simple determinable. Caleb's estate ended automatically when garden use stopped. Southacre was conveyed by conditional language with an express right of entry, creating a fee simple subject to condition subsequent. Ruth's estate continues until Joshua exercises the right of entry.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Southacre does not revert automatically. B: B is wrong because these are not merely covenants.
