Question: After being thrown out of a party, Mina stood outside the apartment and fired several bullets through the living-room window. She knew the party was still crowded but later said she "was not aiming at anyone." One guest was killed. What is the best homicide classification?
Answer A: Murder, because Mina consciously disregarded an extreme risk to human life
Answer B: Involuntary manslaughter only, because Mina did not intend to hit anyone
Answer C: No homicide liability, because the victim was not visible from outside
Answer D: First-degree murder, because any gun death is automatically first degree
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Firing multiple bullets into a room Mina knew was crowded creates an extreme risk of death and shows indifference to human life. A specific target is unnecessary for depraved-heart murder.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Depraved-heart murder covers extreme recklessness. Shooting into an occupied room is far beyond ordinary negligence. C: She knew the room was occupied. Visibility of the individual victim is not required. D: The facts support depraved-heart murder, but not necessarily premeditated intent to kill or a statutory degree trigger.
lb_rule_reference: Knowingly creating an extreme risk of death to people in an occupied space can establish depraved-heart malice.
