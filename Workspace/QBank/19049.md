Question: In a federal antitrust trial, Martha offers a Rule 1006 chart summarizing 10,000 admissible sales records that cannot conveniently be examined in court. She made the records available to Abraham. Abraham asks the court to instruct the jury that the chart is not evidence. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Give the instruction because a Rule 1006 summary is never evidence.
Answer B: Give the instruction unless every underlying sales record is also admitted.
Answer C: Refuse the instruction because a proper Rule 1006 summary may be admitted as evidence.
Answer D: Refuse the instruction only if Abraham signs the chart.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A proper Rule 1006 summary may be admitted as evidence to prove the content of voluminous admissible materials that cannot conveniently be examined in court, if the underlying materials are made available.
