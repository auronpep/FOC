Outline: Strict Products Liability
Question: A portable fire extinguisher must be light enough for ordinary adults to lift quickly. A plaintiff argues that the extinguisher should have a thick steel outer shell that would survive a rare crushing accident. The shell would triple the weight and make the extinguisher too heavy for many users during a fire. How should the proposed alternative design be treated?
Answer A: It automatically proves a design defect because it would prevent one type of damage
Answer B: It may be unreasonable because it undermines the extinguisher's emergency function and creates new safety problems
Answer C: It proves a manufacturing defect because one extinguisher was damaged
Answer D: It eliminates all products liability because fire extinguishers are safety devices
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Risk-utility analysis considers product function and new risks, not only isolated injury reduction.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A design is not required merely because it is safer in one respect. C: The argument challenges product design, not a rogue unit. D: Safety devices can be defective, but this proposed alternative may be unreasonable.
lb_rule_reference: A proposed safer design must be reasonable in light of product function, cost, utility, and new risks; safer in one respect is not automatically required.
