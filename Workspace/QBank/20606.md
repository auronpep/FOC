MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Venue
Question: A woman from the Southern District of State A was injured in a boating accident in the Eastern District of State B. The woman sued two other boaters involved in the accident in a federal action based on diversity. The first was a doctor from the Western District of State C. The second was a nurse from the Eastern District of State C. In which judicial districts would venue be proper for the woman's action?
Answer A: A: The Eastern District of State B.
Answer B: B: The Southern District of State A and the Eastern District of State B.
Answer C: C: The Western District of State C and the Eastern District of State C.
Answer D: D: The Eastern District of State B, the Western District of State C, and the Eastern District of State C.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Therefore, A, B, and C are incorrect.
