Subtopic: Hearsay and Circumstances of Its Admissibility
Outline: Hearsay Exceptions
Question: John receives a federal subpoena demanding business records. Before responding, he meets privately with his lawyer Mary and asks which records must be preserved, how responsive documents should be produced, and how to make lawful objections to overbroad requests. John does not ask Mary to hide, destroy, backdate, or falsify anything. The opposing party moves to compel Mary to testify about the meeting, arguing that any legal advice after a subpoena is suspect. Should the subpoena context itself defeat attorney-client privilege?
Answer A: No. Legal advice about preserving records, producing responsive documents, and making lawful objections remains privileged absent facts showing concealment or fraud.
Answer B: Yes. Once a subpoena has issued, communications with counsel about the subpoena are no longer privileged.
Answer C: Yes. The crime-fraud exception applies whenever the legal advice concerns documents that may be evidence.
Answer D: No, but only because the communication is work product rather than attorney-client privileged.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A subpoena increases the stakes but does not erase attorney-client privilege. John sought legal advice about lawful preservation, production, and objections, and the facts exclude hiding, destruction, backdating, or false statements. The decisive axis is lawful compliance advice versus advice sought to obstruct or defraud.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because the issuance of a subpoena is not an automatic waiver or exception to privilege. C: C is wrong because documents may be evidence without making every legal consultation about them crime-fraud. The exception requires a wrongful purpose. D: D is wrong because the protected object is a client-lawyer communication seeking legal advice. Work product may also arise in discovery settings, but it is not the only or primary route here.
lb_rule_reference: Advice on lawful compliance with subpoena remains privileged
lb_student_diagnostic: Student treats subpoena pressure as automatic crime-fraud instead of reading the requested legal service.
