Question: In a federal civil trial, Rachel testifies on direct about a delivery. Cross-examination covers only the time of day. On redirect, Rachel testifies for the first time that she saw the defendant remove a warning label. The defendant asks to conduct a brief recross limited to that new statement. Rachel's lawyer objects that recross is never allowed. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because recross is categorically forbidden under the Federal Rules of Evidence.
Answer B: Deny recross unless Rachel's lawyer consents.
Answer C: Permit reasonable recross in the court's discretion to address the new matter raised on redirect.
Answer D: Permit recross only if the defendant first proves that Rachel lied on redirect.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The court has reasonable control over the mode and order of examination. When redirect introduces new matter, the court may permit reasonable recross limited to that new matter to preserve fairness.
