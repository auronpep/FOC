Subtopic: Constitutional Protection of Accused Persons (Criminal Procedure)
Outline: Fourth Amendment
Question: An officer sees Noel on a corner and shouts, "Stop, police." Noel immediately runs. While running, Noel throws a packet of drugs into a trash can. Officers catch Noel one block later. Noel moves to suppress the discarded packet as fruit of a seizure at the moment the officer shouted. Was Noel seized when the officer shouted "Stop"?
Answer A: No, because Noel did not submit to the show of authority before discarding the packet
Answer B: Yes, because a police command always creates an immediate seizure
Answer C: Yes, because Noel subjectively felt pressured to run
Answer D: No, because the Fourth Amendment never applies to discarded property
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A show of authority without physical force becomes a seizure only upon submission. Noel fled and discarded the packet before being caught.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: A command alone is an attempted seizure unless the person submits or physical force is applied. C: The rule turns on objective restraint and submission, not only subjective pressure. D: Discarded property can be fruit if discarded after an unlawful seizure. Here, the timing means no seizure had occurred yet.
lb_rule_reference: When police use a show of authority but no physical force, a seizure occurs only if the person submits to that authority.
