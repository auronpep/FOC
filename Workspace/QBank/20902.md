Subtopic: Presentation of Evidence
Outline: Burden of production and burden of persuasion
Question: In a federal civil trial, Noah offers a photograph of broken machinery. Noah testifies that he took the photograph on his phone immediately after the machine failed. Deborah objects that Noah has not proved the photograph is accurate because the phone could have altered the image. What foundation must Noah provide for the photograph to be admitted?
Answer A: He must prove beyond dispute that the phone did not alter the image.
Answer B: He must call a software expert to authenticate every digital photograph.
Answer C: He must produce evidence sufficient to support a finding that the photograph is what he claims it is.
Answer D: He need not provide any foundation because photographs are automatically admissible.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The proponent bears a threshold burden to produce evidence sufficient to support a finding that the item is what the proponent claims. Noah's testimony can be enough if it supports that finding. Possible alteration may affect weight.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because authentication does not require proof beyond dispute. B: B is wrong because a software expert is not categorically required. D: D is wrong because photographs still require authentication.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 901; FRE 104(b)
