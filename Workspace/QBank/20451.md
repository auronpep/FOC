Question: A state denies a discretionary housing grant to undocumented adults while allowing citizens and lawfully present residents to apply. An undocumented adult challenges the rule, arguing that any alienage line must receive strict scrutiny because education rules for undocumented children are treated differently. What is the best response?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny applies because undocumented adults are a suspect class
Answer B: The special rule for public education of undocumented children automatically controls adult housing benefits
Answer C: Rational basis is the likely default because the claimant is an undocumented adult seeking a discretionary benefit
Answer D: No Equal Protection claim exists because undocumented people are not persons
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The classification does not involve a suspect class or the narrow child-education exception.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Undocumented adults are not a suspect class for Equal Protection purposes. B: That doctrine is tied to children and public education, not every benefit for undocumented adults. D: Undocumented people are persons protected by constitutional limits, even when the review tier is deferential.
lb_rule_reference: Undocumented adults are not treated as a suspect class; the special education rule for undocumented children does not automatically extend to adult public-benefit restrictions.
