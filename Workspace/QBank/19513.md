Question: A diversity case is filed in a proper federal district in State A. On the defendant's motion, the court transfers the case to a more convenient federal district in State B under Section 1404(a). The parties dispute whether State A or State B choice-of-law rules apply after transfer. Which choice-of-law rules generally apply?
Answer A: State A's choice-of-law rules, because ordinary Section 1404 convenience transfer carries transferor law
Answer B: State B's choice-of-law rules, because the transferee court always starts fresh
Answer C: Federal common-law choice rules
Answer D: The state law least favorable to the plaintiff
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A defendant should not get a change in governing law merely by obtaining convenience transfer from a proper forum.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Transfer does not always reset law. C: Diversity choice rules come from state law under Klaxon. D: Transfer is not a merits penalty.
lb_rule_reference: In an ordinary plaintiff-chosen proper forum, a Section 1404 convenience transfer does not let the defendant obtain different state law.
