Question: A supplier wins a diversity breach-of-contract case. The governing state contract statute gives prevailing plaintiffs prejudgment interest from the date payment was due. The defendant argues that interest is a procedural matter for federal law only. Should the federal court apply the state prejudgment-interest rule?
Answer A: Yes, because it is part of the state-law remedy for the contract claim
Answer B: Yes, because all interest after filing is controlled by the jury
Answer C: No, because all interest questions are federal procedure
Answer D: No, because diversity jurisdiction eliminates state remedies
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Prejudgment interest on a state-law claim ordinarily follows state law in diversity because it affects the measure of recovery.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: The entitlement and legal standard come from the governing law; it is not automatically a free jury choice. C: Prejudgment interest compensates for the state-law injury and usually belongs to the substantive remedial package. D: Diversity changes the forum, not the state-law nature of the claim or remedy.
lb_rule_reference: In a diversity case, state law ordinarily governs entitlement to prejudgment interest on state-law claims.
