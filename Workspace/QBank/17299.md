Question: Deborah conveyed Cedaracre “to Miriam for life, then back to Deborah and her heirs.” Miriam is alive. How should Deborah's future interest be classified?
Answer A: A reversion, because the future interest is retained by the grantor after a life estate.
Answer B: A possibility of reverter, because the deed says the land will come back to Deborah.
Answer C: A shifting executory interest, because possession will shift from Miriam to Deborah.
Answer D: A right of entry, because Deborah must reenter when Miriam dies.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Deborah has a reversion. A future interest retained by the grantor after conveying a lesser estate, such as a life estate, is a reversion. The deed's phrase “then back to Deborah” makes the retained reversion express but does not change its classification.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because a possibility of reverter follows a fee simple determinable, not a life estate. C: C is wrong because an executory interest is held by a third party and cuts short another interest; Deborah is the grantor and Miriam's life estate ends naturally. D: D is wrong because no condition subsequent or right of entry is involved.
lb_rule_reference: Express reversion / grantor retained future interest
