Question: A buyer orders 300 blue blankets from a merchant seller. The seller has no blue blankets and promptly ships 300 red blankets with a note stating, “We are out of blue blankets; these red blankets are offered only as an accommodation.” The buyer rejects the red blankets and sues for breach of a contract to sell blue blankets. What is the seller's best formation defense?
Answer A: The shipment accepted the buyer's order and breached because the blankets were red.
Answer B: No contract could ever arise because the seller's shipment did not match the order.
Answer C: The nonconforming shipment with seasonable accommodation notice was a counteroffer, not an acceptance of the order.
Answer D: The seller accepted only because shipment is the sole possible method of acceptance for goods.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A nonconforming shipment does not constitute an acceptance if the seller seasonably notifies the buyer that the shipment is offered only as an accommodation. The accommodation shipment is a counteroffer.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A: that would be true without accommodation notice, but the seller gave seasonable notice.
