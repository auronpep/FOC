Question: An airport operated by a city bans in-person solicitation for immediate donations inside secure terminal areas. The rule applies to all organizations and viewpoints. Leafleting is still allowed in designated areas outside security. What forum analysis applies?
Answer A: The terminal is a traditional public forum because the city owns it
Answer B: The rule is judged for reasonableness and viewpoint neutrality in a nonpublic forum
Answer C: The rule is invalid unless it satisfies strict scrutiny
Answer D: The rule is valid even if it bans only one political side from soliciting
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Airport terminals are not traditional public forums, and the solicitation rule is viewpoint neutral.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Public ownership alone does not create a traditional public forum. C: Nonpublic forum restrictions use reasonableness and viewpoint neutrality, not automatic strict scrutiny. D: Even in a nonpublic forum, viewpoint discrimination is forbidden.
lb_rule_reference: In a nonpublic forum such as an airport terminal, speech restrictions must be reasonable and viewpoint neutral.
