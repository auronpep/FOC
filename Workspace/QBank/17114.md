Question: A city imposes a $500,000 forfeiture for a minor municipal offense. The defendant argues that the fine is excessive under the Eighth Amendment as applied to the city through the Fourteenth Amendment. What is the best incorporation route?
Answer A: The claim fails because the Excessive Fines Clause applies only to the federal government
Answer B: The claim can proceed through Fourteenth Amendment incorporation
Answer C: The claim belongs only under procedural due process notice rules
Answer D: The claim wins automatically because all large fines are unconstitutional
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The city is a state actor, and the right applies through the Fourteenth Amendment.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The Excessive Fines Clause is incorporated against states and local governments through Fourteenth Amendment Due Process. C: The challenge is to excessiveness, not lack of notice or hearing. D: Incorporation answers whether the right applies; excessiveness still requires analysis.
lb_rule_reference: The Excessive Fines Clause applies to the states through Fourteenth Amendment Due Process.
