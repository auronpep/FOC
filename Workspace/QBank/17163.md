Question: A state clerk accidentally misplaces a citizen's permit application fee while moving boxes between offices. The citizen sues under the Due Process Clause, alleging only negligence and a small property loss. What is the best due process answer?
Answer A: Every property loss by a state employee is a due process violation
Answer B: The Due Process Clause is not ordinarily violated by mere negligence
Answer C: Strict scrutiny applies because application fees burden travel
Answer D: The clerk must receive a hearing before the citizen can sue
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The claim alleges only accidental negligent conduct, not constitutionally deficient procedure or deliberate deprivation.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Negligent loss alone is not the kind of constitutional deprivation that due process targets. C: The stated injury is negligent fee loss, not a travel restriction. D: The question asks whether the citizen suffered a due-process violation, not what employment process the clerk gets.
lb_rule_reference: Mere negligent loss of property by a state official is not ordinarily a due process violation.
