


BARMATRIX Q#: 22036
Outline_code: 82010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Nature and Ownership of Land
Question: A tenant rented an apartment from a landlord. The apartment was located in an extremely hot state, so the lease agreement provided that the landlord was to provide air conditioning from May to September. In June, the air conditioner broke. Despite several calls and complaints from the tenant, there was no air conditioning in the apartment from the beginning of June. The tenant remained in the apartment although it was very uncomfortable. As of August 1, she was still in the apartment. The apartment was in a jurisdiction that followed the majority rule. Under the circumstances, what remedy does the tenant have?
Answer A: A: The tenant may not withhold the entire rent because she remained in the apartment.
Answer B: B: The tenant may receive a rent abatement because the landlord breached the covenant of quiet enjoyment.
Answer C: C: The tenant may receive a rent abatement because the landlord merely constructively evicted the tenant.
Answer D: D: The tenant may withhold the entire rent because she has been constructively evicted.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: If a landlord substantially and permanently interferes with the tenant's use and enjoyment of the property and the tenant moves out, the tenant may treat the lease as terminated and withhold all rent. Here, although the lack of air conditioning in a hot state was a substantial interference that affected the entire property, the tenant chose to stay, so she cannot now withhold rent. B and C are incorrect because the tenant would be entitled to withhold all rent if she had moved out. D is incorrect because the tenant did not move out.
