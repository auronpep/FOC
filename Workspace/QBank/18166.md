Question: A defendant discloses a retained engineering expert and serves the final expert report. The plaintiff requests every draft of the report and every email with defense counsel, arguing that expert materials are fully discoverable once the expert will testify. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: All draft reports and attorney-expert communications must be produced because the expert will testify
Answer B: Draft reports and many attorney communications are protected, though specified categories such as compensation, facts or data, and assumptions may be discoverable
Answer C: Nothing about a retained expert is discoverable before trial
Answer D: The plaintiff may obtain the drafts only by filing a Rule 11 motion
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The rule balances expert discovery with protection for attorney work product.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Expert testimony does not erase work-product protections for draft reports and many counsel communications. C: Retained testifying experts have disclosure and discovery obligations, including a final report. D: Rule 11 is a sanctions rule, not the device for ordinary expert discovery.
lb_rule_reference: Draft expert reports and many attorney communications with retained experts are protected, subject to specific exceptions such as compensation, facts or data considered, and assumptions provided.
