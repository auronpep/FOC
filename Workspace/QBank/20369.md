Question: Abraham conveyed Cedaracre “to Sarah for life.” Abraham retained the reversion. Sarah later validly bought Abraham's reversion. No other person held any interest in Cedaracre. What estate does Sarah have after buying the reversion?
Answer A: A life estate only, because the original deed permanently limited her estate.
Answer B: A fee simple determinable, because her possession was originally limited by life.
Answer C: Fee simple absolute, because Sarah's life estate and Abraham's reversion merged.
Answer D: A tenancy in common with Abraham, because Abraham was the original grantor.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Sarah has fee simple absolute. Abraham retained the reversion after conveying a life estate. When Sarah later acquired that reversion and no other interests existed, the successive interests merged into fee simple absolute.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Sarah acquired the future interest that followed her life estate. B: B is wrong because no durational land-use limitation exists. D: D is wrong because Abraham conveyed the reversion to Sarah and retained no co-ownership interest.
lb_rule_reference: Estate merger / life estate plus reversion
