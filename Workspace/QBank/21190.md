Outline: Contingent and vested remainders
Question: Isaac conveyed Gardenacre “to Leah for life, then to Rachel if Rachel graduates from college before Leah dies, but if Rachel does not graduate before Leah dies, then to Joseph.” Leah is alive, and Rachel has not yet graduated. How should Rachel's and Joseph's interests be classified?
Answer A: Rachel has a shifting executory interest, and Joseph has a springing executory interest.
Answer B: Rachel has a vested remainder subject to open, and Joseph has no interest.
Answer C: Rachel has a possibility of reverter, and Joseph has a right of entry.
Answer D: Rachel and Joseph have alternative contingent remainders.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Rachel and Joseph have alternative contingent remainders. Both interests wait until the natural end of Leah's life estate. Rachel takes if she graduates before Leah dies; Joseph takes if Rachel does not. Neither interest cuts short Leah's life estate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because neither interest divests a prior fee; both follow a life estate. B: B is wrong because Rachel has not satisfied the condition precedent, and Joseph has an express alternative interest. C: C is wrong because possibilities of reverter and rights of entry are grantor future interests, not interests in Rachel or Joseph.
