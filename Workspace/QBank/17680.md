Question: A town denies a group home a routine permit after officials say at the hearing that the town wants to keep "those people" away and identifies no safety, zoning, or budget reason for the denial. What is the strongest Equal Protection argument?
Answer A: The denial is valid because rational basis means no review at all
Answer B: The denial can fail rational basis if it rests only on bare animus toward the group
Answer C: Strict scrutiny applies automatically because the group is unpopular
Answer D: The denial is valid if any resident dislikes the group
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Animus is not a legitimate government interest, even under rational basis.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Deferential review still requires a legitimate government interest. C: Animus can defeat rational basis without converting the group into a suspect class. D: Bare desire to harm or exclude an unpopular group is not legitimate.
lb_rule_reference: Even under rational basis review, a law based only on bare animus toward an unpopular group lacks a legitimate government interest.
