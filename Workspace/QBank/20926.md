Outline: Delivery and acceptance
Question: Jacob signed a deed conveying Fieldacre to Rachel and gave it to Eli, a neutral escrow holder. Jacob instructed Eli in writing to deliver the deed to Rachel only if Rachel paid the full purchase price by June 1, and Jacob retained no right to retrieve the deed. Rachel paid the full price on June 1, and Eli delivered the deed to her. Who owns Fieldacre?
Answer A: Jacob, because Rachel did not physically receive the deed when Jacob first signed it.
Answer B: Rachel, because Jacob placed the deed beyond his control with a valid escrow condition and the condition was satisfied.
Answer C: Rachel from the moment Jacob handed the deed to Eli, even before Rachel paid the price.
Answer D: Jacob, because a deed can never be delivered through an escrow holder.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source is a deed, and the decisive delivery lock is escrow delivery beyond the grantor's control. Jacob gave the deed to a neutral holder with written instructions and no retained right to reclaim it. Rachel satisfied the condition, so the deed was delivered and title passed to Rachel.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because physical receipt by the grantee at signing is not required when a valid escrow delivery is used. C: C is wrong because the escrow condition delayed delivery until payment. D: D is wrong because third-party escrow delivery can be effective.
lb_rule_reference: Conditional delivery to an independent third-party escrow holder becomes effective when the condition is satisfied
