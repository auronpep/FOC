


BARMATRIX Q#: 22121
Outline_code: 82010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Nature and Ownership of Land
Question: A woman entered into an oral lease with a landlord to rent a house for one year for $1,000 per month. After the year was over, the woman failed to move out. Instead, she simply sent a rent check for $1,000 to her landlord. When the landlord got the check, he deposited it in his bank account. Which of the following statements is most accurate regarding the tenancy?
Answer A: A: The parties have created a periodic month- to- month tenancy.
Answer B: B: The woman is a tenant at sufferance who could be evicted immediately.
Answer C: C: The woman is an at- will tenant who may terminate the tenancy at will.
Answer D: D: The woman is an at- will tenant and either the woman or the landlord may terminate the tenancy immediately.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A periodic tenancy may be created expressly or by implication. Once the landlord accepted one- month rent, he created a periodic month- to- month tenancy by implication. B is incorrect because a tenancy at sufferance occurs when a tenant holds over but fails to pay rent. At that point, the landlord may recover possession and receive its reasonable rental value from the tenant. Here, the woman sent in one- month rent that was accepted by the landlord. This created a periodic tenancy, not a tenancy at sufferance. C and D are incorrect because an at- will tenancy has no fixed duration. Here, the acceptance of the one- month rent check means that the tenancy was for at least a month.
