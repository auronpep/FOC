Question: Miriam conveyed Ironacre “to Aaron and his heirs on condition that the land is not used as a factory; if the condition is breached, Miriam may reenter and retake the premises.” Aaron built a factory on the land. Miriam has not entered, sued, or otherwise acted to terminate Aaron's estate. Who owns Ironacre now?
Answer A: Miriam, because Aaron breached the condition.
Answer B: Aaron and Miriam as tenants in common until a court divides the land.
Answer C: No one, because the deed creates a forfeiture but Miriam has not acted.
Answer D: Aaron, subject to Miriam's power of termination.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Aaron still owns Ironacre, subject to Miriam's power of termination. A fee simple subject to condition subsequent does not end automatically upon breach. Miriam must exercise the right of entry to terminate Aaron's estate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because breach alone does not transfer title under a condition subsequent. B: B is wrong because the deed creates successive defeasible rights, not co-ownership. C: C is wrong because the law does not leave the fee ownerless; Aaron remains owner until termination is exercised.
lb_rule_reference: Condition subsequent / owner before exercise
