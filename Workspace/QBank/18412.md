Question: A 10-year-old rode a bicycle on a neighborhood sidewalk and accidentally bumped a pedestrian while turning around a tree. The child was not operating a motorized vehicle or other adult activity. Which standard of care applies to the child?
Answer A: The adult reasonable-person standard because all negligence defendants are adults in the law's eyes
Answer B: The care of a child of like age, intelligence, and experience
Answer C: The child's own personal best effort, regardless of other children
Answer D: No negligence standard applies to any child under 12
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Riding a nonmotorized bicycle on a sidewalk is an ordinary child activity, so the child standard applies.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Children in ordinary child activities generally receive a child standard. C: The child standard is still objective within the child's age, intelligence, and experience. D: The fact pattern does not provide a blanket immunity rule; the standard is the child standard.
lb_rule_reference: A child engaged in ordinary child activity is usually judged by the care of a child of like age, intelligence, and experience.
