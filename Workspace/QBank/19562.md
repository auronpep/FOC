Question: Rachel leaves a written note for Joshua: “If you clean the well before sunset, I will pay you $600. You need not send me any notice; I will inspect the well tomorrow.” Joshua reads the note and cleans the well before sunset. Rachel later refuses to pay, arguing that Joshua never notified her he had accepted. What is the best result?
Answer A: Joshua accepted by completing the requested performance, and Rachel waived any notice requirement in the offer.
Answer B: No contract formed because unilateral contracts always require separate notice after performance.
Answer C: No contract formed because Joshua did not promise in advance to clean the well.
Answer D: No contract formed because Rachel's note was a gift promise rather than an exchange.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Rachel invited acceptance by performance and expressly stated that no notice was required. Joshua knew of the offer and completed the requested act before the deadline.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because notice may be waived by the offer or unnecessary when the offer so provides. C: C is wrong because the offer did not ask for a return promise. D: D is wrong because payment was sought in exchange for cleaning the well.
lb_rule_reference: common_law
lb_student_diagnostic: Student imposed a notice requirement despite the offer's express waiver.
