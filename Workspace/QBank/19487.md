Question: Benjamin sends a merchant seller an order for 100 identical clay jars at $20 each, requesting prompt shipment. The seller immediately ships 100 conforming jars. Before the jars arrive, Benjamin tries to cancel, arguing that the seller never sent a written acceptance. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The seller accepted by promptly shipping conforming goods.
Answer B: No contract was formed because the seller did not send a mirror-image written acceptance.
Answer C: No contract was formed because shipment is only a promise to accept later.
Answer D: The shipment was a counteroffer that Benjamin was free to reject without consequence.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Under Article 2, an offer to buy goods for prompt or current shipment is ordinarily accepted by prompt shipment of conforming goods. The seller's conforming shipment accepted and performed.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B: Article 2 does not require a separate written mirror-image acceptance in this setting. C: shipment is an acceptance mode, not merely a future promise.
