


BARMATRIX Q#: 22327
Outline_code: 74020101
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Homicide
Outline: Murder
Question: A high school student thought it would be a funny prank to spike the football team's water with a nausea- inducing chemical so they'd all throw up on the field. She thought doing so would draw attention to the misogyny and toxicity of high school football culture. Despite carefully reading the recommended dosage from the package, the student mismeasured the amount she should put in the water cooler and put in much more than was safe. A football player walked up to the cooler and took a sip. The player had a severe peanut allergy, and the chemical the student used was made from peanuts. The player died later at the hospital. The student was charged with first- degree murder. May the student be convicted of first- degree murder?
Answer A: A: Yes, because she deliberately put the chemical in the water cooler intending the players to drink it.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the player's death occurred during perpetration of a felony.
Answer C: C: No, because she acted not with intent to kill but with an abandoned and malignant heart.
Answer D: D: No, because she did not intend to kill anyone.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Depending on the seriousness of the wrongdoing, a defendant who engages in wrongful conduct that results in a homicide is guilty of either involuntary homicide or seconddegree murder. Involuntary homicide is appropriate when the wrongful conduct did not create a very high risk of death or serious injury. Here, poisoning the water to make the players sick was unlikely to cause death or serious injury. In fact, the player died because of a peanut allergy, not through the actions of the chemical itself. A is incorrect because the student did not intend to kill anyone. B is incorrect because the student was not engaging in an inherently dangerous felony. Even if she was, such a murder is traditionally second- degree murder. C is incorrect because the poisoning did not create a high risk of death or serious injury, so it could not be second- degree murder under abandoned and malignant heart.
