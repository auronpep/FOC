Outline: Receipt of stolen property
Question: A seller in an alley offers Dario a new laptop for $40, says "it came from a break-in," and shows that the serial number has been scratched off. Dario buys the laptop and takes it home. Is Dario guilty of receiving stolen property?
Answer A: Yes, because he received control while knowing or believing the laptop was stolen
Answer B: No, because a low price is never relevant to knowledge
Answer C: No, because only the original thief can commit a property offense
Answer D: No, unless Dario later sells the laptop
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The seller's statement and removed serial number show knowledge at the time Dario took control.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Here the low price is paired with direct stolen-property facts. C: Receiving stolen property is a separate offense for later knowing receipt. D: The offense is complete upon knowing receipt and control.
lb_rule_reference: A defendant who receives control over stolen property while knowing or believing it is stolen commits receiving stolen property.
