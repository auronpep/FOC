Outline: Fourth Amendment
Question: At a bus station, an officer asks Mina if she will answer questions. Mina agrees and hands over her driver's license when asked. The officer keeps the license, steps in front of Mina's path, and asks to search her bag. The officer has no reasonable suspicion. When the officer kept Mina's license and blocked her path, what occurred?
Answer A: A seizure occurred because a reasonable person would not feel free to leave without the license
Answer B: No seizure occurred because Mina originally agreed to speak
Answer C: No seizure occurred because the officer did not use handcuffs
Answer D: A seizure occurred only after the officer physically searched the bag
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Retaining identification and blocking departure transformed the encounter into a seizure. Without reasonable suspicion, the detention was unlawful.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: A consensual encounter can become a seizure when circumstances change and police restrain liberty. C: A seizure can occur through show of authority without physical force. D: The detention began earlier when Mina could not reasonably leave.
lb_rule_reference: Retaining a person's identification or travel documents while questioning can make a reasonable person feel not free to leave, creating a seizure that requires justification.
