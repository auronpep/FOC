Question: Moses orally agreed to manage David's vineyard for exactly two years beginning the next day. David later refused to employ Moses and asserted the Statute of Frauds. Moses argues that the contract should be enforceable because he could have quit or been fired within one year. Which statement is correct?
Answer A: The contract is outside the Statute of Frauds because either party might have breached within one year.
Answer B: The contract is within the Statute of Frauds because, by its terms, full performance required two years.
Answer C: The contract is outside the Statute of Frauds because it was for services rather than land.
Answer D: The contract is enforceable only if Moses can show that David benefited from the first year of work.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A fixed two-year service contract cannot be fully performed within one year by its terms. The possibility of breach, firing, or quitting is not full performance. Without a sufficient writing or exception, the Statute of Frauds can make the contract unenforceable.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because breach or early termination is not full performance of the promised two-year term. C: C is wrong because non-land contracts can still fall within the one-year category. D: D is wrong because the facts say David refused before performance; benefit from a year of work is not the stated issue.
lb_rule_reference: Common-law one-year provision
