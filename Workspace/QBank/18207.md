Question: Moses conveyed Pastureacre “to Miriam for life, then to Aaron if Aaron survives Miriam.” Miriam and Aaron are both alive. What future interest does Aaron have?
Answer A: A shifting executory interest, because Aaron's possession is not immediate.
Answer B: A contingent remainder, because Aaron must survive Miriam before taking possession.
Answer C: A possibility of reverter, because Pastureacre might return automatically.
Answer D: A right of entry, because Aaron must satisfy a condition.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Aaron has a contingent remainder. His interest follows Miriam's life estate and does not cut short her estate; it waits for the natural expiration of the life estate. The survival requirement is a condition precedent, so the remainder is contingent.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because an executory interest cuts short a prior estate, and Aaron's interest does not cut short Miriam's life estate. C: C is wrong because a possibility of reverter is retained by a grantor after a fee simple determinable. D: D is wrong because a right of entry belongs to a grantor after a fee simple subject to condition subsequent.
