MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Service of Process
Question: A truck driver, who was a citizen of State A, sued a corporation and one of its managers in federal court in State B. The corporation was located and incorporated in State B and the manager was a citizen of State B. What would be the best method of serving the corporation and the manager?
Answer A: A: Service by the truck driver himself personally giving the summons and complaint to the chief executive officer of the corporation and the manager.
Answer B: B: Service by emailing copies of the summons and complaint to the corporation and the manager.
Answer C: C: Service by leaving copies of the summons and complaint at the offices of the chief executive officer of the corporation and the manager.
Answer D: D: The service required by State B's rules of civil procedure.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Fed. R. Civ. P. 4 contains the requirements for service of process. A is incorrect because Fed. R. Civ. P. 4(c)(2) prohibits a party from serving the complaint, and the truck driver is a party. B is incorrect because Fed. R. Civ. P. 4 does not allow service by email. C is incorrect because Fed. R. Civ. P. 4(e) does not allow an individual to be served at his or her office, and the manager is being sued as an individual. Consequently, D is the correct answer.
