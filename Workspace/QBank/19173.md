Outline: Requirements for valid deed
Question: Boaz signed a deed conveying his vineyard to Ruth and delivered it to her. The deed identified Boaz, Ruth, and the vineyard, and used present words of transfer. Ruth accepted the deed but never signed it. Ruth also never paid the one dollar recited in the deed. The deed was not recorded. Who owns the vineyard?
Answer A: Boaz, because Ruth did not sign the deed.
Answer B: Boaz, because Ruth did not pay the recited consideration.
Answer C: Ruth, because the deed was signed by the grantor, delivered, and accepted.
Answer D: Boaz, because an unrecorded deed is invalid even between the parties.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source is a deed, and the decisive validity gate is execution by the grantor, not signature by the grantee. Boaz signed and delivered a deed with the required terms, and Ruth accepted it. Lack of Ruth's signature, lack of consideration, and lack of recording do not prevent title from passing between the parties.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the grantee usually does not have to sign a deed. B: B is wrong because consideration is not required for a valid deed. D: D is wrong because recording is a priority and notice device, not a validity requirement between Boaz and Ruth.
