Question: In federal court, a defendant serves a request asking the plaintiff to admit that a key contract was signed on March 1. The plaintiff admits it. At summary judgment, the plaintiff tries to argue that the contract was signed on April 1, saying admissions are only discovery evidence. What is the effect of the admission?
Answer A: It is conclusively established in the action unless the court permits withdrawal or amendment
Answer B: It is merely impeachment evidence that the jury may accept or reject
Answer C: It automatically becomes nonbinding if the plaintiff later serves a corrected response
Answer D: It binds the plaintiff only if the defendant separately proves reliance
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 36 admissions are binding for the action unless withdrawn or amended with court permission.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: A Rule 36 admission conclusively establishes the matter in the action. C: Withdrawal or amendment of an admission requires the court's permission. D: The conclusive effect follows from the admission itself unless withdrawn or amended.
lb_rule_reference: A matter admitted under Rule 36 is conclusively established in the action unless the court permits withdrawal or amendment.
