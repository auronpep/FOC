Question: Priya lost a federal securities-fraud action after a final judgment on the merits. She later files another federal action arising from the same stock sale. The defendant asks the court to decide the preclusive effect of the earlier federal securities judgment. Which law generally governs the preclusive effect of the earlier judgment?
Answer A: Federal common law, because the earlier judgment was a federal-question judgment
Answer B: The law of whichever state Priya prefers
Answer C: The law of the forum state automatically, because all preclusion is state law
Answer D: No law applies until the same judge hears both cases
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Prior federal-question judgments get preclusive effect under federal preclusion principles.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: The plaintiff does not choose the preclusion law by preference. C: Federal-question judgments are generally governed by federal common law for preclusion. D: Preclusion does not depend on having the same judge.
lb_rule_reference: The preclusive effect of a prior federal-question judgment is governed by federal common law.
