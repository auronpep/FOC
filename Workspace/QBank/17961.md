Question: A state requires public-school teachers who teach civics and exercise significant discretion over classroom instruction about government to be citizens. A lawful permanent resident challenges the requirement. What is the state's best Equal Protection argument?
Answer A: The political-function exception may allow the state to reserve certain self-governance roles for citizens
Answer B: All state alienage classifications receive rational basis
Answer C: The exception applies to every public job
Answer D: Strict scrutiny is automatically fatal even if the position shapes democratic governance
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Some public positions tied to democratic governance can be limited to citizens.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: State alienage classifications generally trigger strict scrutiny unless an exception applies. C: The exception is limited to positions tied to democratic self-governance. D: The political-function exception can alter the analysis.
lb_rule_reference: States may reserve certain positions intimately related to democratic self-governance for citizens under the political-function exception.
