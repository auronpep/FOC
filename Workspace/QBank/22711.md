Question: A state passed a law that no farm in the state could be sold to a foreign national. A resident alien wanted to buy a large cabbage farm in the state. The resident alien filed suit, seeking a declaration that the law was unconstitutional. Which of the following statements is true?
Answer A: A: The state has the burden of showing a compelling state interest for the law.
Answer B: B: The state has the burden of showing a rational basis for the law.
Answer C: C: The resident alien has the burden of showing there is no compelling state interest for the law.
Answer D: D: The resident alien has the burden of showing there is no rational basis for the law.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Because alienage is a suspect class, state discrimination against aliens will be subject to strict scrutiny. Consequently, the burden is on the state to show that the law is necessary to achieve a compelling state interest. B, C, and D are therefore incorrect.
