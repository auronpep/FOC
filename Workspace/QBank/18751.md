Question: A city revokes a restaurant license after a hearing. The hearing officer is paid a bonus for each license revoked and previously announced that the restaurant "must be shut down." The city argues that notice and a hearing occurred, so due process is satisfied. What is the best due process objection?
Answer A: Due process is satisfied whenever the government gives notice
Answer B: The restaurant has no protected interest because licenses can be revoked
Answer C: A biased decisionmaker can violate due process even when notice was given
Answer D: The issue is substantive due process because restaurants are fundamental rights
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Financial incentives and prejudgment can undermine the neutral adjudication required for a protected interest.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Notice is not enough if the adjudicator is biased. B: A license revocable only under standards can be protected property. D: Restaurant operation is not a fundamental right; the problem is procedural fairness.
lb_rule_reference: Due process requires a neutral decisionmaker when the government adjudicates protected interests.
