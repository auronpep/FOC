Question: Congress adopts a rule governing eligibility of noncitizens for a federal immigration-related benefit. A lawful permanent resident argues that the federal rule must receive the same strict scrutiny that applies to most state alienage classifications. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Federal alienage classifications tied to immigration receive deferential review
Answer B: Strict scrutiny applies because all alienage classifications are treated the same
Answer C: Intermediate scrutiny applies because alienage is quasi-suspect
Answer D: No Equal Protection principle applies to federal action
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Congress has special authority over immigration and naturalization.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: The actor matters; federal immigration-related classifications receive deference. C: Alienage doctrine uses different routes for state and federal actors. D: Equal-protection principles apply to federal action through the Fifth Amendment's Due Process Clause.
lb_rule_reference: Federal alienage classifications tied to immigration and naturalization receive deferential review rather than the strict scrutiny generally applied to state alienage classifications.
