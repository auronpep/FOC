


BARMATRIX Q#: 22297
Outline_code: 75090400
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Constitutional Protection of Accused Persons (Criminal Procedure)
Outline: Right to Counsel
Question: A man was arrested after stealing a smartphone and shooting a security guard and a bystander. A court initially found that the man was mentally incompetent, but after five years of psychiatric treatment, the state put the man on trial for murder. The man asked to represent himself at trial. The request was initially granted, but it was overturned after the man filed several rambling and irrelevant documents. The man was given an attorney and ultimately convicted on all counts. After the man was convicted, he appealed, arguing that his right to self- representation had been unlawfully taken away by the trial court. He pointed out that he was literate and understood the charges against him. The trial court defended itself by saying the man suffered from a severe mental illness to the point that he was not competent to conduct the trial proceeding by himself. Did the trial court unlawfully take away the man's right to self- representation?
Answer A: A: No, because the man was not competent to defend himself.
Answer B: B: No, because the man was on trial for murder.
Answer C: C: Yes, because the man voluntarily and intelligently elected to represent himself.
Answer D: D: Yes, because the man was literate and understood the charges against him.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A defendant has the right to represent himself or herself at trial so long as the defendant is competent to proceed pro se and the defendant makes a knowing and intelligent waiver. Indiana v. Edwards, 554 U.S. 135 (2008); Faretta v. California, 422 U.S. 806 (1975). Here, the man could not represent himself because the man suffered a mental illness to the point that he was not competent to conduct his defense. B is incorrect because a person on trial for murder still has a Sixth Amendment right to defend himself or herself. C and D are incorrect because the man was not competent to defend himself.
