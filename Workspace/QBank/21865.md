Question: Rachel agreed to buy Joseph's small farm. The contract required closing on July 1, with Rachel to tender the purchase price and Joseph to deliver the deed at the same time. On July 1, Joseph arrived at the closing office with the signed deed. Rachel did not appear and did not send payment. Rachel later sued Joseph for failing to transfer the deed. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Joseph breached because a seller must deliver the deed before the buyer pays.
Answer B: Rachel may recover because concurrent conditions are treated as independent promises.
Answer C: Joseph did not breach because Rachel failed to tender the concurrent performance required to mature Joseph's duty to convey.
Answer D: Joseph breached unless he mailed the deed to Rachel before the closing deadline.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: In a land sale closing, payment and deed delivery are typically concurrent conditions. Joseph's duty to convey did not mature when Rachel failed to appear and tender payment at the agreed closing.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because deed delivery and payment were concurrent, not sequential with seller first. B: B is wrong because concurrent conditions are not independent promises. D: D is wrong because the contract required closing performance, not pre-closing mail delivery.
lb_rule_reference: Concurrent conditions / land closing
