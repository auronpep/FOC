Question: Elizabeth orally told John that he could use a lane across her land to reach his field. The next morning, before John used the lane or spent any money improving it, Elizabeth told John that she had changed her mind and revoked permission. What is the best result?
Answer A: John has no easement by estoppel because Elizabeth revoked before John relied on the permission.
Answer B: John has an irrevocable easement because all oral permission to use land is binding for life.
Answer C: John has a prescriptive easement because the permission lasted overnight.
Answer D: John owns the lane because Elizabeth once mentioned that he could use it.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: An ordinary license is revocable unless reliance or another doctrine makes revocation inequitable. Elizabeth revoked before John used the lane or made expenditures in reliance on her permission.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because oral permission is not automatically irrevocable. C: C is wrong because prescription requires adverse use for the statutory period. D: D is wrong because permission to use does not convey title.
lb_rule_reference: Revocation before reliance
lb_student_diagnostic: The repair signal is applying estoppel without a reliance event.
