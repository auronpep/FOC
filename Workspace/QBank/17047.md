Question: Boaz granted Naomi, as owner of Shop Lot, “the right to park two passenger cars in the two marked spaces beside the rear entrance.” Naomi later began parking six customer cars in the open area around the marked spaces during business hours. Boaz objected because the additional cars blocked deliveries to his building. What is the best result?
Answer A: Boaz may stop the additional parking because the express easement is limited to two marked spaces.
Answer B: Naomi may use the entire rear area because a parking easement is never limited by the number of spaces stated in the grant.
Answer C: Naomi may park any number of cars if the cars belong to customers rather than employees.
Answer D: Boaz may terminate Naomi's entire easement because any excessive parking automatically ends the easement.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The grant expressly identifies the number and location of parking spaces. Naomi may use the two marked spaces, but using the broader rear area for six customer cars materially exceeds the express scope.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because express limits control. C: C is wrong because customer status does not expand the easement. D: D is wrong because misuse may be enjoined without automatically terminating the entire easement.
lb_rule_reference: Express parking easement / numerical limit
