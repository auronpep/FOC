Outline: Fee simple absolute
Question: Naomi conveyed Grayacre “to Ruth until the premises cease to be used as a bakery.” Ruth continues to operate a bakery on the premises. What interests exist now?
Answer A: Ruth has a fee simple absolute because the limitation has not yet been violated.
Answer B: Ruth has a fee simple determinable, and Naomi has a possibility of reverter.
Answer C: Ruth has a life estate, and Naomi has a reversion.
Answer D: Ruth has a fee simple subject to condition subsequent, and Naomi has a right of entry.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The word “until” is durational language. Ruth has a fee simple determinable, and Naomi has a possibility of reverter. Current compliance prevents termination for now, but it does not transform Ruth's estate into a fee simple absolute.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because a defeasible fee remains defeasible before breach. C: C is wrong because the deed is not measured by anyone's life. D: D is wrong because the deed uses durational language, not conditional language with a reserved right of entry.
lb_rule_reference: Fee simple determinable / interest before breach
