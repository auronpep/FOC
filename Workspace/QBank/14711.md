Question: In which of the following cases is the defendant most likely to be convicted if she is charged with receiving stolen property?
Answer A: A: The defendant bought a car from a man, who operates a used car lot. Before the purchase, the man told the defendant that the car had been stolen, which was true. Unknown to the defendant, the man is an undercover police agent who is operating the lot in cooperation with the police in exchange for leniency in connection with criminal charges pending against him.
Answer B: B: The defendant bought a car from a man. Before the purchase, the man told the defendant that the car was stolen. The man had stolen the car with the help of his friend, who, unknown to the defendant or the man, was an undercover police agent who feigned cooperation with the man in the theft of the car.
Answer C: C: The defendant bought a car from a man. Before the purchase, the man told the defendant that the car was stolen. Unknown to the defendant, the man had stolen the car from a parking lot and had been caught by the police as he was driving it away. He agreed to cooperate with the police and carry through with his prearranged sale of the car to the defendant.
Answer D: D: The defendant bought a car from a man. Before the purchase, the man told the defendant that the car was stolen. Unknown to the defendant, the man was in fact the owner of the car but had reported it as stolen and had collected on a fraudulent claim of its theft from his insurance company.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: B is correct. Here, a stolen car is sold to the defendant after he is told of its stolen status. The fact that the friend, who had helped the man steal the car, was an undercover cop does not change the "stolen" status of the car. Therefore, this is the situation in which the defendant is most likely to be convicted.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect. In this answer choice, the car was stolen but subsequently recovered by the police before the sale to the man, thus losing its status as a stolen item at the time the defendant purchased it. C is incorrect. The car was stolen here but subsequently recovered by the police before the sale to the man, thus losing its "stolen" status at the time the defendant received it. D is incorrect. In this answer choice, the property was never stolen.
