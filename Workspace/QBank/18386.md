Question: In a federal prosecution, the government designates Miriam, the lead investigative agent, to sit at counsel table. Miriam is expected to testify later about the investigation. The defendant asks that Miriam be excluded with all other witnesses. The prosecutor states that Miriam is the government's designated representative. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Miriam must be excluded because a testifying agent is never exempt from sequestration.
Answer B: Miriam may remain as the government's designated representative for the party, even though she may testify.
Answer C: Miriam may remain only if the defendant is allowed to choose a second government representative to exclude.
Answer D: Miriam must testify before opening statements if she wants to remain in the courtroom.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A party that is not a natural person may have one designated officer or employee representative remain. Government investigative agents are commonly treated within the designated-representative exception when properly designated.
