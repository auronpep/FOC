Question: Before large-scale document production in federal court, Sarah obtained a court order stating that disclosure of privileged or work-product material in that litigation would not waive protection in any federal or state proceeding. A privileged memo was later produced during the review. What is the effect of the court order?
Answer A: The disclosure is not a waiver if it falls within the Rule 502(d) order's terms.
Answer B: The order binds only the parties and has no effect in later proceedings.
Answer C: The order protects attorney-client privilege but not work product.
Answer D: The order is ineffective unless Sarah also proves the production was inadvertent under Rule 502(b).
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: FRE 502(d) allows a federal court to order that disclosure connected with the pending litigation does not waive attorney-client privilege or work-product protection, and that protection carries to other federal or state proceedings. The decisive axis is court-ordered nonwaiver, not ordinary inadvertence analysis.
