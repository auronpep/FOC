Outline: Attorney-client
Question: Reuben is charged with securities fraud. Hoping for leniency, Reuben tells the prosecutor, “I am willing to tell you what I told my lawyer, Abigail, about the transactions.” Before any proffer session occurs and before Reuben reveals the substance of any communication with Abigail, Reuben withdraws his cooperation offer. The prosecutor then subpoenas Abigail and argues that Reuben waived attorney-client privilege by offering to disclose the communications. Was the attorney-client privilege waived by Reuben's uncompleted cooperation offer?
Answer A: No. An uncompleted offer or intent to disclose does not waive the privileged attorney-client communications when no significant part of the communication was actually disclosed.
Answer B: Yes. Stating a willingness to cooperate with a prosecutor waives all attorney-client communications on the proposed subject.
Answer C: Yes. Because a prosecutor was involved, the conversation became a plea negotiation and attorney-client privilege no longer applies.
Answer D: No, but only because Reuben's statement to the prosecutor would be hearsay if Abigail testified about it.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Waiver generally requires actual disclosure of privileged material or another effective waiver act. Reuben expressed willingness to disclose what he told his lawyer, but he withdrew before any significant privileged communication was revealed. The decisive axis is waiver completion. Plea/cooperation labels and hearsay concepts do not substitute for an actual disclosure of the attorney-client communication.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because intent or willingness to waive is not the same as actual disclosure or effective waiver. C: C is wrong because prosecutor involvement does not automatically erase attorney-client privilege. Plea-negotiation rules and attorney-client waiver are separate issues. D: D is wrong because hearsay is not the reason the lawyer's testimony is blocked. The privilege remains because no waiver occurred.
lb_rule_reference: Waiver generally requires actual disclosure or effective waiver act
lb_student_diagnostic: Student confuses waiver intent with a completed waiver act.
