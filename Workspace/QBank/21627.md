Question: Isaac conveyed Vineyardacre “to Jacob and his heirs so long as the land is used as a vineyard, and if the land is not so used, then to Rachel and her heirs.” Jacob is still using the land as a vineyard. What future interest does Isaac retain?
Answer A: A possibility of reverter, because the deed uses “so long as.”
Answer B: A right of entry, because Jacob's estate is conditional.
Answer C: A reversion, because Isaac is the grantor.
Answer D: No future interest; Rachel has the future interest as an executory interest.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Isaac retains no future interest. Although “so long as” is durational language, the deed gives the future interest to Rachel, a third party. Jacob has a fee simple subject to executory limitation, and Rachel has a shifting executory interest.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the future interest is not retained by Isaac. B: B is wrong because the deed does not reserve a power of termination in Isaac. C: C is wrong because Isaac conveyed the future interest to Rachel rather than retaining the remaining estate.
