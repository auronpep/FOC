Question: In a federal contract trial, Rebekah offers a photocopy of a signed services agreement to prove the price term. Isaac objects, “Only the original can prove contract contents.” Isaac does not dispute authenticity and identifies no unfairness. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because copies can never prove contract contents.
Answer B: Sustain the objection unless Rebekah proves the original was lost.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because a duplicate is admissible to the same extent as the original absent a genuine authenticity question or unfairness.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if the jury is instructed that the copy is not evidence.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 1003 generally permits a duplicate to the same extent as the original unless a genuine question about authenticity or unfairness exists. A lost-original showing is not required for an admissible duplicate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A and B ignore the duplicate rule. D is wrong because the admitted duplicate is evidence. B: A and B ignore the duplicate rule. D is wrong because the admitted duplicate is evidence. D: D is wrong because the admitted duplicate is evidence.
