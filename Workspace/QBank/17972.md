Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Specific and General Jurisdiction
Question: A Vermont collector bought a rare map from a Montana dealer after one email exchange. The dealer listed the map at an in-person Montana antiques fair. The collector initiated the email, paid from Vermont, and arranged for a third-party courier to pick up the map in Montana. The dealer did not advertise in Vermont, ship to Vermont, or have other Vermont contacts. The collector sued the dealer in Vermont for breach of warranty. Is the single remote sale enough for specific jurisdiction in Vermont?
Answer A: Yes, because any contract with a Vermont resident is purposeful availment of Vermont
Answer B: Yes, because the collector paid from a Vermont bank account
Answer C: No, because the facts show a buyer-initiated Montana transaction with no meaningful Vermont-directed conduct by the dealer
Answer D: No, because warranty claims cannot be litigated outside the seller's home state
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A single contract with a forum resident is not enough when the defendant did not target, ship to, negotiate in, or perform in the forum.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The buyer initiated the contact and took pickup in Montana. The dealer did not create meaningful Vermont contacts. B: The buyer's unilateral payment source is not the dealer's purposeful forum contact. D: Warranty claims can be heard in a forum with defendant-created suit-related contacts.
lb_rule_reference: Contract-based PJ looks to the whole relationship; the resident status of the counterparty alone does not establish purposeful availment.
