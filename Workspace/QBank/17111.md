MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Torts
Subtopic: Other Torts
Outline: Defamation
Question: A caller falsely told a podcast host that a local nurse stole medication from patients. The host had no reliable source for the claim but repeated it on the next episode, saying, "A caller says this nurse is stealing medication." The accusation was false. Can the nurse treat the podcast host as a publisher of the statement?
Answer A: Yes, because repeating the accusation to listeners is republication
Answer B: No, because the host used the words "a caller says."
Answer C: No, because podcasts are not publications
Answer D: Yes, but only if the caller is also sued
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Attribution to a caller does not automatically insulate the host from liability for republishing the false charge.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Repeating defamatory content with attribution can still be publication. C: Publication means communication to third parties, regardless of medium. D: A plaintiff can sue a republisher without joining the original speaker.
lb_rule_reference: One who repeats or republishes a defamatory statement can be liable as a publisher even if the statement is attributed to someone else.
