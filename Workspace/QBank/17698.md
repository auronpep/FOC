Outline: Scope of discovery
Question: The plaintiff retains an engineer to testify that a machine was defectively designed. Before the expert-disclosure deadline, the plaintiff identifies the engineer by name but refuses to provide a written report, saying the defendant may depose the engineer later. What should the court require?
Answer A: Nothing more, because identifying the expert by name satisfies the disclosure obligation
Answer B: A written report with the expert's opinions, bases, facts considered, exhibits, qualifications, prior testimony, and compensation
Answer C: No report, unless the expert was consulted before the complaint was filed
Answer D: Production of all communications between the expert and counsel without limitation
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 26 requires a detailed report for retained experts who will testify.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A retained testifying expert must provide the required written report. C: The report requirement turns on testifying retained-expert status, not prefiling consultation. D: The rule requires specific expert disclosures and protects many attorney-expert communications and draft reports.
lb_rule_reference: A retained expert who will testify must provide a written report containing the required opinions, bases, facts, exhibits, qualifications, prior testimony, and compensation information.
