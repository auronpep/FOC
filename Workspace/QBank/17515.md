Question: Gideon conveyed Homeacre “to Esther and her heirs, but if Esther stops using the land as a residence, Gideon or his assigns may enter and terminate the estate.” Gideon later validly assigned all rights retained in Homeacre to Peter. The jurisdiction permits assignment of powers of termination. Esther then stopped using the land as a residence. Who may enforce the power of termination?
Answer A: Only Gideon, because a right of entry is always personal even when the deed says “assigns.”
Answer B: No one, because Esther's breach automatically ended her estate without any enforcement act.
Answer C: Peter, because he received the assigned power of termination under the stated modern rule.
Answer D: Esther, because Gideon's assignment converted her estate into fee simple absolute.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Peter may enforce the power of termination. The deed permits enforcement by Gideon or his assigns, and the stem states that the jurisdiction permits assignment of powers of termination. Esther's breach gives Peter the right to act, but it does not automatically end Esther's estate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because it ignores both the deed language and the stated jurisdictional rule. B: B is wrong because a fee simple subject to condition subsequent requires exercise of the power. D: D is wrong because assigning the power to Peter did not release it to Esther or extinguish the condition.
