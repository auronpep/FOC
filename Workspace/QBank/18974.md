Question: The district court certifies a consumer class action. The defendant immediately files a notice of appeal and argues that class certification is so important that the court of appeals must hear it as of right. What is the correct appellate route?
Answer A: Automatic appeal as of right because class certification is important
Answer B: A discretionary Rule 23(f) petition for permission to appeal
Answer C: No possible review ever, even after final judgment
Answer D: Immediate appeal only if the case also contains a federal question
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 23(f) provides a permission-based path for class-certification orders.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Importance alone does not create ordinary appellate jurisdiction. C: Class-certification issues may be reviewed through proper appellate routes. D: Rule 23(f) is not limited to federal-question cases.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 23(f) allows a discretionary petition for permission to appeal a class-certification order; it does not create automatic appeal as of right.
