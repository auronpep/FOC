Question: Paul conveyed Chapelacre “to John for life, then to Peter and his heirs.” John and Peter are both alive. What interest, if any, does Paul retain?
Answer A: A reversion, because Paul will take back Chapelacre when John dies.
Answer B: A possibility of reverter, because John's life estate will end automatically.
Answer C: No interest, because Peter's vested remainder in fee simple exhausts the remaining estate.
Answer D: A right of entry, because Peter must enter after John dies.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Paul retains no interest. John has a life estate, and Peter has a vested remainder in fee simple. Because the deed fully disposes of the estate after John's life estate, there is no reversion left in Paul.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the property goes to Peter, not back to Paul, when John dies. B: B is wrong because a possibility of reverter follows a fee simple determinable, not a life estate. D: D is wrong because Peter's remainder becomes possessory automatically at the natural end of John's life estate and does not require reentry.
lb_rule_reference: Vested remainder in fee / no retained grantor interest
