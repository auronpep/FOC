Question: Abraham executed and delivered a valid deed conveying Blackacre “to Sarah for life.” The deed did not give Blackacre to anyone else after Sarah's death. Sarah is alive and in possession. What future interest, if any, does Abraham retain?
Answer A: A reversion, because Abraham conveyed less than his full estate and did not give the remaining interest to a third party.
Answer B: A possibility of reverter, because Sarah's interest will end automatically at death.
Answer C: A right of entry, because Sarah's estate is limited in duration.
Answer D: No interest, because Sarah has present possession.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Abraham retains a reversion. A reversion arises when a grantor conveys less than the grantor's full estate and does not dispose of the remaining future possession to someone else. Sarah has a life estate; when it ends, possession returns to Abraham or Abraham's successor unless Abraham has transferred the reversion.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because a possibility of reverter follows a fee simple determinable, not an ordinary life estate. C: C is wrong because a right of entry follows a fee simple subject to condition subsequent. D: D is wrong because Sarah's present possession does not eliminate Abraham's retained future interest.
