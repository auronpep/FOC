Outline: Motion for judgment on the pleadings
Question: A defendant believes the plaintiff's complaint contains frivolous allegations. The defendant drafts a Rule 11 sanctions motion and files it with the court the same day it serves the motion on the plaintiff. The plaintiff objects that the defendant skipped the safe-harbor procedure. Is the plaintiff's objection correct?
Answer A: No, because Rule 11 motions may always be filed immediately
Answer B: Yes, because the defendant filed before giving the plaintiff 21 days to withdraw or correct
Answer C: No, because Rule 11 applies only to discovery papers
Answer D: Yes, but only because complaints can never be sanctioned under Rule 11
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 11's safe harbor requires service first and delays filing while the opposing party has 21 days to withdraw or correct the challenged paper.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A party-filed Rule 11 motion generally must be served and held for the 21-day safe-harbor period before filing. C: Rule 11 applies to pleadings, written motions, and other papers, but not discovery papers governed by other rules. D: Complaints are subject to Rule 11. The defect is the skipped safe-harbor sequence, not immunity for complaints.
lb_rule_reference: A Rule 11 sanctions motion must be made separately, served under Rule 5, and not filed or presented to the court if the challenged paper is withdrawn or corrected within 21 days after service.
