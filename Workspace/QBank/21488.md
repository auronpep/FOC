Outline: Uniform Commercial Code (UCC)
Question: Moses agreed to buy a movable grain grinder from Ruth for his farm. The written agreement included delivery and a one-day installation by Ruth's workers. Eighty percent of the price was for the grinder and twenty percent for delivery and installation. A dispute later arose over whether “installed” included a test run. Which answer best states the source-law starting point?
Answer A: Common law controls because labor is mentioned in the agreement.
Answer B: UCC Article 2 controls because the predominant purpose is the sale of a movable good.
Answer C: Real-property law controls because the grinder will be used on land.
Answer D: No contract law applies until the parties prove that the installation was successful.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: This is a mixed transaction, but the predominant purpose is the sale of the movable grinder. The installation is incidental to the sale. UCC Article 2 is the starting point for interpreting the content of the goods contract.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because some labor does not necessarily make the transaction a services contract. C: C is wrong because use on land does not make the grinder real property. D: D is wrong because source-law classification comes before performance success.
lb_student_diagnostic: Mixed-transaction source-law swap.
