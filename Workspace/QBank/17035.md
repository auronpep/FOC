Subtopic: Privileges and Other Policy Exclusions
Outline: Attorney-client
Question: Jacob sues Leah for fraud and alleges that Leah must have consulted her lawyer Samuel before making the disputed transaction. Jacob asks the court to review all of Leah's emails with Samuel in camera and then order production. Jacob offers only the fraud allegations in his complaint and no nonprivileged facts showing that Leah used Samuel's advice to plan or conceal fraud. Leah asserts attorney-client privilege. What is the proper privilege route?
Answer A: The court should order production because a fraud claim automatically places all communications with counsel within the crime-fraud exception.
Answer B: The court must refuse even in camera review because attorney-client privilege is absolute whenever a lawyer is consulted privately.
Answer C: The court should find waiver because legal advice becomes discoverable whenever it is relevant to the opposing party's theory.
Answer D: The court should require a threshold factual basis for crime-fraud review or production; bare fraud allegations are not enough.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Crime-fraud is an exception to privilege, not a label that defeats privilege whenever fraud is pleaded. Jacob offers only allegations and no factual basis showing that Leah used legal advice to further or conceal fraud. The proper route is to require a threshold showing before in camera review or production. The decisive axis is exception threshold, not the seriousness of the pleaded claim.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because a fraud claim does not automatically pierce attorney-client privilege. The proponent must support the exception with a factual basis. B: B is wrong because attorney-client privilege is not absolute. In appropriate circumstances, courts may use in camera review to evaluate crime-fraud, but only after the required threshold showing. C: C is wrong because relevance and usefulness do not waive attorney-client privilege. Privilege can block relevant evidence unless waiver or an exception applies.
lb_rule_reference: Crime-fraud threshold / in camera review route
lb_student_diagnostic: Student either pierces privilege too easily or treats privilege as immune from any exception-screening procedure.
