MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Torts
Subtopic: Other Torts
Outline: Nuisance
Question: A property owner hired an independent demolition company to remove an old concrete structure next to apartments. The contract gave the demolition company control over methods, but the owner knew the work would create heavy dust unless water suppression was used. The owner instructed the company to skip water suppression to save time. Dust entered nearby apartments for several days and made them unusable. Are the tenants likely to hold the property owner liable in a nuisance or negligence claim based on the demolition dust?
Answer A: No, because the demolition company was an independent contractor that controlled the manner and means of work
Answer B: Yes, because the owner gave unsafe instructions and cannot use the contractor label to avoid that direct fault
Answer C: Yes, but only because every principal is vicariously liable for torts committed on the principal's land
Answer D: No, because nuisance requires intentional conduct and the owner only wanted faster demolition
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The owner's liability is strongest as direct negligence: it knew water suppression was needed and instructed the contractor not to use it. Contractor status does not immunize the owner from its own conduct.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The independent-contractor rule does not protect the owner from its own unsafe instruction, and demolition dust may also involve duties that cannot be avoided by delegation. C: Principals are not automatically vicariously liable for all independent-contractor torts. The correct route is direct negligence or a recognized exception. D: Nuisance can rest on intentional, negligent, reckless, or abnormally dangerous conduct depending on the facts. The owner's unsafe instruction supplies fault.
lb_rule_reference: A principal is usually not vicariously liable for an independent contractor, but liability may attach for nondelegable duties, inherently dangerous work, or the principal's own negligent hiring or instructions.
