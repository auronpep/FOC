


BARMATRIX Q#: 19525
Outline_code: 94030100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Removal to Federal Court
Question: An artist, a citizen of State A, sued a writer, a citizen of State B in state court in State B for injuries sustained during a fistfight in State B. The artist claimed $30,000 in damages. The writer filed a counterclaim for $80,000 in damages based on the same fight.&#10;&#10;The artist would like to remove the action to federal court based on diversity jurisdiction. May the artist do so?
Answer A: A: No, because the writer's counterclaim is permissive.
Answer B: B: No, because the artist filed the action against the writer.
Answer C: C: Yes, because the amount in controversy is now over $75,000.
Answer D: D: Yes, because the writer's counterclaim is compulsory.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Only defendants may remove an action to federal court. Here, since the artist brought the action, he is the plaintiff and cannot request removal.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect because the writer's counterclaim is a compulsory counterclaim since it arises from the same transaction or occurrence. C is incorrect because the fact the plaintiff is now facing a claim for over $75,000 would not allow the plaintiff to remove the case to federal court. Additionally, the fact the defendant's counterclaim is compulsory would not allow the plaintiff to remove an action to federal court. Therefore, D is incorrect.
