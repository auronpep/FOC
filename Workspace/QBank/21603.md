Question: Miriam signed a written power authorizing Aaron to convey Miriam's north field to Esther. Aaron signed and delivered a deed purporting to convey both Miriam's north field and Miriam's south field to Esther. Esther accepted. What is the result?
Answer A: Esther owns the north field, but Miriam retains the south field because Aaron lacked authority to convey it.
Answer B: Esther owns both fields because Aaron had written authority to convey some of Miriam's land.
Answer C: Miriam retains both fields because Aaron exceeded his authority as to one parcel.
Answer D: Esther owns the south field only if she paid value for both fields.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source is an agent-executed deed, and the decisive validity gate is parcel-specific written authority. Aaron had written authority to convey the north field, so that portion can pass. He had no written authority to convey the south field, so the deed fails as to that parcel and Miriam retains it.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because authority to convey one parcel does not authorize conveyance of another. C: C is wrong because an unauthorized addition does not necessarily void the authorized conveyance if the parcels are separable. D: D is wrong because value does not cure the lack of written authority for the south field.
lb_rule_reference: Partial validity when agent exceeds written authority
