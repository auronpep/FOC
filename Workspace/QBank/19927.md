Question: A city denies a permit for a small group home for adults with intellectual disabilities. The zoning board admits the home satisfies all normal spacing and safety rules but denies the permit after residents say they are afraid of "those people" living nearby. What is the best Equal Protection analysis?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny applies because disability is a suspect classification
Answer B: Rational basis applies, but bare fear or animus is not a legitimate basis for the denial
Answer C: The denial automatically survives because rational basis means no real review
Answer D: Intermediate scrutiny applies because zoning affects housing
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Rational basis is deferential, but the city cannot rely on private prejudice or irrational fear as its legitimate interest.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Disability classifications generally receive rational basis review under Equal Protection doctrine. C: Deferential review still requires a legitimate interest and rational relationship. D: Importance of housing does not change the disability classification tier.
lb_rule_reference: Disability classifications generally receive rational basis review, but bare fear or animus toward a group is not a legitimate government interest.
