Question: After waiting until all the customers had left, a man entered a small grocery store just before closing time. He went up to the lone clerk in the store and said, "Hand over all the money in the cash register or you will get hurt." The clerk fainted and struck his head on the edge of the counter. As the man went behind the counter to open the cash register, two customers entered the store. The man ran out before he was able to open the register drawer. On this evidence the man could be convicted of
Answer A: A: robbery.
Answer B: B: assault and robbery.
Answer C: C: attempted robbery.
Answer D: D: assault and attempted robbery.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: C is correct. The man was unable to take possession of any personal property of another and thus, did not commit the crime of robbery. He did, by threatening the clerk and going behind the counter, take substantial steps toward the commission of the offense of robbery, and did have the intent to commit the robbery. Therefore, he should be convicted of attempted robbery.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect. The man never took the personal property and therefore never actually committed the crime of robbery. B is incorrect. As explained above, the man did not commit robbery because he did not take personal property. He also did not commit criminal assault, which is a specific intent crime. Mere words are insufficient to create a reasonable apprehension of imminent bodily harm for purposes of criminal assault. D is incorrect. As explained above, the man did commit attempted robbery, but he did not commit assault because mere words are insufficient to create a reasonable apprehension of imminent bodily harm.
