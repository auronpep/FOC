Question: Isaac contracted to build a stone wall for Naomi. The contract stated, “Final payment is due only upon a certificate of completion from Rachel, an independent mason.” Isaac finished most of the wall, but several sections leaned visibly. Rachel honestly refused to issue the certificate because the wall was unsafe. Naomi did not interfere with Rachel's inspection. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Naomi must pay because Isaac substantially performed most of the wall.
Answer B: Naomi must pay unless Rachel proves Isaac acted in bad faith.
Answer C: Isaac may recover the full contract price because certificate conditions are treated as covenants.
Answer D: Naomi's final payment duty has not matured because the express certificate condition did not occur and Naomi did not prevent it.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Final payment was expressly conditioned on the independent mason's certificate. Because the certificate was honestly refused for safety defects and Naomi did not prevent its issuance, the final payment duty did not mature.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because substantial performance does not automatically satisfy an express certificate condition. B: B is wrong because Rachel's refusal was based on the condition's standard, not Isaac's bad faith. C: C is wrong because the certificate language was a condition to payment, not merely a covenant.
