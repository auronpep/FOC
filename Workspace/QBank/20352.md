Outline: Delivery and acceptance
Question: A landowner decided to make a gift to his son of a parcel of land he owned. He properly prepared a deed for the transfer of the land according to state law and handed it to his son, saying “I want you to own this land.” Nine months later, the landowner met a woman and fell deeply in love with her. Soon thereafter, he had a fight with his son about the woman. He told his son to return or destroy the deed because he was “revoking his gift.” The son made a photocopy of the deed and gave the deed back to the landowner. The landowner then threw the deed in a fire. Two months later, the landowner died, leaving all of his real and personal property to the woman. Who owns the land?
Answer A: A: The woman, because the son returned the deed to the landowner.
Answer B: B: The woman, because the landowner told the son he was revoking his gift.
Answer C: C: The son, because he kept a photocopy of the deed.
Answer D: D: The son, because the landowner gave him the deed.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: C is incorrect because whether or not the son made a photocopy wouldn't change the analysis.
