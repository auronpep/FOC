Question: The owner of land located in State A transferred title of the land to a buyer. The buyer, a resident of State B, subsequently filed a diversity suit against the owner in the federal district court of State A, seeking to cancel the deed on the ground that the owner's transfer was fraudulent, which he committed in State B. Will State B's choice of law rules apply to this lawsuit?
Answer A: A: No, because the suit was filed in State A, State A's choice of law rules will apply.
Answer B: B: No,becausethe property is located in State A, State A's choice of law rules will apply.
Answer C: C: Yes, becauseState B is where the fraud took place.
Answer D: D: Yes,because State B is where the buyer lives.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: B is correct. The forum court will apply the choice of law rules of State A because the property is located there.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect. No matter where the suit was filed, the forum court would still need to apply the choice of law rules of the situs. C is incorrect. The location of the fraud is not relevant to determining which choice of law rules to apply. D is incorrect. The buyer's domicile is relevant for jurisdictional purposes but not to determine which choice of law rules to apply.
