Question: Daniel signed a deed granting Hannah “an easement for Hannah to use the marked parking space on Daniel's lot during market days for the rest of Hannah's life.” Hannah owns no land that benefits from the parking space. What is the best classification of Hannah's right?
Answer A: An easement in gross because the right benefits Hannah personally and no dominant parcel is identified.
Answer B: An easement appurtenant because all easements must benefit land.
Answer C: A revocable license because Hannah owns no neighboring parcel.
Answer D: A profit because parking allows Hannah to earn money at the market.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: An easement in gross benefits a person rather than a parcel. The deed expressly grants Hannah an easement, but no dominant land is benefited. The right is therefore an easement in gross.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because easements in gross do not require a dominant parcel. C: C is wrong because a deeded easement is not revocable merely because it is in gross. D: D is wrong because a profit requires removal of resources from land.
lb_rule_reference: Easement in gross / no dominant parcel
