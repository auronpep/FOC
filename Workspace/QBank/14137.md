


BARMATRIX Q#: 14137
Outline_code: 92080202
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Motions
Outline: Renewed Motion for Judgment as a Matter of Law
Question: A railroad brought an action for fraud against a group of personal injury lawyers in federal district court. The railroad alleged that the lawyers engaged with purported medical experts in a scheme to inundate the railroad with cases involving tortious exposure to asbestos, which lacked any merit. The railroad presented various forms of circumstantial evidence, including expert testimony. The railroad's expert testified that several of the clients in the previous asbestos cases who had been diagnosed by the purported medical experts as suffering from asbestosis, in fact, had no asbestos-related disease. Both the lawyers and the purported medical experts testified and denied the allegations against them. The railroad moved for a judgment as a matter of law, which the court denied. The jury returned a verdict in favor of the personal injury lawyers. The railroad moved for a renewed judgment as a matter of law. Which standard should the court use in ruling on the railroad's renewed motion for judgment as a matter of law?
Answer A: A: The court should view the evidence and all reasonable inferences therefrom in the light most favorable to the personal injury attorneys.
Answer B: B: The court should view the evidence and all reasonable inferences therefrom in the light most favorable to the railroad.
Answer C: C: The court should consider the credibility of the witnesses and resolve conflicts in testimony.
Answer D: D: The court should order new trial because of the policy against entering judgment as a matter of law against attorney-litigants.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A is correct. When ruling on a renewed motion for judgment as a matter of law (“JMOL”), the court must review the evidence in favor of the non-moving party. The personal injury attorneys are the non-moving party under this motion for JMOL, so this is the standard the court should use.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B is incorrect. The railroad is the party moving for JMOL. As stated above, when ruling on a renewed motion for JOML, the court must review the evidence in a light most favorable to the non-moving party (here, the personal injury attorneys). C is incorrect. When ruling on a renewed motion for JMOL, the court may not consider the credibility of the witnesses or attempt to resolve conflicts in testimony. Instead, the court must determine whether a reasonable jury would have a sufficient legal basis to find for the nonmoving party on the issue, viewing the evidence and all reasonable inferences therefrom in the light most favorable to the non-moving party (the personal injury attorneys). D is incorrect. There is no policy that prohibits courts from entering judgments as a matter of law against attorney- litigants.
