


BARMATRIX Q#: 22777
Outline_code: 94010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Federal Subject-Matter Jurisdiction
Question: A man from State A sued a woman from State B for an automobile accident that occurred in State B. The man filed suit in State A federal court for $150,000 in damages. The man served the woman himself by leaving notice of the lawsuit at her home. The woman moved to transfer the case to State B federal court. May the court grant the woman's motion?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the court has the authority to transfer the action to State B federal court.
Answer B: B: Yes, because service was improper.
Answer C: C: No, because the court may only dismiss the claim and require the man to refile.
Answer D: D: No, because venue is proper in State A federal court.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Because the defendant was not from State A and the cause of action did not arise in State A, the man's lawsuit was not properly commenced in State A. Consequently, the court has the authority to transfer the claim to the State B federal court where the woman is a citizen. B is incorrect because improper service would not take away the court's authority to transfer the case. C is incorrect because the court can transfer an improperly filed case. D is incorrect because venue would not be proper in State A because the defendant was not from there and the cause of action did not arise in State A.
