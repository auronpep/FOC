Question: In a products case, the plaintiff seeks defense counsel's memo ranking each witness as "strong," "weak," or "dangerous," with notes about cross-examination strategy. The plaintiff shows that several witnesses are unavailable and argues that the memo is the best remaining account of the investigation. What is the likely result?
Answer A: The entire memo must be produced because unavailable witnesses create substantial need
Answer B: The memo must be produced because witness credibility is always discoverable
Answer C: The court should protect the lawyer's mental impressions and legal theories, even if it orders production of separable factual material
Answer D: The memo is not work product unless it was signed by the client
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Opinion work product is specially protected; courts must guard mental impressions when work-product discovery is allowed.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The requested rankings and strategy notes reveal counsel's mental impressions and legal theories. B: Discoverability of a topic does not eliminate opinion work-product protection. D: Work-product protection does not require a client signature.
lb_rule_reference: Courts must protect an attorney's mental impressions, conclusions, opinions, and legal theories when ordering work-product discovery.
