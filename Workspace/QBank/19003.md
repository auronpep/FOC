Question: In a federal negligence trial, Ruth calls Moses to testify that he personally saw Adam's delivery cart roll through a red light. Adam objects that Ruth has produced no photograph, video, or document of the intersection. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because testimony is inferior to photographic or documentary proof.
Answer B: Sustain the objection unless Ruth first proves that no recording of the intersection exists.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because Moses's firsthand testimony is a proper type of evidence if based on personal knowledge.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if Moses's testimony is treated as demonstrative evidence rather than testimonial evidence.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Firsthand witness testimony is a proper proof vehicle. If Moses perceived the event, the absence of a photograph, video, or document does not make the testimony inadmissible.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because live testimony is not inferior as a category. B: B is wrong because a proponent need not prove the absence of other proof before offering testimony. D: D is wrong because Moses's testimony is testimonial evidence, not demonstrative evidence.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 602; FRE 611(a) / testimonial evidence
lb_student_diagnostic: Tests proof-vehicle identity: testimonial evidence can prove a fact without a supporting exhibit.
