Subtopic: Privileges and Other Policy Exclusions
Outline: Attorney-client
Question: After creditor Leah obtains a judgment against him, Jacob emails lawyer Sarah: “Please prepare a deed transferring my farm to my brother and make it look as if I signed it last spring, before Leah's judgment. That way she cannot reach the farm.” Sarah refuses to prepare the deed. Leah later moves to compel production of Jacob's email. Is Jacob's email protected by the attorney-client privilege?
Answer A: Yes. The email is privileged because Jacob was asking a lawyer to prepare a legal document.
Answer B: No. The email seeks legal services to create a false instrument and defeat a creditor, so the crime-fraud exception defeats the privilege.
Answer C: Yes. The privilege remains intact because Sarah refused to participate and was not aware of any completed fraud.
Answer D: No, but only if the backdated deed was actually prepared and filed before Leah moved to compel the email.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Attorney-client privilege does not protect communications made to obtain legal advice or legal services in furtherance of future or ongoing crime or fraud. Jacob's email asks Sarah to create a backdated deed so a creditor cannot reach the property. The decisive trigger is the requested fraudulent legal service, not whether the lawyer joined the scheme or whether the deed was completed.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the legal-document label is exactly the trap. The communication asks the lawyer to create a false instrument for a fraudulent purpose. C: C is wrong because the lawyer's innocence or refusal does not save the client's communication when the client sought to use legal services to further fraud. D: D is wrong because completion is not required. The exception can apply to communications seeking help with future or ongoing fraud even if the scheme fails.
lb_rule_reference: Crime-fraud exception for future fraudulent use of legal services
lb_student_diagnostic: Student sees lawyer involvement and legal-document format but misses the illegal service requested inside the communication.
