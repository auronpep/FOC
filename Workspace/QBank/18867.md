Question: Naomi was traveling and asked Daniel to receive any deed for her while she was away. Boaz signed a deed conveying Wheatacre to Naomi and handed it to Daniel, saying, “This deed is for Naomi, and the land is hers now.” Daniel accepted the deed for Naomi. Naomi later accepted the benefit. Did delivery occur?
Answer A: No, because Naomi did not personally receive the deed from Boaz.
Answer B: No, because Daniel was not a recording officer.
Answer C: Yes, because Boaz delivered the deed with present intent to Naomi's authorized agent.
Answer D: Yes, but only if Boaz died before Naomi returned.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source is a deed, and the decisive delivery lock is delivery to an authorized recipient with present transfer intent. Naomi authorized Daniel to receive the deed, and Boaz stated that the land was Naomi's now. Delivery to the grantee's agent can satisfy the delivery requirement.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because personal receipt by the grantee is not always required. B: B is wrong because delivery does not require use of a recording officer. D: D is wrong because Boaz's death is not the fact that creates delivery.
lb_rule_reference: Delivery to the grantee's authorized agent can satisfy delivery
