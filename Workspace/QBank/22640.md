Outline_code: 42010107
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Constitutional Law
Subtopic: Separation of Powers
Outline: Thirteenth, Fourteenth, and Fifteenth Amendments
Question: Four men attacked an amateur boxer at a bus stop because the boxer was African- American. The four men were arrested and charged with violation of the African- American Hate Act, which Congress passed in order to add additional seriousness and punishment to crimes that involve racially- motivated violence against African- Americans. The men challenged the Act, arguing that it was unconstitutional. Is the Act unconstitutional?
Answer A: A: Yes, because it adds additional punishment to crimes traditionally prosecuted by the states.
Answer B: B: Yes, because it favors certain criminal victims based on their race.
Answer C: C: No, because the Act seeks to enforce the same protections available to all citizens.
Answer D: D: No, because racially- motivated violence is a badge or incident of slavery.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The Thirteenth Amendment prohibits slavery, and the Thirteenth Amendment's Enabling Clause gives Congress the power to prohibit almost any discriminatory action if that discriminatory action can be classified as a badge or incident of slavery. Racially- motivated violence is a badge or incident of slavery. United States v. Cannon, 750 F.3d 492 (5th Cir. 2014). Consequently, the Act is constitutional. A and B are therefore incorrect. C is incorrect because it references the protections given by the Fourteenth Amendment, and the Fourteenth Amendment deals with state actions.
