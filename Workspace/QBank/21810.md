Question: A homeowner invited a neighbor over for dinner. The homeowner knew a loose basement step looked normal from above but collapsed unless stepped on at the edge. The homeowner said nothing. The neighbor went downstairs to get a stored chair and fell through the step. Which duty did the homeowner owe under traditional entrant categories?
Answer A: A duty to warn of the known hidden danger
Answer B: A duty to inspect the entire house for unknown hazards
Answer C: No duty because social guests enter at their own risk
Answer D: Strict liability for every injury to a guest
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A social guest is typically a licensee, and the homeowner knew of a hidden danger unlikely to be discovered.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: That broader inspection duty belongs to invitees. Licensees receive warnings of known hidden dangers. C: Less protection does not mean no protection; known hidden dangers must be warned about. D: Premises liability here is negligence duty, not strict liability.
lb_rule_reference: A land possessor generally must warn licensees of known hidden dangers the licensee is unlikely to discover.
