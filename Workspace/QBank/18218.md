Question: A city bans all hand-to-hand leafleting in every public park to reduce litter. Speakers may post messages on a city website, but they may not hand flyers to willing park visitors anywhere in the parks. What is the best analysis?
Answer A: The ban is automatically valid because it is content neutral
Answer B: The ban may fail because it broadly eliminates a traditional form of communication in public parks
Answer C: The ban receives rational basis because litter is not speech
Answer D: The ban is viewpoint discrimination because it applies to every leaflet
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A total park-wide leafleting ban may burden more speech than necessary and leave inadequate alternatives.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Content neutrality is not the end of TPM review. C: The regulation directly burdens leafleting, a protected expressive activity. D: Equal application to all leaflets is not viewpoint discrimination; the issue is tailoring.
lb_rule_reference: Content-neutral TPM restrictions in traditional public forums must be narrowly tailored to significant interests and leave open ample alternative channels.
