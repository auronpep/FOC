Question: A retailer sends Lydia, a merchant seller, an order for 200 lamps at a stated price, requesting prompt shipment. The next morning, Lydia emails, “I accept your order and will ship the lamps this week.” Before Lydia ships anything, the retailer tries to revoke the order. Was a contract formed before the attempted revocation?
Answer A: No, because an order for goods can be accepted only by actual shipment.
Answer B: Yes, because Lydia accepted by a prompt promise to ship.
Answer C: No, because Lydia's email was not a mirror-image acceptance unless it repeated every term in the order.
Answer D: No, because Lydia did not sign a paper contract.
Correct Answer: B
