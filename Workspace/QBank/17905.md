Question: A government official moves to dismiss based on qualified immunity, arguing that the complaint shows no violation of clearly established law. The district court conclusively denies immunity on that pure legal question. The official seeks immediate appeal. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Immediate appeal may be available because immunity from suit can be effectively lost if review waits until final judgment
Answer B: Immediate appeal is available for every denied motion to dismiss
Answer C: Immediate appeal is never available before final judgment
Answer D: The order is appealable only because discovery will be expensive
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Certain immunity denials fit the narrow collateral order doctrine when they present a legal issue.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Ordinary denials are not final; immunity is a special context. C: Collateral order and statutory exceptions exist. D: Cost is not the key; effective loss of immunity from suit is.
lb_rule_reference: Collateral order doctrine requires a conclusive order, important issue separate from merits, and effective unreviewability after final judgment.
