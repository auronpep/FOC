Question: In a federal trial, Lydia offers testimony from Priscilla. The judge excludes the testimony. Lydia's lawyer asks to make an offer of proof outside the jury's hearing. The judge responds, “No. I have ruled, and I will not let you describe the excluded testimony in any form.” Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The judge may control the form of the offer, but should allow Lydia a reasonable way to make the substance of the excluded testimony part of the record.
Answer B: The judge properly refused because an offer of proof is never allowed after exclusion.
Answer C: The judge properly refused because an offer of proof would violate the rule of completeness.
Answer D: The judge was required to let Lydia present the excluded testimony to the jury.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A proponent generally must make the substance of excluded evidence known to preserve error. The court may direct the form of the offer and keep it from the jury, but it should not prevent any reasonable record of the excluded testimony.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because offers of proof are specifically used after exclusion. C: C is wrong because completeness is not the issue. D: D is wrong because preserving the record does not require exposing the jury to excluded evidence.
