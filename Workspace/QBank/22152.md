Outline_code: 31010500
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Evidence
Subtopic: Presentation of Evidence
Outline: Impeachment
Question: A man injured his knee when his neighbor's dog got loose and knocked the man to the ground. The man claimed that he had previous knee problems, but that a recent surgery had left him pain- free before the accident. Now, the man claimed he had constant knee pain again. The man sued his neighbor for his injuries. At trial, the man sought to call his doctor to the stand to testify at a follow- up appointment that the man told him that he felt no pain at all in his knee after the surgery. Is the doctor's testimony admissible?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the man's statement was made for the purpose of medical treatment.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the man is subject to cross examination.
Answer C: C: No, because the man's statement is inadmissible hearsay.
Answer D: D: No, because the man is not unavailable.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Under FRE 803(4), a statement made to a healthcare professional for the purpose of obtaining medical treatment is an exception to the hearsay rule. Here, the man told the doctor that he felt no pain during a follow- up appointment for his surgery. This statement thus qualifies under the exception. B is incorrect because the fact the man is subject to cross- examination would not make the statement admissible if it was in fact hearsay. C is incorrect because the man made the statement for the purpose of medical treatment. D is incorrect because this hearsay exception does not depend on the declarant's availability.
