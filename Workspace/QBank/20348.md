


BARMATRIX Q#: 20348
Outline_code: 31010504
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Evidence
Subtopic: Presentation of Evidence
Outline: Conviction of crime
Question: A witness, testifying on behalf of the prosecution, stated that he saw the defendant shoot the victim three times in the back. During the defendant's case, the defendant's attorney offered two properly authenticated judgments of conviction. One showed that the witness had been convicted two years ago of attempted murder, which was a felony. The other showed that the witness had been convicted one year ago of knowingly making false statements in an application for a business license, which was a misdemeanor. Over objection by the prosecution, the court should admit the judgment(s) of conviction of
Answer A: A: attempted murder only.
Answer B: B: knowingly making false statements in an application for a business license only.
Answer C: C: both crimes.
Answer D: D: neither crime.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Under FRE 609, convictions may be used to impeach the credibility of a witness. A court may not exclude proof of such convictions if they are for felonies or for misdemeanors involving dishonesty. Since attempted murder is a felony, and since the other crime was a misdemeanor involving dishonesty, evidence of both was admissible.
Wrong Answer Explanations: Therefore, A, B, and D are all incorrect.
