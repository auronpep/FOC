Question: In a federal civil trial, Jacob testifies for the defendant. On the plaintiff's case in rebuttal, the plaintiff calls Deborah, who has known Jacob for years, to testify that in her opinion Jacob is an untruthful person. The defendant objects that Jacob's character for truthfulness has not previously been attacked. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because opinion testimony about truthfulness is never permitted.
Answer B: Sustain the objection because character for untruthfulness may be proved only by a conviction.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because a witness's credibility may be attacked by opinion testimony about the witness's character for untruthfulness.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if Deborah first describes specific lies by Jacob.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: FRE 608(a) allows a witness's credibility to be attacked by reputation or opinion testimony about the witness's character for untruthfulness. The rule limits support for truthful character until after attack, but it does not require a prior attack before a party may attack a witness's character for untruthfulness.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because opinion testimony is an allowed form under Rule 608(a). B: B is wrong because conviction impeachment is not the only method of attacking truthfulness. D: D is wrong because specific instances are not required and are not the proper direct-proof form under Rule 608(a).
lb_rule_reference: FRE 608(a) / opinion or reputation attack for untruthfulness
lb_student_diagnostic: Tests attack-versus-support sequencing and the permitted opinion/reputation forms for untruthfulness.
