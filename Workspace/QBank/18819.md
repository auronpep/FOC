Outline: Motion for new trial
Question: Judgment on a jury verdict is entered on March 1. The losing party files a Rule 59 motion for new trial on April 5. No statute extends the deadline. Is the new-trial motion timely?
Answer A: Yes, because Rule 59 motions may be filed any time before appeal
Answer B: Yes, because 60 days is always available for civil post-trial motions
Answer C: No, because April 5 is more than 28 days after March 1
Answer D: No, because new-trial motions are allowed only before verdict
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The motion was filed outside the Rule 59 deadline.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Rule 59 has a 28-day deadline from entry of judgment. B: The Rule 59 deadline is not 60 days. D: New-trial motions are post-verdict/post-judgment motions.
lb_rule_reference: A motion for new trial must be filed no later than 28 days after entry of judgment.
