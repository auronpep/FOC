MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Motions
Outline: Motion for judgment on the pleadings
Question: A plaintiff files a federal complaint. The defendant serves a Rule 12(b)(6) motion on May 1. On May 15, before the court rules and before the plaintiff has amended before, the plaintiff files an amended complaint adding factual details to the same claim. The defendant objects that the plaintiff needed leave of court. Was leave of court required?
Answer A: No, because the plaintiff amended once within 21 days after service of the Rule 12(b)(6) motion
Answer B: Yes, because any amendment after a motion to dismiss requires leave
Answer C: Yes, because a party may amend as of course only before any defendant appears
Answer D: No, but only because the court had not yet ruled on the motion
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Rule 15(a)(1) permits one amendment as a matter of course within 21 days after service of a Rule 12(b) motion. The amendment was timely and first-in-time.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Rule 15(a)(1) expressly gives a limited amendment-as-of-course period after Rule 12(b), (e), or (f) motions. C: The rule uses service of the pleading, responsive pleading, and specified Rule 12 motions as timing anchors, not mere appearance. D: The key is service of the Rule 12 motion and the 21-day period, not whether the court has ruled.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 15(a)(1) permits one amendment as a matter of course within 21 days after serving the pleading or, if a responsive pleading or Rule 12(b), (e), or (f) motion is served, within 21 days after that service.
