Subtopic: Privileges and Other Policy Exclusions
Outline: Attorney-client
Question: Lydia owns a delivery business. Before using a proposed tax strategy, she meets privately with her lawyer Deborah, discloses all material facts, and asks, “Can I do this lawfully, or should I stop if the law does not allow it?” Deborah gives legal advice. Months later, the government alleges that the tax strategy was fraudulent and subpoenas Deborah to testify about Lydia's consultation. Lydia objects on attorney-client privilege grounds. Does the crime-fraud exception automatically defeat the privilege?
Answer A: Yes, because the government later alleged that the proposed tax strategy was fraudulent.
Answer B: Yes, because attorney-client privilege never protects advice about conduct that could violate tax law.
Answer C: Yes, unless Deborah specifically told Lydia that the strategy was illegal and Lydia immediately abandoned it.
Answer D: No. A confidential request for legal advice about whether proposed conduct is lawful remains privileged unless Lydia sought advice to further crime or fraud.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The crime-fraud exception is not triggered by hindsight allegations alone. Lydia fully disclosed the proposed conduct before acting and asked whether she could proceed lawfully. That is a compliance-advice route, not a request to disguise, falsify, destroy, or evade. The decisive axis is the client's purpose in seeking legal advice.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because an allegation of fraud does not itself establish that the legal consultation was in furtherance of fraud. B: B is wrong because privilege often protects advice about whether contemplated conduct is lawful. That protection encourages clients to seek legal guidance before acting. C: C is wrong because privilege does not depend on the lawyer giving a particular answer or the client proving immediate abandonment. The key is whether the client sought lawful compliance advice rather than fraud assistance.
lb_rule_reference: Crime-fraud boundary for compliance advice
lb_student_diagnostic: Student overuses the crime-fraud exception whenever illegal conduct appears near a legal consultation.
