


BARMATRIX Q#: 22113
Outline_code: 82010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Nature and Ownership of Land
Question: A landlord owned a home with an attached garage apartment. The landlord leased the home and apartment to a lawyer for four years. The lease stated that the lawyer would pay $1,000 per month and that the lawyer could not assign or sublease the premises. The lawyer lived in the house for two years and assigned his interest to a professor who was unaware of the prohibition on assignments. Two months later, the landlord conveyed his interest in the property to a doctor. Four months after that, the professor sublet the garage apartment to a student for $300 per month. The student paid rent to the professor for the first two months then stopped paying rent and refused to leave the apartment. That month, the professor sent the doctor $700 and said that he needed to get the remaining $300 from the student. The doctor then filed suit against the student for the $300. Should the court rule in the doctor's favor?
Answer A: A: No, because only the professor is liable for the unpaid rent.
Answer B: B: No, because only the professor or the lawyer is liable for the unpaid rent.
Answer C: C: Yes, because the student is in privity of estate.
Answer D: D: Yes, because the doctor was not given notice of the sublease.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: As a sublessee, the student is neither in privity of estate nor in privity of contract with the doctor and the doctor cannot collect rent from him. However, the doctor can collect from the professor because he is in privity of estate because he was assigned the lawyer's interest. The doctor can also collect from the lawyer who remains in privity of contract by signing the original lease. A is incorrect because the lawyer would also be liable under privity of contract. C is incorrect because the student as sublessee is not in privity of estate. D is incorrect because it doesn't change the analysis if the doctor was or wasn't given notice of the sublease.
