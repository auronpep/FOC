Question: A state requires all applicants for a firefighting job to pass a strength test. The test is facially neutral and job-related on its face. Statistics show a racial disparity in pass rates, but there is no evidence of discriminatory purpose. Under Equal Protection, what is the likely scrutiny trigger?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny automatically applies because the statistics show racial disparity
Answer B: Heightened scrutiny does not apply on impact alone absent discriminatory purpose
Answer C: The test receives intermediate scrutiny because employment is important
Answer D: The test is invalid per se because any racial disparity violates Equal Protection
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Disparate impact may matter as evidence, but statistics alone do not trigger strict scrutiny.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Equal Protection requires discriminatory purpose for heightened scrutiny of a facially neutral rule. C: Importance of the opportunity is not a scrutiny category. D: Constitutional doctrine distinguishes disparate impact from discriminatory purpose.
lb_rule_reference: Disparate impact, without discriminatory purpose, does not by itself trigger heightened scrutiny under Equal Protection.
