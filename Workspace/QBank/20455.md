Question: On a Rule 12(b)(6) motion, the defendant submits new affidavits contradicting the complaint. The court relies on the affidavits and dismisses without telling the plaintiff the motion is being converted or allowing the plaintiff to submit evidence. What is the likely error?
Answer A: The court considered outside matters without excluding them or giving conversion safeguards
Answer B: The court could freely weigh affidavits because Rule 12(b)(6) is an evidence motion
Answer C: The court was required to accept the affidavits as true
Answer D: The only possible result was immediate trial
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: If outside evidence is considered, Rule 12(d) requires conversion and reasonable opportunity.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Rule 12(b)(6) tests pleading sufficiency. C: That is not the Rule 12(b)(6) standard. D: Proper conversion leads to Rule 56 procedure, not automatic trial.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 12(d) protects parties from surprise conversion.
