Question: In a federal civil trial, Abraham sues a delivery company. Abraham requests exclusion of all witnesses from the courtroom. The company designates Deborah, its safety director, as its trial representative. Abraham asks the judge to exclude Deborah because she may later testify. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Deny the request to exclude Deborah if she is the company's designated representative, though the court may control improper disclosure or testimony access as allowed by rule.
Answer B: Grant the request because every witness must be excluded once a party invokes sequestration.
Answer C: Grant the request unless Deborah promises not to testify.
Answer D: Deny the request only if Deborah is an expert witness.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: FRE 615 requires exclusion of witnesses on request but contains exceptions, including a designated representative for an entity party. The court may still issue appropriate protective orders, but the representative is not automatically excluded merely because she may testify.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because sequestration has explicit exceptions. C: C is wrong because the representative exception does not depend on a promise not to testify. D: D is wrong because the entity-representative exception is separate from the essential-person exception.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 615 / sequestration exceptions
lb_student_diagnostic: Tests witness-sequestration exceptions and party-representative routing.
