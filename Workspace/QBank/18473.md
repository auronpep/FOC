Question: In a bench trial, plaintiff finishes presenting evidence on liability. Defendant asks the judge to enter judgment because plaintiff failed to prove an essential element. Defendant labels the request "JMOL." What is the correct procedural mechanism?
Answer A: Rule 50 JMOL, because JMOL governs all trials
Answer B: Rule 52(c) judgment on partial findings
Answer C: Rule 56 summary judgment, because trial has begun
Answer D: Rule 55 default judgment, because plaintiff rested
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: In a bench trial, Rule 52(c) is the proper mechanism after a party has been fully heard.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Rule 50 applies to jury trials. C: Rule 56 is pretrial. D: Resting at trial is not default.
lb_rule_reference: In a nonjury trial, the court may enter judgment on partial findings after a party has been fully heard on an issue and the court finds against that party on that issue.
