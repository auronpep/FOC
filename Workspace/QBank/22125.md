MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Nature and Ownership of Land
Question: A garage door company leased a warehouse from a landlord under a five- year lease. When the company took possession of the warehouse, it discovered that the activities of an HVAC company, the previous warehouse tenant, had left a toxic residue in the warehouse. The company immediately notified the landlord. The landlord said that it didn't know anything about the toxic residue and didn't think it was responsible for cleanup. The company sued the landlord, claiming that the landlord had breached the implied warranty of habitability. Should the court rule in the company's favor?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the company immediately notified the landlord of the toxic residue.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the company's tenancy was under a long- term lease.
Answer C: C: No, because this is a lease for a commercial warehouse.
Answer D: D: No, because the landlord did not know about the toxic residue until the company notified it of the problem.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Commercial leases do not include an implied warranty of habitability. Consequently, the landlord could not violate it. Consequently, A, B, and D are incorrect.
