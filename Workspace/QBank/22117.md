Question: A landlord rented a house to a student on a month- to- month lease. Four months later, the landlord assigned his interest in the lease to a developer. The lease agreement said nothing about assignments. Which of the following is the most accurate statement regarding the lease?
Answer A: A: The lease was terminated when the landlord assigned his interest.
Answer B: B: The landlord is still the landlord under the lease because the assignment was invalid.
Answer C: C: The developer is now the landlord under the lease because it was a residential lease.
Answer D: D: The developer is now the landlord under the lease.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Only an at- will tenancy terminates when a landlord assigns his or her interest to another. A periodic tenancy does not terminate on assignment. Thus, the tenancy did not terminate on assignment and the developer is now the landlord under the lease. A is incorrect because a periodic tenancy is not terminated on assignment. Here, there was a periodic tenancy because the student's lease was month- to- month. B is incorrect because there was nothing in the facts to make the lease invalid. C is incorrect because the fact that this was a residential lease does not change the analysis.
