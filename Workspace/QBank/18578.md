Question: Matthew conveyed Fieldacre “to Mark and his heirs, but if Mark uses the land as a tavern during Mark's life, then to Luke and his heirs.” Mark later used the land as a tavern during his life. Who owns Fieldacre after Mark begins the tavern use?
Answer A: Matthew, because the condition caused a possibility of reverter.
Answer B: Mark, until Matthew exercises a right of entry.
Answer C: Luke, because Mark's estate automatically shifted to Luke.
Answer D: Mark and Luke as tenants in common.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The deed creates a fee simple subject to executory limitation in Mark and a shifting executory interest in Luke. Because the future interest is given to a third party rather than retained by Matthew, the estate automatically shifts to Luke when the stated event occurs.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because a possibility of reverter belongs to the grantor after a fee simple determinable; here the future interest is in Luke. B: B is wrong because no right of entry is retained by Matthew. D: D is wrong because the condition causes a shift in ownership, not co-ownership.
lb_rule_reference: Fee simple subject to executory limitation / shifting executory interest
