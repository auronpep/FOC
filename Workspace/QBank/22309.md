


BARMATRIX Q#: 22309
Outline_code: 75070000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Criminal Law and Procedure
Subtopic: Constitutional Protection of Accused Persons (Criminal Procedure)
Outline: Fourth Amendment
Question: A gas station was experiencing a great deal of losses due to shoplifting. The gas station asked the police to conduct surveillance for the store to stop the thefts. A police officer set up cameras around the store, including one in the gas station's unisex bathroom. Instead of recording the camera's observations, a police officer watched the cameras live so he could immediately arrest anyone caught shoplifting on camera. One day, the officer saw a woman walk into the bathroom with a six pack of beer, hide it under her sweater, and walk out the gas station door. The officer stopped the woman in the parking lot and arrested her. At trial, the woman asked for the evidence against her to be suppressed. Should the court grant the woman's motion?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the camera violated the woman's reasonable expectation of privacy.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the camera's observations were not recorded.
Answer C: C: No, because the gas station requested that the police surveil its property.
Answer D: D: No, because the gas station bathroom is a public place.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Under the Fourth Amendment, government searches and seizures without probable cause are prohibited in places where a person has a reasonable expectation of privacy. Here, the woman had a reasonable expectation of privacy in the bathroom, a police officer was watching the camera feed, and the shoplifting problem at the gas station would not provide sufficient probable cause for the search. B is incorrect because recording the camera's observations would not change the analysis. C is incorrect because the fact that the gas station asked for the police to surveil the property would not make government observation of the bathroom lawful. D is incorrect because a person has a reasonable expectation of privacy in a public bathroom.
