Outline: Nonresponsive answers
Question: In a federal negligence trial, Leah asks Jacob, “Did you inspect the wagon after the accident?” Jacob answers, “Yes, and the mechanic told me that Joseph removed the brakes.” Joseph immediately moves to strike the mechanic statement. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Strike Jacob's entire answer because any answer containing inadmissible matter is entirely excluded.
Answer B: Leave the entire answer because Jacob first said yes.
Answer C: Strike the entire testimony of Jacob because he mentioned a mechanic.
Answer D: Strike the volunteered mechanic statement while allowing the responsive answer that Jacob inspected the wagon to remain.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The court can strike only the improper portion of a mixed answer. Jacob's “yes” directly answers the question; the extra statement relaying the mechanic's assertion is volunteered and independently objectionable.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the court need not strike the responsive portion. B: B is wrong because a responsive beginning does not immunize a volunteered inadmissible addition. C: C is wrong because mentioning a mechanic does not require striking all of Jacob's testimony.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 103(a)(1); FRE 611(a) / partial answer strike
