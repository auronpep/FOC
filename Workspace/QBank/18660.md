Outline_code: 33040302
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Evidence
Subtopic: Hearsay and Circumstances of Its Admissibility
Outline: Not requiring unavailability of declarant
Question: The plaintiff was injured when the ladder on which she was standing collapsed without warning. Immediately following the accident, the plaintiff was taken to the hospital, where she remained for approximately six hours. The plaintiff later sued the manufacturer of the ladder. At the trial, the plaintiff's attorney next offered a properly authenticated hospital record. After examining the record, the defendant's attorney, outside the presence of the jury, moved to exclude a portion of the record that read: “History: Ladder collapsed. Patient fell.” The motion to exclude that portion of the record should be
Answer A: A: granted, because it can be excluded without causing any physical damage to the record.
Answer B: B: granted, because it has no bearing on the plaintiff's medical condition.
Answer C: C: denied, because the history was taken for the purpose of diagnosis or treatment.
Answer D: D: granted, since hospital personnel are not experts in determining the causes of accidents.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: FRE 803(4) and (6) permit the introduction of hospital records containing statements made by the patient for purposes of diagnosis. Since the circumstances that led to the injury are relevant to a diagnosis of the injury, they may be included in the record offered in evidence.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A and B are therefore incorrect. D is incorrect since the statements in the record were not opinion and therefore do not require the testimony of experts.
