Question: A state allows public employees to swap shifts voluntarily for religious observance if the swap does not reduce staffing or burden coworkers. A taxpayer argues any accommodation of religion establishes religion. What is the best response?
Answer A: The accommodation is automatically unconstitutional because it mentions religion
Answer B: The accommodation is not automatically establishment if it relieves religious burdens without sponsoring religion or imposing impermissible burdens
Answer C: The accommodation is constitutional only if identical religious beliefs are held by all employees
Answer D: The accommodation is invalid because the Free Exercise Clause forbids voluntary shift swaps
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The facts describe a modest accommodation that preserves staffing and avoids coercion.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Religious accommodations are often permissible and do not automatically establish religion. C: Accommodation does not require universal religious agreement. D: Free Exercise does not forbid voluntary accommodations; it often supports protecting religious exercise.
lb_rule_reference: Government may accommodate religious exercise without automatically violating the Establishment Clause, but accommodations cannot impose impermissible burdens or sponsor religion.
