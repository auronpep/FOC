Question: A federal court orders a defendant to produce safety-test reports by a specific date. The defendant knowingly refuses to comply and offers no substantial justification. The plaintiff seeks sanctions. Which sanction is within the court's Rule 37 authority?
Answer A: Only a monetary fine, because issue and evidence sanctions are unavailable for discovery violations
Answer B: Deeming certain facts established or prohibiting the defendant from supporting designated defenses, if the sanction is appropriate
Answer C: Automatic default judgment, because any violation of a discovery order ends the case
Answer D: No sanction unless the plaintiff proves the reports would have changed the outcome at trial
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Those are expressly recognized sanctions for disobeying a discovery order.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Rule 37 authorizes a range of nonmonetary sanctions for failure to obey discovery orders. C: Default can be available in severe cases, but it is not automatic for every violation. D: The rule does not require proof that the withheld evidence would alter the final outcome before any sanction may issue.
lb_rule_reference: If a party fails to obey a discovery order, the court may impose sanctions including deeming facts established, prohibiting claims or defenses, striking pleadings, staying proceedings, dismissal, default, contempt, and reasonable expenses.
