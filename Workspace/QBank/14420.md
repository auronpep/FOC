Question: A buyer ordered from a seller 500 bushels of No. 1 Royal Fuzz peaches, at a specified price, "for prompt shipment." The seller promptly shipped 500 bushels, but by mistake shipped No. 2 Royal Fuzz peaches instead of No. 1. The error in shipment was caused by the negligence of the seller's shipping clerk. Which of the following best states the buyer's rights and duties upon delivery of the peaches?
Answer A: A: The seller's shipment of the peaches was a counteroffer and the buyer can refuse to accept them.
Answer B: B: The seller's shipment of the peaches was a counteroffer but, since peaches are perishable, the buyer, if it does not want to accept them, must reship the peaches to the seller in order to mitigate the seller's losses.
Answer C: C: The buyer must accept the peaches because a contract was formed when the seller shipped them.
Answer D: D: Although a contract was formed when the seller shipped the peaches, the buyer does not have to accept them.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: D is correct. By shipping non-conforming goods (the No. 2 peaches rather than No. 1), the seller was accepting the buyer's offer, thus forming a contract, and breaching it at the same time. Without any evidence that the seller tried to notify the buyer or offer the No. 2 peaches as an accommodation, the buyer may simply reject the shipment. A
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect. The seller's shipment of non-conforming goods is not considered a counter-offer, but rather, a breach of the contract, and the buyer may accept or reject, as stated above. B is incorrect. Again, the shipment of the non-conforming goods is not a counter-offer. And when a buyer receives non-conforming perishable goods, he is only obligated to follow directions to protect and sell them to mitigate losses for the seller, not ship them back. C is incorrect. A contract was formed, but the buyer still has the right to reject non-conforming goods.
