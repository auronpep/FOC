Question: A woman sued a company in federal district court in State A. She claimed that she had been injured by one of the company's products and could no longer work. The company's attorney did not believe the woman was as injured as she claimed and would like to have the woman physically examined by a doctor. What should the company's attorney do?
Answer A: A: File notice of request to take a physical examination.
Answer B: B: Serve the woman with a notice of deposition.
Answer C: C: Obtain a court order requiring the woman to undergo an examination.
Answer D: D: Subpoena the woman to appear at a physical examination.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Therefore, A, B, and D are incorrect.
