Question: A city requires adult-entertainment venues to keep performers at least six feet from patrons. The city relies on evidence about prostitution, assault, and alcohol-related misconduct near close-contact performances. The rule applies regardless of message or viewpoint. How should the court classify the rule?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny applies automatically because all expressive conduct receives strict scrutiny
Answer B: No First Amendment review applies because nude dancing is never expressive
Answer C: The rule is likely a content-neutral conduct regulation aimed at secondary effects
Answer D: The rule is invalid per se because it burdens adult entertainment
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The city targets proximity-related effects, not the dance's message.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Content-neutral regulation of expressive conduct can receive intermediate-style review. B: Nude dancing may contain expressive elements, though protection is limited. D: Burdens on adult entertainment are analyzed under the applicable First Amendment framework, not a per se invalidity rule.
lb_rule_reference: Nude dancing has expressive elements, but content-neutral regulations aimed at secondary effects may receive intermediate-style review.
