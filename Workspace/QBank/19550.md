Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Transfer
Question: A diversity plaintiff files in a federal district in State A where venue is improper. Rather than dismiss, the court transfers the case to a proper federal district in State B under Section 1406. The plaintiff argues State A choice-of-law rules should travel with the case. Is the plaintiff correct?
Answer A: Yes, because all federal transfers preserve transferor law
Answer B: Yes, because plaintiff chose State A
Answer C: No, because a transfer curing improper venue generally uses the transferee forum's choice-of-law rules
Answer D: No, because diversity jurisdiction disappears after transfer
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Section 1406 cures a defective filing; it does not reward the plaintiff with law from an improper forum.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Transferor-law carryover is not automatic for every transfer. B: A plaintiff's improper venue choice does not lock in favorable law. D: Transfer does not destroy diversity jurisdiction.
lb_rule_reference: Transferor-law carryover is associated with proper-forum Section 1404 convenience transfers, not improper-venue cure.
