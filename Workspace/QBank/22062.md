


BARMATRIX Q#: 22062
Outline_code: 83050101
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Mortgages
Outline: Mortgage
Question: A buyer bought a tract of land from the seller. In order to finance the purchase, the buyer obtained a mortgage from a mortgage company. The deed to the buyer was properly recorded, but the mortgage was not. A year later, the buyer sought a loan from a bank. The bank agreed to give the buyer a loan secured by a mortgage on the land. The bank properly recorded its interest. A week later, the mortgage company properly recorded its original mortgage for the land purchase. The buyer stopped paying both mortgages. The bank foreclosed, but it did not include the mortgage company as a party. A contractor bought the land at the foreclosure sale without knowing about the mortgage company's original mortgage. A week later, the mortgage company foreclosed on the land. May the mortgage company foreclose on the land even though it is now owned by the buyer?
Answer A: A: Yes, because the mortgage company was not included as a party to the bank foreclosure.
Answer B: B: Yes, because the mortgage from the mortgage company was used to purchase the property.
Answer C: C: No, because the buyer did not know about the mortgage company's original mortgage.
Answer D: D: No, because the mortgage company did not promptly record its mortgage.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Parties with interests subordinate to the foreclosing party are necessary parties to a foreclosure action. If they are not included in the foreclosure action, their interest remains despite a foreclosure and sale. Here, since the mortgage company was not a party to the bank's foreclosure, its interest remains, and it can foreclose on the buyer. B is incorrect because the fact that the mortgage company's mortgage was used to purchase the property does not change the analysis. C is incorrect because it does not matter that the buyer did not know of the mortgage company's interest. D is incorrect because the mortgage company's failure to record before the bank would only hurt any claims it had against the bank.
