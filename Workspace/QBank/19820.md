Outline: Freedom of speech and expression
Question: A city requires tattoo artists to use sterile equipment, complete infection-control training, and obtain a health license. The rule applies regardless of the tattoo's image or message. A tattoo artist argues that because tattoos are expressive, the city may not impose any licensing rule. What is the best answer?
Answer A: The rule is invalid because expressive conduct can never be regulated
Answer B: The rule is content based because tattoos contain images
Answer C: The rule is a content-neutral regulation of health risks associated with expressive conduct
Answer D: The rule is valid only if it is the least restrictive possible health rule
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The city regulates noncommunicative health risks without targeting tattoo messages.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Expressive activity can be subject to content-neutral health and safety rules. B: The rule turns on sanitation and licensing, not image or message content. D: Content-neutral conduct regulation does not require the least restrictive means standard of strict scrutiny.
lb_rule_reference: A content-neutral licensing rule for the noncommunicative health risks of tattooing is analyzed differently from a ban on tattoo messages or viewpoints.
