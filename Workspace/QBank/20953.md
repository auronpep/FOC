Question: City law made it a misdemeanor to ignite large fireworks in a crowded public plaza. During a festival, Omar lit a large illegal firework in the middle of the crowd. It exploded sideways, struck a spectator in the head, and killed her. What is the best homicide classification?
Answer A: Felony murder, because a death occurred during an unlawful act
Answer B: Involuntary manslaughter, because the dangerous misdemeanor proximately caused death
Answer C: No homicide liability, because Omar did not intend the firework to strike anyone
Answer D: First-degree murder, because fireworks can be deadly
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Omar committed a misdemeanor designed to prevent dangerous explosions in crowds. That unlawful act directly caused the spectator's death. Involuntary manslaughter is supported.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The predicate is a misdemeanor, not a felony-murder predicate. The better theory is unlawful-act involuntary manslaughter. C: Unlawful-act manslaughter does not require intent to kill. The dangerous unlawful act caused death. D: The facts do not show intent to kill, premeditation, or an enumerated first-degree trigger. Involuntary manslaughter best fits.
lb_rule_reference: Unlawful-act manslaughter can apply when a misdemeanor or non-felony unlawful act is sufficiently dangerous and proximately causes death.
