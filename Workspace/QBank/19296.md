


BARMATRIX Q#: 19296
Outline_code: 94010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Federal Subject-Matter Jurisdiction
Question: A man sued a doctor in federal court. The doctor decided to answer the complaint. Which of the following potential defenses will not be waived if doctor does not include the defense in his answer?
Answer A: A: Insufficient service of process.
Answer B: B: Improper venue.
Answer C: C: Lack of personal jurisdiction.
Answer D: D: Lack of subject matter jurisdiction.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The defense of lack of subject matter jurisdiction can be raised at any time, even on appeal. However, under Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(b), insufficient service of process, improper venue, and lack of personal jurisdiction must be raised at the time a party responds to the complaint either by filing a motion or an an-swer. Therefore, A, B, and C are incorrect.
