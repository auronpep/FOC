Question: A buyer orders 500 bushels of grade No. 1 wheat from a merchant seller for prompt shipment. The seller ships 500 bushels of grade No. 2 wheat without any explanation or notice that the shipment is only an accommodation. The buyer rejects the shipment and sues for breach. What is the best analysis?
Answer A: The shipment was only a counteroffer because it was nonconforming.
Answer B: The nonconforming shipment accepted the order and breached the contract.
Answer C: No contract was formed because the seller did not mirror the offer exactly.
Answer D: The buyer's rejection of the wheat waived any breach claim.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Under Article 2, a nonconforming shipment ordinarily operates as an acceptance and breach unless the seller seasonably notifies the buyer that the shipment is offered only as an accommodation. There was no accommodation notice.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A: a nonconforming shipment is a counteroffer only if accompanied by a seasonable accommodation notice. C: Article 2 does not apply the common-law mirror-image rule this way. D: rejecting nonconforming goods does not waive the buyer's breach claim.
