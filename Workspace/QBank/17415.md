Question: A city council opens meetings with brief prayers delivered by invited clergy from different faith traditions. Members of the public are not required to participate, and the prayers do not disparage other faiths or pressure attendees to join. What is the best Establishment Clause analysis?
Answer A: The practice is automatically invalid because any prayer at a government meeting establishes religion
Answer B: The practice may be permissible given historical tradition and lack of coercion or disparagement
Answer C: The practice is automatically valid because any historical religious practice is constitutional
Answer D: The practice is valid only if the city excludes all clergy and uses secular statements
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The facts align with permissible legislative-prayer practice.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Legislative prayer has historical support when not coercive or discriminatory. C: History matters, but coercion, proselytizing, and discrimination can still create problems. D: The Establishment Clause does not require government to remove all religious references from public life.
lb_rule_reference: Legislative prayer may be permissible when consistent with historical tradition and not used to proselytize, disparage, or coerce participation.
