Question: A city inspector tells a restaurant owner, "Your permit renewal is complete, but unless you pay me $1,000 personally, I will delay signing it for months." The owner pays the inspector to avoid the delay. What is the best theft-related offense?
Answer A: Extortion, because the inspector obtained payment by exploiting official authority
Answer B: Robbery, because the owner paid out of fear
Answer C: No offense, because the owner chose to pay
Answer D: False pretenses, because the inspector lied about the renewal being complete
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The inspector wrongfully used official power to obtain money not owed to him.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: There was no immediate force taking from person or presence. The official-position threat fits extortion. C: Payment coerced by wrongful official pressure is not clean consent. D: The payment was obtained through threat of official delay, not by transferring title because of a false present fact.
lb_rule_reference: A public official commits extortion when the official obtains property not owed by exploiting official position or threatening official action.
