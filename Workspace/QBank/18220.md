Question: A city park rule bars amplified sound after 10 p.m. to protect nearby residents from nighttime noise. Speakers may use amplification during the day and may speak without amplification after 10 p.m. The rule applies to all topics and viewpoints. What standard applies?
Answer A: The rule is invalid because speech in a park can never be regulated
Answer B: The rule must be the least restrictive possible way to reduce noise
Answer C: The rule is judged as a content-neutral time-place-manner restriction serving noise-control interests and leaving alternatives
Answer D: The rule is content based because amplified speech is louder than unamplified speech
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The rule applies without regard to message, serves a significant noise interest, and leaves other channels.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Traditional public forums allow reasonable content-neutral time-place-manner restrictions. B: Content-neutral time-place-manner rules need narrow tailoring, not strict-scrutiny least restrictive means. D: Volume is a manner of speech, not the communicative content.
lb_rule_reference: In a public forum, a content-neutral time-place-manner restriction must serve a significant interest, be narrowly tailored, and leave open ample alternative channels.
