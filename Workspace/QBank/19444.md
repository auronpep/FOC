Question: Moses conveyed Whiteacre “to Esther for life, then to Aaron and Ruth as tenants in common.” Aaron later conveyed his entire remainder interest to Esther. Ruth did not convey her interest. Which statement best describes Esther's interest after Aaron's conveyance?
Answer A: Esther owns fee simple absolute in all of Whiteacre because she acquired one remainder interest.
Answer B: Esther owns her life estate and Aaron's remainder share, but Ruth's remainder remains outstanding.
Answer C: Aaron's conveyance is void because remainders are never transferable.
Answer D: Ruth's remainder is destroyed because Esther now owns the present estate.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Esther owns her life estate and Aaron's remainder share, but Ruth's remainder remains outstanding. Aaron could transfer his vested remainder share, but Esther did not acquire Ruth's share. Esther therefore did not become owner of the entire fee simple absolute.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because acquiring one co-remainderman's share does not eliminate another co-remainderman's interest. C: C is wrong because vested remainder interests are generally transferable. D: D is wrong because the life tenant cannot destroy Ruth's remainder by acquiring someone else's future interest.
lb_rule_reference: Estate merger / partial remainder acquisition
