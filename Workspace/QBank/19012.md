Outline: Counterclaims
Question: Plaintiff seeks legal damages. Defendant serves an answer denying liability on June 1. No counterclaim is filed. Plaintiff first requests a jury orally at the final pretrial conference in October. Defendant objects that the jury right was waived. Is defendant likely correct?
Answer A: No, because a jury may be demanded orally at any time before trial
Answer B: No, because legal damages claims can never waive jury trial
Answer C: Yes, because plaintiff failed to serve a timely written jury demand after the last relevant pleading
Answer D: Yes, but only because defendant filed a counterclaim
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The October oral request came long after the 14-day Rule 38 window.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Rule 38 requires a timely written demand. B: The jury right can be waived by missing the demand deadline. D: The facts state no counterclaim was filed.
lb_rule_reference: A party must serve a written jury demand no later than 14 days after service of the last pleading directed to the jury-triable issue.
