Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Personal Jurisdiction
Question: Plaintiff and Defendant, both State A citizens, are in a car crash in State A. Defendant owns a small bank account in State B but has no other contacts with State B. Plaintiff sues Defendant in State B and attaches the bank account, arguing that the property gives State B power to decide the entire car-crash claim. What is the best analysis?
Answer A: State B may decide the entire car-crash claim because any property in the forum creates personal jurisdiction
Answer B: State B may decide the claim because the crash injured Plaintiff, and plaintiffs choose the forum
Answer C: State B likely lacks personal jurisdiction for the unrelated car-crash claim despite the attached bank account
Answer D: State B has jurisdiction only if the account contains enough money to satisfy the whole judgment
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The claim arose in State A between State A parties, and the State B account is unrelated collateral.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The property is unrelated to the claim. Due process still requires an appropriate jurisdictional connection. B: Plaintiff's forum choice does not create personal jurisdiction over Defendant. D: The due-process problem is missing claim-related contacts, not the amount in the account.
lb_rule_reference: Attaching a defendant's unrelated property in the forum does not avoid due-process limits for a claim unrelated to that property.
