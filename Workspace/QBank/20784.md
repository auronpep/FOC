Outline: Mistake / Lack of Mutual Assent
Question: Ruth agrees to buy a breeding ram from Boaz for $12,000. Before signing, both Ruth and Boaz review recent veterinary records showing that the ram is fertile. Unknown to both, the records were for a different ram with the same tag number, and this ram was permanently sterile before the contract was made. The parties did not allocate the risk of sterility. Ruth refuses to pay and seeks to avoid the contract. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Ruth may not avoid the contract because the mistake concerns the value of the ram only.
Answer B: Ruth may avoid the contract because both parties were mistaken about an existing fact that was a basic assumption and materially affected the agreed exchange.
Answer C: Ruth may avoid the contract only if Boaz knew the ram was sterile.
Answer D: Ruth may not avoid the contract because the contract was supported by consideration.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A mutual mistake defense applies when both parties were mistaken at the time of contracting about an existing fact that was a basic assumption of the bargain, the mistake materially affected the exchange, and the party seeking avoidance did not bear the risk. Fertility was central to the sale of a breeding ram, and neither party allocated that risk to Ruth.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the mistake is not merely about market value; it concerns the ram's existing fertility, the core fact underlying the bargain. C: C is wrong because mutual mistake does not require the other party's knowledge or fraud. D: D is wrong because consideration and mutual mistake are separate gates; a contract can be supported by consideration and still be voidable for mistake.
