Question: Daniel hired Mary to repair a set of office chairs for $4,000. Mary completed the repairs on time. The contract also required Mary to send Daniel a written maintenance checklist within one week after the repairs. Mary forgot to send the checklist, though Daniel could still use all of the chairs and suffered only a small cost to create his own checklist. Daniel refused to pay anything. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: Daniel owes nothing because any broken promise prevents payment from becoming due.
Answer B: Daniel must pay the contract price, less any proven damages from the missed checklist.
Answer C: Daniel owes only the reasonable value of the repairs because Mary breached a separate covenant.
Answer D: Daniel may treat the contract as totally breached because the checklist promise appeared in the same writing.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Mary completed the chair repairs, so Daniel's main payment duty matured. The missed checklist is a covenant breach that may support damages, but on these facts it does not discharge Daniel's duty to pay for the completed repairs.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because it collapses every covenant into a condition. C: C is wrong because the contract remains enforceable; recovery is not automatically reduced to quantum meruit. D: D is wrong because appearing in the same writing does not make the checklist promise a condition or total breach.
