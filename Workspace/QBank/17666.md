Outline: Mistake / Lack of Mutual Assent
Question: Hannah buys a small storefront from Paul because she privately hopes that her son will move nearby and help operate it. Paul does not know about that reason. After signing, Hannah's son decides to move to another city. Hannah seeks rescission, arguing that her reason for buying the storefront failed. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Deny rescission because Hannah's private collateral motive was not a shared basic assumption of the contract.
Answer B: Grant rescission because any buyer's reason for entering a contract is a basic assumption.
Answer C: Grant rescission because Paul did not warn Hannah that her son might move.
Answer D: Deny rescission only if the storefront increased in value after signing.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: A private motive or expectation known only to one party is not a shared basic assumption of the contract. The failed personal plan does not create a mutual mistake or lack of assent.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because not every motive is a basic assumption. C: C is wrong because Paul had no duty to warn about Hannah's private family plan. D: D is wrong because market value after signing is not the basis for denying rescission.
lb_rule_reference: Collateral motive / no defense
