Question: A plaintiff sues Driver A and Driver B for the same highway accident. Driver A asserts that Driver B was negligent and must reimburse Driver A for any amount Driver A pays the plaintiff. What is Driver A's claim against Driver B?
Answer A: A counterclaim, because it is a claim asserted by a defendant
Answer B: A crossclaim, because it is a claim by one co-party against another arising from the same accident
Answer C: An impleader claim, because Driver B may be liable for the plaintiff's damages
Answer D: A required-party motion, because Driver B's fault must be adjudicated
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Driver A and Driver B are co-defendants, and the reimbursement claim arises from the same transaction or occurrence.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A counterclaim is against an opposing party, not a co-defendant. C: Impleader brings in a nonparty; Driver B is already a party. D: Driver B is already a party, and the issue is the proper claim label.
lb_rule_reference: A counterclaim is asserted against an opposing party; a crossclaim is asserted against a co-party and must satisfy Rule 13(g).
