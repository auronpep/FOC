Outline: Clergy and penitent
Question: After a service, Simon whispered to his brother James in the hallway, “I took the missing funds.” Pastor Amos happened to walk by and overheard the whisper. Simon later claims clergy-penitent privilege to stop Pastor Amos from testifying. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection, because a pastor heard the statement inside a church building.
Answer B: Overrule the objection, because Simon was not communicating to Pastor Amos for spiritual counseling.
Answer C: Sustain the objection unless James also testifies.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if Simon was outside the sanctuary.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The privilege protects confidential communications made to clergy in the spiritual capacity. Simon whispered to James; Pastor Amos merely overheard. The decisive axis is intended clergy communication, not location.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because Church location and clergy presence do not create privilege without a communication to clergy. C: C is wrong because James's testimony is not needed to decide whether Simon communicated to Pastor Amos. D: D is wrong because The sanctuary line is not the privilege boundary; the communication route is.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 501 / communication must be directed to clergy
lb_student_diagnostic: Student is using place and title rather than asking who the statement was addressed to and why.
