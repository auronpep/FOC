Question: A manufacturer sues a distributor over whether a supply contract permits early termination. Another distributor with a separate but identical contract moves to intervene to assert its own contract claim against the manufacturer. The motion is filed early, before discovery begins. Which statement best describes permissive intervention?
Answer A: The second distributor must be allowed to intervene because its contract is identical
Answer B: The court may allow intervention if the motion is timely and the claim shares a common question, while considering undue delay or prejudice
Answer C: The distributor can intervene only if it satisfies numerosity, commonality, typicality, and adequacy
Answer D: Intervention is barred because the distributor could file a separate lawsuit
Correct Answer: B
lb_rule_reference: Permissive intervention requires a timely motion and a claim or defense sharing a common question of law or fact with the main action; the court must consider undue delay or prejudice.
