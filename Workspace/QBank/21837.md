Outline_code: 93110300
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Appealability and Review
Outline: Scope of Review
Question: A concert promoter sued a rock singer for breach of contract in federal district court after the rock singer failed to perform at a charity concert in State A. The federal district court ruled in favor of the rock singer, finding his signature had been forged on the contract. The concert promoter appealed, arguing the signature was really the rock singer's. What standard of review should the appellate court use?
Answer A: A: De novo, because the validity of the signature is a question of law.
Answer B: B: Clearly erroneous, because the validity of the signature is a question of fact.
Answer C: C: Abuse of discretion, because the validity of the signature is a question of law.
Answer D: D: Abuse of discretion, because the validity of the signature is a question of fact.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A is incorrect because a question of law is an issue that must be decided by applying legal principles (for example, whether a police officer had probable cause to arrest a suspect). C and D are incorrect because abuse of discretion is the standard applied to the discretionary decisions of a trial court, such as leaves to amend.
