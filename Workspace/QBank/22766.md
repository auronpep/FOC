Stem / Fact Pattern: Which of the following motions is lost if a party fails to make it before filing an answer to a complaint?
Answer A: A: Motion to dismiss for lack of subject matter jurisdiction.
Answer B: B: Motion to dismiss for lack of personal jurisdiction.
Answer C: C: Motion for a more definite statement.
Answer D: D: Motion to dismiss for insufficient service of process.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Under Fed. R. Civ. P. 12(e), if a party finds that a complaint is so vague or ambiguous that the party cannot reasonably frame a response, the party may move for a more definite statement. However, such a motion must be made before the party responds by filing an answer or other reply. A is incorrect because lack of subject matter jurisdiction can be raised at any time, even on appeal. B and D are incorrect because they can be raised at the time the party files a motion or his or her answer.
