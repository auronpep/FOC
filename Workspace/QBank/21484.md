Question: In a federal fraud trial, Thomas testifies for the defendant. The prosecutor impeaches Thomas with reputation testimony that he is untruthful. The defendant then calls Philip to testify that Thomas once returned a lost purse containing money. The defendant offers the story to prove Thomas is a truthful person. The prosecutor objects. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Admit the testimony because Thomas's truthfulness has been attacked.
Answer B: Admit the testimony because returning lost money is a specific truthful act.
Answer C: Exclude the testimony because support for truthfulness must be by reputation or opinion, not proof of specific truthful acts.
Answer D: Exclude the testimony only if Thomas denies returning the purse.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: After Thomas's character for truthfulness has been attacked, the defendant may offer reputation or opinion evidence supporting truthfulness. But Rule 608 does not permit direct proof of specific truthful acts to support the witness's character for truthfulness.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the attack opens the door only to proper forms of support. B: B is wrong because the specific act is the wrong proof form. D: D is wrong because Thomas's admission or denial of the purse story is not the issue.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 608(a) and 608(b) / specific acts not direct proof of truthful character
lb_student_diagnostic: Tests form-of-proof control: rehabilitation after attack does not authorize specific truthful acts.
