Outline: Rule of completeness
Question: At a federal civil trial, Leah testifies that Abraham said during a meeting, “I will pay only half the debt.” Abraham asks to elicit Leah's testimony that, in the same conversation, he immediately added, “because half has already been paid.” Leah objects that Rule 106 applies only to writings and recordings, not to oral statements. What is the best ruling under the Federal Rules of Evidence?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because Rule 106 applies only to writings and recorded statements.
Answer B: Sustain the objection because Abraham's additional words are inadmissible whenever they are self-serving.
Answer C: Overrule the objection if fairness requires the added words to be considered with the partial oral statement.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if Abraham first proves by a preponderance of the evidence that the debt was actually half paid.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The current federal rule of completeness covers statements, including oral statements, when fairness requires additional parts to be considered at the same time. Abraham's added words directly qualify the partial statement Leah introduced.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong under the current federal rule, which is no longer limited to writings and recordings. B: B is wrong because a completing statement is not excluded merely because it helps the adverse party. D: D is wrong because Rule 106 focuses on correcting the misleading use of the statement; the court need not first resolve the truth of the completed assertion as a merits fact.
