Question: Jeremiah contracted to sell commercial refrigerators to Rachel for $24,000. Rachel received the refrigerators, inspected them, accepted them, and used them in her store, but then refused to pay the price. The refrigerators conformed to the contract. Which remedy is Jeremiah most likely entitled to?
Answer A: Only market damages, because a seller can never sue for the price of goods.
Answer B: Only nominal damages, because Rachel's use of the goods is not a breach.
Answer C: An action for the $24,000 price because Rachel accepted the conforming goods and failed to pay.
Answer D: No remedy because Jeremiah did not resell the refrigerators.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A seller may recover the price of goods accepted by the buyer when the buyer fails to pay as the price becomes due. Rachel accepted and used conforming goods, so an action for the price is proper.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the UCC allows an action for the price in defined situations, including accepted goods. B: B is wrong because failure to pay for accepted conforming goods is a real breach. D: D is wrong because resale is not required where the buyer accepted the goods and failed to pay.
lb_rule_reference: UCC §2-709(1)(a)
