Question: A state prohibits physicians from prescribing lethal medication to help competent adults end their lives. A patient challenges the law under substantive due process, describing the right as a broad right to personal autonomy in all medical decisions. What is the state's strongest response?
Answer A: The claimed right should be carefully described, and physician-assisted suicide is not a fundamental right under federal substantive due process
Answer B: Any serious personal decision is fundamental and receives strict scrutiny
Answer C: The law is invalid because substantive due process always protects medical autonomy
Answer D: The patient wins because procedural due process requires a hearing before any medical regulation
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The doctrine does not accept an overly broad autonomy framing to create a fundamental right.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Fundamental-right status requires careful description and deep rooting, not just seriousness. C: The right cannot be defined at that level of generality for this claim. D: The challenge is substantive, and no hearing rule invalidates the law on these facts.
lb_rule_reference: A claimed substantive due process right must be carefully described and deeply rooted; physician-assisted suicide is not recognized as a fundamental right under federal substantive due process.
