Question: A merchant seller agreed to deliver wool to a merchant buyer each Friday. After two weeks of late deliveries, the buyer said, “I will accept Monday deliveries for now.” Before the next shipment was due, and before the seller changed shipping arrangements or incurred costs, the buyer sent a reasonable written notice requiring Friday delivery again. What is the best result under Article 2?
Answer A: The buyer can never retract a waiver once it has accepted late deliveries.
Answer B: The buyer can retract only if the seller gives new consideration.
Answer C: The buyer's notice is ineffective because late delivery was waived forever.
Answer D: The buyer may retract the waiver by reasonable notification because the seller had not materially changed position in reliance on it.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: Article 2 allows retraction of a waiver affecting an executory portion of the contract by reasonable notification that strict performance will be required, unless retraction would be unjust because of material reliance. Here, no material reliance occurred.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A and C are wrong because waiver is not always permanent. B is wrong because waiver retraction does not require new consideration. The correct analysis turns on reasonable notice and material reliance. B: B is wrong because waiver retraction does not require new consideration.
