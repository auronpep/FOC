


BARMATRIX Q#: 22759
Outline_code: 94030100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Removal to Federal Court
Question: A man met with the board of a local beach club and told them he planned to sue the club and the board individually for violations of the Equal Protection Act. A week later, the man filed his complaint in state court. The club and three members of the board were served on that same day. The other two members of the board were served a week later. The club and four members of the board petitioned for removal two weeks after they were served. The final board member joined the petition for removal two weeks later. The court follows the majority rule for such petitions. For removal to be proper, when must the final board member have joined the petition for removal?
Answer A: A: Within 30 days from when the suit was filed.
Answer B: B: Within 30 days from when the first defendant was served.
Answer C: C: Within 30 days from when the final board member knew about the lawsuit.
Answer D: D: Within 30 days from when the final board member was served.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: In a federal question case, all defendants must consent or join a removal petition for removal to be proper. Individual defendants have 30 days from service to join in an otherwise valid removal petition. Here, the initial removal was requested two weeks after the initial service, making it a valid removal. Under the majority rule, the final board member would then have 30 days from the time he was served to join the valid removal petition. Consequently, A, B, and C are incorrect.
