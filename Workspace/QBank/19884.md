Question: Jacob owns Lot A. Leah owns adjoining Lot B. Leah's deed states that Lot B is “subject to a right of the owner of Lot A to use the stone lane on Lot B for access to the public road.” Which statement correctly identifies the parcels?
Answer A: Lot A is the dominant estate, and Lot B is the servient estate.
Answer B: Lot B is the dominant estate, and Lot A is the servient estate.
Answer C: Both lots are dominant estates because both are described in the deed.
Answer D: Neither lot is servient until Jacob personally crosses the lane every day.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The dominant estate is the land benefited by an appurtenant easement. The servient estate is the land burdened by the easement. Here, the right benefits Lot A by giving access and burdens Lot B because the lane is on Lot B.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: B is wrong because it reverses the benefit and burden. C: C is wrong because a parcel is not dominant merely because it is mentioned. D: D is wrong because daily personal use is not required to identify the burdened land.
