MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Life estates
Question: As part of a divorce settlement between husband and wife, the wife conveyed a parcel of realty “to my ex-husband for life; remainder to my children who survive my ex-husband.” Which of the following interests does the ex-husband have the power to convey to the buyer?
Answer A: A: The right to possess the realty until the ex-husband's death.
Answer B: B: The right to possess the realty until his ex-wife's death.
Answer C: C: The right to possess the realty until the death of the buyer.
Answer D: D: No right to possess the realty.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The ex-husband may convey the interest that he holds. Since his interest is a life estate, A is correct.
Wrong Answer Explanations: Since the ex-wife or the buyer might outlive the ex-husband, B and C are incorrect. Since life estates are alienable, D is also incorrect.
