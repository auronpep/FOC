Question: Congress validly regulates interstate shipment of hazardous chemicals. A state passes a law declaring that the federal statute is void within the state because chemical safety is reserved to the states under the Tenth Amendment. Which statement is most accurate?
Answer A: The state may nullify the federal law because the Tenth Amendment reserves all safety regulation to the states
Answer B: The state may not nullify a valid federal law; the federal statute preempts conflicting state law
Answer C: The state may nullify the federal law unless Congress offers funding
Answer D: The federal law is invalid because states have exclusive authority over anything dangerous
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The Supremacy Clause controls when Congress acts within its powers.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Valid federal law is supreme, and the Tenth Amendment does not authorize state nullification. C: Preemption of conflicting state law does not require a spending offer. D: Congress can regulate interstate shipment of hazardous goods under commerce power.
lb_rule_reference: The Tenth Amendment does not allow a state to nullify or disregard valid federal law; valid federal law is supreme over conflicting state law.
