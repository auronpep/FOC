


BARMATRIX Q#: 20594
Outline_code: 61020104
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Torts
Subtopic: Negligence
Outline: Affirmative duties to act
Question: If the plaintiff asserts a claim against the defendant for the damage that he sustained, the court should find for
Answer A: A: the plaintiff, because his damage resulted from the defendant's failure to act reasonably.
Answer B: B: the plaintiff, because a rescuer is entitled to indemnity from the person whom he or she was attempting to rescue.
Answer C: C: the defendant, because the plaintiff was an officious intermeddler.
Answer D: D: the defendant, because the defendant was unaware that the plaintiff would attempt to rescue him.
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: As a general rule, the courts hold that the coming of a rescuer is foreseeable. This is what is meant by the well-known phrase “Danger invites rescue.” For this reason, one who unreasonably creates a danger to any human being — including himself or herself — creates a risk to a potential rescuer as well and owes a potential rescuer a duty of reasonable care. Thus, if the defendant's conduct was unreasonable, it was a breach of a duty he owed the plaintiff.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B is based on an over-inclusive statement of law — a rescuer's right to collect from the person whom he or she rescued must be based either on the breach of duty to act reasonably or on an implied contract. An officious intermeddler is a person who acts without a legitimate reason. C is incorrect because a human life was at stake, giving the plaintiff a legitimate reason to act. The rule that is characterized by the phrase “Danger invites rescue” makes D incorrect because the rescuer's attempt to rescue the defendant is regarded as foreseeable for the reason given.
