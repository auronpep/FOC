Question: 25 years ago, a seller conveyed Blackacre to a buyer by a warranty deed. The seller at that time also executed and delivered an instrument in the proper form of a deed, purporting to convey Whiteacre to the buyer. The seller thought she had title to Whiteacre but did not; therefore, no title passed by virtue of the Whiteacre deed. Whiteacre consisted of three acres of brushland adjoining the west boundary of Blackacre. The buyer has occasionally hunted rabbits on Whiteacre, but less often than annually. No one else came on Whiteacre except occasional rabbit hunters. 20 years ago, the buyer planted a row of evergreens in the vicinity of the opposite (east) boundary of Blackacre and erected a fence just beyond the evergreens to the east. In fact both the trees and the fence were placed on Greenacre, owned by a neighbor, which bordered the east boundary of Blackacre. The buyer was unsure of the exact boundary, and placed the trees and the fence in order to establish his rights up to the fence. The fence is located 10 feet within Greenacre. Now, the buyer has had his property surveyed and the title checked and has learned the facts. The period of time to acquire title by adverse possession in the jurisdiction is 15 years. The buyer consulted his lawyer, who properly advised that, in an appropriate action, the buyer would probably obtain title to
Answer A: A: Whiteacre but not the 10-foot strip of Greenacre.
Answer B: B: the 10-foot strip of Greenacre but not to Whiteacre.
Answer C: C: both Whiteacre and the 10-foot strip of Greenacre.
Answer D: D: neither Whiteacre nor the 10-foot strip of Greenacre.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: B is correct. Color of title as to Whiteacre is irrelevant, as the buyer has not fulfilled the other elements of adverse possession. The buyer has, however, obtained possession of the 10-foot strip of Greenacre by erecting a fence (exclusive) and planting trees (open and notorious), without permission (hostile), and maintaining his actual possession continuously for the 15-year statutory period.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A is incorrect. The buyer has not acquired Whiteacre because he has failed to exclude others from the property (i.e. the hunters) and has not maintained possession continuously or openly (visiting the land less often than annually). C is incorrect. As explained above, the buyer has not sufficiently proven the elements of adverse possession as to any portion of Whiteacre but has obtained possession of the strip of Greenacre. D is incorrect. As explained above, the buyer has adversely possessed the strip of Greenacre.
