Question: In a federal battery trial, Ruth offers a photograph of her injury taken the night of the incident. The photograph is unpleasant but accurately shows the extent of swelling that Ruth claims was caused by Adam. Adam objects that the jury should decide whether the photograph is too prejudicial. Who decides whether the photograph should be excluded because its probative value is substantially outweighed by unfair prejudice?
Answer A: The jury, because it will see the photograph if admitted.
Answer B: The judge, because admissibility and balancing are court rulings.
Answer C: The jury, unless both parties agree to let the judge decide.
Answer D: The judge, but only after the jury first views the photograph.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The judge determines admissibility, including whether relevant evidence should be excluded under the balancing rule. If the photograph is admitted, the jury evaluates its weight.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A and C are wrong because admissibility balancing is not assigned to the jury.
