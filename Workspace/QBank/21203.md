Question: A state allows residents to buy undeveloped residential land but bars citizens of other states from buying the same land unless they pay a special approval fee. A nonresident citizen challenges the restriction under Article IV. What is the best analysis?
Answer A: Article IV is implicated because ownership of property is an important civil privilege for nonresident citizens
Answer B: Article IV cannot apply because land is local, not interstate commerce
Answer C: The state wins automatically because property law is always local
Answer D: The nonresident must sue as a corporation to invoke Article IV
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: Property ownership is within the kind of important privilege Article IV can protect against nonresident discrimination.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Article IV is about citizen privileges, not whether the land itself moves in commerce. C: Traditional state control does not allow unjustified discrimination against nonresident citizens in protected privileges. D: Article IV protects natural citizens; corporations are excluded.
lb_rule_reference: Article IV can protect nonresident citizens from discrimination in important civil privileges such as owning property, subject to substantial reason and fit.
