Question: In a federal trial, the judge tells the jury, “A fact may be proved by direct evidence, by circumstantial evidence, or by both. You decide what weight to give each.” Noah objects that the instruction should say direct evidence is preferred. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because direct evidence is preferred as a matter of evidence law.
Answer B: Sustain the objection unless the circumstantial evidence is corroborated.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because the instruction properly leaves weight to the jury without creating a hierarchy between proof types.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only in a criminal case.
Correct Answer: C
