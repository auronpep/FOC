Question: A farm bought six seed-sorter machines from a supplier under a contract that included ordinary installation and calibration. Most of the price was allocated to the machines. The writing required each shipment to include a “standard adapter kit,” but did not define the phrase. During the first three shipments under the same contract, the supplier included two hydraulic adapters per sorter and the farm accepted and paid. In several earlier contracts between the same parties, the supplier also included two adapters. The supplier now offers evidence that the general farm-equipment trade treats one adapter as a standard kit. Which statement is least likely to be accepted?
Answer A: Because the transaction is predominantly a sale of goods, UCC meaning rules may apply despite incidental installation and calibration services.
Answer B: The parties' repeated performance under the same contract is a stronger meaning source than a general trade usage if the sources cannot reasonably be reconciled.
Answer C: The general trade usage automatically controls because it reflects industry practice rather than the parties' private conduct.
Answer D: The court should consider agreement sources such as course of performance and course of dealing before using a default gap-filler.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The narrow rule is that UCC agreement sources are ranked when they cannot reasonably be harmonized: express terms come first, then course of performance, then course of dealing, then usage of trade. The trigger facts are a goods-dominant mixed contract, three same-contract shipments, prior transactions, and a conflicting general usage. The decisive locks are source law and hierarchy. C is least likely because it reverses the hierarchy by making general usage automatically superior.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is not least likely because incidental installation and calibration do not defeat Article 2 when goods predominate. B is not least likely because same-contract course of performance is a high-priority meaning source. D is not least likely because agreement sources precede default gap-fillers.
