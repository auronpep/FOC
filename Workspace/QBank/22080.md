Outline_code: 83050101
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Mortgages
Outline: Mortgage
Question: A man owned a piece of land with a mortgage on it held by a bank. The man then got a second mortgage on the same land from a mortgage company. The man then got a loan from a financing company that he used to pay off part of the second mortgage from the mortgage company. Finally, the man got a mortgage from a housing lender that he used to immediately pay off the mortgage held by the bank. All of the mortgages were properly recorded. Who holds the senior mortgage on the property?
Answer A: A: The bank.
Answer B: B: The mortgage company.
Answer C: C: The financing company.
Answer D: D: The housing lender.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: If a person takes out a new mortgage for the purpose of paying off or refinancing a preexisting senior mortgage, the new mortgage takes the senior mortgage's priority position. Here, since the man used the mortgage from the housing lender to pay off the bank mortgage, the mortgage from the housing lender is now the senior mortgage on the property. A, B, and C are therefore incorrect.
