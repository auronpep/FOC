Question: Caleb signed a deed giving Joshua “the right to enter Caleb's meadow each summer for five years to graze sheep and cut and remove hay.” The deed gives Joshua no other use of the meadow. How is Joshua's right best classified?
Answer A: An affirmative easement only, because Joshua must enter the meadow.
Answer B: A revocable license because the right lasts only five years.
Answer C: A profit because Joshua may enter and take produce from Caleb's land.
Answer D: A covenant because the deed limits Joshua's use to summer.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A profit is a nonpossessory right to enter land and remove natural resources or produce from it. Grazing and cutting hay involve taking the land's produce. The limited duration defines the scope but does not convert the right into a license.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because the entry right is incidental to removal of produce, which is the profit trigger. B: B is wrong because a deeded five-year property right is not revocable merely because it is limited in time. D: D is wrong because use limits do not convert a resource-removal right into a covenant.
lb_rule_reference: Profit / taking produce from land
