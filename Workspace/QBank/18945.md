Question: A state funds school districts largely through local property taxes, producing large disparities between wealthy and poor districts. Parents in a poor district bring only a federal Equal Protection claim. What is the likely scrutiny framework?
Answer A: Strict scrutiny applies because wealth is a suspect classification and education is fundamental
Answer B: Rational basis generally applies absent another suspect classification or fundamental-right burden
Answer C: Intermediate scrutiny applies because schoolchildren are politically powerless
Answer D: The claim receives no review because school funding is a state issue
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: The claim does not involve a recognized suspect class or fundamental right for federal Equal Protection tier purposes.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Wealth is not suspect and education is not generally a fundamental right under Equal Protection. C: Age and childhood do not create a general intermediate-scrutiny class here. D: State laws remain subject to federal Equal Protection, even when rational basis applies.
lb_rule_reference: Wealth is not a suspect classification, and education is not generally a fundamental right under Equal Protection.
