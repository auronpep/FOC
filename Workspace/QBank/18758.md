Question: Rebekah signed a final but incomplete agreement to buy “the black ox” from Eli for $900. Before signing, Eli orally promised that he would also deliver a yoke. At the same time, Eli orally said Rebekah could take the white ox instead if she preferred. Rebekah later offers both oral statements. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Admit both statements because the writing was incomplete.
Answer B: Exclude both statements because both were oral.
Answer C: Admit the yoke statement but exclude the white-ox statement.
Answer D: Exclude the yoke statement but admit the white-ox statement.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A partial integration may be supplemented by consistent additional terms, but it cannot be contradicted by prior or contemporaneous terms. The yoke term supplements the sale of the black ox. The white-ox option contradicts the written identification of the black ox.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because incomplete writing does not allow contradiction. B: B is wrong because oral status alone does not bar consistent additional terms. D reverses the supplement/contradict distinction. D: A is wrong because incomplete writing does not allow contradiction. B is wrong because oral status alone does not bar consistent additional terms. D reverses the supplement/contradict distinction.
