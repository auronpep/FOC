


BARMATRIX Q#: 22669
subject_display: Constitutional Law
topic: Judicial Review
subtopic: Constitutional limitations and justiciability
difficulty: 43020201
Stem / Fact Pattern: Because a revolution in a Central American republic threatens to topple its government, the President of the United States orders federal troops to invade the republic. Congress subsequently passes a resolution directing the President to recall the troops. When the President refuses to do so, a proceeding is instituted in a United States district court seeking an order compelling the President to comply with the congressional directive. Which of the following would be the President’s most effective argument in opposition to that proceeding?
Answer A: A: United States district courts lack power over the President.
Answer B: B: The President is Commander- in- Chief of the armed forces.
Answer C: C: The matter is a non- justiciable political question.
Answer D: D: The presidential order sending troops to the republic amounted to a declaration of war.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: An issue presents a non- justiciable political question when a decision would unduly interfere with the exercise of powers vested by the Constitution in other co- equal branches of government. Although it is by no means certain that this is such an issue, C is the only one of the arguments listed that has any possibility of success. It is not clear how a federal court could go about enforcing process against the President if he or she refused to obey a judicial order, but it is generally understood — and was so held in United States v. Nixon, 418 U.S. 683 (1974) — that the federal courts have jurisdiction over the President. A is therefore incorrect. B is incorrect because, although the President is Commander- in- Chief of the armed forces, his or her power as such is subject to limitations imposed by Congress. The United States Constitution gives Congress — not the President — the power to declare war. Thus, if the presidential order sending troops to the republic was a declaration of war, it would be constitutionally invalid. D is therefore incorrect.
