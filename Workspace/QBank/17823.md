Outline: Larceny by trick
Question: Victor rents a camera for one afternoon by using a fake name and fake address. At the moment he receives the camera, Victor intends to keep it permanently. The rental shop intends to transfer only temporary possession, not title. What is Victor's best theft offense?
Answer A: Larceny by trick, because deception obtained possession only while title remained with the shop
Answer B: False pretenses, because all fraud involving property is false pretenses
Answer C: Embezzlement, because Victor lawfully possessed the camera after the rental
Answer D: No offense, because rental shops assume customers might not return items
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The shop gave temporary possession based on fraud; Victor intended from the start to keep the camera.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: False pretenses requires obtaining title, and the shop transferred only possession. C: Possession was procured by fraud with intent to keep it from the start, so larceny by trick is the better label. D: Business risk does not consent to fraudulent permanent taking.
lb_rule_reference: If the victim intends to transfer only temporary possession because of deception, and the defendant intends to keep the property, the offense is larceny by trick.
