Outline: Mode and order of witnesses
Question: In a federal civil trial, the court grants Noah's request to exclude witnesses from the courtroom. Noah then asks the court also to order counsel and the excluded witnesses not to share transcripts or summaries of trial testimony with excluded witnesses before they testify. The opponent argues that Rule 615 only removes people from the courtroom and cannot address access to testimony outside court. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Deny the request because a witness-exclusion order can never regulate access to trial testimony outside the courtroom.
Answer B: Deny the request because exclusion from the courtroom automatically covers all communications and no further order is possible.
Answer C: Grant the request if the court finds such additional restrictions appropriate to prevent disclosure of or access to trial testimony.
Answer D: Grant the request only if Noah proves that an excluded witness has already committed perjury.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: A Rule 615(a) order excludes witnesses from the courtroom. The court may also enter additional orders prohibiting disclosure of trial testimony to excluded witnesses and prohibiting excluded witnesses from accessing trial testimony.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A ignores Rule 615(b). B is wrong because courtroom exclusion alone is not the same as an additional disclosure/access order. D invents a perjury prerequisite.
