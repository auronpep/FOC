Question: Hannah agreed to sell Eli the unborn lambs expected from a named flock during the spring season. The writing fixed a total price but was silent about when the lambs would be picked up after birth. Eli offers evidence of trade usage about pickup timing for such lambs. Which answer best addresses the source-law issue?
Answer A: Common law governs because the lambs were unborn when the contract was signed.
Answer B: UCC Article 2 governs because the sale concerns goods, including unborn young of animals identified to the contract.
Answer C: Real-property law governs because the lambs would be born on a farm.
Answer D: No contract can exist because unborn animals cannot be the subject of a contract.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Article 2 treats goods to include certain unborn young of animals identified to the contract. The fact that the lambs were unborn does not prevent a goods contract, so Article 2 supplies the meaning route.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because future or unborn status does not automatically remove the transaction from Article 2. C: C is wrong because the farm location does not convert the transaction into land. D: D is wrong because future goods can be the subject of a contract to sell.
lb_student_diagnostic: Future goods treated as no contract.
