Question: A diversity action is filed in federal court. A state statute says venue is proper only in the county where the defendant resides. Federal venue statute Section 1391 makes venue proper in the federal district where a substantial part of the events occurred. The events occurred in the chosen federal district. Which venue rule controls?
Answer A: The state county venue rule, because the case involves state-law claims
Answer B: Section 1391, because a valid federal statute directly controls federal venue
Answer C: The rule that produces dismissal, because Erie discourages forum shopping
Answer D: Neither rule, because diversity cases have no venue requirements
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Erie does not override a governing federal statute.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A valid federal venue statute directly governs venue in federal court. C: Forum-shopping concerns do not defeat a federal statute on point. D: Federal venue rules apply.
lb_rule_reference: A valid federal statute on point controls in federal court under the Supremacy Clause framework before twin-aims balancing.
