Question: A named plaintiff seeks to represent all employees in a wage class. The named plaintiff's proposed theory would maximize recovery for supervisors like herself but reduce or eliminate recovery for hourly workers who make up most of the class. Which Rule 23(a) prerequisite is most directly threatened?
Answer A: Numerosity, because conflicts always mean the class is too small
Answer B: Adequacy, because the representative may not fairly protect absent members with conflicting interests
Answer C: Typicality, because typicality requires identical damages for every member
Answer D: Commonality, because any internal disagreement destroys every common question
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A representative whose theory sacrifices absent members' interests may be inadequate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The issue is conflict of interest, not class size. C: The clearest problem is an intra-class conflict impairing fair representation. D: A conflict can defeat adequacy even if common questions exist.
lb_rule_reference: Adequacy requires that the representative parties fairly and adequately protect the interests of the class; conflicts between the representative and absent class members can defeat certification.
