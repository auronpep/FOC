Outline: Life estates
Question: A landowner conveyed a parcel of land “to my son for life, remainder to my daughter.” Two years later, without the landowner's permission, the son quitclaimed all of his interest in the land to his wife. What is the state of the land's title?
Answer A: A: Life estate in daughter, remainder in fee simple to landowner.
Answer B: B: Fee simple absolute in daughter.
Answer C: C: Life estate per autre vie in wife, remainder in fee simple in daughter.
Answer D: D: Life estate in wife, remainder in fee simple in daughter.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Therefore, A, B, and D are incorrect.
