MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Jurisdiction and Venue
Outline: Waiver of Service
Question: A football player sued a doctor in federal district court. If the complaint was mailed to her and she waived formal service, how long does the doctor have to answer the complaint?
Answer A: A: Within 14 days after receipt of the complaint.
Answer B: B: Within 21 days after receipt of the complaint.
Answer C: C: Within 21 days after the complaint was mailed.
Answer D: D: Within 60 days after the request for waiver was mailed.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: If a Rule 12 motion is not made, a defendant who was mailed the complaint and who waived formal service must answer within 60 days after the request for waiver was mailed. If a defendant is served formally with a summons and complaint, he or she must answer within 21 days after service. If the court grants a motion for a more definite statement, the answer is due within 14 days. Therefore, A, B, and C are incorrect.
