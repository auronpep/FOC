Question: A state allows resident citizens to buy undeveloped farmland but bars citizens of other states from buying any farmland unless they have lived in the state for five years. The state offers no evidence that nonresident buyers create a special land-use problem. What is the best Article IV analysis?
Answer A: The rule is suspect because it discriminates against nonresident citizens in an important property-related privilege without a shown substantial reason
Answer B: The rule is valid because states have absolute power over land within their borders
Answer C: The rule is invalid only if the buyer is a corporation
Answer D: The rule must be upheld because property ownership is recreational
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The state has burdened nonresident citizens' property acquisition without evidence of a special problem and fit.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: State land regulation remains subject to Article IV limits. C: Natural citizens, not corporations, are the Article IV plaintiffs. D: The rule broadly burdens property acquisition, not merely a recreational permit.
lb_rule_reference: Article IV can protect nonresident citizens against discrimination in important property-related rights, subject to the state's substantial reason and close-fit defense.
