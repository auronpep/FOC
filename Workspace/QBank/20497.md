Question: After a public argument with Rowan, Camille bought a rifle, learned Rowan's usual jogging route, and hid behind a storage shed along the route the next morning. When Rowan passed, Camille fired immediately and killed him. She argues that the shooting itself took only a second. What is the most serious homicide offense supported?
Answer A: First-degree murder, because the ambush facts show premeditation and deliberation
Answer B: Second-degree murder only, because the fatal shot occurred immediately when Rowan appeared
Answer C: Voluntary manslaughter, because the killing followed a public argument
Answer D: Involuntary manslaughter, because Camille fired only once
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The shooting was quick, but the decision to kill was formed before the act. Camille bought a rifle, studied Rowan's route, hid, and waited. Lying in wait and planned execution support first-degree murder.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: Premeditation is measured by the defendant's prior formation of intent, not only the time between seeing the victim and firing. C: A prior argument does not supply legally adequate provocation at the time of killing, and the next-day ambush shows cooling and planning. D: One planned fatal shot can be first-degree murder. Number of shots is not the degree test.
lb_rule_reference: Lying in wait, weapon preparation, and a planned ambush can show premeditation and deliberation for first-degree murder.
