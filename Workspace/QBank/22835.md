


BARMATRIX Q#: 22835
Outline_code: 95070100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Civil Procedure
Subtopic: Pretrial Procedures
Outline: Pleadings
Question: A man and a woman had a car accident. The man was a citizen of State A, and the woman was a citizen of State B. The man sued the woman in federal district court in State B for $100,000 in damages. In her answer, the woman counterclaimed for $75,000 in damages. No further pleadings were served by either party, and the man's claim was dismissed for want of prosecution. The woman still wanted to recover the $75,000 that she believed the man owed her. What is the woman's best course of action to recover the $75,000?
Answer A: A: File her own claim against the man for $75,000.
Answer B: B: Move for summary judgment on her counterclaim.
Answer C: C: Move for a new trial on her counterclaim.
Answer D: D: Apply for a default judgment against the man.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: A counterclaim, just like a normal claim, has to be responded to by an answer. Because the man filed no further pleadings, he failed to respond to the counterclaim, and the woman can request a default judgment from the court. A is incorrect because the woman does not have to file an entirely new claim to recover her $75,000. B is incorrect because a summary judgment is based on the evidence, while a default judgment is based on a party's failure to answer, which is the situation here. C is incorrect because the woman does not have to go through a whole new trial.
