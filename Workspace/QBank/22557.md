


BARMATRIX Q#: 22557
Outline_code: 51060100
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Contracts
Subtopic: Remedies
Outline: Measure of damages in major types of contract and breach
Question: On January 3, a retailer of lumber ordered from a lumber wholesaler 1,000 2″ × 4″ fir boards, each 8 feet in length, for delivery by January 15. When the wholesaler delivered the fir boards on January 15, they were received by the retailer's manager, who informed the retailer that delivery was made but that the boards delivered by the wholesaler were only 7 feet long. The retailer intended to notify the wholesaler immediately but was busy and forgot to do so. On January 16, economic conditions caused the price of lumber to double, and on January 17, the retailer notified the wholesaler that the boards were only 7 feet long, returned them to the wholesaler, and demanded that the wholesaler furnish 8- foot boards at the contract price. If the wholesaler refuses to do so, and the retailer asserts a claim against the wholesaler for breach of contract, what recovery is the retailer entitled to?
Answer A: A: A sum equivalent to the difference between the contract price and the fair market value of 7- foot boards on January 15.
Answer B: B: A sum equivalent to the difference between the contract price and the fair market value of 8- foot boards on January 15.
Answer C: C: A sum equivalent to the difference between the contract price and the fair market value of 8- foot boards on January 17.
Answer D: D: Nothing.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: A buyer who rightfully rejects nonconforming goods is entitled to damages for nondelivery. Under UCC §2- 713(1), these consist of the difference between the contract price and the market price at the time when the buyer learned of the breach. Since the retailer learned of the nonconformity on January 15, B is correct. C is therefore incorrect. A would be the correct measure of damage if the retailer had kept the 7- foot boards but is incorrect because he returned them to the wholesaler. Rejection of nonconforming goods may be made within a reasonable time after receiving them. Although the retailer did not act immediately upon discovering the nonconformity, the return of the goods two days later was probably within a reasonable time. Even if two days was not a reasonable time, however, the wholesaler's agreement to their return would prevent him from making that assertion. D is therefore incorrect.
