Question: Jacob signed and delivered a deed conveying his store building to Leah. Leah handed Jacob a check for the price. The deed did not state that delivery depended on the check clearing, and Jacob did not say that title would wait until payment cleared. Leah accepted the deed, but the check later bounced. Who owns the store building?
Answer A: Jacob, because the price was not actually collected.
Answer B: Jacob, because a sale deed is invalid unless consideration is paid in cash.
Answer C: Leah only if she pays the price before recording the deed.
Answer D: Leah, because the unconditional delivered deed passed title; Jacob's payment problem is a separate remedy issue.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source is a deed, and the decisive validity gate is unconditional delivery of a properly executed instrument. Jacob delivered the deed without making title conditional on the check clearing, and Leah accepted. The bounced check may support a money claim or other remedy, but it does not automatically defeat the deed's title-transfer effect.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because failure of payment after unconditional delivery does not automatically revest title. B: B is wrong because deed validity is not dependent on payment in cash. C: C is wrong because recording does not supply the missing event; title already passed by delivery and acceptance.
lb_rule_reference: Failure of consideration after unconditional deed delivery
