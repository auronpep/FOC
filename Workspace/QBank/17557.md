Outline: Injurious falsehood (trade libel)
Question: A competitor falsely told three named distributors that a bakery's packaged cookies contained illegal dye. The competitor knew the claim was false. The three distributors canceled identified purchase orders totaling a stated dollar amount. Which tort best fits the bakery's claim?
Answer A: Injurious falsehood or trade libel, because the false product statement caused specific pecuniary loss
Answer B: Battery, because the statement indirectly harmed the bakery
Answer C: Defamation only, because product disparagement is the same as personal reputation injury
Answer D: No tort, because competitors may make knowingly false product claims without liability
Correct Answer: A
Correct Answer Explanation: The statement disparaged the product and the lost orders supply special damages.
Wrong Answer Explanations: B: No harmful or offensive contact is involved. C: Trade libel focuses on economic interests in products or business quality. D: Knowingly false product disparagement with special damages can be actionable.
lb_rule_reference: Trade libel requires a false disparaging statement about goods, property, or business quality, fault or malice as required, and specific pecuniary loss.
