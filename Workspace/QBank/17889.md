Question: Leah mortgaged Lot 2 to Aaron Bank. After the mortgage was recorded, Leah granted Isaac a recorded easement over Lot 2 to reach Isaac's parcel. Isaac knew of the prior mortgage. Aaron Bank later properly foreclosed the mortgage in a proceeding that foreclosed junior interests. Noah bought Lot 2 at the foreclosure sale and refused Isaac access. What is the best result?
Answer A: Isaac's easement necessarily survives because it was recorded before the foreclosure sale.
Answer B: Isaac's easement necessarily survives because easements are never affected by mortgage foreclosure.
Answer C: Isaac's junior easement may be extinguished by proper foreclosure of the prior mortgage.
Answer D: Isaac now owns Lot 2 because a foreclosed easement becomes fee title.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: An easement granted after a prior recorded mortgage is a junior interest in the servient land. Proper foreclosure of the prior mortgage can extinguish junior interests if the foreclosure proceeding reaches those interests. The easement's later recording does not make it senior to the earlier mortgage.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because recording after the mortgage does not defeat the earlier mortgage priority. B: B is wrong because easements can be affected by foreclosure priority. C is correct for the reasons above. D: D is wrong because extinguishment of a junior easement does not give the easement holder fee title.
lb_rule_reference: Mortgage priority / junior easement extinguished by foreclosure
lb_student_diagnostic: The trap is treating recording as a generic survival rule instead of asking priority and foreclosure effect.
