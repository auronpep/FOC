Question: A defendant serves its answer. Forty days later, it discovers an indemnity agreement with a subcontractor and wants to file a third-party complaint against the subcontractor for indemnity. What must the defendant do under Rule 14?
Answer A: File the third-party complaint as of right because derivative liability is alleged
Answer B: Obtain leave of court before serving the third-party complaint
Answer C: Amend the answer only; third-party complaints are never allowed after an answer
Answer D: Wait until trial and ask the jury to allocate fault to the subcontractor
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: More than 14 days have passed since the defendant served its original answer, so the defendant needs court leave for Rule 14 impleader.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The timing window for impleader without leave has passed. C: Later impleader can be allowed with court leave. D: The question asks the procedure for adding a third-party claim.
lb_rule_reference: A defending party may, as third-party plaintiff, serve a third-party complaint on a nonparty who may be liable to it for all or part of the claim. But if more than 14 days after serving its original answer, the defending party must obtain leave of court.
