Outline: Motion for judgment on the pleadings
Question: After filing an employment discrimination complaint, Hannah is fired in alleged retaliation for filing the lawsuit. She seeks to add the termination event to the case by supplemental pleading. The defendant argues Rule 15(d) can never cover post-filing events. Is the defendant correct?
Answer A: Yes, supplemental pleadings are limited to pre-filing events
Answer B: Yes, post-filing events require a brand-new lawsuit in every case
Answer C: No, the court may permit a supplemental pleading for the later termination event on just terms
Answer D: No, because no court permission is ever needed for supplementation
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The alleged retaliation occurred after the original pleading.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Supplemental pleadings are specifically for later events. B: Rule 15(d) permits supplementation with court approval. D: Rule 15(d) requires court permission.
lb_rule_reference: Rule 15(d) allows supplementation on just terms for later events; it is distinct from amending preexisting allegations.
