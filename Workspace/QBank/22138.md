Outline_code: 82010000
MBE Subjects (1 of 7): Real Property
Subtopic: Ownership
Outline: Nature and Ownership of Land
Question: A tech company rented an office space from a landlord for 10 years. Part of the company's brand was to decorate everything with a new bright color each year. The company told the landlord that brand identity was very important to its business, so the landlord agreed to repaint to office with a new color chosen by the company over holiday break every year. The company agreed to pay $1,000 per month rent. On the holiday break of the lease's second year, the landlord refused to paint the office a new color. Which of the following statements is true?
Answer A: A: The company may not paint the office because it would constitute waste.
Answer B: B: The company may paint the office and take the cost of labor and paint out of its rent.
Answer C: C: The company may refuse to pay rent and remain in the office space.
Answer D: D: The company may treat the lease as terminated if it vacates the premises.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Here, the refusal to paint the office is a breach of an express covenant. Importantly, it is not an actual or constructive eviction because the company has not lost use of the premises. Statutes in many jurisdictions allow a tenant to engage in self- help and take the cost of that out self- help out of the lease. Consequently, this is the best answer. A is incorrect because ameliorative waste permanently increases the value of the property. Here, a simple paint job is unlikely to be permanent or increase the premise's value. C and D are incorrect because the breach of the agreement to paint does not rise to the level of an eviction.
