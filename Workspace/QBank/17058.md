Outline: Requirements for valid deed
Question: Daniel signed a deed describing his field and using words of present conveyance, but the grantee line was blank when he handed the paper to Esther for safekeeping. Daniel gave Esther no authority to fill in a name. After Daniel died, Esther wrote her own name in the blank and recorded the document. Who owns the field?
Answer A: Esther, because Daniel signed the paper and Esther later recorded it.
Answer B: Esther, because possession of a signed deed automatically gives authority to complete any blank.
Answer C: Daniel's estate, because the deed did not identify a grantee when delivered and the later unauthorized completion and recording did not cure the defect.
Answer D: Esther, because no consideration was required for the transfer.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The title source claimed is a deed, and the decisive validity gate is grantee identity at delivery. A deed must identify an ascertainable grantee, and Esther had no authority to add herself later. The unauthorized completion and recording do not cure the missing grantee, so title remains in Daniel's estate.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because recording does not validate an instrument that lacked an identified grantee. B: B is wrong because mere possession of the paper does not create authority to complete it. D: D is wrong because the no-consideration rule does not cure the missing grantee and unauthorized completion.
