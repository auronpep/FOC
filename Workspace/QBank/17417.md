Outline: Reliance damages
Question: Peter contracted with Deborah to build a custom display for Deborah's store. Before Deborah breached, Peter reasonably bought custom materials for $14,000. After the breach, Peter could not use the materials for another job but sold them for $5,000 salvage. Peter cannot prove expected profit with certainty. What is Peter's reliance recovery for the materials?
Answer A: $14,000, because that is what Peter spent.
Answer B: $9,000, the material cost reduced by the $5,000 salvage value.
Answer C: $5,000, because salvage value is the only objective number.
Answer D: The full contract price, because the materials were custom.
Correct Answer: B
Correct Answer Explanation: Reliance damages are reduced by value the injured party retains or recovers from the expenditures. Peter spent $14,000 but salvaged $5,000, leaving a $9,000 reliance loss.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because it ignores the salvage offset. C: C is wrong because salvage reduces the loss; it is not the loss itself. D: D is wrong because custom materials do not automatically entitle Peter to the full contract price.
lb_rule_reference: Reliance damages / salvage offset
