Question: Abigail, a farmer who did not regularly sell irrigation equipment, bought irrigation pumps from a merchant supplier. The supplier's standard purchase form, signed at the bottom by both parties, stated that the pumps would be delivered by July 1. In small print on the back, the supplier's form also stated that no modification was effective unless made in a signed writing. Abigail did not separately sign or initial that clause. On June 28, the supplier orally asked for a July 15 delivery date because a truck had broken down, and Abigail orally agreed. Abigail then tried to cancel on July 5. Which statement best describes the effect of the oral July 15 agreement?
Answer A: It fails because a goods-contract modification always requires new consideration.
Answer B: It fails automatically because any signed form containing a no-oral-modification clause bars oral changes.
Answer C: It may be effective because Article 2 does not require consideration for a modification, and this merchant-form clause was not separately signed by the nonmerchant buyer.
Answer D: It is barred by the parol evidence rule because the July 1 delivery date appeared in the signed writing.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Under Article 2, a modifying agreement needs no consideration. A signed agreement can require signed written modifications, but a merchant-supplied form imposing that requirement must be separately signed by the other party unless the parties are merchants. The trigger facts are the goods transaction, the merchant supplier's form, Abigail's nonmerchant status, and the absence of a separate signature on the no-oral-modification clause. The decisive locks are source law and formal-modification status.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because UCC modifications do not require consideration. B: B is wrong because Article 2 gives special treatment to a merchant-form no-oral-modification term when the other party is not a merchant and has not separately signed it. D: D is wrong because the oral agreement was made after the writing, so PER is the wrong timing route.
lb_rule_reference: UCC 2-209(1), (2) / merchant-form no-oral-modification clause
