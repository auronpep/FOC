Question: In a federal employment-discrimination trial, Benjamin asks Priscilla, “What did your lawyer tell you in confidence about whether to settle this case?” Priscilla objects. What is the best ruling?
Answer A: Overrule the objection because settlement advice is always relevant.
Answer B: Overrule the objection because Priscilla is on the witness stand.
Answer C: Sustain the objection only if the lawyer is also present in court.
Answer D: Sustain the objection because the question calls for a confidential attorney-client communication.
Correct Answer: D
Correct Answer Explanation: A question seeking confidential legal advice from a client's lawyer calls for privileged attorney-client communication unless waiver or an exception applies. None is stated.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: A is wrong because relevance does not defeat privilege. B: B is wrong because taking the stand does not automatically waive all privileged communications. C: C is wrong because the lawyer's physical presence is not required for the privilege to apply.
lb_rule_reference: FRE 501/FRE 611 / privileged communication question
