Question: In a federal boundary dispute, Sarah offers a certified copy of a recorded deed from the county recorder's office. Abraham objects that Sarah must call the recorder to authenticate the copy. How should the court rule?
Answer A: Sustain the objection because a custodian must always testify in person.
Answer B: Sustain the objection unless Abraham admits the deed was recorded.
Answer C: Overrule the objection because a properly certified copy of a public record can be self-authenticating.
Answer D: Overrule the objection only if the jury is told the copy is weaker than the original.
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Certain certified copies of public records are self-authenticating and require no extrinsic authentication witness. A duplicate/copy is not automatically weaker.
