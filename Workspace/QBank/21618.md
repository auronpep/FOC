Outline: Amendment of Pleadings
Question: Before limitations expired, Deborah sued a driver for negligence arising from a March 1 collision. After limitations expired, Deborah seeks to amend to add a claim for negligent entrustment based on the same collision and same vehicle. May the amendment relate back?
Answer A: No, a new legal theory never relates back
Answer B: No, relation back is limited to correcting spelling errors
Answer C: Yes, because the amendment arises out of the same collision described in the original complaint
Answer D: Yes, because all amendments relate back
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: The original pleading gave notice of the factual occurrence.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: The same conduct, transaction, or occurrence controls. B: It can cover claims and some party changes. D: Same occurrence and other Rule 15(c) requirements still matter.
lb_rule_reference: Relation back can apply to a new legal theory if the factual occurrence is the same.
