Question: A plaintiff filed a federal civil-rights claim and a related state negligence claim arising from the same incident. The federal court dismissed the federal claim early under Rule 12(b)(6). No diversity exists. The defendant asks the court to decline supplemental jurisdiction over the remaining state claim. What may the court do?
Answer A: The court must keep the state claim because it once shared a common nucleus
Answer B: The court must dismiss the state claim for lack of Article III power
Answer C: The court may decline supplemental jurisdiction after dismissing all federal claims
Answer D: The court may keep the state claim only if it exceeds $75,000
Correct Answer: C
Correct Answer Explanation: Section 1367(c) gives discretion to decline in this posture.
Wrong Answer Explanations: A: Section 1367(c) permits decline after all original-jurisdiction claims are dismissed. B: The claim may fall within Section 1367(a); the issue is discretionary decline. D: Amount in controversy is not required for supplemental jurisdiction attached to a federal-question anchor.
lb_rule_reference: Under Section 1367(c), a federal court may decline supplemental jurisdiction after dismissing all claims over which it had original jurisdiction.
