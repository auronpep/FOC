# Christian Surface Style Guide for Hearsay Questions

## Goal

The question should read like it belongs to a Christian bar-prep study group, not like a generic MBE item with one renamed character.

## Required posture

Use explicit Christian settings while keeping the legal issue ordinary.

Good surfaces:

- a campus ministry worship-night planning dispute;
- a private Christian bar-prep small group;
- mission-trip vehicle logs;
- retreat-center medical intake forms;
- fellowship-hall vendor invoices;
- Bible-study group text messages;
- Christian bookstore security camera notes;
- worship-team equipment rentals;
- charity 5K volunteer radio calls;
- youth camp nurse records;
- devotional-podcast production files;
- Christian legal-aid clinic intake notes.

## Avoid clutter

Do not add facts that test:

- Establishment Clause;
- Free Exercise;
- clergy-penitent privilege;
- ecclesiastical abstention;
- church discipline;
- religious-discrimination claims;
- denominational doctrine disputes;
- tax-deductibility of donations;
- charitable immunity.

## Use faith as setting, not law

Correct: `At a private Christian bookstore's Bible-study night, Ruth heard...`

Incorrect: `Because Ruth is a Christian, her statement is more reliable...`

## Name bank

Preferred names: Peter, Paul, John, Mary, Martha, Esther, Daniel, Ruth, Timothy, Lydia, Stephen, Barnabas, Hannah, Naomi, Joseph, Abigail, Samuel, Joanna, Priscilla, Aquila, Andrew, Philip, Elizabeth, Anna, Silas, Phoebe.

Avoid antagonist-coded names: Judas, Cain, Jezebel, Ahab, Herod, Delilah, Pharaoh, Haman, Goliath, Pilate.

## Christian texture examples by Hearsay route

| Route | Safe Christian surface |
|---|---|
| Effect on listener | warning at a mission-trip packing night offered to show notice |
| Present sense impression | volunteer calls out a license plate during church parking-lot incident |
| Excited utterance | youth-camp counselor blurts statement immediately after accident |
| Business record | Christian bookstore invoice or retreat center intake record |
| Public record | public fire marshal report about rented hall, not church governance |
| Prior ID | witness identifies suspect after worship-night theft |
| Party admission | vendor statement offered against vendor after retreat-equipment dispute |
| Confrontation | criminal prosecution using formal police interview after ministry-event assault |

## Memory-hook rules

A Christian memory hook may be devotional in tone, but it cannot replace legal reasoning.

Good: `Study-group hook: before hunting exceptions, ask what the statement is being used to prove.`

Bad: `The statement is admissible because Christians tell the truth.`
