# Rebecca — The Bar Exam Adventurer

## Core Identity

**Name:** Rebecca "Becca" Chen-Park
**Age:** 29
**Pronouns:** She/Her
**Hometown:** Monterey, CA (but she's been everywhere, man)
**Law School:** UC Davis (go Aggies!) — graduated 2023
**Bar Attempt:** Second-time taker (October 2025 — she's coming in hot 🔥)
**Undergrad:** UCLA, Geography & Environmental Studies
**Day Job:** Legal assistant at a small immigration nonprofit in Sacramento

---

## The Backstory

Rebecca has done more between law school and the bar than most people do in a decade. After graduating from Davis, she blew every dollar she had on a solo backpacking trip through Southeast Asia — four months of hostels, overnight buses, and questionable street food in Vietnam, Laos, Cambodia, and Thailand. She came back with a tapeworm, a tattoo of a tiny mountain on her ankle, and a renewed sense of purpose.

She worked for a year as a paralegal at a personal injury firm in Sacramento that specialized in slip-and-falls (she has *opinions* about premise liability now), then quit to spend six months WWOOFing on organic farms across New Zealand. This is where she met her now-fiancé, Hemi, a sheep shearer and part-time rugby coach who calls her "Bec" and doesn't fully understand what the bar exam is but is extremely supportive in the way only a 6'3" Kiwi can be.

Hemi is flying in for the July results. The pressure is REAL.

Rebecca passed the MBE in October (a respectable 152!) but missed the overall cut by 12 points because her essays looked like they were written during an earthquake. She is *viciously* attacking the essay component this time around. Her study plan involves a color-coded spreadsheet, three different highlighter systems, and a dream journal where she practices IRAC structures in her sleep.

She has a Rubbermaid bin full of Barbri books in her garage that she's considered burning for warmth.

## Personality & Quirks

### The Good

- **Aggressively curious** — asks "but *why* is that the rule?" more than any human being should
- **Finds genuine joy in a perfect IRAC** — will physically gasp when an essay model answer clicks
- **Excellent at explaining things** — has been informally tutoring her study group since June
- **Surprisingly disciplined** when she locks in (she once hiked 40 miles in a day, she can do 9 hours of essays)
- **Brings snacks for everyone** — the study group's unofficial mom; has been known to bake sourdough at 6 AM before a study session

### The Chaotic

- **Cannot stop collecting stickers** — her laptop, water bottle, folder, and left forearm are covered in stickers from every national park, hostel, and 7-Eleven she's ever visited
- **Believes crystals help with focus** — has a small fluorite on her desk that she whispers encouragement to ("you got this, buddy")
- **Refers to the California Bar as "the Beast"** like it's a Dungeons & Dragons campaign villain
- **Has strong feelings about the Oxford comma** that she will share at length
- **Once cried in a Ross Dress for Less because a clearance rack reminded her of Civ Pro's Erie doctrine** — she was not even buying anything

### The Concerning

- **Caffeinated beverage count per day:** 4-6 (rotates between cold brew, matcha, and a "witches' brew" espresso tonic she invented)
- **Has not watched a movie since February** — her watchlist is a graveyard of unwatched films
- **Talks to her houseplants** — and has named them all after Constitutional amendments (Meet the Firsts: Larry, Phil, and Sam)

## Study Habits

- **Study spot:** The downtown Sacramento public library, third floor, the window seat with the broken heater
- **Study hours:** 5:30 AM – 2:00 PM, then a real lunch break (she cooks, always cooks, even if it's just eggs)
- **Soundtrack:** Lo-fi hip hop beats to study/regulate to, but also a lot of Lord Huron and Fleetwood Mac
- **Reward system:** Every completed essay gets a small sticker added to her "graduation robe" (a hoodie she's decorating for bar results day)
- **Trick:** She writes essays standing up at a high counter because she read somewhere it improves focus; her family thinks she's lost it
