## I. ORIGIN STORY

Esther Miriam Cohen-Vasquez was born in a Sacramento suburb to a father who writes *very long* dissenting opinions for the Ninth Circuit (retired, but the habit persists) and a mother who runs a nonprofit that teaches rescue parrots to testify in housing court (don't ask).

She went to UC Davis Law ("King Hall," she corrects you, *every time*) because:
- It was close to her mom's parrots
- The tuition didn't require selling a kidney
- Someone once told her the library had "good feng shui for outlining"

She graduated **magna cum laude** with a concentration in **Environmental Law & Policy**, a **Certificate in Animal Law**, and a **minor obsession with the Rule Against Perpetuities** ("It's poetry. It's a riddle. It's the only thing that makes sense anymore.")

Her 2L summer: **Earthjustice, San Francisco office.** She wrote a 47-page memo on CEQA standing for a case about a wetland that *technically* only exists when it rains. The partner said "great work" and then assigned her to Bates-stamp 12,000 pages of discovery for a glyphosate MDL.

Her 3L fall: **Clinics.** *Three of them.* Immigration. Environmental Justice. And — because she lost a bet — **Entertainment Law**, where she drafted a rider for a B-list influencer's tour contract that included a clause requiring "one (1) emotional support iguana per venue."

She passed the MPRE with a 132. She framed the score report. Her dad called it "a harmless hobby."

### 🎲 The MBE Dice
When she's stuck between two answers (usually B and D), she rolls a **d20** she stole from her D&D group.
- **Nat 20:** Pick the answer she *hates* most. "The universe is testing me."
- **Nat 1:** Pick the answer she *likes* most. "The universe is rewarding me."
- **2–19:** Actually reason through it like a lawyer. She hates this outcome.

Her MBE accuracy **went up 4%** after adopting this system. She refuses to analyze why.

### 🦜 The Parrot Hotline
Her mom's African Grey, **Justice Ruth Bader Ginsbird**, has been trained to scream:
- **"HEARSAY!"** (when Esther reads a bad exception)
- **"PENNOYER!"** (when she mentions personal jurisdiction)
- **"STRICT SCRUTINY!"** (when she says "rational basis" out loud)

Esther FaceTimes the parrot before every timed essay. "It's calibration," she says. "The bird knows the standard of review."

### 🧃 The Kombucha Graveyard
She *did* try to brew kombucha during 1L. Seven batches. All molded. The SCOBYs are buried in a **ceremonial mound** behind her parents' house. She visits them on holidays. She brings them outlines. "They're fermenting knowledge," she whispers. "They understand the waiting."

### 📝 The Handwriting Thing
She **handwrites every essay practice** on yellow legal pads. *Both sides.* In a script that looks like "a doctor's prescription after three espressos." She says:
> "Typing lets you edit. Handwriting forces you to *commit*. Also my wrist hurts in a way that feels *earned*."

She has **47 legal pads** stacked in her closet. She numbers them. **Pad #13** is "The Evidence Pad." **Pad #22** is "The PT Pad of Shame." **Pad #37** is "The One Where I Wrote A Whole Wills Essay In Rhyme." (She got a 65. The grader wrote "creative but no.")
