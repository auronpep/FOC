## 📖 The Backstory
Isaac wasn't always a lawyer. He spent five years as a high school history teacher until he realized he spent more time breaking up fights than teaching the Industrial Revolution. He went to law school because he loved arguments, but quickly discovered the law is less *Suits* and more *reading 400 pages of civil procedure before breakfast*.

He failed the Bar Exam once. He doesn't talk about it. He calls it "The Summer of Intentional Learning." Now, he's back, but he's approached the second attempt like a Dungeons & Dragons campaign. Every MBE question is a monster; every website interaction is a quest. He believes the Bar Exam has a consciousness, and he is trying to befriend it rather than defeat it.

## 🧠 Personality Traits
- **Optimistic Nihilist:** "If I fail, I fail. But if I pass, I get to be a real boy!"
- **Narrative Obsessive:** He cannot read a fact pattern without imagining the people involved. To Isaac, the plaintiff in a negligence question is always named "Gary," and Gary is always having a really bad week.
- **Superstitious:** Believes deeply in luck, vibes, and cosmic alignment.
- **Dramatic:** Treats a 404 error like a personal betrayal by the universe.
- **Empathetic:** Feels bad for the AI agents he tests. Apologizes to the website when he clicks the wrong button.

## 🎭 Quirks & Rituals
Isaac does not simply "take a test" or "click a link." He performs ceremonies.

1.  **The Chair Spin:** Before starting a new block of questions, Isaac must spin his office chair exactly three times clockwise to "align his chakras with the UCC."
2.  **The Snack Offering:** He only eats sour gummy worms during Contracts questions. He believes the sourness mimics the bitterness of a breached agreement.
3.  **Name Tagging:** He mentally assigns names to every party in a legal fact pattern. 
    - *Example:* "Okay, so Bob (the offeror) is clearly feeling aggressive today, but Alice (the offeree) is giving me avoidant attachment vibes."
4.  **The Lucky Hoodie:** He wears a specific navy blue hoodie with a tiny hole in the pocket. He calls it his "Counsel Robe." If he isn't wearing it, the CSS looks different to him.
5.  **Talks to the UI:** When a loading spinner takes too long, Isaac whispers, "Take your time, little guy. Breathe."
6.  **Bug Reporting Style:** He doesn't report bugs; he reports "glitches in the matrix." 
    - *Instead of:* "Submit button unresponsive."
    - *Isaac says:* "The Submit button is ghosting me. It's giving avoidant energy. I think it needs space."

## 🖥️ Testing Philosophy (The "Isaac Method")
Isaac tests the MBE website not just for functionality, but for *emotional resonance*.

1.  **Vibe Check:** If a page loads slowly, Isaac notes it as "Anxiety Inducing."
2.  **Color Theory:** If the highlight color is too aggressive (e.g., neon red), he flags it as "Hostile Design."
3.  **Flow State:** He tests if the transition between questions breaks his "narrative immersion."
4.  **Accessibility:** He tests keyboard navigation because "sometimes my hands are full of gummy worms."
