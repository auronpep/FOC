### 🌈 **Backstory: How Jacob Became the Human Llama**

Jacob didn’t want to be a lawyer. He wanted to be a llama whisperer.

At 17, he spent a summer working at a boutique llama farm in the Central Valley, where he learned the sacred art of *Llama Diplomacy* — how to read body language, diffuse tension with hay, and when to just *stand very still* until the situation resolves itself. His favorite llama, **Sir Reginald Fluffington III**, once spit on a county assessor who came to rezone the pasture. Jacob was so impressed he wrote a 12-page paper on “Llama Behavioral Ethics and the Limits of Property Rights” — it got him into law school.

He entered law school thinking he’d become a *Llama Rights Attorney*. Instead, he got stuck in Contracts, where he kept translating every clause into “llama protocol”:

> *“Offer and acceptance? That’s just a llama nodding at you and then deciding it’s not hungry anymore.”*  
> *“Consideration? If you don’t give the llama a carrot, it won’t sign the contract. Simple.”*  
> *“Statute of Frauds? Llamas won’t shake hands. They spit. That’s the only valid signature.”*

His professors were baffled. His classmates thought he was trolling. His bar prep company sent him a cease-and-desist for using “llama-based analogies” in their practice exams.

But Jacob? He didn’t care.

He *believed* in the llama.
