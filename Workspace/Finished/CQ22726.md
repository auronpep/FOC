### 7. Full right-answer explanation (exam-register)

**C is correct.** Miriam had more than an at-will job. Her tenure letter promised lifetime employment unless the university removed her for cause, so she had a protected property interest in continued employment. **Gold Key:** a public employee who can be fired only for cause must receive notice and a meaningful chance to respond before termination. The trustees revoked her tenure and fired her immediately without contacting her or letting her answer the charge. That made the process unlawful.

**Silver Key:** when the call asks whether the manner of firing was unlawful, stay on process before chasing the reason for the firing. The offensive livestream explains why the university acted, but the decisive fact is that it acted first and gave no pre-termination opportunity to respond.
