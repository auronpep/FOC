### 9. Full black-letter-law verification AND legal reasoning for the right answer
**Student black-letter-law answer explanation:** Relevant evidence is generally admissible. The still image makes a disputed damages fact more probable: Naomi's injury severity. The graphic quality does not automatically exclude it, because Rule 403 uses a balancing standard, not a bright-line ban. The erased source video also does not defeat admission. The proponent showed ordinary reuse and erasure, not bad-faith destruction, and the still image is a photographic exhibit tied to the scene.

**Lawyer-confirmation authority:** The official Federal Rules of Evidence in the U.S. Code, as amended to January 23, 2026, confirm the rule stack. FRE 401 defines relevance by tendency plus consequence. FRE 402 makes relevant evidence admissible unless another authority excludes it. FRE 403 permits exclusion only when probative value is substantially outweighed by listed dangers such as unfair prejudice. FRE 1001 treats photographs broadly and defines originals and duplicates for writings, recordings, and photographs. FRE 1002 requires an original only when proving content unless another rule or statute provides otherwise. FRE 1003 admits duplicates absent a genuine authenticity question or unfairness. FRE 1004 permits other evidence of content when originals are lost or destroyed without bad faith.
