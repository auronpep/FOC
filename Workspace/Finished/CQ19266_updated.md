## crossover_intersection_map

| Crossover           | Boundary                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Evidence            | “No evidence” is a trial-record fact, not an Evidence-rule admissibility issue. |
| Contracts           | Agreement is only the story element; the tested law is Civil Procedure.         |
| Jury trial          | Jury rights do not override Rule 50’s evidentiary gate.                         |
| Sanctions/bad faith | Filing bad faith is not the Rule 50 sufficiency standard.                       |

## review_truth

Rule 50 JMOL turns on whether a reasonable jury has a legally sufficient evidentiary basis on the targeted issue, not sympathy, bad faith, or automatic jury submission.
