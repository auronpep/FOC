## 7. Full Right-Answer Explanation

A is correct. The permit is for personal, recreational use of the state's natural resources, not for earning a living or exercising a fundamental right. Gold Key: noncommercial recreational access to a state's natural resources is not the kind of fundamental Article IV privilege that forces equal resident and nonresident treatment. Silver Key: when the answer set offers clause labels and scrutiny words, first ask whether the activity is economic or fundamental; if it is just recreation in state-managed resources, the police-power/natural-resource answer is the lane.
