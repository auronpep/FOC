### 9. Full black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning
The tested rule is unchanged: in a diversity case, a federal court applies state law when the state rule is part of the state-created right, including a products-liability repose rule that extinguishes the claim. The Rules of Decision Act, 28 U.S.C. § 1652, provides that state laws are rules of decision in federal civil actions where they apply. Erie R. Co. v. Tompkins, 304 U.S. 64 (1938), rejects federal general common law in diversity. Guaranty Trust Co. v. York, 326 U.S. 99 (1945), treated a state limitations rule as binding in a diversity case when it was bound up with the state-created right. Hanna v. Plumer, 380 U.S. 460 (1965), preserves the boundary: a valid conflicting federal rule can control procedure, but none is supplied here. The governing state products-liability statute is the primary rule defining the claim's life span. Because it says no action may be brought after the fixed first-sale period, the federal court applies it and dismisses. Bank validation: PASS.
