**D is correct.**

**Gold Key:** Attempt follows the actor’s criminal purpose and the facts as the actor believes them to be. Factual impossibility is no defense. Lydia’s mistake about the powder does not save her.

**Silver Key:** This is a 2×2 actor-liability matrix. Do not ask “was the powder real?” first. Split the actors: Peter knew fake; Lydia believed real.

## 7. Full wrong-answer explanations

**A is wrong.** It is half right. Lydia is guilty, but Peter is not. Peter knew the powder was not cocaine, so he lacked the intent to sell cocaine.

**B is wrong.** This is the dominant trap. It treats “the powder was not cocaine” as ending the attempt question for everyone. **Gold Key:** Factual impossibility is no defense when the actor intended the target crime and took a substantial step. That rule catches Lydia.

**C is wrong.** It reverses the actor map. Peter knew fake; Lydia believed real. The answer assigns guilt to the wrong actor and innocence to the actor who had the attempt intent.

## 8. Black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning

The tested rule is unchanged from the original: attempt requires intent to commit the target offense plus a substantial step; factual impossibility is not a defense, while a person who lacks the target-crime intent is not guilty of attempt. Model Penal Code § 5.01(1)(c) frames attempt around conduct that, under the circumstances as the actor believes them to be, constitutes a substantial step toward the crime. ([Open Casebook][1]) Federal pattern instructions likewise state that factual impossibility is not a defense to attempt and cite *United States v. Medina-Garcia*. ([MA Bankruptcy Court][2]) *Medina-Garcia* states directly that factual impossibility is not a defense to a charge of attempt. ([Justia Law][3])

Applied here: Lydia intended to sell cocaine and took substantial steps by repackaging and selling the powder. The fact that the powder was not cocaine is factual impossibility. Peter, by contrast, knew the powder was not cocaine and therefore lacked intent to sell cocaine. The original official key and the transformed key both remain **D**.
