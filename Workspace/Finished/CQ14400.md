The source row tests buyer-side rescission for mistake; it credits the hidden, undiscoverable condition and rejects sterility/breeding, paperwork/pedigree, and known behavior/risk explanations. Section 154 allocates risk when the parties allocate it, when a party knows it has limited knowledge and treats it as sufficient, or when a court reasonably allocates the risk to that party.
