## 7. Full right-answer explanation

B is correct.

**Silver Key:** When three answers offer different admission purposes and one says no exception applies, do not count the three as a majority. Test every purpose and every hearsay layer.

**Gold Key:** For hearsay within hearsay, every layer must independently avoid hearsay treatment or fit a hearsay exception.

Hannah is testifying to what Daniel said to Hannah. Daniel’s statement also contains what Daniel allegedly told the floor manager. That creates two layers. Daniel’s statement to the manager may matter for notice, because the manager is the relevant listener. But Daniel’s later retelling to Hannah is a separate out-of-court statement. It is not offered to show notice to Hannah, and no exception is supplied for that layer. Because one layer fails, the reported statement is inadmissible.

## 8. Full wrong-answer explanations

**A. admissible, to prove that the defendant had notice of the defect.**
This is the dominant trap. It sees the best fact in the stem: Daniel says he warned the floor manager before Mary fell. **Gold Key:** A notice theory can help with the warning to the manager, but it does not automatically clear Daniel’s later retelling to Hannah. Hannah’s notice is not the defendant’s notice.

**C. admissible, to prove both that the mat was defective and that the defendant had notice of the defect.**
This choice tries to win by combining both attractive uses. The defect use treats the statements as proof that the mat was actually loose, and the notice use still leaves Daniel’s later statement to Hannah uncleared. **Silver Key:** In a multiple-theory array, test each theory separately. Combining two bad routes does not make one good route.

**D. admissible, to prove that the mat was defective.**
This choice focuses on the physical condition. To prove that the mat was defective, both out-of-court layers are being used for the truth of what Daniel asserted. No exception is supplied for either layer.

FRE 801(c) defines hearsay as a statement the declarant does not make while testifying at the current trial or hearing and that a party offers to prove the truth of the matter asserted. **Title:** Double hearsay layer check
**Signal:** Witness repeats what one person said about what that same person told someone else.
**Student move:** Number the layers before choosing the purpose.
**Tiny rule:** Every hearsay layer needs its own non-hearsay purpose or exception.
**Trap:** Notice clears only the warning heard by the manager; it does not clear the later retelling to the witness.
**Confidence:** ANCHOR_ASSISTED

## Gold Key(s)

**GK-EVIDENCE-DOUBLE-HEARSAY-01**
For hearsay within hearsay, every layer must independently avoid hearsay treatment or fit a hearsay exception.

**GK-EVIDENCE-NOTICE-LISTENER-02**
A statement offered to show notice works only for the listener whose notice matters; a later retelling to a different listener still needs its own admissibility path.

## Silver Key(s)

**SK-EVIDENCE-MULTIPLE-THEORY-01**
When three answers offer different admission purposes and one says no exception applies, do not count the three as a majority; test every purpose and every hearsay layer.
