## 9. Full Black-Letter-Law Verification AND Legal Reasoning for the Right Answer
The tested issue remains Torts, Negligence, Negligence per se, and the verified outline code is `61020302`. Restatement (Third) of Torts: Liability for Physical and Emotional Harm section 14 states the negligence-per-se rule for unexcused statutory violations when the statute protects against the type of accident and protects the plaintiff's class. Restatement section 26 supplies the factual-causation rule: tortious conduct must be a but-for cause of the harm, subject to the multiple-sufficient-causes doctrine in section 27.

The research check does not contradict the supplied key.
