## 6. Full Right-Answer Explanation

**B is correct.** Rule 14 gives the trial court discretion to grant severance or other relief when joinder appears prejudicial, but severance is not automatic. Under **Gold Key: Rule 14/Zafiro severance standard**, even mutually antagonistic defenses do not require separate trials as a matter of law. The defendant must show a serious risk that the joint trial compromised a specific trial right or prevented the jury from making a reliable judgment.

The trial court used the ordinary less drastic remedy: a limiting instruction telling the jury to consider the voice notes only against Stephen. **Silver Key: Cut the absolutes first.** The answer choices saying "whenever," "every item," or "absolute right" are too rigid for Rule 14. Unless the voice notes directly and powerfully implicated Lydia in the narrow Bruton/Samia sense, the court may rely on the jury's ability to follow the instruction. On these facts, denial of severance is probably not reversible error.

## 7. Full Wrong-Answer Explanations

### Choice A

What makes A attractive is the fairness instinct. If evidence is inadmissible against Lydia, a student may think Lydia must have a right to keep it out of the courtroom entirely. The breaker is that joint trials routinely include evidence admitted against one defendant but limited as to another. Rule 14 does not create an absolute exclusion right. **Gold Key: Bruton boundary.** A limiting instruction usually works unless the codefendant statement is the kind of direct, facially incriminating confession that the jury cannot realistically ignore.

### Choice C

What makes C attractive is the efficiency preference for trying alleged co-conspirators together. That preference is real, but the word "absolute" breaks the choice. Rule 14 expressly gives courts discretion to sever defendants or provide other relief when joinder appears prejudicial. The government has no unlimited right to keep a joint trial when a defendant shows serious uncured prejudice.

### Choice D

What makes D attractive is the head-to-head defense posture: Lydia says Stephen acted alone, and Stephen says Lydia designed the plan. That feels like a trial where one defendant can win only by hurting the other. The breaker is **Gold Key: Rule 14/Zafiro severance standard**. Mutually antagonistic defenses are not prejudicial per se. The defendant must show a serious risk to a trial right or a reliable verdict, and less drastic measures such as limiting instructions often suffice.

## 8. Full Black-Letter-Law Verification and Legal Reasoning

**Governing rule:** Federal Rule of Criminal Procedure 14(a) permits a court to order separate trials, sever defendants' trials, or provide other appropriate relief if joinder appears to prejudice a defendant or the government. The rule is discretionary, not automatic.

**Zafiro v. United States, 506 U.S. 534 (1993):** Zafiro holds that Rule 14 does not require severance as a matter of law when codefendants present mutually antagonistic or mutually exclusive defenses. Severance is appropriate only when there is a serious risk that a joint trial would compromise a specific trial right of a properly joined defendant or prevent the jury from making a reliable judgment. Zafiro also confirms that less drastic measures, including limiting instructions, often suffice.

**Bruton/Samia boundary:** Bruton v. United States, 391 U.S. 123 (1968), recognizes a narrow Confrontation Clause problem where a nontestifying codefendant's confession directly inculpates the defendant in a joint trial despite a limiting instruction. Samia v. United States, 599 U.S. 635 (2023), confirms that Bruton is a narrow exception and that limiting instructions remain effective when the statement does not directly inculpate the defendant.

## review_truth

A properly joined criminal defendant gets severance only when the joint trial creates a serious, uncured risk to a specific trial right or to a reliable verdict; antagonistic defenses and one-defendant-only evidence do not automatically require separate trials.
