**C is correct.** Lydia was standing next to the velvet case when Daniel used intimidation to freeze her and take the handbells. **Gold Key:** robbery does not require property to be on the victim's body or physically attached to the victim; immediate presence covers property close enough for the victim to control or protect but for the robber's force or intimidation. **Silver Key:** lock the call to person-or-immediate-presence. ## 8. Full wrong-answer explanations

**A.** This is the dominant trap. It shrinks "person or immediate presence" into a physical-attachment requirement. **Gold Key:** immediate presence is broader than attachment. Property beside the victim can qualify when force or fear prevents control.

**B.** This gives the right result for the wrong reason. **Silver Key:** the call asks whether the property was from the person or immediate presence when taken. Later escape does not supply that element.

**D.** This choice talks about ownership. The concert sponsor may own the handbells, but the call asks whether the handbells were within Lydia's immediate presence and control. The ownership point is a wrong element.
