## 7. Right-Answer Explanation

**C is correct.** Lydia had the right to remove the wax-melting vats.

**Gold Key — GK-REALPROP-TRADE-FIXTURE-01:** A tenant may remove *trade fixtures* — items installed for the tenant's commercial business — when three conditions are all met: (1) installed for trade/business purposes; (2) removal occurs before the lease terminates; (3) tenant repairs any damage caused by removal. No lease designation and no magic words are required. The doctrine applies automatically from the facts.

All three conditions are satisfied: Lydia installed the vats for her candle-making business (trade purpose ✓), removed them two weeks before the lease expired (before lease end ✓), and filled all the bolt holes (damage repaired ✓). Barnabas's claim fails on the law.

## 9. Black-Letter Verification and Legal Reasoning

**Governing rule — Trade Fixtures (lawyer-confirmation layer):**
Under the common-law trade fixture doctrine, a commercial tenant may remove personal property installed during the tenancy for use in the tenant's trade or business, subject to three conditions: (a) the items were installed for commercial/trade purposes rather than personal residential use; (b) removal occurs before the lease terminates (or within a reasonable time where the tenant holds over with landlord consent); and (c) the tenant restores the premises by repairing any damage caused by removal.

**Authority:** Restatement (Second) of Property (Landlord and Tenant) § 12.2 (trade fixtures as tenant's personal property removable during tenancy); the doctrine is universally recognized at common law across American jurisdictions. See also American Law of Property §§ 12.85–12.86 (trade fixture exception to the general accession doctrine).

## Gold Key(s)

**GK-REALPROP-TRADE-FIXTURE-01 — Trade Fixture Removal Exception**

A tenant may remove trade fixtures — items installed for the tenant's commercial business — before the lease ends, provided the tenant repairs any damage caused by removal. No lease designation or magic words are required. The doctrine applies automatically when its three elements are met: (1) installed for business purposes; (2) removed before lease termination; (3) tenant repairs damage caused by removal.

- **Type:** exception
- **Unlocks:** Trade fixture removal right; defeats the magic-words distractor (B) and the absolute-bar distractor (D); confirms C.
- **Trigger:** Tenant removes business-installed equipment before lease ends and repairs damage; landlord claims fixture.
