### 7. Full Right-Answer Explanation

C is correct. Daniel formed the intent to steal before Lydia consented, lifted Lydia's personal property from the quilt, and thereby completed the taking and carrying away required for larceny. Gold Key: the carrying-away element is satisfied by slight movement when the intent to steal and lack of consent are present. Silver Key: timestamp the consent; permission that arrives after the taking does not rewrite the earlier completed act.

### 8. Full Wrong-Answer Explanations

A sells the idea that Daniel was caught before he could finish the theft because the stand never reached the tote bag. Gold Key: larceny does not require escape, concealment, or pocketing; lifting the stand from its place is enough asportation, so attempt merges into the completed crime.

B is attractive because Lydia eventually told Daniel to take the stand. Silver Key: the answer turns on the moment Daniel first lifted the stand, not on Lydia's later forgiveness or gift decision.

D borrows a real property-crime label and tries to make Daniel's later permission look like entrusted possession. Embezzlement requires lawful possession before the conversion, and Daniel had no such possession when he lifted the stand.

### 9. Black-Letter-Law Verification and Legal Reasoning

The tested rule, legal issue, and credited outcome are unchanged: common-law larceny is the trespassory taking and carrying away of another's personal property with intent to permanently deprive the owner. The Department of Justice Criminal Resource Manual section 1006 states the larceny elements as wrongful taking and carrying away, absence of consent, and intent to deprive, citing United States v. Barlow, 480 F.2d 1245, 1251 (D.C. Cir. 1972): https://www.justice.gov/archives/usam/criminal-resource-manual-1006-larceny. Daniel's lifting of the stand supplied the taking and carrying away while he lacked consent and intended to steal. Lydia's later permission does not negate the completed trespassory taking. Attempt is absorbed once the larceny is completed. Embezzlement does not fit because Daniel did not start with lawful possession.

### 10. Rigor and Difficulty Preservation

The variation preserves the same timing trap: the student must separate the completed taking from the later gift permission and must know that slight movement is enough for asportation.
