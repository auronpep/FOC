### 7. Full right-answer explanation (exam-register)

**A**. Complete diversity is not present. An unincorporated association (LLC or partnership) is a citizen of every state of which its members or partners are citizens. The citizenship of the LLC is traced through any entity layers to the underlying natural persons. ### 9. Black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning for the right answer

The supplied row tests QID 19533, a Civil Procedure item on the citizenship of unincorporated entities for diversity jurisdiction. The official key is B: complete diversity is not present because the LLC takes the citizenship of all members, including the Arizona partner traced through the member partnership.

For diversity jurisdiction under 28 U.S.C. § 1332, a corporation is a citizen of (a) the state of incorporation and (b) the state of its principal place of business. 28 U.S.C. § 1332(c)(1). An unincorporated association, by contrast, is a citizen of every state of which its members are citizens; the citizenship of entity members is traced through any entity layers to the underlying natural persons. Carden v. Arkadelphia Mills, 277 U.S. 482 (1928); see also Bayer AG v. Housey Pharmaceuticals, Inc., 340 F.3d 1367 (Fed. Cir. 2003) (LLC citizenship traced through all members and their entity layers). The transformed outcome is identical: because Mary is an Arizona citizen and her citizenship is traced through the member limited partnership to the LLC, the LLC is an Arizona citizen, and complete diversity is destroyed.
