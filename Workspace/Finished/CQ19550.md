### 7. Full right-answer explanation (exam-register)

**A**. A § 1406(a) transfer curing an improper-venue filing generally applies the transferee forum's choice-of-law rules. The rule is the converse of Van Dusen: where the original forum was improper, the transferor-law carryover does not apply; the transferee court applies its own forum's choice-of-law rules. The leading statement is in Ferens v. John Deere Co., 494 U.S. 516 (1990), which limited Van Dusen's transferor-law carryover to proper-forum § 1404(a) convenience transfers. **Gold Key:** § 1406(a) cure transfers use transferee choice-of-law rules; § 1404(a) convenience transfers from a proper forum use transferor rules.

The supplied row tests QID 19550, a Civil Procedure item on choice-of-law rules after a § 1406(a) transfer. The official key is C: the § 1406(a) cure generally uses the transferee forum's choice-of-law rules.
