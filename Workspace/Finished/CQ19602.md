**Full black-letter-law verification AND legal reasoning for the right answer**

Tested rule: Diminished capacity, where recognized by the jurisdiction, is a doctrine that allows a defendant to introduce evidence of mental impairment to negate the specific-intent or heightened mental element of a crime.
