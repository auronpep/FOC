A sealant used to waterproof lantern batteries for military disaster-response kits gives off a fine vapor in component workshops. Long exposure to the vapor can damage workers' lungs. ## 7. Full Right-Answer Explanation

C is correct. Section 18 gives a Senate committee the power to decide whether an agency closure order will have legal effect after the agency has found a statutory violation. That is not ordinary oversight. It is congressional action with legal effect outside the Article I lawmaking process.

Gold Key: A congressional committee cannot be given a legislative veto over executive action. If congressional action changes legal rights, duties, or enforcement consequences, it must satisfy bicameralism and presentment unless the Constitution itself supplies a narrow exception.

Silver Key: In this answer array, separate supervision from legal effect. Reports, hearings, funding limits, and later statutes are supervision tools; a committee approval condition that decides whether an agency order takes effect is the veto problem.

## 8. Full Wrong-Answer Explanations

D is attractive because Congress may oversee agencies it creates. The breaker is the word "supervise" doing too much work. Silver Key: supervision is not the same as a committee having final legal control over whether an agency order becomes effective.

## 9. Full Black-Letter-Law Verification And Legal Reasoning For The Right Answer

The tested rule, legal issue, and credited outcome are unchanged from the source item. The rule is the legislative-veto rule: Congress may not reserve to one house, one committee, or another congressional subset the power to approve, disapprove, or alter executive action with legal effect unless the action goes through the constitutionally required lawmaking process.

Authority floor: Article I, Section 7 requires bicameral passage and presentment for federal lawmaking. The Supreme Court in INS v. Chadha, 462 U.S. 919 (1983), held that a one-house legislative veto over executive action violated the bicameralism and presentment requirements. Constitution Annotated also treats the legislative veto as invalid under Article I, Sections 1 and 7. Sources used for verification: https://constitution.congress.gov/browse/article-1/section-7/clause-2/, https://constitution.congress.gov/browse/essay/artI-S7-C2-4/ALDE_00013647/, and https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep462/usrep462919/usrep462919.pdf.

Applied here, the Senate Readiness Committee's refusal prevents the Closure Order from taking effect. That committee action has legal consequence for the workshop, the Agency, and the Department of Justice. Congress can write standards, create agencies, require reports, hold hearings, amend the statute through ordinary legislation, or use other constitutional oversight tools. It cannot make a committee approval decision the legal trigger for executive enforcement. The source key is confirmed, and the bank validation verdict is PASS.
