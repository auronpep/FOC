The item is bank-valid, but it exposes a C3 curriculum gap. The current deck needs a tiny anchor for solicitation.

### 7. choice_by_choice_walkthrough

#### A. Pavel is not guilty because Rosa refused and no agreement formed

1. **Student-accessible C3 signal:** The choice talks about refusal and lack of agreement. That sounds like conspiracy, not solicitation.
2. **Student label:** Conspiracy-frame trap.
3. **What a true/responsive version would look like:** A no-liability answer would need to say the request was not communicated or that Pavel lacked intent for Rosa to commit arson.
4. **Lawyer confirmation:** Solicitation does not require agreement. Refusal by the solicited person does not undo the completed solicitation.

#### B. Pavel is guilty because the request was communicated with intent that Rosa commit the crime

1. **Student-accessible C3 signal:** This choice matches the charge and the facts: request, communication, and criminal intent.
2. **Student label:** Solicitation-completion residual.
3. **What a true/responsive version would look like:** This is the true-and-responsive statement once the tiny anchor is taught.
4. **Lawyer confirmation:** This is correct. Solicitation is complete when the defendant intentionally communicates a request, command, or encouragement that another person commit a crime.

#### C. Pavel is not guilty because he did not personally attempt to burn the van

1. **Student-accessible C3 signal:** The choice answers attempt. The call is solicitation.
2. **Student label:** Attempt-frame trap.
3. **What a true/responsive version would look like:** This answer might matter if Pavel were charged with attempted arson. It does not answer solicitation.
4. **Lawyer confirmation:** Solicitation punishes the intentional request itself. Pavel did not need to personally attempt to burn the van.

#### D. Pavel is guilty only if Rosa was paid in advance

1. **Student-accessible C3 signal:** “Only if” creates a requirement. The stem and call give no payment requirement.
2. **Student label:** Invented payment element.
3. **What a true/responsive version would look like:** Payment could be evidence of intent, but it is not required.
4. **Lawyer confirmation:** Compensation is not an element of solicitation.

### 10. final_student_script

“The charge is solicitation. Do not import conspiracy or attempt. Pavel asked Rosa to commit arson, and Rosa read the request. Solicitation completes on the communicated request, even though Rosa refused. Choose B.”

### 11. remediation_card

**Card:** Solicitation Completes on the Ask.

**Tiny rule:** Solicitation is complete when a defendant intentionally communicates a request, command, or encouragement that another person commit a crime.

**Trap:** The other person refuses, no agreement forms, or the defendant does not personally attempt the crime.

**Move:** Stay on the charged inchoate offense. Solicitation is the request. Conspiracy is agreement. Attempt is the defendant’s own substantial step.
