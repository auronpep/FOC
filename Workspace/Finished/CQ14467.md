## 8. Black-letter verification and right-answer legal reasoning

The governing rule is unchanged: a breach of fewer than all installment-payment obligations does not automatically create a claim for the whole unmatured balance. Restatement (Second) of Contracts § 243(3) states that where the only remaining duties are payment of money in installments not related to one another, nonperformance as to less than the whole does not create a claim for damages for total breach. ([Open Casebook][1])

The acceleration trap is also unchanged. An acceleration clause is a contract term that makes all payments due if specified conditions occur; without such a term or other valid trigger, the creditor’s ordinary recovery is for the amounts already due. ([Legal Information Institute][2])

The transformed item preserves the official outcome.
