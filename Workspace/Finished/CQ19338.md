## 9. Full black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning

The credited answer is correct under Article III and the federal-question statute. Congress implemented that power for federal district courts in 28 U.S.C. 1331, which gives district courts original jurisdiction over civil actions arising under the Constitution, laws, or treaties of the United States. Lydia's complaint asserts that the state licensing amendment violates the U.S. Constitution, so the case arises under federal law. Her residence in the same state as the state official does not defeat federal-question jurisdiction.

The source stem named a state department as the defendant, which could invite an untested sovereign-immunity issue.
