**SK-REAL_PROPERTY-NUISANCE-FRAME-01** — In a strongest-argument nuisance array, first ask which choice ties the condition to the plaintiff's use, safety, or enjoyment of the land. Cut community-opinion, market-value, and no-other-remedy answers as off-frame.
