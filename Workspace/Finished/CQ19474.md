## A) PASS-1 TRANSFORM REPORT

### 1. Final question

Lydia operated a private online shop that sold Scripture-memory candle sets. She contracted with Stephen to supply a month's order of beeswax candles packed in blue metal tins. When Stephen learned that blue tins were temporarily unavailable, Lydia told him, "Use kraft gift boxes this month; I will accept them." Stephen bought enough kraft boxes for the order and packed the entire month's candles in them. The evening before delivery, Lydia sent Stephen a notice stating that she would require blue tins after all.

Which statement is most accurate under Article 2?

### 2. Distilled Core Question

After a buyer waives a packaging term for this month's goods and the seller materially relies by buying and using the substitute packaging, may the buyer retract the waiver on the eve of delivery?

### 6. Student black-letter-law answer explanation

B is correct. Article 2 lets a party retract a waiver for future performance by reasonable notice, but that permission stops when retraction would be unjust because the other side materially changed position in reliance on the waiver. Lydia waived the blue-tin packaging term for this month's candle order. Stephen then bought kraft boxes and packed the entire month's candles in them. Gold Key: under UCC 2-209(5), waiver retraction is blocked when material reliance would make the retraction unjust. Silver Key: do not stop at the word "retract"; scan the stem for the other side spending money, changing production, or otherwise locking in performance after the waiver.

### 7. Full right-answer explanation

B is correct because Lydia's notice came after Stephen materially relied on her waiver. The original contract required blue tins, but Lydia told Stephen to use kraft gift boxes for the month and said she would accept them. Stephen then bought the substitute boxes and packed the entire month's order. Article 2 does not require new consideration for this waiver route, and it does not let Lydia freely undo the waiver when Stephen has materially changed position in reliance. Gold Key: a UCC waiver affecting future performance can be retracted only by reasonable notice, and not when material reliance would make retraction unjust. Silver Key: the answer array turns on the retraction limit, not on whether the original packaging term still existed on paper.

### 8. Full wrong-answer explanations

A is attractive because the original writing really did call for blue tins, and kraft boxes are different packaging. The breaker is the waiver. Lydia's later demand does not erase the consequence of her earlier instruction after Stephen materially relied by buying and using the substitute packaging.

C is attractive because students often import the common-law modification instinct: a changed deal sounds like it should need fresh consideration. The breaker is the Article 2 waiver route. Gold Key: UCC 2-209 separates modification consideration from waiver; a waiver is not defeated merely because no new consideration was given.

D is attractive because Article 2 does permit retraction of a waiver affecting future performance. The breaker is the word "always." Silver Key: when a choice says the waiver can always be retracted before delivery, check the reliance facts before accepting it. Stephen materially changed position after Lydia's waiver, so retraction may be ineffective.

### 9. Full black-letter-law verification AND legal reasoning for the right answer

The tested legal issue is unchanged from the source item: under UCC Article 2, whether a party who waived a term affecting executory performance can retract that waiver after the other party materially relied on it. The credited outcome is unchanged: the attempted retraction is ineffective if retraction would be unjust because of material reliance.

UCC section 2-209(1) provides that an agreement modifying a contract within Article 2 needs no consideration to be binding. UCC section 2-209(4) allows an attempted modification or rescission that fails formal requirements to operate as a waiver. UCC section 2-209(5) provides that a party who has made a waiver affecting an executory portion of the contract may retract the waiver by reasonable notification that strict performance will be required, unless retraction would be unjust in view of a material change of position in reliance on the waiver. Source: D.C. Code section 28:2-209, https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/28%3A2-209.

Those authorities confirm the official key. Lydia's instruction to use kraft boxes waived the blue-tin packaging requirement for the month's executory shipment. Stephen materially relied by purchasing substitute packaging and packing the whole order. Because retraction the night before delivery would be unjust in view of that material reliance, the Article 2 retraction rule does not let Lydia insist on blue tins after all. The source row's supplied outline code, `55010101`, is a valid UCC-applicable-law code, but the deeper verified outline code for the tested waiver rule is `56050604`.
