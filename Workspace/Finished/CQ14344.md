## 7. Full Right-Answer Explanation

**A is correct.** Congress may enforce the Thirteenth Amendment by appropriate legislation. That enforcement power is not limited to state action. It can reach private conduct when Congress rationally determines that the conduct is a badge or incident of slavery. Racial discrimination against Black persons in business transactions fits that enforcement lane, so a statute forbidding that discrimination by public and private actors is likely constitutional. **Gold Key:** the Thirteenth Amendment is the Reconstruction Amendment that can reach private racial discrimination; do not import the Fourteenth Amendment state-action limit into it. **Silver Key:** in a yes/because answer array, a correct "yes" is not enough - the reason must name a real constitutional source.

## 8. Full Wrong-Answer Explanations

- **B - local-transactions / Commerce Clause objection.** The choice is attractive because the statute reaches every business transaction, including local deals that may feel too small for federal regulation. The breaker is that the statute does not need to survive only as a Commerce Clause statute; the Thirteenth Amendment enforcement power supplies a separate constitutional source for legislation aimed at racial discrimination as a badge or incident of slavery.
- **C - Tenth Amendment objection.** The choice sells the familiar federalism reflex that ordinary business dealings are usually state-regulated. The breaker is that the Tenth Amendment reserves only powers not delegated to the federal government, and the Thirteenth Amendment expressly delegates enforcement power to Congress.
- **D - general-welfare justification (dominant trap).** The choice is tempting because it says "yes," matches the statute's broad moral purpose, and sounds like a flexible federal power. The breaker is that there is no freestanding congressional power to regulate for the general welfare; general welfare matters in the taxing-and-spending lane, not as an independent source for this statute. **Gold Key:** general welfare is not a standalone regulatory power. **Silver Key:** do not let the correct yes/no outcome rescue the wrong constitutional source.

## 9. Black-Letter Verification And Legal Reasoning

**Tested rule:** Congress may enforce the Thirteenth Amendment through appropriate legislation, and that enforcement authority may reach private conduct when Congress rationally identifies the regulated practice as a badge or incident of slavery.
