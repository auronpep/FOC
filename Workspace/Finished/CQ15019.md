The researched authority supports the supplied official key. Uniform Common Interest Ownership Act Section 3-111 provides that a unit owner is not liable solely by reason of being a unit owner for injury or damage arising from the condition or use of the common elements, and that an action alleging a wrong done by the association is brought against the association rather than the unit owner. Delaware's current common-interest statute says the same in 25 Del. C. UCIOA Section 3-117 and state analogues also keep association-creditor remedies away from the owner's non-unit personal property, reinforcing that association liability is not ordinary joint and several personal liability of all owners.
