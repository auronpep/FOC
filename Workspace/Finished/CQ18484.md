**A is correct.** A suppressed post-indictment lineup does not automatically erase every later courtroom identification by the same witness. The prosecution must show that the in-court identification comes from a source independent of the unconstitutional lineup. Gold Key: after an uncounseled post-indictment lineup, the lineup identification is out, but a courtroom identification can still come in if the witness has an independent crime-scene basis for recognizing the defendant. Silver Key: match the answer's direction to the call; if the court should permit the evidence, the answer must say "Yes" and give the independent-source reason.

Lydia's testimony gives that independent source. She had several minutes of face-to-face observation, bright lighting, an uncovered face, and a distance of less than three feet. Those facts let the court treat her in-court identification as rooted in her own perception of the robbery rather than in the suppressed lineup.
