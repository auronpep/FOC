### 9. Full black-letter-law verification and right-answer legal reasoning

The source row identifies QID 20004 as a Contracts item testing UCC 2-306(1), requirements quantity, good-faith zero requirements, and the effect of a stated estimate.  UCC 2-306(1) states that an output or requirements term means the party’s actual output or requirements as may occur in good faith, subject to the limit that quantities may not be unreasonably disproportionate to a stated estimate or comparable prior output or requirements. ([Legal Information Institute][1]) Official-comment material explains that good-faith variations may be large enough to result in discontinuance; a shutdown for lack of orders may be permissible, while a shutdown merely to curtail losses may not be. ([Justia][2])
