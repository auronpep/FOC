## 9. Black-Letter-Law Verification and Legal Reasoning

The tested rule, issue, and credited outcome are unchanged from the source item. A facially neutral government action can violate Equal Protection if discriminatory purpose was a motivating factor. The plaintiff may prove that purpose through direct and circumstantial evidence, including official statements and departures from normal procedure. If racial purpose is shown, strict scrutiny may apply even though the law does not classify by race on its face.

Authority floor: Village of Arlington Heights v.
