## 7. Full Right-Answer Explanation
D is correct. Lydia died before Matthew, so she could not take the loft under Matthew's will. Gold Key: a devise to a named person who predeceases the testator lapses unless the will or an applicable anti-lapse statute supplies a substitute taker; a failed nonresiduary devise falls into the residue. Here the will gave no alternate taker, and the stem says no applicable statute addresses the situation. The lapsed devise therefore passes under the residuary clause to the Emmaus Literacy Mission.

Silver Key: sort the answer choices by the failure type. The loft did not disappear, so ademption is the wrong lane. The named taker disappeared, so lapse is the lane that answers the call.

## 8. Full Wrong-Answer Explanations

B is attractive because it turns silence into flexibility: the will did not expressly say "only if Lydia survives me." The breaker runs the other direction.
