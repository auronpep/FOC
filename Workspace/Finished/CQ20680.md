Esther signed a written agreement to buy Paul's lakeside cottage parcel for $615,000. The closing was set for July 18.

### 7. Full Right-Answer Explanation

B is correct. Esther and Paul mutually agreed before closing to release each other from the land-sale contract. The original sale of land had to be in writing, but the later agreement was a rescission, not a new land-sale promise. Gold Key: for a non-goods contract, a mutual rescission can discharge the parties even if the original contract was within the Statute of Frauds. Silver Key: separate the original deal from the later exit deal; the writing rule attaches to the land sale, while the call asks whether the already-released buyer can sue for breach.

### 8. Full Wrong-Answer Explanations

A sells the fairness detour. Paul's mention of a possible higher offer sounds opportunistic, but the breaker is timing: Esther had already agreed to release him before she tried to revive the deal.

C sells the reliance vocabulary. The stem gives a possible future higher offer, not a completed resale or a reliance fact that must carry the result. Silver Key: when the clean release already decides the call, do not let a later fairness fact replace the discharge rule.

D sells the dominant trap. Land-sale contracts normally trigger a writing instinct, so this answer feels careful. Gold Key: the later oral agreement is a mutual rescission of a non-goods contract, not a fresh land conveyance promise that must itself be signed.

### 9. Full Black-Letter-Law Verification and Legal Reasoning

The tested rule, legal issue, and credited outcome are unchanged. The original contract is a land-sale agreement, so the Statute of Frauds writing frame is real. But the later exchange is an agreement of rescission that discharges the remaining duties under the existing contract. The source row states the Restatement (Second) of Contracts section 148 rule for oral rescission despite a written original agreement in a non-goods contract. Restatement (Second) of Contracts section 283 also defines an agreement of rescission as an agreement under which each party agrees to discharge the other's remaining duties of performance. Those authorities confirm the official key: Esther cannot recover after agreeing to cancel the contract.
