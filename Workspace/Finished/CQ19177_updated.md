**GK-CONLAW-FOURTEENTH-STATE-03** — The Fourteenth Amendment Equal Protection Clause is the state-action source; do not use it as the direct source for Congress.

**GK-CONLAW-NO-GENERAL-POLICE-04** — Congress has enumerated powers, not a general police power; find the specific federal power that fits.
