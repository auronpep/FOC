### 9. Full black-letter-law verification AND legal reasoning for the right answer

The transformed item preserves the source rule, issue, and credited outcome: a qualified accident investigator may give an expert opinion about speed when the opinion is based on training, experience, and physical evidence. The official key maps to new **B**, and current legal authority does not contradict it.

Current Federal Rule of Evidence 702, as amended effective December 1, 2023, allows a witness qualified by knowledge, skill, experience, training, or education to testify in opinion form if the proponent shows the expert's specialized knowledge will help the trier of fact, the testimony rests on sufficient facts or data, and the principles and methods are reliably applied. The official federal rules publication lists the amended Rule 702 text, including the "more likely than not" gatekeeping language and the reliable-application requirement. [1] The Rule 702 notes also confirm that experience may be a sufficient foundation for expert testimony when the expert explains how that experience supports the opinion. [2]

The adjacent opinion rules support the distractor audit. Rule 701 governs lay opinion and channels testimony based on scientific, technical, or specialized knowledge into Rule 702, but it does not create a one-opinion-per-issue limit. [3] Rule 704 states that an opinion is not automatically objectionable merely because it embraces an ultimate issue, reinforcing that overlap with a disputed fact such as speed does not itself exclude the opinion. Daniel's opinion is therefore admissible: speed is a fact in issue, accident reconstruction can help the factfinder, the stem gives Daniel qualifying training and experience, and his opinion is tied to physical evidence at the scene. Signal: experienced accident investigator offers a speed range from physical evidence.

Student move: ask whether the witness's own training, experience, and scene evidence satisfy Rule 702.

Tiny rule: expert speed opinions do not need an opposing lay opinion or a scientific-certainty label.

Trap: picking the proper-result answer for the wrong opening-the-door reason.

Confidence: `ANCHOR_ASSISTED`.

## Gold Key(s)

**GK-EVIDENCE-RULE702-QUALIFICATION-01** - Rule 702 allows an expert to qualify through knowledge, skill, experience, training, or education; an experience-based accident investigator may give a speed opinion when the opinion helps the factfinder and rests on sufficient facts and reliable application.

Type: `rule`. Trigger: an experienced accident investigator gives a speed opinion from scene evidence. Authority: Fed. R. Evid. 702. Last-minute review: true.

## Silver Key(s)

**SK-EVIDENCE-EXPERT-ARRAY-01** - In a proper/improper array, read the because-clause before locking an answer; a proper-result choice still loses if the reason is not the admissibility rule.

Type: `answer_array`. Trigger: two choices reach the same admissibility result but give different reasons. Tested choice: A. Outline code: `32020802`. Last-minute review: true.
