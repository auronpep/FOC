## Core Legal Rule Preserved

**Model Penal Code § 2.06(1)(a):** A person is an accomplice if "with the purpose of promoting or facilitating the commission of the offense, he solicits such other person to commit it; or aids or agrees or attempts to aid such other person in planning or committing it."

**Key Points:**
- Purpose to promote/facilitate the crime
- Active encouragement/affirmative act
- Intent that crime be committed

**Black-Letter Rule:** One who intentionally encourages another to commit arson is an accomplice, even if words alone do not create a new danger.
