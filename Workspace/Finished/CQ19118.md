### 9. Black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning for the right answer

The supplied row tests QID 19118, a Civil Procedure item on Rule 15(c) party-change relation back. The official key is A: yes, if the Rule 15(c) notice and mistake requirements are satisfied.

Rule 15(c)(1)(C) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure provides that an amendment changing a party against whom a claim is asserted relates back to the date of the original pleading if, within the Rule 4(m) period for service, the new party received notice of the action and knew or should have known that the action would have been brought against it but for a mistake concerning the identity of the proper party. The same transaction or occurrence requirement of Rule 15(c)(1)(B) and the no-prejudice requirement also apply. The advisory committee notes explain that the rule is designed to balance the plaintiff's interest in having the claim decided on the merits against the new party's interest in receiving timely notice. The transformed outcome is identical: the amendment may relate back because Alpha Logistics received the complaint within Rule 4(m) and knew Martha meant to sue it.
