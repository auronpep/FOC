### 6. Full Right-Answer Explanation

A is correct. Caleb received Covenant Orchard in fee simple when Naomi deeded it to "Caleb and his heirs." Gold Key: in this conveyance, "and his heirs" are words of limitation, not words giving Ruth a present estate. Because Caleb owned the full fee simple, his later warranty deed to Peter conveyed the full estate unless the deed expressly limited it. Nothing limited Peter's estate, and Ruth's nonjoinder did not matter because Ruth owned no share to withhold. Silver Key: when the answer set keeps handing the heir a piece, first ask whether the first grantee ever had less than fee simple. If not, the heir-based answers collapse.

### 8. Black-Letter-Law Verification and Legal Reasoning

The tested rule is unchanged: a deed to a named grantee "and his heirs" gives the named grantee a fee simple absolute; the heirs take no present interest merely because they are mentioned in that phrase. A fee-simple owner may convey the entire estate, and a modern deed-construction statute that presumes transfer of the grantor's entire estate unless expressly limited confirms that result.

Authority floor: the source stem supplies the governing jurisdictional statute. As a real-law analog, Tex. Prop. Code Sec. 5.001 provides the same fee-simple presumption for conveyed or devised land unless the estate is expressly limited or a lesser estate is created by construction or operation of law, and states that common-law words formerly needed to transfer fee simple are unnecessary. See https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/Docs/PR/htm/PR.5.htm#5.001. Cornell LII's Wex fee-simple entry likewise summarizes the traditional "to X and their heirs" language and the modern fee-simple presumption. See https://www.law.cornell.edu/wex/fee_simple.

The researched authority confirms the official key. Naomi's deed gave Caleb fee simple. Caleb's later deed to Peter, with no express limitation, conveyed the full estate. Ruth never had an ownership interest to retain, join, share, or survive into. Bank validation verdict: PASS.
