## 7. Full Right-Answer Explanation
Robbery requires a larcenous taking (intent to steal). Peter's sincere, honest claim of right to the specific scabbard negates the intent to steal, even though the act of taking it may be battery or another offense.

## 8. Full Wrong-Answer Explanations
A: Robbery requires larcenous intent. A sincere claim of right can negate intent to steal even if force is used.
C: A sincere claim of right can negate larcenous intent even if mistaken, provided it concerns specific property.
D: Force is central to robbery and other offenses; it does not cure missing larcenous intent.

## 9. Full Black-Letter-Law Verification
A defendant who honestly believes specific property is his own may lack intent to steal that property, even if using force can create other offenses (Model Penal Code § 223.1). Peter’s honest belief negates the specific intent for robbery.
