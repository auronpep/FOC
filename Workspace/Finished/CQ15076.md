## 9. Full Black-Letter-Law Verification AND Legal Reasoning for the Right Answer
The tested issue remains Real Property, Titles, Title acquired, and the verified outline code is `84040103`. The jurisdiction's 10-year period had already run in Esther's favor because the source fact states that the character and duration of her possession caused her to become owner under adverse possession law. Once title has vested by adverse possession, the adverse possessor's title has the ordinary attributes of title and can be alienated by conveyance or devise. The later two-year possessor has not completed the 10-year period and is not in privity with Esther, so he does not own the lot. A quiet-title action is not the source of the adverse possessor's title; it is a way to obtain judicial recognition of title. A later oral apology or oral waiver to the former record owner does not transfer the vested land title because a surrender or transfer of an interest in land generally must be in writing or arise by operation of law.

The authority floor is satisfied by real property and Statute of Frauds authority. In Nimro v. Holden, the Maryland Court of Special Appeals relied on Trustees of Broadfording Church of the Brethren v. Western Maryland Railway Co., 262 Md. 84, 277 A.2d 276 (1971), for the rule that title through adverse possession is acquired when the limitation period expires, by operation of law, without any need for a judicial determination, and that the title can be alienated by conveyance or devise. The same opinion also states the vested-title point that adverse possession title is not divested by parol abandonment, re-entry by the former owner, or failure to continue possession. Restatement (Second) of Contracts sections 110(1)(d), 125, and 127 frame land-interest transfers within the Statute of Frauds, and statutes such as Neb. Rev. Stat. section 36-103 codify the same writing principle for interests in land, including assignments and surrenders. - Civil procedure: quiet title is procedural confirmation, not a condition precedent to ownership.
- Possession doctrine: Paul's later hostile possession may start a clock but does not complete title.

## review_truth
Once adverse possession vests title, the possessor may convey that title by deed without first getting a quiet-title decree, and oral words do not transfer land title.
