## final_student_script

Court-ruling layer. The affidavit is offered to the judge as foundation for admissibility. Do not hunt for a hearsay exception for the affidavit. Gold Key: Rule 104(a) lets the judge consider hearsay on preliminary admissibility questions. **B**.

## remediation_card

**REM-EVIDENCE-PRELIM-104-01 — Preliminary-question layer before hearsay exceptions**

**Signal:** foundation evidence offered to the court while the judge rules on admissibility.
**Student move:** ask whether the judge is deciding admissibility before asking whether the foundation evidence has a hearsay exception.
**Tiny rule:** on preliminary admissibility questions, the judge may consider hearsay unless privilege rules block it.
**Trap:** exception hunting on the foundation affidavit.
**Confidence:** PURE_ANCHOR.

## Gold Key(s)

**GK-EVIDENCE-PRELIM-HEARSAY-01**
For a preliminary admissibility question, the judge may consider hearsay; Rule 104(a) frees the court from the evidence rules except privilege.
Type: `rule`. Trigger: foundation evidence offered to the court for an admissibility ruling. Authority: FRE 104(a).

**GK-EVIDENCE-DYING-CIVIL-02**
A federal dying declaration is not limited to homicide prosecutions; it may also apply in a civil case when the declarant is unavailable and believed death was imminent.
Type: `exception`. Trigger: a statement under belief of imminent death offered in a civil wrongful-death case. Authority: FRE 804(b)(2).

## Silver Key(s)

**SK-EVIDENCE-BANNER-DRONE-01**
Lock the layer: the call asks what the judge may consider while ruling, not whether the affidavit is admissible to the jury.
Type: `call_focus`. Trigger: “considered by the court in ruling on admissibility.”
