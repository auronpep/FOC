### 6. Full Right-Answer Explanation

B is correct. Paul's statement was made on a medical intake questionnaire supplied by his physician during a routine checkup. It described medical history and past symptoms that were reasonably pertinent to diagnosis or treatment: clamminess and squeezing chest pain the previous evening. That is exactly the FRE 803(4) lane.

The photocopy problem does not block admission. Under FRE 1003, a duplicate is admissible to the same extent as the original unless there is a genuine question about authenticity or it would be unfair to use the duplicate. The stem gives no such problem.

Gold Key: FRE 803(4) covers medical history and past symptoms, not just symptoms happening at the exact moment of the visit, when the statement is made for diagnosis or treatment and is reasonably pertinent to it.

Gold Key: A photocopy/duplicate does not require a separate showing that the original is unavailable unless authenticity or fairness is genuinely in dispute.

Silver Key: When the choices split into "Yes" reasons and "No" reasons, do not stop at the admissibility direction. The winning answer must survive both gates: the hearsay exception and the duplicate rule.
