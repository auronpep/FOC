### 1. Final question

Daniel owned a secluded Christian retreat cabin site beside a lake. He conveyed the cabin site to Esther by deed. The deed gave Esther's site a right to cross a private gravel lane and footbridge that served several cabin owners. Esther later refused to pay, arguing that the sentence was only about the crossing privilege. The dispute concerns only the payment sentence. ### 2. Distilled Core Question

When a deed gives a landowner a crossing right but separately obligates the owner and successors to pay a share of maintenance costs for the crossing, what land-interest category does the payment obligation belong in?

### 7. Full right-answer explanation (exam-register)

**Gold Key:** A payment duty to maintain land-related facilities, imposed on owners and successors, is an affirmative real covenant; it is not a profit, license, or negative easement.

**Silver Key:** Classify the clause by what it commands, not by the adjacent use right: payment points to covenant; permission points to license.

The payment sentence commands Esther, her heirs, and assigns to pay one-third of reasonable annual repair costs for the lane and footbridge. That is an affirmative promise tied to land-related maintenance, not a right to take resources, a mere permission, or a restraint on use. The separate crossing right may be routed as an easement, but the call isolates the payment sentence, so the payment sentence routes as an affirmative real covenant.

### 8. Full wrong-answer explanations

**B — profit.** The attractive move is to connect the payment with preservation of the lane and footbridge. The breaker is that a profit lets the holder take resources from land; this clause commands payment and removes nothing.

**C — negative easement.** The attractive move is to treat the payment as discouraging careless use of the crossing. The breaker is that a negative easement restrains a use of land; this clause imposes an affirmative payment duty.

**D — license.** The attractive move is to follow the nearby crossing privilege and classify the whole deed as permission. The breaker is the call focus: the dispute concerns only the payment sentence. A license is permission to use land; this sentence is a promise to pay. **Silver Key:** classify by command, not by the adjacent use right.
