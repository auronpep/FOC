## Full Black-Letter-Law Verification And Legal Reasoning

The tested rule is the condition-subsequent/power-of-termination distinction plus the supplied modern transferability rule. A fee simple subject to condition subsequent does not end automatically when the condition occurs; the holder of the right of entry, also called a power of termination, must take action to reclaim the estate. Cornell Wex states the exam-level distinction: for a fee simple subject to condition subsequent, the grantor can take action to reclaim ownership, while a fee simple determinable ends immediately and automatically. Restatement of Property section 155 defines a power of termination as a future interest created in the transferor when an estate is transferred subject to a condition subsequent. Restatement of Property section 153 treats powers of termination as future interests, and Restatement (Third) of Property: Wills and Other Donative Transfers section 25.2 groups retained interests such as rights of entry as reversionary interests.

On assignability, the item expressly supplies the governing jurisdictional rule: powers of termination may be assigned. That rule controls the answer. Modern authority recognizes that rights of entry may be treated as property interests and, in jurisdictions that allow alienability, assigned like other property interests. In *El Dorado Land Co. v. City of McKinney*, 395 S.W.3d 798 (Tex. 2013), the Texas Supreme Court described a right of reentry as a future interest that can become possessory after termination of a fee simple subject to condition subsequent and recognized Texas's rule that rights of reentry are freely assignable like other property interests.

Authority layer: Restatement of Property sections 153 and 155; Restatement (Third) of Property: Wills and Other Donative Transfers section 25.2; *El Dorado Land Co. v. The legal issue and credited outcome are unchanged: the assignee of a validly assigned power of termination may enforce the power after the condition is breached, and the estate does not terminate automatically.
