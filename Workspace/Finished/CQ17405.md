# Distilled Core Question

A defendant intentionally kills another in a fit of rage after a public, heated confrontation. Does the heat-of-passion defense reduce murder to voluntary manslaughter?

# Review Truth

Adequate provocation is judged by the **objective** reasonable person standard: would a reasonable person lose self-control under those circumstances? The defendant's subjective emotional state does not determine adequacy.
