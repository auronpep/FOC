9. **Full black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning for the right answer**

The tested rule is statutory interpleader jurisdiction. 28 U.S.C. § 1335(a)(1) gives district courts original jurisdiction over an interpleader action when two or more adverse claimants of diverse citizenship claim the money or property, and § 1335(a)(2) requires the stakeholder to deposit the money or property in the court registry or give a proper bond. State Farm Fire & Cas. Co. v. Tashire, 386 U.S. 523, 530 (1967), confirms that statutory interpleader requires only minimal diversity among claimants. Fed. R. Civ. - B recovery: deposit is not forbidden; statutory interpleader permits deposit or a proper bond.
- C recovery: the stakeholder's shared citizenship with one claimant does not defeat statutory interpleader when claimant diversity and deposit/bond are present.

## outline_mastery_map

Placement: 95070301 - Pretrial Procedures > Multiple Parties and Claims > Joinder of claims.

## crossover_intersection_map

Civil Procedure crossover: Rule 22 interpleader, statutory interpleader under 28 U.S.C. § 1335, and the difference between claimant diversity and stakeholder diversity.

Jurisdiction crossover: statutory interpleader's minimal diversity does not require the stakeholder to be diverse from every claimant.

Pretrial procedure crossover: depositing the fund with the court supports statutory interpleader rather than defeating it.

## review_truth

For statutory interpleader, diversity is measured among the adverse claimants, not between the stakeholder and every claimant, and the stakeholder must deposit the fund or give bond.
