This item teaches that conviction evidence can do two jobs when both routes are independently satisfied.

Fills:

* dishonesty-conviction impeachment;
* other acts for identity;
* dual-purpose matrix handling.

## crossover_intersection_map

Evidence crossover: impeachment and character/other-acts.

C3 crossover: answer-array Silver Key plus Gold Key escalation.

## review_truth

A fraud conviction can impeach a testifying witness when it required dishonesty or false statement, and a similar prior act can also prove identity when the defendant claims someone else committed the charged act.
