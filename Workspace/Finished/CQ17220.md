## A) PASS-1 TRANSFORM REPORT

### 1. Final Question

In a federal civil action, Lydia must prove that Paul received a written notice canceling a paid contract to tailor choir robes for a private Easter concert. Lydia introduces evidence that the notice was sealed in an envelope addressed to Paul, properly stamped, and dropped into a mailbox. Paul then testifies that he never received the notice.

What effect should Paul's testimony have?

### 2. Distilled Core Question

In a federal civil case, what happens to a mailing presumption after the opposing party testifies that the notice was not received?

### 3. Final Answer Choices

A. The court must find that Paul did not receive the notice because his denial is direct evidence and Lydia's mailing proof is only circumstantial.

B. Paul now has the burden of persuading the jury that he did not receive the notice.

D. The jury must find that Paul received the notice because Lydia established the mailing presumption.

### 4. Correct Answer

### 6. Full Right-Answer Explanation

C is correct. Lydia's mailing proof creates a federal civil presumption of receipt. Paul's denial gives the jury evidence of nonreceipt, so the presumption no longer requires a finding for Lydia. The factfinder may still believe Lydia's mailing proof, but Lydia keeps the burden of persuasion.

Gold Key: FRE 301 shifts only the burden of production. It does not shift the burden of persuasion unless another federal statute or rule says so.

Silver Key: When the answer choices fight over "must," "now has the burden," and "may," lock onto who decides weight after rebuttal: the jury does, and the original burden-holder keeps persuasion.

### 7. Full Wrong-Answer Explanations

D is attractive because the mailing facts sound complete and official: addressed, stamped, and mailed. The breaker is that a rebutted federal civil presumption is not conclusive. Gold Key: FRE 301 makes Paul come forward with rebuttal evidence; it does not force the jury to find receipt after he does.

B is attractive because Paul's denial feels like it should make him prove the opposite fact. The breaker is the direction of the burden. FRE 301 gives Paul a production burden only; Lydia's persuasion burden stays with Lydia.

A is attractive because direct testimony sounds stronger than circumstantial mailing proof. The breaker is that evidence type does not decide weight automatically. The jury may credit either proof once both sides have evidence in the record.

### 8. Black-Letter Verification And Legal Reasoning

The tested rule, legal issue, and credited outcome are unchanged from the source item. Federal Rule of Evidence 301 provides that, in a civil case, the party against whom a presumption is directed has the burden of producing evidence to rebut it, but the rule does not shift the burden of persuasion, which remains on the party who had it originally. See Fed. R. Evid. 301, available through Cornell Legal Information Institute: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28a/courtrules-Evid/article-III.

Here, Lydia carries the underlying burden to prove receipt of the cancellation notice. Proof of proper mailing supports a presumption of receipt. Paul's testimony that he never received the notice is enough rebuttal evidence to satisfy production. The jury may then decide whether receipt occurred, but Lydia still bears persuasion. The credited answer remains C after the shuffle.
