### 9. Black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning for the right answer

The supplied row tests QID 19590, a Torts item on compensable property damage in negligence. The official key is C: the cracked panel and bent wheel are compensable property damage.

Negligence requires (1) duty, (2) breach, (3) causation, and (4) damages. Damages may take the form of bodily injury, property damage, or, in some cases, economic loss. Property damage is actual harm: the owner has been deprived of the full use and value of the property. See, e.g., Restatement (Second) of Torts §§ 217–218 (personal-property harms). The size of the loss affects the amount of recovery, not the existence of liability. The transformed outcome is identical: $740 in repair costs is compensable property damage.
