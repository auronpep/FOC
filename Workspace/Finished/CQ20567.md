Mary, a citizen of State Jordan, runs a private business that rents hand-painted “Psalms & Parables” stage panels for paid community programs. She sued Barnabas, a citizen of State Carmel, in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of State Carmel for breach of a paid restoration contract involving those panels.

Federal venue statute § 1391 provides that venue is proper in a federal district where a substantial part of the events or omissions giving rise to the claim occurred. **Which venue rule should the federal court apply?**

## 8. Full black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning

The credited outcome is unchanged from the original: the federal venue statute controls. Section 1391 states that it governs venue for civil actions in federal district courts and permits venue in a district where a substantial part of the events or omissions giving rise to the claim occurred. Here, the events occurred in the Northern District, so § 1391 supplies the controlling federal venue rule. ([Legal Information Institute][1])

The Erie analysis does not displace a valid federal statute that directly controls the issue. In *Stewart Organization, Inc. v. Plumer* likewise separates the unguided Erie inquiry from situations where valid federal procedural law directly governs. ([Justia Law][3])
