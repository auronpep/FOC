### 8. Full Black-Letter Verification And Legal Reasoning

The tested rule, legal issue, and credited outcome are unchanged. This is a Civil Procedure removal item testing the 30-day removal period after an initially nonremovable diversity case becomes removable.

Under 28 U.S.C. 1441(a), a defendant may remove a state-court civil action to the proper federal district court when the district court would have original jurisdiction. Under 28 U.S.C. 1332(a), diversity jurisdiction requires an amount in controversy exceeding $75,000 and citizens of different states. Under 28 U.S.C. 1446(b)(3), if the case stated by the initial pleading is not removable, the notice of removal may be filed within 30 days after the defendant receives an amended pleading, motion, order, or other paper from which removability may first be ascertained.
