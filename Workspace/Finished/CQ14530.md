### 7. Full right-answer explanation

**B is correct.** The call gives away the lane: Ruth is assumed to win. That means the option fight is over. The only live task is the remedy. **Silver Key:** when the stem says to assume the buyer prevails, stop fighting option formation and price the property bargain, not the option paper. **Gold Key:** a winning buyer’s land-sale expectancy is measured by the fair market value of the contracted-for property minus the contract price, plus any proper incidental or consequential losses. Here, the contract price was $470,000, so the recoverable base is the amount, if any, by which the Lantern Courtyard’s fair market value exceeded $470,000. A court applying expectation damages asks for the loss in value of the promised performance, and land-sale buyer damages are commonly expressed as market value of the contracted-for property minus the contract price.
