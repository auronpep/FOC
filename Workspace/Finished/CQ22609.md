## 6. Full Right-Answer Explanation

B is correct. Race-based action by a public employer normally triggers strict scrutiny, but this fact pattern gives the narrow fact that matters: the same government department was found to have discriminated against applicants of Korean heritage. A temporary hiring preference in a consent decree can be constitutional when it is directed at remedying that employer's own proven discrimination.

Gold Key: Government race classifications are subject to strict scrutiny, but a specific, identified past violation by the same government entity can be a compelling remedial interest; general diversity or societal discrimination is not enough.
