### 9. Black-letter verification and right-answer legal reasoning

The outline routing is **33040400**, the Evidence code for **Hearsay > Confrontation Clause**; the code reference expressly routes the Confrontation Clause as a hearsay bar in a criminal trial to 33040400 rather than Criminal Procedure. 

Under Crawford, testimonial statements by absent witnesses are admitted only when the declarant is unavailable and the defendant had a prior opportunity to cross-examine. Crawford also identifies business records as the kind of hearsay exception generally covering statements that are not testimonial by nature. ([Legal Information Institute][1])

Grand-jury testimony and police interrogation about past events for prosecution are testimonial. The Supreme Court materials summarized in the Sixth Amendment confrontation discussion identify prior grand-jury testimony and police interrogations as core testimonial statements. ([Justia Law][2])

Current Confrontation Clause framing remains testimonial-hearsay focused. Smith v. Arizona reaffirmed that the Clause bars absent-witness testimonial statements unless the witness is unavailable and there was a prior chance for cross-examination; Smith concerns substitute expert testimony and does not change the ordinary business-record result here. ([Justia Law][3])

B: Student treats oath and formality as a reliability cure. Recovery: ask whether the testimony was subject to prior cross-examination.

C: Student treats video accuracy as confrontation compliance. Recovery: ask whether the statement was made in a police interview about past events for prosecution.
