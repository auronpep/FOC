The tested issue remains Criminal Law and Procedure, Inchoate Crimes, Conspiracy, with verified outline code `71040200`. The MBE/common-law rule is that conspiracy is a distinct offense and generally does not merge into the attempted or completed target offense. Conspiracy punishes the agreement; attempt punishes a substantial step taken with intent to commit the target crime.

Leading authority supports the separate-offense framing. In *Iannelli v.
