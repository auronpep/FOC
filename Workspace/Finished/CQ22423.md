## 7. Full right-answer explanation (exam-register)

**B is correct.** Lydia's rush order was an offer to buy goods for immediate shipment. Under the UCC, a seller can normally accept that kind of offer by promptly shipping goods, even nonconforming goods. **Gold Key:** the accommodation exception changes the result. If the seller seasonably notifies the buyer that the nonconforming shipment is offered only as an accommodation, the shipment is not an acceptance. It is a counteroffer.

Barnabas shipped only 250 bookmarks instead of the 600 Lydia ordered, and the note traveled with the shipment. The note expressly said Barnabas did not have 600 bookmarks and was sending 250 as an accommodation for Lydia's deadline. That notice prevents the shipment from operating as an acceptance. No enforceable contract was formed on Lydia's original order unless Lydia accepts Barnabas's counteroffer.

**Silver Key:** When a seller ships less or different goods, pause before applying the default "shipment equals acceptance" rule. Look for the attached note. Accommodation language turns the shipment into a counteroffer.

## 8. Full wrong-answer explanations

### Choice A

This answer is attractive because Lydia really did place a clear rush order, and an order can be the first step in contract formation. The breaker is that an order is only an offer. It does not form a contract by itself. The live question is whether Barnabas accepted that offer, and his accommodation notice kept the short shipment from being an acceptance.

### Choice C

This answer is attractive because it reaches the same "No" result and points to a true fact: Barnabas did not ship the ordered quantity. The breaker is the reason. A nonconforming shipment alone can still accept a goods order under UCC 2-206, creating a contract and breach. What prevents formation here is not simply the short quantity; it is the seasonable accommodation notice.

### Choice D (Dominant Trap)

This answer is attractive because UCC 2-206 normally allows acceptance by prompt shipment, and the seller did ship some of the ordered goods. The breaker is the accommodation exception. **Gold Key:** shipment of nonconforming goods is not acceptance if the seller seasonably notifies the buyer that the shipment is offered only as an accommodation. ## 9. Full black-letter-law verification AND legal reasoning for the right answer

**Governing authority:** UCC 2-206(1)(b). The rule appears in the model UCC text at Cornell LII, https://www.law.cornell.edu/ucc/2/2-206, and in enacted state code examples such as D.C. Code 28:2-206, https://code.dccouncil.gov/us/dc/council/code/sections/28:2-206.

**Rule:** An order or other offer to buy goods for prompt or current shipment invites acceptance by a prompt promise to ship or by prompt shipment of conforming or nonconforming goods. But a shipment of nonconforming goods does not constitute acceptance if the seller seasonably notifies the buyer that the shipment is offered only as an accommodation to the buyer.

**Application:** Lydia's rush order for 600 bookmarks was an offer to buy goods for immediate shipment. Barnabas shipped 250 bookmarks, making the shipment nonconforming by quantity. The shipment was accompanied by a notice saying Barnabas did not have 600 bookmarks and was sending 250 as an accommodation for Lydia's deadline. That notice satisfies the accommodation exception. The shipment therefore did not accept Lydia's offer. It operated as a counteroffer, so the parties did not form an enforceable contract on Lydia's original order.

When a goods seller ships less or different goods, do not stop at the shipment. Check whether the note turns the response into an accommodation counteroffer.

Navigates the default shipment-as-acceptance rule versus the accommodation exception.

Fills:

## crossover_intersection_map

- UCC 2-508 cure: cure presupposes a contract and breach; accommodation prevents acceptance.
- UCC 2-601 perfect tender: buyer rejection rules matter after tender under a contract; this item is earlier, at formation.
- UCC 2-712 cover: cover is a breach remedy and does not arise when the shipment is only a counteroffer.

## review_truth

Under UCC 2-206(1)(b), a seller who ships nonconforming goods with a seasonable notice that the shipment is offered only as an accommodation has made a counteroffer, not an acceptance.
