## 9. Black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning

**Tested rule:** Under M'Naghten's Case, 8 Eng. Rep. 718 (1843), insanity excuses only when, because of disease of the mind, the defendant did not know the nature and quality of the act or did not know the act was wrong. The University of Minnesota Law Library's classic-case page identifies M'Naghten's Case as 8 Eng. Rep. 718 and summarizes the right-wrong/nature-and-quality formulation.
