**Full right-answer explanation (exam-register)**

D is correct. Under FRE 401, evidence is relevant only if it tends to make a fact of consequence more or less probable than it would be without the evidence. The fact of consequence here is whether Stephen holds racist views. The donation to a general community relief fund — a fund Stephen had no specific reason to believe would direct aid to minority citizens in particular, and that served many neighborhoods, only some of which were substantially minority — does not tend to make the non-racism fact more probable. At most, the evidence shows Stephen is charitable, a trait that is not at issue. Relevance fails, and the testimony is inadmissible. 

Silver Key: When two choices both exclude the evidence, find the first gate that fails. FRE 401 (relevance) is checked before FRE 404/405 (character). If the evidence does not tend to prove a material fact, relevance fails and the character-method rules are never reached.
