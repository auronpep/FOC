### 9. Black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning for the right answer

The supplied row tests QID 19455, a Civil Procedure item on Rule 15(d) supplemental pleadings. The official key is C: the court may permit a supplemental pleading for the later termination event on just terms.

Rule 15(d) of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure provides that the court may, on just terms, permit a party to serve a supplemental pleading setting out any transaction, occurrence, or event that happened after the date of the pleading to be supplemented. The rule is the principal mechanism for adding post-filing events to an existing action; Rule 15(a) governs amendments to existing pleadings, while Rule 15(d) governs supplemental pleadings for new events. The advisory committee notes explain that supplementation is appropriate where the new events are connected to the original pleading and the court can impose just terms to avoid prejudice. The transformed outcome is identical: the alleged retaliation is a post-filing event that may be added by supplemental pleading on just terms.
