### 7. Full right-answer explanation

D is correct. Hannah knows the check is forged, presents it as genuine, and does so to get money. Gold Key: uttering punishes the knowing use or presentation of a forged writing as genuine; the defendant does not have to be the person who made the forgery. Silver Key: separate the making act from the passing act before choosing the offense label.

### 8. Full wrong-answer explanations

A sells the inspection excuse. That is attractive because the bank still has a chance to catch the false check. The breaker is that uttering is the knowing offer of the forged instrument as genuine with intent to defraud; successful payment is not required.

B sells the maker-only view of forgery crimes. That is attractive because Hannah did not create the false signature. The breaker is the Gold Key: uttering covers the later presentation of a known forged writing as genuine.

C sells a proceeds-sharing frame. That is attractive because Hannah hopes to split cash with her cousin. The breaker is that embezzlement requires lawful possession followed by conversion; Hannah is passing a forged instrument, not converting property entrusted to her.

### 9. Full black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning

The tested rule, legal issue, and credited outcome are unchanged. Uttering occurs when a person offers, passes, or presents a forged instrument as genuine, knowing it is forged, with intent to defraud.

The authority floor is satisfied by rule-number statutes. Pennsylvania 18 Pa.C.S. 4101(a)(3) includes uttering a writing known to be forged within forgery. Florida Stat. 831.02 separately defines uttering forged instruments as uttering and publishing a false, forged, or altered writing as true while knowing it is false or forged and intending to defraud. California Penal Code 470(d) likewise covers uttering, publishing, passing, attempting, or offering to pass a forged check or similar instrument as true and genuine while knowing it is false and acting with intent to defraud. Hannah's conduct fits that rule because she knowingly presents the forged check as genuine for cash.
