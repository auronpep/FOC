## 7. Full Right-Answer Explanation

B is correct. Central Hudson starts with a threshold question: is the commercial speech about lawful activity and not misleading? If the answer is no, the speech does not receive First Amendment protection, and the court does not move to the substantial-interest, direct-advancement, or tailoring steps.

Timothy's advertisement offered stolen goods for sale. The proposed transaction was unlawful. Gold Key: commercial speech only enters the protected Central Hudson track if it concerns lawful activity and is not misleading. Silver Key: do not start at the prongs about government interest and fit until the ad clears the lawful-activity gate. Because this ad failed at the gate, the First Amendment challenge fails.
