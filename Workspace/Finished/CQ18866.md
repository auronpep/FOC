## 9. Full Black-Letter-Law Verification and Legal Reasoning

The governing subject is Real Property, adverse possession. The verified outline placement is `84040101`, listed verbatim in `OUTLINE_CODES_COMPLETE.md` under Titles, Deeds, and Conveyancing > Adverse Possession > Requirements.

The black-letter rule is unchanged from the source row. A claimant acquires title by adverse possession only by possessing land in a manner that is actual, open and notorious, exclusive, hostile, and continuous for the statutory period. A temporary absence does not necessarily destroy continuity when it is consistent with the claimant's continued dominion. But an interruption caused by the true owner's ouster is different: it breaks the required continuity and the claimant must start a new statutory period.
