# Block 2: 17-section Case Study
1. **Source QID:** 14831
2. **Legal Subject:** Evidence (Competency)
3. **Black-Letter Rule:** Every person is competent to testify if they have personal knowledge (FRE 602) and understand the duty to testify truthfully (FRE 603).
4. **Original Fact Pattern:** Child victim of carjacking; prosecution wants to admit testimony; defendant objects based on age/competency.
5. **Original Call:** "The son's testimony should be"
6. **CUT (Substantive Change):** Child victim/carjacking -> Child witness to church arson. Defendant/Father -> Church Janitor.
7. **CLASH (Divergent Conflict):** The clash between the natural desire to protect a child witness from testifying against her father and the legal standard of witness competency.
8. **CALL (Variant Prompt):** "The child's testimony should be:"
9. **Divergence Strategy:** Use a highly emotional family conflict within a church setting to test the threshold for child competency.
10. **Choice A Logic (Correct):** Correctly identifies the two legal pillars of competency: personal knowledge and the oath/truth-telling obligation.
11. **Choice B Logic (Distractor):** Baits with "cognitive ability/age," which is the most common student error when evaluating child competency.
12. **Choice C Logic (Distractor):** Baits with "unfairly prejudicial," highlighting the emotional impact of a child testifying against a parent, which isn't the FRE 403 standard.
13. **Choice D Logic (Distractor):** Baits with "insufficiently probative," incorrectly tying the value of testimony to the age of the witness.
14. **Original Pick Rates:** B (Correct) was the original key (85%), D (11%).
15. **Mapped Pick Rates (Inherited/Predicted):** A: 85% (Inherited/Mapped), B: 10% (Predicted), C: 2% (Predicted), D: 3% (Predicted).
16. **Dominant Trap Identification:** Choice (B).
17. **Review Truth Validation:** Matches the standard application of FRE 601-603.
