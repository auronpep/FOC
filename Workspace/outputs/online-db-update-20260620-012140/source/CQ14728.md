## 5. Correct answer

## 6. Full right-answer explanation

D is correct.

**Gold Key:** When a person with the required criminal intent causes an innocent person to do the physical act, the guilty person can be treated as committing the offense through an innocent agent. The innocent person’s lack of mens rea does not erase the guilty person’s liability.

**Silver Key:** When the answer choices form a role-label ladder — no crime, attempt, accessory, innocent agent — ask two facts only: who had the guilty mind, and whose hand moved the property?

Here, Lydia had the guilty mind. Timothy supplied the physical movement of the projector, but he thought it belonged to Lydia. Timothy’s innocence is not a shield for Lydia. Lydia used Timothy as the hand that carried out her taking. The completed carrying away occurred when Timothy carried Peter’s projector out the front door.

## 7. Full wrong-answer explanations

### A. Not guilty of larceny or attempted larceny, because Timothy did not commit any crime.

A is wrong.

It correctly notices that Timothy did not commit a crime. But that is the trap. The question is not whether Timothy is guilty. The question is whether Lydia is guilty. **Gold Key:** Timothy’s innocence does not erase Lydia’s liability if Lydia used Timothy as an innocent agent.

### B. Not guilty of larceny but guilty of attempted larceny, because Lydia never acquired possession of the projector.

B is wrong.

This is the dominant trap. It focuses on Lydia’s hands instead of Lydia’s agency. Lydia did not need to personally hold the projector. Timothy’s carrying was enough if Lydia used him as her innocent agent. **Silver Key:** do not ask whose hands touched the property first; ask whose guilty mind directed the taking.

### C. Guilty of larceny as an accessory to Timothy.

C is wrong.

This gets the result partly right — Lydia is criminally responsible — but gives the wrong role label. Timothy lacked the guilty mind, so he is not the principal thief to whom Lydia is merely an accessory. Lydia is the principal actor through Timothy as an innocent agent.

## 8. Black-letter-law verification and legal reasoning

Lawyer-confirmation layer: larceny at common law is the unlawful taking of another’s property with intent to deprive the owner of it. Cornell’s Wex summary states that larceny is the common-law illegal taking of another’s property with intent to deprive the owner. ([Legal Information Institute][1]) The innocent-agent principle is also consistent with Model Penal Code § 2.06(2)(a), which treats a person as legally accountable when, acting with the culpability sufficient for the offense, the person causes an innocent or irresponsible person to engage in the conduct.
